# The brief's frame

What every brief says, by kind; `tools/brief.py` composes one from this
file and the queue. **The pass writes this file**, under `tools/explore/test_brief.py`'s ceiling (1226); a row's
number is its checklist row (`docs/audit/README.md`).

## opening

You are a worker lane on the attrition repo, running on **{model}**. Your
item is **{item}**, on {track}. I am `commander`, on `worktree-replan-pdb`; your base is `{base}`.

Read `docs/QUEUE.md`'s opener, then what the item cites.

## booked

**The frame and the draw delta are the booking; the rest is the item
before's hypothesis** — a mechanism, **the direction**: which side
acted (778, 1076), which made a record, with the record that shows it. **A type or object a booking names is a
supposition too** (1213): the arm's predicate says what enters.
**Name each unit a booking names by `TypeIndex` and domain before
reading its chain** (1267): `RON_DEBUG_UNIT` prints `TI`, `TY` and the
name (1846).

## lanes

**Code is not fenced** (DECISIONS 55): touch what your word needs;
the lanes below say where other words sit; a build where theirs
touches: one line to me. **Grep the integration branch, `docs/AI.md` and the
journals for a function before building it** (1601). **Your
