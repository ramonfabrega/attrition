# The brief's frame

What every brief says, by the kind of brief that says it; `tools/brief.py`
composes one from this file and the queue. **The steering pass writes this
file**, under `tools/explore/test_brief.py`'s ceiling (1226); a row's
number is its row in `docs/audit/README.md`'s brief checklist.

## opening

You are a worker lane on the attrition repo, running on **{model}**. Your
item is **{item}**, on {track}. I am the `attrition` session on
`worktree-replan-pdb`, your commander; your base is `{base}`.

`CLAUDE.md` is yours already. Read `docs/QUEUE.md`'s opener, then what
is listed under "Read first", then the documents the item's words cite.

## booked

**The frame and the draw delta are the booking. Anything else in it is
the item before's hypothesis** — a mechanism, and **the direction too**:
which side acted (778, 1076). An event the booking names is cited with
the record that shows it, or it is not yet an event. **A type or an
object a booking names is a supposition too** (1213): a chapter is its
census row's functions, and the listing of the arm's predicate says what
enters it. **Name each unit the booking names by its `TypeIndex` and
domain before reading its chain** (1267): the `GUY` block's `type`, then
`enums/TypeIndex.txt`; a type number says its keying.

## lanes

**Code is not fenced** (`docs/DECISIONS.md` entry 55). Touch what your
word needs, in any module. The lanes below are where the other words
sit, as information: if your build lands in a function theirs is likely
in, say so in one line and carry on. Take `ccc update` before your gate
and measure on the merged tree; a conflict in code is yours to resolve
if you land second.

## before-reading

Before any reading of the original, in this order:

1. **Say the draw delta on the word's frame**, both sides, from the
   trace, and **ask the trace for the frame's seeds**: a draw record's
   seed makes every roll on the frame readable (922). A completion's
   draws take the two consecutive blocks' `BUILDDATA` beside them (926).
2. **Print both sides' event on the word's frame** (1076, 1085, 1088,
   1097): both order stacks (`RON_STACKS`); the arguments of the call a
   value parts through; ours' rounds where the first row is a wound
   (`RON_DEBUG_AMMO`); the caller of a `kill_current_order` that ended a
   chase on one side.
3. **List every instance of the parting event on the disk** (1058) —
   every strike on the target, every launch of the piece — and **grep
   each value the booking names in the dump** before any instrument is
   booked (737).
4. **Read the first parting's field list, not its unit** (758), both
   directions, every unit on the block; **walk the object back to its
   first parted field** before calling a block quiet (1046). **A first
   row is no cause until the same key is read on every other unit of
   the window** (1155); **an actor that is gone parts no key** — list
   the units on one block and not on the next (1199).
   A parked row on the word's frames is a candidate (1060); a captain's
   members' rows are read on the same block (958); a group move names
   the slot's `GROUPDATA` both sides of the gap (875); a route parting
   reads every proxied step on disk (914).
5. **Count the writers of the field that parted**, by its offset in
   every spelling, never by its name alone (823, 869); rows booked under
   one mechanism have their writers counted first (889).
6. **Grep the compared pin for the word's record** (772), and **pipe the
   widening's `RON_FIRSTS=1` print through `python3 tools/standing.py`**
   — a chapter's `chNN f…` print too (1250): a standing field this crate
   reads is named in your journal with both sides' values (769, 834,
   1162, 1187). A row three widenings pin is a rule, not a residue
   (1176). **A row that agrees is quiet, not proven** while another
   writer reached the state or a value it reads is wrong elsewhere (898,
   1039).
7. **Run `python3 tools/seams.py --item {item} --field <first parted
   field>`** (writers, readers) and `--unwritten <Struct>` (1397,
   1382, 1400): `SEAM`s, a comment saying "not
   modelled" or "a seam" without the word, such paragraphs or lists of
   a specification (830, 860, 1146, 1159, 1193, 1253, 1240, 1340, 1337,
   1322). **When one
   side spent nothing on the frame, run it on the chain of the order the
   other side took** (1233). A seam that says no capture holds its arm is
   an absence as of the day it was written: run its `scan:`, or write one
   (1132).
8. **A parked row is the item before's wording, never a citation**:
   grep its nouns — a function against the export's index, a `NEVER`
   against `blind.rs`, a rule against its document's struck text (1010,
   1033).

## reading

- **Arithmetic reads the listing first**, end to end (730, 766); a
  listing read in slices overlaps them by a line and trims headers by
  pattern (1032).
- **A premise that names a callee's tail cites the caller's branch**
  (933), names every entry to the block that holds the call (995), and
  asks which event each arm needs (879). A `SEAM` the listing can decide
  cites the listing's branch before it is written as an assumption (910,
  964).

- **An inlined idiom called "the same as" a named function has that
  function's listing quoted beside it** (1065). **A vtable slot the
  export names by a folded function is read off the PE** (1090). **A
  function whose export prints `unaff_` arguments is read at each call
  site's listing**, caller by caller (1166).

## capture

- **Grep the disk before booking a capture**; the booking cites what the
  disk could not answer. A packet for a round asks first whether the
  capture printed `AMMO` (1079, 783).
- **A value no dump prints takes a packet, not a detail capture**: at
  the word's own frame, read as a logger frame, or at the decision's
  tick, named with what was decided (`docs/EMULATOR.md` §8). The brief
  names the function it enters; `Game::do_frame` is not enterable (1026,
  685).
- **The window is sized to the word**: the six blocks before it for
  `samegame.py`, 250 after, stated in blocks (799). A stanza `check:`
  that pipes `samegame.py` into a count wraps the call in `(… || true)`
  (1080). A measurement names its instrument (881).
- **A capture names its lane with its cover**: never `cover=1` on the
  click-free lane (1047). A stanza with `@` lines runs on a DLL built
  from your own tree (936). A new pair's stanza names the `GAME INFO`
  line that carries its setting (979, 992).
- **Wait on a capture the way the harness can see**:
  `tools/gamelog/waitrun.sh <viadriver log>` backgrounded, its exit
  wakes you, then end your turn; read the log before acting on the exit.
  Minutes waited on another lane's capture go in your journal.
- **The lane says its own state** (1180): `source
  tools/gamelog/winelaunch.sh; ron_lane_state` prints `free`, `stale`
  or `held by`. **Wait for `free` or `stale`** — a stale lock is the
  next launch's to take over, never a hand's to remove; the click-free
  runner holds the lane through its restore and releases it after
  (1234). **A first take that dies before frame 0 is taken again once**
  (1150).

## chapter

- **A chapter is three arms of one staging at most.** If the booking
  names more, take its first three, tell me in one line, and leave the
  rest. **A chapter that opens with a parting ends there**: the staging
  walked, the capture taken, the word pinned with its widening, the
  section written; the word is the next item's, booked by its frame.
- **An issuer chapter's staging is walked before its capture** (794):
  off an existing dump when every predicate is a printed field (821), a
  staging-only packet otherwise (808), and through the command's own
  entry (885). A chapter whose precondition is staged by combat walks
  its staging first (828).
- **A staging booked on a function's name greps the function's writers
  and gates first** (903, 968).
- **`blind.rs` is a pin an issuer chapter moves** (950), and a document
  that cites an address only for context chooses between the address
  and the pin (943). A `cover=1` batch is ranked by the `NEVER` rows
  each staging names (1033).
- **A script that stages nothing still exits `success: true`** (1170):
  walk it with `stage_walk` first, and read a bare `tech` as the report
  it is. **A command to another player's units holds `be` across the
  pump** (1205): the seat is read at issue and again at the walk. **A
  take with `@` lines is read with `python3 tools/gamelog/issuesmatch.py
  <trace> --none-refused` first** (966, 1256).
- **A table row that will not fit says where the row goes** (932, 949).

## build

- **Per reading, a killer that tests the claim's own unit and that the
  readings disagree on** (789), run as a mutation on the built tree
  (864). **Run it through `tools/mutate.py`**, which refuses a dirty
  tree: commit first (784, 907, 1391).
- **A mutation is scored against the walk** — `cargo test --release -p
  rondata <the pin>` — and the landing names the pin that failed; `git
  diff --stat` is non-empty first, **and the mutation means what it is
  for** (918, 1018). **Score it by the exit code and the failed tests'
  names**, never by a pattern on the output. A mechanism you call built
  has a unit test that fails without it. A mutation no walk holds is a
  finding: name the arm; it is what the rules track stages next.
- **The tests a re-pin touches run green before a mutation is scored**
  (1083). A floor or widening test takes `let _pins = Pins::hold();` and
  `pin_eq!`/`pin_ne!`/`pin!`, so one run shows every pin that moved,
  and `tools/repin.py <log> --write` rewrites them (1349, 1411).
  "Stands from N" says whether N is the window's first block or the
  gap's first frame.
- **A gate's own test uses a key the measured table lacks** (1126). **A
  fixture for a sea or air unit sets the domain in all three fields** —
  the type's `kind`, the type's `combat`, the unit's `kind` (1130).
- **Your own test run is `--test-threads 2`, or under
  `tools/memcap.sh`** (1081, 1086, 1089); the thread-width guard's red
  is not your mutation's.
- **A divergence your change introduces that you can see is built, or
  named as a regression, never parked** (864); every closed chapter and
  every open word must hold. **A change on a shared path — a landing, a
  snap, a group move — runs the widenings whose captures reach it before
  the gate** (1313): `RON_FIRSTS` grepped for `inside_up` going to −1
  names the disembarks.
- **A command the worktree guard refuses is written to
  `$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/tmp/` and run from there; `no verdict (error)` is an
  outage — back off on a Monitor, five minutes and up to an hour**;
  what needs a human is one line to me, and the turn ends (1315).

## landing

- **The word moves with its value diff beside it**: the record, the
  field and both sides' values on the word's frame, or on the frame the
  state first parted, walked back from it. Re-pin the constant with the
  word's delta in its comment and the word's block in its widening.
- **Write the documents and the journal, commit, then gate.** The gate
  is `python3 tools/release_gate.py ~/code/fun/attrition/game
  --test-threads 4 --lane`, to a file, never piped, backgrounded; read
  its exit before you say anything landed, and quote its `Lane verdict:`
  and `Gate steps:` lines (969). Its verdict is a second commit.
- **When `ccc update` refuses on two sections appended at one anchor, or
  on a count two lanes re-pinned, merge by hand and keep both** (1161);
  a re-pinned count is measured again on the merged tree — the compared
  pin is the union of every window walked (1151). **Read your run's
  `docs/RUNS.md` section whole after the update**: the union merge
  splices (1285). **A landing that moves another lane's word says so**
  (1289).
- **A word that reaches its trace's end owes five things** (1217): an
  `ENDPOINTS` row and its `Endpoint` part, the `AI_WORDS` row closed,
  the compared pin's window dropped with its fields re-pinned, the
  coverage driver's word block a constant, and a capture of the last
  blocks.
- **The constants and their widenings are yours to pin; the queue's
  lines are mine.** Do not touch `docs/QUEUE.md`, `docs/PARKED.md`,
  `docs/JOURNAL.md` or `docs/DECISIONS.md`: report what should book and
  what should park, the next word by its frame and its delta, and I
  write them.
- **Your journal is `docs/journal/<the day you land>-item-{item}.md`**,
  yours alone. Name every module you touched. Put anything for the
  steering pass on one line headed "for the Loop".
- **When you are done, send me one line** — `ToolSearch("select:
  SendMessage")`, then `SendMessage` to `attrition` (1412): your tip
  SHA, that `git log <base>..<branch>` is non-empty, and the `Lane
  verdict:` and `Gate steps:` lines (1406). Ninety minutes after your
  spawn without a landing, send the step you are on and whether a gate,
  a packet or a capture runs.
