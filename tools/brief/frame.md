# The brief's frame

What every brief says, by kind; `tools/brief.py` composes one from this
file and the queue. **The pass writes this file**, under `tools/explore/test_brief.py`'s ceiling (1226); a row's
number is its checklist row (`docs/audit/README.md`).

## opening

You are a worker lane on the attrition repo, running on **{model}**. Your
item is **{item}**, on {track}. I am `commander`, on
`worktree-replan-pdb`; your base is `{base}`.

Read `docs/QUEUE.md`'s opener, then "Read first", then the documents
the item's words cite.

## booked

**The frame and the draw delta are the booking. Anything else in it is
the item before's hypothesis** — a mechanism, **the direction** — which
side acted (778, 1076), which side created a record — and an event it
names is cited with the record that shows it. **A type or an
object a booking names is a supposition too** (1213): a chapter is its
census row's functions, and the arm's predicate says what enters it.
**Name each unit the booking names by `TypeIndex` and domain before
reading its chain** (1267): the `GUY` block's `type`, then
`enums/TypeIndex.txt`; a type number says its keying.

## lanes

**Code is not fenced** (`docs/DECISIONS.md` entry 55): touch what your
word needs; the lanes below say where the other words sit, and a build
in a function theirs is likely in is one line to me. Take `ccc update`
before your gate, measure on the merged tree, and resolve a conflict
yourself if you land second.

## before-reading

Before any reading of the original, in this order:

1. **Say the draw delta on the word's frame**, both sides, from the
   trace, and **ask the trace for the frame's seeds**: a draw record's
   seed makes every roll readable (922). A completion's draws take the
   two consecutive blocks' `BUILDDATA` (926).
2. **Print both sides' event on the word's frame** (1076, 1085, 1088,
   1097): `RON_STACKS` both sides; the arguments of the call a value
   parts through; ours' rounds where the first row is a wound
   (`RON_DEBUG_AMMO`); the caller of the `kill_current_order` that ended
   a chase.
3. **List every instance of the parting event on disk** (1058) and
   **grep each value the booking names in the dump** before booking an
   instrument (737).
4. **Read the first parting's field list, not its unit** (758), both
   directions, every unit on the block; **walk the object back to its
   first parted field** before calling a block quiet (1046). **A first
   row is no cause until its key is read on every unit of the window**
   (1155); **a gone actor parts no key** — list the units on one block
   and not the next (1199).
   A parked row on the word's frames is a candidate (1060); a captain's
   members are read on the same block (958); a group move names
   the slot's `GROUPDATA` either side of the gap (875); a route parting
   reads every proxied step (914).
5. **Count the writers of the field that parted**, by its offset in
   every spelling, never by its name alone (823, 869); rows booked under
   one mechanism count their writers first (889).
6. **Grep the compared pin for the word's record** (772) **and
   `coverage.rs`'s unread list for the parted field** — a key there is
   compared by nothing, so quiet is blind (1475) — and **pipe the
   widening's `RON_FIRSTS=1` print through `python3 tools/standing.py`**,
   a chapter's `chNN f…` and a sibling's `standing …` too (1250, 1471): a
   standing field this crate reads goes in your journal with both sides'
   values (769, 834, 1162, 1187); a row three widenings pin is a rule,
   not a residue (1176); **a row that agrees is quiet, not proven** while
   another writer reached the state or a value it reads is wrong
   elsewhere (898, 1039).
7. **Run `python3 tools/seams.py --item {item} --field <first parted
   field>`** (writers, readers) and `--unwritten <Struct>` (1397,
   1382, 1400): `SEAM`s and a specification's gap paragraphs (830, 860,
   1146, 1159, 1193, 1253, 1240, 1340, 1337, 1322). **When one side spent
   nothing on the frame, run it on the chain of the order the other side
   took** (1233). A seam that says no capture holds its arm is
   dated: run its `scan:`, or write one (1132).
8. **A parked row is the item before's wording, never a citation**:
   grep its nouns — a function against the export's index, a `NEVER`
   against `blind.rs`, a rule against its document's struck text (1010,
   1033).

## reading

- **Arithmetic reads the listing first**, end to end (730, 766); a
  listing read in slices overlaps them by a line and trims headers by
  pattern (1032).
- **A premise that names a callee's tail cites the caller's branch**
  (933), names every entry to the block that holds the call (995), and
  asks which event each arm needs (879). A `SEAM` the listing can decide
  cites the listing's branch before it is written (910, 964).

- **An inlined idiom called "the same as" a named function has that
  function's listing beside it** (1065); **a vtable slot the export
  names by a folded function is read off the PE** (1090); **a function
  whose export prints `unaff_` arguments is read at each call site's
  listing** (1166).

## capture

- **Grep the disk before booking a capture**; the booking cites what the
  disk could not answer; a packet for a round first asks whether `AMMO`
  was printed (1079, 783).
- **A value no dump prints takes a packet, not a detail capture**: at
  the word's own frame, read as a logger frame, or at the decision's
  tick, named with what was decided (`docs/EMULATOR.md` §8); the brief
  names the function it enters, and `Game::do_frame` is not one (1026,
  685). **On a packet, run the unit's whole `process` first and read
  its call trace** (1526).
- **The window is sized to the word**: the six blocks before it for
  `samegame.py`, 250 after, stated in blocks (799). A stanza `check:`
  that pipes `samegame.py` into a count wraps the call in `(… || true)`
  (1080). A measurement names its instrument (881). **`--end-frame` is
  the game's last frame when the window reaches it** (1529); a lobby
  that ends itself takes `--allow-early-end` (1551).
- **A capture names its lane with its cover**: never `cover=1` on the
  click-free lane (1047). A stanza with `@` lines runs on a DLL built
  from your tree (936). A new pair's stanza names the `GAME INFO`
  line that carries its setting (979, 992).
- **Wait on a capture with `tools/gamelog/waitrun.sh <viadriver
  log>`**, backgrounded: its exit wakes you, so end your turn; read the
  log before acting on the exit; minutes waited on another lane's
  capture go in the journal.
- **The pool picks the lane** (1568, 1569): never set
  `RON_CAPTURE_LANE`; the receipt names the lane, `ron_lane_state` its
  state (1180); a stale lock is the next launch's, never a hand's; the
  runner holds the lane through its restore (1234). **A first take that dies before frame 0 is taken again once**
  (1150); a traceback with no receipt is that death, and `waitrun.sh`
  says so (1503). **No `mkdir -p` before a
  launch**: the runner refuses an existing output directory (1513).

## chapter

- **A chapter is three arms of one staging at most**: if the booking
  names more, take the first three and say so in one line. **A chapter
  that opens with a parting ends there**: staging walked, capture taken,
  word pinned with its widening, section written; the word is the next
  item's, by its frame.
- **An issuer chapter's staging is walked before its capture** (794):
  off an existing dump when every predicate is a printed field (821), a
  staging-only packet otherwise (808), and through the command's own
  entry (885). A chapter whose precondition is combat walks its staging
  first (828).
- **A staging booked on a function's name greps the function's writers
  and gates first** (903, 968).
- **`blind.rs` is a pin an issuer chapter moves** (950), and a document
  that cites an address for context alone chooses between the address
  and the pin (943). A `cover=1` batch is ranked by the `NEVER` rows
  each staging names (1033).
- **A script that stages nothing still exits `success: true`** (1170):
  walk it with `stage_walk` first; a bare `tech` is a report. **A command to another player's units holds `be` across the
  pump** (1205): the seat is read at issue and at the walk. **A
  take with `@` lines is read with `python3 tools/gamelog/issuesmatch.py
  <trace> --none-refused` first** (966, 1256).
- **A table row that will not fit says where the row goes** (932, 949).

## build

- **Per reading, a killer that tests the claim's own unit and that the
  readings disagree on** (789), run as a mutation on the built tree
  (864) **through `tools/mutate.py`**, which refuses a dirty tree —
  commit first (784, 907, 1391) — and a one-string command (1543).
- **A mutation is scored against the walk** — `cargo test --release -p
  rondata <the pin>` — and the landing names the pin that failed; `git
  diff --stat` is non-empty first, **and the mutation means what it is
  for** (918, 1018). **Score it by the exit code and the failed tests'
  names**, never by an output pattern. A mechanism you call built has a
  unit test that fails without it. A mutation no walk holds is a
  finding: name the arm; the rules track stages it next.
- **A re-pin's tests run green before a mutation is scored** (1083). A floor or widening test takes `let _pins = Pins::hold();` and
  `pin_eq!`/`pin_ne!`/`pin!`, so one run shows every pin that moved,
  and `tools/repin.py <log> --write` rewrites them (1349, 1411);
  "stands from N" says whether N is the window's first block or the
  gap's first frame.
- **A gate's own test uses a key the measured table lacks** (1126). **A
  fixture for a sea or air unit sets the domain in all three fields** —
  type `kind`, type `combat`, unit `kind` (1130).
- **Your own test run is `--test-threads 2`, or under
  `tools/memcap.sh`** (1081, 1086, 1089) — `20` for the whole suite
  (1543); the thread guard's red is not your mutation's; one filter a
  `cargo test` (1551); **kill a detached suite by its pid**, never by
  `pkill -f` on a name every lane shares (1527).
- **A divergence your change introduces is built, or named as a
  regression, never parked** (864); every closed chapter and open word
  holds. **A change on a shared path — a landing, a snap,
  a group move — runs the widenings whose captures reach it before the
  gate** (1313): `RON_FIRSTS` grepped for `inside_up` going to −1 names
  the disembarks.
- **A command the worktree guard refuses runs from
  `$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/tmp/`** — no `timeout` on macOS (1537); **`no verdict (error)` is an outage — back off on a
  Monitor, five minutes and up to an hour**; what needs a human is one
  line to me, and the turn ends (1315).

## landing

- **The word moves with its value diff beside it**: the record, the
  field and both sides' values on the word's frame, or on the frame the
  state first parted, walked back. Re-pin the constant with the
  word's delta in its comment and the word's block in its widening.
- **Run the whole suite once; write the documents and the journal,
  commit, then gate.** The gate is `python3 tools/release_gate.py
  ~/code/fun/attrition/game --test-threads 4 --lane --tests <the
  word's filters>` (1567), to a file, never piped, backgrounded; read
  its exit before saying anything landed, and quote its `Lane verdict:`
  and `Gate steps:` lines (969); its verdict is a second commit.
- **`ccc update att-<n>` takes the lane's ref, never a SHA** (1557);
  **when it refuses on two sections at one anchor or a count two lanes
  re-pinned, take `--keep-conflicts`, keep both and commit the merge
  yourself** (1161); a re-pinned count is measured again on the merged
  tree, the compared pin being the union of the windows walked (1151). **Read your run's
  `docs/RUNS.md` section whole after the update** (1285); **a landing
  that moves another lane's word says so** (1289).
- **A word that reaches its trace's end owes five things** (1217): an
  `ENDPOINTS` row and its `Endpoint` part, the `AI_WORDS` row closed,
  the compared pin's window dropped with its fields re-pinned, the
  coverage driver's word block a constant, and a capture of the last

- **The constants and their widenings are yours to pin; the queue's
  lines are mine** (`docs/QUEUE.md`, `PARKED.md`, `JOURNAL.md`,
  `DECISIONS.md`): report what books and parks, and the next word by its

- **Your journal is `docs/journal/<the day you land>-item-{item}.md`**,
  yours alone: every module you touched, and one line headed "for the
