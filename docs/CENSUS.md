# The census — the executable as the denominator

`tools/census.py`, regenerated at every steering pass (DECISIONS 41). Every
function in the executable, grouped by the class Ghidra's export files it
under, against the functions `docs/` cites by address and the functions any
coverage trace entered. **It is the map, not a score**: a cited function is
one a reading named, an entered one is one a run reached — constructors and
walkers count — and neither says the predicate inside was checked. The three
counters DECISIONS 29 names are all relative to what has been inspected;
this table's denominator is the binary itself. A "diff-backed" column, hand
tagged from each document's coverage section, is owed and not yet here.

Regenerate with the coverage traces `rondata::blind::TRACES` pins — the
twenty-two that enter everything the corpus's ninety enter (`docs/ORACLE.md`,
"Coverage is back"; the corpus lives outside the repo) — from the repo
root, adding `--never` for the blind list grouped by class:

    python3 tools/census.py --top 30 [--never] \
        $(for f in run29 run16 run50 run218 run49 run20 run44 run24 run47 \
                   run48 run74 run18a run32 run30 run66 fuzz-424242 run14 \
                   run15 run22 run64 run906 run314; do
            echo "$LOGS/rontrace-$f.log"; done)

and paste the output under the dated heading below, replacing the last one.
**Until 2026-09-27 this said "the three coverage traces"** (run53, run54,
run906), and eighty-six more with `HIT` records stood beside them on disk:
every capture since run14 that ran at `cover=1` under CrossOver's int3
forest, and the queue lane's since run906. The three entered 7,180; the
corpus enters 7,664 (item 923).

the list `rondata::blind::NEVER` pins address by address, so the guard
names the function that moves in either direction.

"Cited" is a function the export lists, cited `name@00xxxxxx` anywhere under
`docs/`; the census's 1,133 also counts thirteen data addresses no index
names. `report.py`'s `blind` verb counted 1,112 until item 923: it did not
join a citation broken after its `@`, as the census has since parked 835.

**What run314 was, and why it entered one.** Chapter twenty-three's script
whole, under `cover=1`, on the queue lane, the same game as the golden run
through frame 642 (`docs/RUNS.md` run314). Its six `@` lines were all
**refused, refusal 2**: the issuer's prologue is not the shipped one. The
coverage instrument writes a `jmp` over the first five bytes of every
listed function at attach, and the DLL checks each issuer's first bytes
against the shipped prologue before it calls it. So under the current
tracer, **no issuer chapter can be captured with coverage on any lane**,
and parked 697's hang on the click-free lane is the second of two walls,
not the only one. The staging's own lines ran, and the Airbase's launch
(`Object::do_launch`, first on 606) is what came off the list.
