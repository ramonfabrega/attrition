## Summary

**Counts by ruling (60):** twin 13 · twin-differs 7 · absorbed 10 · unmodelled 2 · dead 12 · ui 9 ·
saveload 5 · editor 1 · replaced 1.

The brief's expectation that `nuke` is mostly the interface's and Conquer the World's holds for seven of
its ten rows (`Nuke::setup`, `get_name`, `on_field_change`, `draw_ctw`, `NukeOut::update`,
`num_current_nukes`, `NukeOut::setup_terrain_terraforming`); the other three — `add_nuke`,
`been_damaged_before`, `do_damage` — are the playing blast, which `crates/sim/src/nuke.rs` ports.

**Proposed census classes:**

- `on_field_change$` → `ui`: `Nuke::on_field_change`, and outside this group `BorderSpline::`,
  `GraphicPieces::`, `Surf::`, `Light::`, `Storm::`, `CircleRender::`, `ConquestGraphicPieces::`,
  `ConquestWin::on_field_change`.
- `^Nuke::(setup|get_name|on_field_change|draw_ctw)$` → `ui` (four rows here; `draw_ctw` is also
  caught by `^draw`).
- `place_tree$` → `ui` (`ObjectsData::place_tree`).
- Two **caller rules** no name pattern can express, worth a census helper: callers all `*Out::` or
  `Scene::render` → `ui` (`Nuke::setup`, `ObjectsData::place_tree`, `NukeOut::num_current_nukes`);
  callers all `ScenarioEditor::` → `editor` (`Objects::remove_good`).
- `blind` `RESIDUE` candidates, each with the killer "a trace entering it": `ObjectData::assault_benefit`,
  `ObjectData::cargo_inside`, `Object::repair_damage`, `Object::update_los` (with `docs/AI.md`
  §383/§384 and residue-3505 already covering `support`, `los`, `hits`, `support_cost`, `max_range`,
  `has_range`, `recharge`, `execute_events`).

**Proposed replaced group:** **"save, load and the per-turn checksum (`DataWalk`)"** — pattern
`::(walk_data|must_walk|reload_data|get_new_data)$` plus the class `^CheckSums::`. Catches here
`Object::walk_data`, `Object::must_walk`, `ObjectsArray::get_new_data`, `Objects::walk_data`,
`Objects::reload_data`; elsewhere every `*::walk_data`, `OrderList::get_new_data`,
`GatherPointList::get_new_data`, and the `CheckSums::check_*` family. Evidence: the walk serializes or
checksums state and changes none on output; it is entered by every lockstep run only because
`CommandManager::process_turn` → `CheckSums::check_all` walks every active unit once a turn (parked
3893a).
