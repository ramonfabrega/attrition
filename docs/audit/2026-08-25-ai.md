# The AI — the blind second reading

The twelfth mechanic, and the largest. `docs/AI.md` §2 covers twenty
subsections over some fifteen large functions, plus the scripting VM and its
55 host functions. The evidence behind those subsections is **not uniform**,
and that shaped the audit:

- **§2.18, §2.19, §2.20** (`create_units`, `create_buildings`,
  `produce_building`) carry an explicit provenance line saying they rest on
  *one* reading — the implementation's — and were promoted out of §14's
  errata only on 2026-08-25. The trace's function-coverage report
  (`docs/ORACLE.md`) has all three on the never-executed list.
- **§2.3, §2.4, §2.6** (the census, the step machine, the make list) were
  confirmed behaviourally by run18 and run19: step ladders frame for frame,
  the make list's occupied slots, nine expiry observations.

So the readers were spent unevenly, and run in two waves (the orders audit's
quota lesson — seven long agents in flight is enough to take a day's limit
all at once).

## The blind protocol leaked, and it is this project's own fault

**B1 disclosed it, unprompted and precisely, and the disclosure applies to
all seven readers.** A subagent inherits the project's `CLAUDE.md` as system
instructions, and this repository's `CLAUDE.md` carries a long prose summary
of the AI work in its working-agreement section. The brief's ban on reading
the repository cannot undo that: the file is in the reader's context before
the brief is.

What `CLAUDE.md` states outright about this mechanic, and therefore what no
reader could have re-derived independently:

- `build_flags & 0x8000000` is live and is `TechType::set_research`'s mark,
  not a `BUILD_FLAGS` digit — so `create_buildings`' civic block runs and
  `is_military_trainer` is not dead code.
- `research_techs` reaches the 9,999,999 overflow guard and draws nothing.
- The step ladders `1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,0` and `1,3,4,5,6,7,8,0`; `make_stuff` at
  `production_ai+0x1fa` and `+0x236`.
- The make list fills slots 0, 5 and 8; `expire_all`'s `% 3` residue.
- `TechType::ai[11]`, `UnitType::cols`, the build queue's 20/10/2 capacity.

So the rows below that touch those claims are **not** independent
confirmations, and they are marked as such rather than counted. B2's
"hidden flag bit" headline and B3's `set_research` finding are the two worst
affected — and my brief to B2 steered at the same bit besides, which I told
the user when launching and which compounds it. Everything else in the
reports — the structural derivations, the listing checks, the value formulas,
the draw sites — is untouched by the leak, and that is the bulk of it.

**The fix, for the next mechanic:** run blind readers from a working
directory whose `CLAUDE.md` does not narrate the mechanic — either a stripped
copy, or move the working agreement's running commentary out of `CLAUDE.md`
and into a document the subagent does not inherit. This is the first audit
where the project's own note-keeping habit has worked against the method, and
it will recur every time, because `CLAUDE.md` is where the handoff prose
lives by design.
