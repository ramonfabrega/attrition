# Territory adjudication 5 — the building family

The third reading (`docs/audit/README.md`) of list `adj-5.tsv`: one disagreement and nine
compared-field claims on the building family. First readers: `2026-11-06-territory-unit-2.md`,
`-build-1.md`, `-build-2.md`; second readers: `-second-7.md`, `-second-8.md`. Each row was settled
against the decompile export (`~/ghidra-projects/decomp/funcs/`), the crate at the tree of
`bdc20a93b`, and the harness's comparators (`crates/rondata/src/diff/harness.rs`, `golden.rs`,
`coverage.rs`). Adjudicated on Opus 5.5, 2026-11-06. Read only; no cargo, no capture.

What "compared" means below, measured: the per-frame building comparison is
`harness.rs:705`–`722` (`damage`, `damage_frac`), `:727`–`745` (`myhits`, `construct_hits`) and the
queue rows (`build.rs`, `queue_diverged`); a unit's wound is `harness.rs:198` (`damage`); a city's
`trade_val` is `harness.rs:1240`. A building's own target (`attack_ox`/`attack_whom`/`recharging`) is
compared **only** by the golden widening and **only for a type with a `wall_cycle`** (the anti-air
cycle, `golden.rs:2405`–`2409`, `:2421`–`2425`): a Tower's, Fort's or city centre's target is not a
compared field, and shows only through its victim's `damage` and the draw stream. The `CARAVAN`
record is read once, by a full-dump test on run64's 6171 (`world.rs:2100`), and is not compared per
frame. `BUILDQUEUE`'s `queue_size` and `CITIES/CITY`'s `flags` sit on `UNREAD`
(`coverage.rs:189`, `:233`).

## Summary

Ten rows: **B 1**, **confirmed 8**, **uncompared 1**, refuted 0, not printed 0, ambiguous 0.

- B: `get_capture_value` (all three callers filter the decoy, in both programs).
- Confirmed: `can_make`, `get_base_garrison_arrows`, `max_range`, `attack`, `hits`,
  `check_enemy_adjacent`, `get_building_range`, `restart_trade_route`.
- Uncompared: `get_garrison_arrows`, for the target field. The arithmetic agrees. B's second defect is real:
  `compare_target`'s ×2/÷2 reads `bd.garrison_attack`, which is 0 in play (`fight.rs:5595`).

**Corrections to the readers' field claims.** Three of them, checked against the comparators:
- `attack_ox`/`attack_whom` are compared only for `wall_cycle` types (`golden.rs:2405`–`2425`), never for
  a Tower, Fort or city centre.
- `myhits` is what `BuildData::hits` reads, not what it writes, so it cannot show the Disband shrink.
- The `CARAVAN` record is read once, in run64's full-dump test (`world.rs:2100`), not compared per frame.
- A building's arrows draw nothing: `report.py when Build::do_attack` is empty on run353, where three
  Towers kill. So none of these terms parts the draw stream until it moves a kill's frame.

**The disk scan behind every (b).** `rg -c '^   attack_ox [0-9]'` over all 3,725 files in the Logs
directory: **four dumps hold a building with a target at all**. They are run353 (2,091 building-frames),
run342 (1,498), run405 (243) and run29's islands window (4). All are Towers (`orig_type` 439) except
run405's Radar (524), and none is garrisoned (`inside_down >= 0`: 0 rows). Nine Maya windows (run973,
973b–f, 1234b, 1248, 1411b) hold 0. So no dumped frame of any capture on disk carries a city centre or a
Fort with a target, or a garrisoned shooter. Every building-combat row here is unreached for that reason,
not because a compared field was blind to it.

**The items, cheapest first.**
1. *A comparator line, dumps on disk*: compare `attack_ox`/`attack_whom` for every armed building in the
   harness (the golden parser already reads them, `golden.rs:2006`). It costs nothing now (four dumps hold
   any), and it is the field that names `compare_target`'s ×2/÷2 the day a capture holds a garrisoned
   shooter (0062e3d0).
2. *A harness query on disk*: list the pinned captures whose parsed leader `rare` has bit 26, Obsidian
   (`leader.rs:821` parses it; a text grep cannot split the dump's run-together decimal bytes). This
   settles whether the Obsidian arm of `BuildData::attack` is a capture item or a dead one (0062e610).
3. *A harness probe on disk*: for every dock on every dumped frame, the ring verdict of
   `check_enemy_adjacent` against the own-cell owner; any disagreeing frame is a ready window (00638d70).
4. *A golden case on a dump already on disk*: drive run353 through 1010, with `trade_val` and the
   surviving caravans' paths compared (0073d070).
5. *One staged chapter, one capture, entering four rows at once*: a Mayan city with a Temple, a Tower
   whose owner holds FORTRANGE2 or tech bit 66, and a General garrisoned. Two enemy units approach it,
   inside a dumped window. That enters 0062e340 (Maya), 0062e470 (temple and general range), 00639990
   (upgrade arrays), 0062e610 (general attack) and 0062e3d0 (`compare_target` with a garrison).
6. *A golden chapter*: a Disband queued at a building under fire, comparing its `damage` and death frame
   (0062e740; the port needs `crate::single`).
7. *A capture or a unit test*: a human queue order on a mixed building group (Barracks + Stable) for
   `can_make`'s missing `queue_here` (0062db10). The unit test is cheaper and moves no score.
