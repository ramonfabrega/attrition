## Summary

**Counts (60):** twin 21 · twin-differs 9 · absorbed 1 · unmodelled 2 · ui 10 · editor 2 · dead 2 · saveload 1 ·
replaced 12.

**The rubric's own miss.** Thirty of the sixty have a twin in `crates/sim` (21 `twin`, 9 `twin-differs`) — most
with `Class::method@va` spelled in the doc comment — and are filed `open`/`layout` only because the Rust fn is not
`fn <short>`: the crate's convention is `group_<short>` (`group_action_recall`, `group_action_patrol`,
`group_is_attacking_to`, …), a renamed method (`open_slot`, `seat_sort`, `set_transport`, `group_loc`,
`group_refresh_order`, `GroupState::copied`, `form_compute`), or a view method (`group_moving`). Proposed for
`census.territory_kind`: accept `fn group_<snake>` (and `fn <snake>` inside a `*_view.rs` impl) whose doc spells
`Class::method`, as `port`. That alone would move about 25 of this file's rows out of the open territory.

**Proposed census classes.**
- **Caller-class `ui` mark**: a function every caller of which is in `Options`, `Console`, `IFace*`, `Scene` or
  `ScenarioEditor` is `ui`. Catches here: `GroupData::test_garrison`, `get_valid_spellcaster`, `get_spellcaster`,
  `show_interface`, `one_member`, `valid_target`, `GroupsData::find_group_const`, `Group::can_activate`,
  `Group::toggle`, `Group::dbg_jump_to_waypoint` (10).
- **`*Out` before `layout`**: `territory_kind` returns `layout` for any swept function before `is_ui_body` is asked,
  so the 12 swept `GroupOut::issue_*` bodies are `layout`, not `ui`. Reordering (or the replaced rule below) files them.
- **`walk_data` in `DEAD_WEIGHT`** (the save/load walker, beside `log_data`): `Group::walk_data` here, and every
  `*::walk_data` outside the `orders` stem.
- **`^HotKeyGroup` class / `::action_hotkey$`**: `Group::action_hotkey` (control groups are selection sets).
- **Callerless body** (no call site, no vtable slot) as `dead`: `Group::set_ordered`; and the three
  `GroupOut::issue_{scramble,recall,alarm}` whose `Options` handlers inline them.
- **Unissued command / scenario trigger**: a function reached only from a `CommandPackage` arm whose byte no
  `add_command` site writes, or only from `ScenarioData::issue_order`: `Group::action_repair` (and
  `CommandPackage::process_repair`, outside this file).
- **Editor-only `dbg_`**: `::dbg_pack_or_unpack$`, `::dbg_jump_to_waypoint$` by name — not a bare `^dbg_`, since
  `dbg_jump_to_action` is live through `ScenarioFuncSet::add_unit` and has a twin.

**Proposed replaced group.** The 12 `GroupOut::issue_*` join **the command packaging and manager** by the name rule
`^GroupOut::issue_` (Ramon's to accept): each packs the selection into a command packet that `rondata::commands`
parses in their place.

**`unmodelled`, ranked** (none is lockstep-entered):
1. **`Group::action_siege_attack@00706ff0`** — entered by the coverage trace run1136 (item 3502, golden, not a
   lockstep run) and by any Alt-right-click attack. The `siege_attack` command (0x05) is parsed by
   `rondata::commands` and acted on by nothing in `sim`. On a mixed selection the original sends the siege members
   to attack and has the rest `GUARDORDER` their leader. Shown by `COMMANDMANAGER=1`'s `process_siege_attack` line
