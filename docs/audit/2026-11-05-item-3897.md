**Method.** One reader, a `lean` subagent on Opus 5.5 (model inherited), was

  producer's lifetime as well as the dispatcher (Loop 3895).

Its brief named no section, no predicate and no first reading's conclusion. It

## Part 2: adjudication

**Row 1 agrees with §385.1** on verdict, on the end predicate and its 15-frame
gate, and on `cannon_time_who`'s four writers. It also agrees on the issuer's
gate, on bit 2's one setter, and on the clears in solo, scenario, editor and
playback. §385's `TurnControl::walk_data` "names no cannon field" is the same
fact as the reader's "called only with a checksum walker": nothing saves or
loads `who`.

**The reader's playback route is §385's own.** §385.1 names it as outside v1: a
recording made in a GameSpy game that called a cannon time. A v1 recording comes
from a solo game, where the issuer refuses, so it carries no `0x4d`.

**The reader's save route, settled to the function: closed in v1.** The question
is whether a save that carries bit 2 can be loaded into a v1 game. I read three
functions in the export:
- **Which header a bit-2 save carries.** `SaveGame::save_game`, lines
  111–117, picks the header from the semaphore. `ptr[2] & 2` gives
  `RoNCTWSave`. Otherwise `ptr[0] & 4` (bit 2) gives `RoNMultiSave`, and a
  clear bit gives `RoNSave`. Conquest runs through `run_solo`, which clears bit
  2 first. So a save carries bit 2 exactly when its header is `RoNMultiSave`.
- **The menu load.** `Main::run_game_loop`'s `GAME_LOAD_SOLO` case reads the
  header after `LoadGame::load_game_screen`. It calls `Game::run_solo(1,0,0)`
  only on `RoNSave`. On `RoNMultiSave` it shows a popup and loads nothing.
  `Game::run_solo`'s conquest arm refuses `RoNMultiSave` the same way.
- **The in-game load.** `LoadGame::load_game`, lines 104–107, sends a
  `RoNMultiSave` with `game->playing` set to its error exit (code 5). It does
  so before any walk.
- **The lobby picker.** `SetupWin::open_save_game` accepts only `RoNSave` when
  bit 2 is clear.

So a v1 game never walks a semaphore with bit 2 set, and the reader's first
loophole closes. The same three sites are its killer: a solo trace that enters
`Game::walk_data` on a `RoNMultiSave` file.

**Rows 2–4 agree with §385.2** on the chain, the registry gate, the ID-bit arm
and the instrument. The reader read two extra conditions off the listing on
`check_80386`'s path: the 8086 test and `check_80286 != 2`. Both narrow the
route further and change no verdict. The reader counts 166 of 167 `cover=1`
traces with `cpunormspeed`, and §385 counts 139 of 139 pinned traces. The
reader's one exception is a log outside `blind::TRACES`. No trace on disk enters
`cpuspeed`, so the exception cannot carry a row. It is parked as 3897a.
