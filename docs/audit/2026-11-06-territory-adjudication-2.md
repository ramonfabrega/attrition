`docs/RUNS.md`); (c) the rule against the decompile (`~/ghidra-projects/decomp/funcs/…`), with the

Verdicts: **confirmed** (a compared field, an unreached case: the capture that enters it is the
item), **uncompared** (printed, not compared: a widening or parser line is the item), **not
printed**, **refuted** (the crate carries it). Where a row's two readers name different fields,
the verdict is on the field that would actually part first, and the other is said.

The comparators named below: per-unit orders, `diff::order::compare_orders`
(`crates/rondata/src/diff/order.rs:307`), whose `OrderDump` fields are all compared but

(`coverage.rs:3542`); the group pool, `second::widen_records_from_groups`
(`crates/rondata/src/diff/second.rs:252`), every `GroupDump` field (`role` on an army's group
only, `second.rs:359`), on the windows that ask for `records` and skipping a slot the original
holds empty (`.filter(|g| g.num > 0)`, `second.rs:267`).
