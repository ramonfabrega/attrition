## The ratio, and what it measured

152 against 102 is 1.5x — under the doubling that stops the loop, so the
loop continues. But the frame price has risen twelvefold over three
windows, and the reason is visible in the queue rather than the workers.
The handoff named item 305 as headline-nearest. Item 302's own landing,
written hours earlier, says the Great Lakes word at 7679 is a figure's
draw — `Guy::set_anim+0x97a` from `Unit::move_step+0x823` here, from
`Guy::inc_time+0x271` in the original — "and not the AI's", and that the
make list's residue "is not where the word is". Nothing on the board
named that frame. The wave ran the nearest residue rows, each closed a
record row, and each moved nothing, which is exactly what the journal
predicted. The workers were right about everything they touched; the
board was wrong about what to touch.

Sixteen of the eighteen open items named no score. The queue had been at
its ceiling for weeks, so every booking was a forced triage under a rule
only the commander had — "least score-connected parks" — and the items
that survived triage were the ones with the most text, not the most
score. **The defect was the population, not the cap.**

## Rulings

1. **A finding parks by default.** The queue takes only what names the
   headline's frame, a floor, or a takes-chain to one; everything else
   books straight into `docs/PARKED.md`. Fifteen items left the queue
   under this rule today and none was dropped. Three stand: 312, 304, 308.
2. **The headline slot is never empty.** With no item on the word's
   frame, the next brief is to widen every dumped record on that frame and
   name the cause — the twenty-minute shape 294, 295 and 301 all followed.
   Item 312 is that brief for 7679.
3. **Loop items are the pass's.** Tooling, guards and the queue's rules
   live in the parked file's Loop section and are never spawned to a
   worker; the pass's agenda file points there. Ramon's framing: loop
   improvement is what the steering pass is *for*.
4. **The journal is a directory.** `docs/journal/<date>-item-<N>.md`, one
   file per landing, the worker's; `docs/JOURNAL.md` is the chronicle to
   today and the passes' own entries after. A worker edits neither the
   journal file nor the queue; it reports, the commander books and writes
   the scoreboard line, and the floors test stays the worker's so a
   forgotten line fails the gate. Ramon raised this one; the 27-of-58
   number settled it.
5. **A number is measured on the tip** (the commander's first row,
   ratified as written): after the worker's last `ccc update`, or the
   report says which tree. The "one layer shallow" diagnosis of 294/295/302
   is refuted with it — 301's item was right and the mid-flight rejection
   was the error.
6. **Merge, gate, push, reap — one chain** (the second row, ratified). A
   `land` verb is filed with ccc as item 313; until then the chain is one
   shell line in the commander's brief.
7. **The ledger fails on a phantom.** A number the queue refers to as
   `item N`, `takes N` or `N closes` that no revision ever booked fails
   the build. Made to fail first on the tree as it stood — 304 and 224 —
   then both booked. Item 279's two regex defects landed in the same
   change: the paren group now takes `(N)` and `(N/M)` only, and `item N`
   is case-insensitive; each was shown failing on its own input before the
   fix, and the fix's first run named the real item 7 the coordinate had
   hidden — Orders, done 2026-08-21 — which is ledgered as landed.

## What the pass did not do

- **It did not wire the suspend.** `worktree-loop-301-suspend` stays
  banked; 304 is the named precondition and now exists. The pass checked
  that the branch's tip is `4521ccc` on `9c29ca8`, not that it merges.
- **It did not re-measure the width.** Two workers at four threads each is
  27 GB against 128 and nothing today contradicted it.
- **It did not change the cap.** 18 items at 8 lines stands; the rule
  about who gets in is what changed.
- **lore's tool lane was not searchable for conflict text** — the merge
  count came from git, which is the stronger source anyway.
