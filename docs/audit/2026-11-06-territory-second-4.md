## Summary

Counts: **carried 1** (`scan_and_kill_bad_tcoord`), **differs 10** (`cast_sabotage`, `cast_bribe`,
`cast_counterintel`, `cast_double_agent`, `get_range`, `SpellType::cast`, `unpay_cast_costs`, `move_ocean_goody`,
`object_placed`, `get_cost`), **missing 9** (`Region::release`, `find_map`, `find_special`, `cast_depopulate`,
`cast_blow_tread`, `refresh_good_z`, `mark_forest`, `is_cancel_alliance`, `is_declare_war`), **not-a-rule 0**.

Where the crate is most clearly wrong on a rule a normal skirmish reaches:

1. **`TypeData::get_cost`'s Beaver Pelts arm** (entered, run1016–run1021): a leader holding Beaver Pelts pays 0 for a
   caravan, Merchant, Dutch Merchant or Fur Trapper; the crate charges full price. Field: the producing building's
   `queue[0].cost` and the leader's resources/`bucket` (`LEADERDATA`), compared.
2. **No craft is priced** (`get_cost`'s spell arm, with `unpay_cast_costs`' resource half; both entered): Restock
   Supplies (`2w/2m/2c/2o/2f`), Research Project, Move Capital Here and Declare War cost nothing here, and an
   interrupted paid cast hands nothing back. Field: leader resources on the cast and on the interrupt, compared.
3. **`SpellType::cast`'s absent arms** (entered): lock/unlock gate, Restock Supplies (`mana_burn = 0`), research
   project, move capital, paradrop, the immortals and air defense do nothing in the crate. Field: per arm — the
   gate's state in `BuildDump`, the caster's `mana_burn` (compared).
