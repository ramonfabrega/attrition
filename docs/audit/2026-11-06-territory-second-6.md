## Summary

Sixteen functions: **carried 3** (`Wall::kill_at_tile`, `Wall::update_los`,
`WorldData::is_city_at`), **differs 9** (`UnitData::total_damage`,
`UnitData::max_range`, `UnitData::armor`, `Wall::start_me`,
`Wall::activate`, `WallData::armor`, `WallData::is_seen`,
`Wall::decrement_stats`, `Wall::increment_stats`), **missing 4**
(`UnitData::get_next_action`, `UnitTypeData::get_kill_value`,
`WorldData::is_really_seen`, `World::analyze_map`), **not-a-rule 0**.

Where the crate is most clearly wrong on a rule an ordinary skirmish
reaches:

1. **`UnitData::armor@00610160`** (entered, run1018–1022, run1176): the
   patriot terms inside the rally gate — `thecitizen_armor_bonus` near
   The Citizen and `themonarch_cav_armor` on cavalry near The Monarch —
   are not carried (`crates/sim/src/combat/fight.rs:501-503`), and both patriots
   come from a government any player can take. Shows as the victim's
   `UNITDATA/OBJECT` `damage`/`damage_frac`, printed and compared.
2. **`UnitData::max_range@0060fe90`** (entered, run1017–1022): the
   obsidian-archer and rum-naval rare terms, and the Liberty/Eiffel siege
   terms, are not carried (`fight.rs:545-549`, `:577-581`); rares sit on
   ordinary maps. Shows as stand-off positions and strike frames
   (`GUY x/y`), printed and compared.
3. **`Wall::activate@0063e4b0`** (entered, run1016–1021): a finished
   AIRDEFENSE (TypeIndex 523) takes `job_counter = 0x40000000`; the
   crate writes 0 (`crates/sim/src/cities/city.rs:2327`). `BUILDDATA/WALLDATA`
   `job_counter`, printed and compared — a single-field value diff on the
   activation frame of any Industrial-age anti-aircraft building.

Close behind: the trainer list's order after an in-place upgrade
(`Wall::set_type` → `decrement_stats`/`increment_stats`, not carried) and
the Senator's building armour (`WallData::armor`).

## Not established

- `UnitOrder` vslot `+0x14`: the base slot is `Window::get_button`, so
  which order classes answer non-zero (and what the `flags & 4` exemption
  means) is not named; `get_next_action`'s skip set is stated by its
  shape only.
- Whether `sim`'s `squad_members` includes dead figures, which decides
  whether the shrink's sum (`lib.rs:5314`) over- or under-counts against
  `total_damage`'s dead-figure term.
- Who reads an active AIRDEFENSE's `job_counter`; the entry says the
  field parts, not what it moves next.
- `LeaderData +0x3f8` in `in_enemy_territory` is taken as the placer's
  city count (as `claim_reaches(city_num, …)` takes it); not checked
  against the type record.
- The readers of `Region.flags & 4` / `& 8` and of `World +0x58..+0x74`
  set by `analyze_map`; not traced.
- `Wall::start_me(0)`'s kept-bits arm is a reading; no capture on disk has
  two overlapping unstarted sites where one closes.
