## What was found

- **Nothing regressed.** Every floor is a guard and the gate is green on
  the tip; the score lines did not change in any of the tranche's booking
  commits. What happened is a stall, not a slide.
- **The per-landing price halved and the per-word price went undefined.**
  Sixteen landings at twelve USD each is the cheapest tranche on record,
  and none moved a frame. The sixth pass's convention — book by frame and
  draw delta, the mechanism as the previous item's hypothesis — was
  followed to the letter in every brief, and it made the items short and
  honest: 439 was five minutes, 423 seven lines, and three findings were
  withdrawn inside the tranche instead of a pass later. It did not make
  them productive, and the reason is not in the convention.
- **Two tracks, two stories.** The AI lane widened its frame first (408:
  172 blocks, every record), and every item after it was a measurement —
  a `LEADERS=9` window (414), a scoped probe (422, 430, 432), a grep of a
  dump already on disk (438) — that killed a reading with data. Six items
  later it holds a defect confirmed against the original's own chains, a
  coupling priced at −925 frames, and an item (442) with two numbers to
  hit on one frame and a falsifier standing. That is the loop working, at
  fifteen USD an item. The rules lane pinned a word (415), read a value
  diff **six frames past it**, called the 140 units a seating error, and
  four items read decompile on that premise (426, 434, 435, 437, 439)
  before the whole-cast widening at the word's own frame (441) showed the
  seating exact and the divergence one order. Nine items on the rules
  lane; the widening was the ninth.
- **The rule that would have stopped it was already written and nothing
  checked it.** `CLAUDE.md` says to widen every record on the frame before
  naming a cause, and the sixth pass said it again for the headline slot.
  It was not ordered ahead of the readings because no guard knew whether a
  word's widening existed, and a value diff at the wrong frame looks
  exactly like one. Prose broken within the week, again; the fix is the
  guard.
- **The stall was a lost notification, and the definition of done put the
  commit after the gate.** A lane that finishes, gates, and is never woken
  has its work on the floor and its branch clean — which `CLAUDE.md`'s own
  check, `git log <base>..<branch>`, reads as a lane that never started.
  The 90-minute status rule did not fire because the lane was not running.
  Committing before the gate costs nothing and makes the branch carry the
  work.
- **The AI tranche was reconstructing what the AI's dump would print.**
  `want`, `divisor`, `val`, `gfree`, the four offers — every one recovered
  by probe, by replay through `make_me`, or by a grep of a record captured
  for something else. 367 has been "the next pass's first build" twice
  and is not built. It is the one Loop item with a score behind it.
- **The census moved where the tranche worked, and not in the order
  family.** `CommandManager` 2 → 16 is item 365's issuer table; the
  tranche's combat reading went through `Unit::think`'s neighbours and
  `create_units`, never an order class. Six uncited order classes are
  still six.
- **The waiting ban held** on both lanes — under a dollar of task-file
  reads across the tranche, against 63 USD on one lane on 09-06.

## Rulings

1. **A word is pinned with its widening** (DECISIONS 43 §1):
   `rondata::diff::testkit::WIDENINGS` names, for every pinned word
   constant, the test that compared every dumped record on the word's own
   frame — or the open item that owes it — and
   `floors::the_widening_behind_each_pinned_word_exists` reads the table.
   Two words have none on file: East Indies' 9711 (parked 444) and chapter
   one's 626 (parked 445). Made to fail first on both `None` rows before
   the items existed, and on a misspelt test name.
2. **A worker commits before it gates** (§2), in `CLAUDE.md`'s worker
   paragraph.
3. **The `Golden:` line composes lowest chapter first** (417, §3):
   `floors::the_handoff_s_golden_line_is_the_pinned_word` reads both
   chapter constants, requires the first `w<frame>` to be the lowest and
   every chapter's word on the line; made to fail on the old line, which
   led with 626 over a pin of 616.
4. **No conflict marker survives in the tree** (420, §4):
   `docs_guard::no_conflict_marker_survives_in_the_tree`, made to fail on
   a fixture line appended to `tools/gamelog/captures.txt`.
5. **Six clauses in `CLAUDE.md`** (§5): the payoff probe scoped to its
   frames (431); a widening's framing is a hypothesis and a stanza names
   what kills each reading (419); a slot index is not an identity (424); a
   commander's mid-gate message says it waits (433); an announced action
   is performed in its turn (436); a refusal with an in-repo remedy is
   taken and reported (421).
6. **A purchase draws nothing** is in DECISIONS 43 §6 (416).
7. **The lane is reaped.** `att-365`'s floor was byte-identical to the
   salvage; nothing was on it that the tree does not hold.
