## The adjudication

Six adjudicators over 84 rows: the 18 disagreements between a first reader and its blind second, and 66 claims — rows
both readers ruled `unmodelled` or `twin-differs` and at least one said the field that would show it is compared, which
would mean lockstep parts the moment a capture reaches the case. Of the disagreements the first reader held on 14 and
the blind reader on 4 (`CollCheck::fill_slots`'s SEAM is stale and the code already matches; the three Great-Sahara-shaped
masks; `get_capture_value`'s callers carry the decoy tests). Of the claims: **confirmed 55** (the field is compared and the
case never arose on a pinned game), **confirmed and already parting 2** (`Groups::process`'s `role`, a record row on
run357's 5772..5776; `Unit::process_healing`'s Iroquois arm, two wound rows pinned in the Iroquois pair's closing window),
**uncompared 10** (printed, but the key is on the unread pin or the bit is outside the compared mask), **not printed 1**,
**refuted 2** (`CityData::is_seen` inert; the Aztec speed arm, measured inert on run1256b), and every rule as both readers
stated it held against the decompile where it was checked.

**Why lockstep never parted on any of them, structurally** (adjudications 4 and 6): a lockstep game is pinned on its draw
stream; records are compared only on dumped blocks, and a `cover=1` twin dumps a closing window or a 100-frame stride; a
widened window's record rows that part are pinned as a count, not asserted zero (run1492's word window holds 181); the
coverage pins are per key, never per bit or per state — `unit_masks` is compared on 7 bits and `unit_masks2` on 5,
`job_counter` only while a site is unfinished, `myspeed` on widening windows only — and the comparator skips a dump key
the crate emits no row for (`harness.rs:4939`), so a counter the crate never emits cannot part. That last two are the
instrument gaps the night found: **a parsed mask word with one compared bit falls off both the `UNREAD` and the
`UNCOMPARED` pins, and no guard lists its unread bits** (the Iroquois cloak bits are set on thousands of unit-frames of a
closed pair while the crate holds 0), and **a per-record key the crate emits for units only is "compared" for every
record** (the technologies' `num_queued`). Both are Loop items for the next pass to file (the backlog line is the
commander's while the loop runs).

**Measured on the archive** (3,725 dumps): a building holds a target in four dumps, Towers and a Radar, none garrisoned,
so every building-combat rule is unreached because the case never arose; the busiest frame holds 34 live corpses against
the original's pool of 100; every cast order on disk is a craft the crate carries; no leader on disk holds Beaver Pelts;
every pinned lobby is one human against one AI, so no alliance has ever been tested — `process_attrition`'s treaty
