## Summary

Counts by verdict over the twenty: **carried 2** (`Type::sum_rules_cost`, `Unit::modify_group_order`,
each with a small difference noted that no stock skirmish reaches); **differs 11** (`process_healing`,
`process_attrition`, `process_cloak`, `do_garrison`, `pitch_aircraft`, `check_fuel`, `do_trade`,
`think_carry_military`, `think_caravan`, `update_speed`, `is_seen`); **missing 6** (`get_score_value`,
`has_repeat_air`, `action_unqueue`, `action_come_out`, `think_carry_idle`, `update_ceo_position`);
**not-a-rule 1** (`dbg_jump_to_action`). Total 20.

The three where the crate is most clearly wrong on a rule a normal skirmish reaches:

1. **`Unit::process_attrition@005e11a0`** (entered, run1016–1021). The live caller
   `lib.rs:7779` passes `mutual_treaty: false`; allies are `at_war` false, so an allied unit in an
   ally's territory takes `peace_attrition` (8 frames, supply-proof) where the original exempts it
   (`diplos == 2` both ways). Any team lobby with an ally. Field: `UNITDATA attrition` and `myhits`,
   printed and compared.
2. **`Unit::process_healing@005e0670`** (entered). The Iroquois heal in friendly territory and the
   French/Versailles supply heal are not applied (`supply.rs:368` computes the period, nothing uses
   it); the garrison heal gives +1 to every figure instead of one point down the chain. An Iroquois
   or French player's wounded land unit. Field: `myhits`, printed and compared; `healing` printed,
   unread.
3. **`Unit::process_cloak@005e1c20`** with **`UnitData::is_seen@00607a60`** (both entered). The
   Iroquois forest cloak (`unit_masks \|= 0x800` on a `& 0x30 == 0x30` cell) and the own/allied-land
   `unit_masks2 0x8000` are never set, so `is_seen` never hides those units. Field: an enemy's target
   choice (`whom`) or `seen` rows; the masks themselves are uncompared.
