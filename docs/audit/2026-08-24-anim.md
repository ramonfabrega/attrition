# Adjudication — the animation clock

2026-08-24. First reading: `docs/ANIM.md` (Fable, the session that landed
`crates/sim/src/anim.rs`). Blind second reading:
`docs/audit/2026-08-24-anim-reading.md` (Opus, one reader, ~20 minutes, no
access to the document, the module or the commit). Adjudicated by the first
reader, every disagreement taken back to the decompile or the listing;
verdicts that changed the Rust are marked.

The two readings agree on the whole structure — the `UnitAnimCat` table
(transcribed identically, 38 dwords at file offset `0x6f2b70`), the early
returns of `set_anim`, the `openlist` gate, the captain/group-idle gate,
the 70/83/96 and 70/80/90 tables, the attack and bird rolls, `end_time`
from `AnimMgr::frames` with 3 for a missing slot, the wrap loop and its

the gaia piece by `(seed + o) % 3`, the player piece by age/set/crew/gender,

category-0 animation, `Animal::do_idle`'s tautological guard and its four
draws, the arrival on the guy's own `des`. Both matched run13's twelve fish
and run12's forty animals 52/52 by the same method.
