# Territory adjudication — list 6

The seventy-first tranche's third reading (`docs/audit/README.md`), over the 12 rows of
`adj-6.tsv`: every row a **compared-field claim** (`CLAIM`), where both readers agree the crate
differs and at least one says the dump field that would show it is printed and compared.
Adjudicator: Opus 5.5. Each row is settled against the decompile export
(`~/ghidra-projects/decomp/funcs/`) and `crates/` at the worktree's tip (bdc20a93b); the field
against `crates/rondata/src/diff/coverage.rs` (the `UNREAD` pin and `UNCOMPARED_BY_THE_INSTRUMENT`)
and the comparator that reads it.

**What "compared" means here.** Two instruments, and a claim has to name which. The **lockstep
score** is the draw stream plus the stride's `UNITDATA` blocks: a lockstep trace dump prints no
`BUILDDATA`, `CITY`, `LEADERDATA` or `AMMO` at all (run1145, the German pair's lockstep twin: 43
blocks, 9,892 `UNITDATA`, 0 of the other four). `harness::compare` (the `CITY` record, `BUILDDATA`,
units) and `widen_walk`'s leader rows (`crates/rondata/src/diff/harness.rs:4904`) run only on the
frames a full-detail window prints. So a field "printed and compared" parts a word only when a window
covers the frame, or when the difference reaches a draw or a stride unit first. And the leader
comparator walks **the crate's** rows and skips a dump key it has no row for
(`harness.rs:4939`, `let Some(&y) = t.get(k) else { missing… continue }`): a dump field past the
crate's rows is printed, parsed and never compared.

**And a registered field is not a compared bit.** `UnitDump.unit_masks` is noted as compared
(`harness.rs:87`), but only seven of its bits have rows (`0x2`, `0x10`, `0x1000`, `0x10000`,
`0x80000`, `0x100000`, `0x2000000`: `harness.rs:287–289`, `:386`, `:2113`, `:2155–2183`); the
compared pin cannot see a bit nobody rows. Row `0060a6a0` turns on it.

(`UnitData::is_cloaked`) · refuted **1** (`CityData::is_seen`, inert in its one caller's domain) ·

**`Build::clean_queue`, the night's sharpest claim, stands.** A technology queued at any building
that closes or is converted is taken off unit record 0's counts (the Citizen) and never off
`tech[who].queued[t]`, so `researching(t)` stays true and the owner can never queue it again. The
original has no counter to strand: `researching` walks the live queues. No lockstep game parted on it
because the stranded count is not a compared field (the crate emits 352 `num_queued` rows and the
comparator skips the dump's other 454), because the five capture windows on disk hold 1,831
building-frames of queued research and not one of those buildings closes or changes hands mid-window,
and because the effect is a later refusal the losing AI rarely lives to ask for.
