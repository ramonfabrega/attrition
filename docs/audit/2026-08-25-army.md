## Headline

The arithmetic was doubly confirmed almost everywhere, which is the record's
pattern; the readers' surplus was **names from the symbol file**. Both read
`rise.pdb` directly for the enums the export does not ship — `ArmyStatus`,
`STRATEGY_*`, `CITY_*`, `OBJECT_*`, `LeaderFlagIndex`, `LeaderFlag2Index` —

Eleven verdicts changed the document and six changed Rust, and the largest
is the one the first reading had **wrongly called settled**: the two
strength averages in `find_target` are over `sea_combat`, not `combat` —
the listing's loop register is the leader plus 8, so its `+0x944` is
`+0x94c` (B.20). The others are the familiar kinds: a direction (the group
sort is ascending, A.13), a base (the chase's angle is measured from the
target, B.15), a field (`num_captains`, not `num_units`, in two finders —
B.49, B.50 — the same substitution the transport audit's B.51 caught), a
predicate's shape (the difficulty-1 gate skips on either clause, B.24), a
key (the weak army's exemption is the city's **founder**, B.27), an
inverted switch (`defense_mod` selects allies above `0x100` and enemies
below, B.22), and ~~a fold read as a reversal (the `sub eax, 0x80000000`
that opens every `sin_table` call is `docs/MOVEMENT.md`'s sine fold, so the
formation steps run *along* the muster angle — A.36, A.62, A.67)~~ **a
reversal read as a fold — overturned 2026-09-28** (item 1052 from the

Two readings were refuted by the export: B.59's "`find_local_army` has no

## Method note

The shape the transport audit recommended held again — first reading,
captures staged from the trace's frame numbers, blind readers briefed
while they ran, adjudication against both — and this time the readers
were handed the captures and used them: B.5 and B.43 are confirmed against
run20 and run26 in the reports themselves. Two new things. First, both
readers went to `rise.pdb` for the enums the export does not carry, and
that was worth five verdicts; the export should ship those enums next to
`TypeIndex.txt`. Second, the first reading had marked one item "settled
in the listing" and was wrong about it — the listing had been read
without its base register. A "settled" in a document is a claim like any
other; the blind reader's job is to re-derive it, and B did.
