`action_air_attack_ground`, `GroupData::get_loc`, `action_disband`, `Map::scale_land_area`,

Where the crate is most clearly wrong on a rule a normal skirmish reaches:

1. **`Map::make@0068bc90`'s territory-limit copy** (entered). The style XML overrides the border
   limits — Nile Delta and Sea Power 48/8/8, New World colonized 32/4/4 — and the original's
   `compute_reg_territory` reads them from `WorldData`; the crate's border pass reads the
   `rules.xml` constants 44/4/4 (`crates/sim/src/territory.rs:168`) and has no colonized triple.
   Every border on those three map styles would part. Field: `WORLD`'s
   `player_territory_limit*`/`colonized_territory_limit*` scalars (printed, uncompared, parked
   912) and the cells' `who`/`who2` (compared).
2. **`Group::action_patrol@007030c0`'s air hand-off** (not entered). The original hands the group
   to the air arm when the leader or any member `is_plane` (helicopters excluded); the crate
   tests domain Air (`crates/sim/src/group.rs:3523`), helicopters included, and its air arm then
   orders no non-plane — so a patrol of helicopters, or of land units with one helicopter, issues
   nothing. Field: `UNITDATA GroupPatrolOrder/PATROLORDER` (printed and compared).
3. **`Group::action_disband@0070e260`'s building arms** (not entered). A finished building
   disbanded by a player queues `DISBAND`, an unfinished one is disbanded at once; the crate's
