1. **`Object::attempt_launch`** (entered, run1017..run1176): missing. Every hit on an
   Airbase or Carrier holding a ready fighter scrambles it onto an air patrol at the
   attacker; the crate's `do_damage` skips the step (`docs/COMBAT.md:841-842` "Not
   modelled"). Field: the fighter's air order and `inside_up_who`, printed and unread
   (`coverage.rs:281`).
2. **`Objects::add_death`** (entered, run1018..run1176): differs. The original keeps 100
   death records and evicts the oldest non-blocking one when full; the crate's list is
   unbounded. Field: `DEATH_OBJS`, printed and compared (`death:extra`), plus `hold_frames`.
3. **`ObjectData::num_aircraft_here`** (entered, run1019..run1177): differs. The original
   counts aircraft *homed* at the base, flying ones included; `base_can_carry` and
   `train_first_point` count the garrison only (`airbase.rs:421`). Field: a plane's
   `inside_up_who` and its order, printed and unread.

Close behind: `check_target`'s tail (a city-bound building on unowned land is no target;
not carried) and `is_infiltrated`'s arm in `BuildData::is_seen` (not carried).
