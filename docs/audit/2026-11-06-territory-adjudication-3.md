checks (a) whether the field that would show the difference is really compared — against
`crates/rondata/src/diff/coverage.rs` (`UNREAD` at :85, `UNCOMPARED_BY_THE_INSTRUMENT` at :3381) and the
comparator that reads it (`crates/rondata/src/diff/harness.rs` `compare` :11, `widen_block_rows` :1764,
`diff::leader`); (b) why no pinned capture parted; (c) the rule against the decompile
(`~/ghidra-projects/decomp/funcs/`). Kinds: all rows are `CLAIM`.

Verdict key: **confirmed** — a compared field, an unreached case, the capture that enters it is the item;
**uncompared** — printed, not compared, a widening is the item; **not printed**; **refuted** — the crate
carries it.

Comparator facts used throughout (read once, cited by row):

- `compare` (`harness.rs:11`) runs on every capture. It compares a unit's container (`inside_up`, walked to
  the top, against `inside`/`inside_unit`: `harness.rs:134-164`, `InsideDivergence`), its hit-point record
  (`myhits`, `damage = max_health − health`, `damage_frac`, `hold_frames`: `harness.rs:194-226`), the
  firing record, orders (`order_diverged`), and `DEATH_OBJS` both directions (`valid first_frame cur_anim`,
  `missing`/`extra`: `harness.rs:560-640`; `gpiece` pinned uncompared, `coverage.rs` `("DeathDump", "gpiece")`).
- `widen_block_rows` adds direct rows, among them `attrition` and `sheltered` (`unit_masks2 & 0x40000`),
  `spell_time`, `mana_burn`, and `unit_masks` only bit by bit: `& 2` (mirror), `& 0x100000`, `& 0x80000`,
  `& 0x1000` (blown), `& 0x10000` (fought), `& 0x10` (contact) (`harness.rs:2080-2185`), and `compare` adds
  `unit_masks & 0x2000000` and `unit_masks2 & (0x10 | 0x200 | 0x800 | 0x20000)` (`harness.rs:254-290`). No row reads `unit_masks & 0x800`, `unit_masks & 0x400080`/`0x80`, or
  `unit_masks2 & 0x8000`/`0x60`/`0x80`. Because `UnitDump.unit_masks`/`unit_masks2` are *parsed*, they are
  off `UNREAD`; because some bit is compared, they are off `UNCOMPARED_BY_THE_INSTRUMENT`. Neither pin can
  say a bit is uncompared — that gap is this list's recurring finding.
- `UNREAD` (printed, parsed by nothing) carries on `GAME/FRAME/UNITDATA`: `healing inside_up_who …`; on
  `UNITDATA/OBJECT`: `healing inside_down inside_down_who launch_frames near_o near_who`.
