# Territory reading — group `build-1`

The seventy-first tranche's reading fan-out (steering pass, 2026-11-06). Reader: Opus 5.5. Group
file: 60 functions of the `build`, `buildqueue` and `buildtype` stems (`Build::`, `BuildData::`,
`BuildQueue::`, `BuildQueueData::`, `BuildTypeData::`, `MiningList::`), classified `open`, `name`
or `layout` by `tools/rubric.py --tables 0`. One ruling per function, read from the decompile
export (`~/ghidra-projects/decomp/funcs/<Class>/<name>@<va>.c`) against `crates/sim` and the
mechanic documents. `entered` is the TSV's lockstep column (the first run, or `-`).

A note on the `layout` kind in this group: nearly every `layout` row has a sweep under
`crates/sim/src/sweep/` that runs the original under unicorn against an in-test model
(`emu_obj` / `row`), so the sweep proves the reading, not a consumer. The ruling below asks
whether `Sim` has a consumer doing the same job under another name.
