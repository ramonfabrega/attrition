## Part 1 — the blind report

All fifteen came back **UNREACHED**, with no row unsure. Three shared findings
carry rows 3–15.

- **A. No live object's most-derived class is `Wall`, `WallOut`, `WallData`,
  `Object`, `ObjectOut` or `ObjectData`.** `Object::Object@00646e80` has three
  callers: `UnitData::UnitData` at `606691`, `BuildData::BuildData` at
  `62f393` and `Wall::Wall` at `63e3bc`. Each one overwrites the vptr with no
  call in between. A concrete `Wall` is built only by
  `eh_vector_vbase_constructor_iterator(…, Wall::Wall, Wall::~Wall)`, at three
  places:
  - `ObjectsArray::get_new_data@006450d0` case 2 deserialises a wall that
    already exists.
  - `Objects::find_free@0065ad60`'s wall arm runs when `param_2` is neither 0
    nor 2000. All seven callers pass 0 or 2000.
  - `Objects::init@0065ea80`'s loop runs to `Game::num_def_walls` (`+0x6b4`),
    which is written only 0, at `58dd8f` and `59519d`.
- **B. Slot `+0x140` is never dispatched on an object.** The six
  `call [reg+0x140]` sites are Window (`41c20c`, `41c578`), `graphsys`
  (`58cdae`, `598fd0`) and `unittypes[i]`, a `UnitType` (`65614b`, `723e7a`).
  There is no `mov`+`call reg` form and no vcall thunk.
- **C. The four destructors are reached only by `jmp` from
  `__unwindfunclet$…`**, and for `~Wall` also by being pushed as a callback
  by the dead wall-construction sites and by `Wall`'s vector deleting
  destructor. Normal destruction inlines them in `~UnitData@0046f3c0` and
  `~BuildData@00471270`.

## Part 2 — adjudication

**Every verdict agrees.** Both readings call all fifteen unreached, and for
each row the predicate is the same one. The evidence differs in three places.
None of them is a disagreement about a verdict, and each is settled by the
bytes.

The copy counts are §383's table exactly. Every rel32 caller of the four
destructors resolves, through `INDEX.tsv`, to a function named
`__unwindfunclet$…` of `UnitData`, `BuildData`, `Wall`, `WallOut`, `WallData`
or `Wall`'s vector deleting destructor.

1. **`~Object`'s funclet count: 9, not 8.** The reader's list omits
   `a895b7` (`__unwindfunclet$??0BuildData@@QAE@XZ$6`). §383.6 gives no
   count. The ninth caller is another funclet, so the verdict stands, and
   the `RESIDUE` row says nine.
2. **The Wall premise.** §383.2 rests rows 4, 5 and 9 on `Wall::Wall`'s
   existing `RESIDUE` row (`num_def_walls` written only 0). The reader
   re-derived it independently and closed the other two routes as well:
   `find_free`'s wall arm (its seven callers re-scanned here: `5ab9b4`,
   `60e0e9`, `65d1ae`, `65e137`, `796c6c`, `9b9d53`, `9b9e1c`) and
   `get_new_data`'s case 2, which needs a wall to exist first. This agrees
   with §383.2 and makes the premise stronger.
3. **`action_board_ship`'s callers: three, not two.** §382.2 cites
   GOLDEN §144.4's board-ship chain under command 0x0f. The scan finds
   `action_board_ship@00700010` called at `700106` (itself), `70e818`
   (`Group::finish_insert`) and `948f3e` (`process_board_ship`). The
   listing at `70e80a..70e818` shows `finish_insert` reading the inserted
   order's `+0x64` and passing its target on, so it re-issues a BOARD_SHIP
   order the unit already holds. That is the same circularity as row 1's
   `copy_order`, and it brings no new producer. `add_board_order@005e4d10`'s
   one caller is `70035a`, inside `action_board_ship`. `add_await_board_order`'s
   two are `604e6b` (`check_meet_ship`) and `700397` (`action_board_ship`).
   The verdict stands. Also, `00700010` has one four-byte copy, at `.reloc`
   `f256e6`; it is a byte coincidence in the relocation table, not a
   pointer.

**The falsifiers agreed on.** For every row, it is a `cover=1` trace that
enters the address. For rows 1 and 2 it is also any `UNITS` dump printing a
`GROUPATTACKORDER`, `AWAITBOARDORDER` or board-ship order. For rows 4, 5, 9 and
14 it is also any live object with `Wall`'s table (vptr `00b42cf8`), which
kills `Wall::Wall`'s own row first. For row 10 no capture reaches it without
a patch to the image.
