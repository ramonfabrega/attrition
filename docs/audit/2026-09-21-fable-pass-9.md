## Rulings

- **468, the last link.** A reap is followed by the spawn of the next
  unstarted item on that track, in the same turn. A turn does not end with
  a lane empty while the queue is not. The commander never asks to
  continue. Landed as a clause; the worker-side sentence "merge, gate,
  push and reap are one chain" now names the spawn too.
- **469, lanes are independent.** Two lanes are throughput. Each lands and
  is refilled on its own clock; the width-two clause is marked as a rule
  about content, not timing. Landed as a clause.
- **467, no amend under a live lane.** A red gate on a booking commit is a
  second commit, never an `--amend`. Landed as a clause. A guard would be
  a pre-push hook that refuses a rewritten tip while a lane roster is
  non-empty; not built, because ccc's `land` verb (313) is where it
  belongs, and the clause costs nothing until then.
