(`/Users/rf-studio/.claude/jobs/d388900a/tmp/t71/groups/group-build-2.tsv`), one row per function,

`-` = none). Nothing here is transcribed; only the expression a ruling turns on is quoted.

**The family's shape, read first.** Almost every row is `layout`: a sweep under `crates/sim/src/sweep/`
runs the original under unicorn and compares it with a port written **inside the test** (the
`emu_obj` sweeps carry their own Rust model of the body) or with a crate function under another
name (`ai_place::gather_good`, `cost::kill_value_by`, `build::fundamental`). The census calls it
`layout` because no crate fn spells `Class::method` in its doc. The question per row is therefore
whether the simulation has a consumer that does the job, under what name, and whether it agrees.

## Summary

**Counts (60):** twin 37 · twin-differs 3 · absorbed 4 · unmodelled 2 · ui 5 · saveload 4 · dead 5.

**The census's `layout` mark is mostly a naming miss in this family.** Of the 60 rows, 40 (twin and
twin-differs) have a live crate function doing the job, and most of those
functions' doc comments already spell the original's `Class::method` (`Sim::garrison_limit`,
`Sim::farm_limit`, `Sim::city_level_of`, `economy::taxes`, `stance::build_stance_type`,
`Sim::caravan_verify_road`, `Sim::city_new_caravan` …); the rubric's port test also wants
`fn <snake_case_method>`, which these do not have. A rubric that accepted a doc spelling
`Class::method` (or `@va`) on any non-`sweep/` fn would move most of those 40 to ported (not
counted row by row here); the rest need the spelling added. That is a rubric change, Ramon's to take.

**Proposed census classes:**

- **`blind::RESIDUE` into `is_dead_weight`** — read `rondata::blind::RESIDUE`'s "no reference in
  the executable" rows as dead: `BuildTypeData::is_embassy`, `BuildTypeData::area`,
  `BuildType::set_domain`, `CityData::get_trade_routes`, `CityData::pop_growth`, `Caravan::process`
  here (`area` and `set_domain` also have twins).

  `Caravan::walk_data`, `CaravanLink::walk_data`, `City::walk_data`.

  `BuildType::restore` (and `ObjectType`, `TechType`, `Type`'s pairs).
- **`DISPLAY_NAME`** (world-1's) — `City::generate_name`, `City::set_name`.

  `BuildTypeData::get_kill_value`, `UnitTypeData::get_kill_value`, `TypeData::get_kill_value`,
  `LeaderData::get_mvp_score`.

  aside): `CityData::num_kinds_needed`, `CityData::num_city_buildings`.
