## Rulings

1. **A row is a field, never a unit, and the gated blocks are read at the
   parting** (452, 453; DECISIONS 44 §1): `OrderMismatch::label` is the
   one name for an order row and `unit::rows` uses it; `compare` files the
   collision block, `start_dist` and the angles in `collide_parted`,
   `search_parted` and `angle_parted` on the unit-frames whose position
   parted, counted nowhere and readable everywhere. run89, run94, run100's
   and chapter two's widenings pass unchanged in verdict.
2. **A widening declares its window beside the word** (449; §2):
   `WIDENINGS` rows carry `(lo, hi)`, the two widening tests read their
   bounds from the same constants, and the floors guard requires the word
   strictly inside. Made to fail with chapter two's window at `(630, 640)`.
3. **An item number is minted once, in its file's form** (461; §3):
   `docs_guard::an_item_number_is_minted_once_and_in_its_file_s_form`
   reads the queue, the parked file and the journal directory. Its first
   run found three rows; all three fixed in the same commit.
4. **`docs/RUNS.md` merges by union, and no heading stands twice** (428;
   §4): `.gitattributes` and `docs_guard::no_runs_section_heading_stands_
   twice`, made to fail on a pasted heading. The union driver's one
   failure mode is a hunk applied twice, which the guard sees; a section
   edited on both sides at once is not covered, and the brief's
   section-reservation rule is what covers it.
5. **The lane has a lock** (446; §5): `ron_wine` in `winelaunch.sh`
   refuses to launch while the pid of the game it last launched is alive,
   names the holder, and releases itself on the game's exit. Checked
   live: a held lock refuses with 75, `RON_LANE_FORCE=1` overrides, a
   dead pid is taken over. It does not replace the protocol — astra asks,
   Ramon relays — it closes the one case the protocol could not.
6. **`memcap.sh` has a fixture with teeth** (251; §6): a 1,200 MiB
   process under a 1 GiB cap dies with 137 in two seconds, and an exit
   status under the cap comes through. The two doors stay open and are
   named in the module.
7. **A trailer is the worker's own** (335; §7): a clause in `CLAUDE.md`'s
   fan-out rules; measured on the tranche — seven workers, the commander
   and both passes — every trailer agrees with lore's first-request model.
8. **375 leaves the Loop**: a code guard a worker can take, names no score,
   parked in the ordinary list. **313 stays**, filed with ccc.
9. **PR #4 is merged whole** (458; §8) on entry 38's terms.
10. **Four clauses in `CLAUDE.md`**: a row is a field and the gated
    blocks are read at the parting (448); grep this crate for a field
    before reading the original's writers of it (443); a probe of a
    mid-frame decision runs inside the decision (456); a trailer is the
    worker's own (335). And two facts where the rules already speak: the
    lane lock in the Wine bullet, the union merge in the file table.
