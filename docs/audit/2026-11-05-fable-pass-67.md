> (3880) **A melee that closes whole is red at the booking gate on its audit row, never at its lane gate** (the sixty-seventh's commander, 3873): `diff::audit::every_whole_scoreboard_word_is_audited` holds every whole word on an audited line to an `audited!` row or an `OWED` line, and a melee's lane gate filters (`airbase_melee:: sea_melee:: … first_parting battery_floors blind:: coverage::`) never run `diff::audit::`, so 3873's lane gate was green on the fifth melee closing whole and the scoped booking gate on `f9eb0373e` was red on it alone — a second commit (`6eaeec7e2`) and a re-gate, 12.5 release minutes, exactly as the fourth melee's closing did at 3862 (`f28d7fbe5`).
