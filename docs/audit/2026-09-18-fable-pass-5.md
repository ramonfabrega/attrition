## What was found

- **Nothing measured "how done" absolutely.** All three of DECISIONS
  29's counters are relative to what has been inspected. The lab said so
  on 09-09 (methodology §4) and proposed the inventory; the census is
  that inventory built, and it becomes the fourth counter.
- **The loop is serial by construction, not by habit.** No recording
  holds an AI decision, every scored capture is an idle human against an
  AI, and the harness maps two command kinds; so every rule so far was
  verified through the AI's own orders, one decision at a time.
- **The tools for a staged game exist and have run**: `rontrace.cmd`
  (run16), `!ffwd` at ~500 frames a second, the lab's click-free lane
  with native `issue_move_to` from the DLL (L09, L15: two launches
  matched on every projection), `ai off`/`human who`, and in
  `Unit::think` the auto-engage call precedes the AI-off gate.
- **The long captures cannot be the sim's finish line**: 24,000 frames
  end in the Medieval at best; five ages would never be diffed when they
  close. The `age` cheat between chapters of a golden record is the
  cheap lever; a lobby starting age is the AI track's later business.
- **Two stale claims corrected.** The blind list was not frozen — it was
  unfrozen 09-08 (802 cited, 650 entered, 152 never) and `docs/PARKED.md`
  still said "101 of 617"; and the memory file said `cover=1` page-faults.
  The blind *readings* were the low-yield thing the diff-first rule
  starved; the blind *list* is free and the census subsumes it.
- **The postmortem, reread.** BHG's Const System is the sim/renderer
  split, written in 2003 as a compiler-assisted firewall against desyncs;
  four to six full-time testers balanced the rules for eight months;
  "claiming done before it is" was their named failure. `GameAccess`
  looks like the firewall's game-world side — unverified, a type-record
  read away.
