## Disagreements, adjudicated

1. **Accuracy.** A: `to_hit + (attack_dist / 96) × attenuate`. B2: `to_hit −
   attenuate × ⌊attack_dist / 192⌋`, minimum 5. **B.** The multiply is by
   `−0x2aaaaaab` with a `>> 32` then `>> 5` and the sign fix — `/ −192`; the
   first reader took the `>> 5` as a second `/ 2` of a `/ 96`. And
   `UnitType::init` stores `ATTENUATE` as an absolute value, so the engine
   subtracts. Landed in §9.1, `combat::accuracy`, the harness test.
2. **The one-in-five retarget draw.** A: taken when the target is not a
   combat unit and the order lacks flag `0x10`. B2: taken on every frame the
   block runs with a unit target — `Random::get` is evaluated before the
   `||` — and it is the *result* that is ignored for a combat-unit target;
   the block runs for a captain with a non-mandatory, non-group order (or the
   group leader) when not recharging. **B.** Landed in §8.2 step 0 and
   `process_unit_combat`.
3. **Siege at a unit.** A: an unpacked siege unit in range shoots the unit.
   B2: an unpacked `unit_flags2 & 4` type that is siege, with a unit target,
   inserts an `ATTACK_GROUND` order at the target's position and returns.
   **B** — the first reader had read the block and not written it. Landed in
   §8.2 step 1 and `fire_ammo` (a targetless shot at the point).
4. **`total_time`.** B2: zero becomes one. **New**; landed.
5. **The lead.** B2: a moving unit target has the landing point pushed
   `sinx × total_time`, `− cosx × total_time`. **New**; landed with the natural
   operands (the target's heading and speed), noted as such.
6. **The splash search area.** A: tiles within `radius[min(splash_area / 4,
   10)]`, read as a circle. B2: the spiral table walked to `radius[k]`, `k =
   splash_area / 4 + GLOG_MISC (1)` capped at `GLOG_WORLD (10)`, a `(2k+1)²`
   square of **cells**. **B** — the `+ GLOG_MISC` is an enum value of 1 the
   first reader took for a tag. Landed in §9.3 and `combat::splash_cells`.
7. **Building splash distance.** A: `|Δx| − x_size × 0xc0` only. B2: both
   axes by symmetry, the y term in a lost register. **Both** — neither can
   read it; the symmetric reading is taken and the loss is said.
8. **The anti-air building gate.** A: a `LOOKOUT`/`OBSERVATIONPOST` with
   ANTI_AIR, or any recharging building, decrements and returns. B2: a
   building *without* ANTI_AIR, or one of those two, decrements and returns;
   other ANTI_AIR buildings skip the gate and are refused at the fire step.
   **B** — the first reader inverted the `goto`. Landed in §8.6.
9. **The flock roll's type test.** A: "temple or town or not a dock". B1:
   fort (`is_fort`), temple or town, and the second draw only for a siege
   attacker. **B** — `+0xfc` on a building type is `is_fort`, not `is_dock`
   (which is `+0x108`). Landed in §7.2.
10. **Militia arrows.** A: militia count nothing. B2: militia-line units and
    militia-tech workers count `(militia upgrade attack / 10 + 2) / 3`.
    **B** — the branch after the `has_tech` test, which the first reader did
    not read to its end. Landed in §8.6.
11. **The `0x30` tile test.** A: fog, on the target's tile in both
    `valid_target_const` and `is_in_range`. B2: `is_in_range` reads the
    *attacker's* tile (`px, py` are its own position); the bits are a
    terrain class (`check_hit` reads `0x20` as ocean). **B** on both counts;
    `valid_target_const` does read the target's. Landed in §12.1 and §13.2.
12. **`fire_proj`.** A: 1 iff `attack != 0`, from `UnitType::init`. B2: then
    rewritten by `GraphicEvents::init_unit_events`, sometimes to zero, and
    `fight` strikes only when non-zero. **New**, unresolved — open question 8.
13. **The flank bonus' mask register and scale.** Both readers and the
    document agree the `EDX` read is a lost register and that `cavalry_flank_
    bonus`/`vehicle_flank_bonus` are plain integers consumed `>> 8`. Stays
    open (questions 1–2).
