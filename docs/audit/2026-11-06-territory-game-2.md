`game`, `gameinfo`, `gamemath`, `miscaccess`, `setup`, `syncpoint` and
`unitbalance` stems' open rows (52 functions; `tools/rubric.py --tables 0`'s
open territory). Read by name from the decompile export

group file's `entered_by_lockstep` column (`L` = run1016–1021, the six `cover=1`
twins; `-` none; other runs named). "No caller" claims were checked twice: by
name over the export, and by a scan of `riseofnations.exe` for every `E8`/`E9`/
`0F 8x` branch into the address and every absolute pointer to it in any section
(the scan item 3409 ran for `is_no_rush`, repeated here). Nothing below is
transcribed; only the expression a ruling turns on is quoted.
