# The queue

Where the work stands and what comes next — the file a fresh session reads
first, and the one a subagent never sees. `CLAUDE.md` is the rules; this is
the state; `docs/journal/` is the story. There is no backlog file: a finding
that names no score is in the journal of the item that found it (DECISIONS 125).

This file **deletes**; `queueledger.py` fails the build on a number that
leaves in silence or is referred to and never booked; `docs_guard.rs` bounds
it by items (18 at 8 lines, the handoff 32). **A finding that names no
score stays in its item's journal**: only the headline's frame, a floor, or a takes-chain books here.

## How to maintain this file

- **End of session:** rewrite "Where things stand" from scratch, headline first and whether it moved; delete
  finished items — their story is in `docs/journal/`. Shorten only what you touch; never widen a line to beat a count.
- **A residue item is booked by its frame and its draw delta** (DECISIONS 42); a mechanism in its title is the previous
  item's hypothesis, not a build order, and an absence it cites ("ours has no X") names the probe that showed it (Loop 2704).
- **A finding parks by default, in its item's journal** (`## Parked`, `- **<item><letter>. title**`; DECISIONS 125): what names no
  score is written there; this file takes only what names a headline's frame, a floor, or
  a takes-chain to one; **a worker parks what names the headline's frame
  and the commander books it at the merge.** (The two never-empty slots of
  DECISIONS 41 went with the milestone, DECISIONS 133 §7.)
- **A worker never books a number and never edits this file or
  `docs/JOURNAL.md`.** It reports; the commander books and writes these
  lines; the story is `docs/journal/<date>-item-<N>.md`. **A pinned
  constant is the worker's to re-pin; the line is the commander's**, and
  so are `census::SIMULATION_BACKED` and `SIMULATION_PORT_BACKED`, re-pinned at booking (Loop 2367, 3093).
  The chain — merge, book, spawn, gate, push, reap — is `docs/LOOP.md`'s.
- **The opener names no item between passes**: mid-tranche it reads "continue the tranche", and the lanes line
  under "The queue" says what is out and what each lane takes next; a booking rewrites that line, not the opener
  (DECISIONS 133 §7: the last opener sent a fresh session to cut builders on four landed steps).
- **The floors and these lines move together** — `FLOORS`, `LONG_WORD_*`,
  `GOLDEN_WORD_*`, `ENDPOINTS`, `AI_WORDS` with `Scoreboard:`, `Long captures:`,
  `Golden:` (`<name> closed`, or `every chapter closed` first), `Endpoint <frame>:`,
  `Second pair:`, `Third map:`, `Census:`, `Blind:`, `Rubric:` (the worker re-pins `RUBRIC_UNIT_TYPES_SEEN`, the commander writes it; DECISIONS 90),
  `Melee:` (`… of <length> <n> off`, pinned by `*_MELEE_CLOSING_OFF`, Loop 3842) and `lower map first — <map>` — read
  literally and **never wrapped**, or the count guard matches this line
  instead (twice, 09-19). **A line whose every pinned row is whole may take the closed form the ten lines above use**
  (the words, then the count of its rows; `, 0 off` after it on `Melee:`); the guards take it only while every row is whole, and a named regression asks
  for the rows written out again (DECISIONS 133 §6). The length guard counts every line to the next `## `.
- **The gate is `python3 tools/release_gate.py <install> --test-threads 8 [--scope <tip>]`** (DECISIONS 72), to
  a file, never piped — a pipe launders the 137 — ending `Gate steps:`, `Admission wait:` and `Host load:`; **whole
  (no `--scope`) at the drain and at a pass** (DECISIONS 132 §5); **a worker's takes
  `--lane --tests --test-threads 4`** (the brief's, 1623), runs its filters and the paperwork (132 §6), and quotes
  `Lane verdict:` (969).
