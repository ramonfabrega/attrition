# Item 368 — 9182 is not the step-6 purchases, and the census that skipped the human

*2026-09-18, Opus 5, worker on `worktree-att-368`.*

Booked as "9182's step-6 purchases, and the word is still on them". It is
not the step-6 purchases. **The word did not move** — 9182 still, 9362
for the count. What moved is two floors and one whole family of
explanation.

## Re-measuring first, as the brief asked

`run53: word parts at 9362, sequence at 9182 of 24000`. Ten draws either
side, parting at index 1: ours `make_stuff+0x221`, theirs
`use_market+0x1ed`. §42's table holds.

The brief's mechanism is **downstream of the parting**. `use_market` runs
first thing in `make_stuff`, so its draw count is index 0–2 and the
step-6 expiry draws are index 3 onward. Whatever makes the original spend
three market draws is also what refuses its step-6 buys — one vector, not
two mechanisms.

`RON_DEBUG_LEADER` already printed the make list; it did not name the
types or their costs. Five lines later it did, and the frame became

## What I read, and what it ruled out

- **`use_market@006c91c0`, again, line for line.** The once-flag clears
  on a candidate passing the *sell test*, not on a sale; the wealth
  branch is `stock[2] − buy < need[2]` with 2 = `WEALTH` from the
  `TypeIndex` enum; `market_sellable`'s second clause is
  `resource_cap[g]/2 <= income[g]`, both at the offsets
  `LeaderDataEncrypt` gives. `epoch[2]` is `data_encrypted + 0xf0`, which
  is where "commerce" comes from. All of it is this crate's.
- **`MakeList::make_me@006c9be0`, read whole for the first time.** Slot 1
  carrying `val 6,000,000` under a `t −1` is not a bug: it is the head
  branch's dedupe, which writes the new best over slot 0 without shifting
  the old head down and then clears `t` on any of 1–3 of the same type.
  Shift bound, dedupe bound (`< 0x79`), the "already ranks higher" break
  and the category slot all check out.

## The cause, found in a capture that was already on disk

§42 booked a capture because "no `LEADERS=9` dump covers 9182". True of
9182 — and not true of the machinery. **run19 is a `LEADERS=9` window
over `[8174, 8192]` and this crate has been comparing the whole record
for weeks.** Its residue holds six `1/MAKE[*].val` rows: Coinage
3,510,000 against 2,700,000; Empire 2,100,000 against 1,800,000;
Mercenaries 165,000 against **216,000** — ours *lower*; Temple 82,500
against 63,000. Types agree, `num` agrees since §42. The values do not,
and one is low while three are high, which no single factor on `base` can
produce.

`ai_research::weight_total` reads five census facts, and four of the five
are in the same residue at nought: `1/active_wars`, `1/wars`,
`1/other_team_terr` 0 against **266**, `1/min_other_team_terr` the same.
It adds `ai[5]` (twice under `min_other`) when behind on territory and
takes `ai[0]/3` rather than `ai[5] + ai[1]` when at war — per-tech terms,
which is why five values are wrong by five different ratios.

Three census loops skip human leaders, and §2.3 step 4 said "every other
**computer** leader". The export says otherwise:
`plan_strategy@006b9620:159-166` gates on `leader_flags & 2`, `i != who`,
`i >= 0` and nothing else; `:1511` and `:1557` the same plus
`treaties[i] & 1`. **Bit 1 is set for every leader every capture
prints** — run97's own header has the human at `leader_flags 7`. On a
one-human-one-AI game the human is the only other leader there is.
`other_team_terr 266` is `0/my_team_terr 266`.

This is the class of error the audits keep finding: the arithmetic was
right and the *predicate* — which leaders count — was not.

## What landed, and what parked

**`census_territory` counts the human.** `1/other_team_terr` and
`1/min_other_team_terr` leave run19's residue, **92 fields → 90**, and
**nothing else in the record moves** — all six `1/MAKE[*].val` stand, so
for these four techs the territory predicate does not pay. The word did
not move. run19 needed no new run to confirm it: the capture was on disk.

**`census_wars` and `census_strategy` park.** Putting the human back into
both makes all five facts agree — and takes run19's residue from 90
fields to **147**, because `active_wars != 0` reaches the danger word and
the region-strategy words too, and the make list then parts on `t`,
`cat`, `city` and `val` across nearly every slot. The facts land and the
consequences do not, which means something downstream of `active_wars`
was being hidden by the nought. A successor that makes a residue worse
before better needs its number visible, so: **90 → 147**.

Part of that successor is that the original runs a full census for its
*human* leader (`0/gatherers 5`, `0/peasants 5` in every dump) and this
crate runs none, so `census_strategy`'s `weaker` test reads the
opponent's `attack` as nought even with the gate fixed.
