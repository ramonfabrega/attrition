## The close, as the listing reads

The listing is `610c30`..`610ca0`.

1. `testb $0x1, 0x68(%ebx)` takes the decoy arm.
2. `incw 0x96(%ebx)` raises the age, and then it is read back.
3. `get_general_upgrade` is called, then `(r + 2) × decoy_time` (from
   `constants + 0xcd0`), then `cltd; subl; sarl` to halve it.
4. `cmpl %eax, %ecx; jl` compares, so the close takes **`life <= age`**,
   checked after the increment.
5. On the close: `push 0` (a float `0.0`), `push −1`, `push 0`, and
   `call *0x150(%eax)`, then return.
6. **The vtable slot was read off the PE** (rule 1090). `Unit::vftable` is
   at `0xb417d0`, and its `+0x150` holds `0x60ee50`, which is
   `Unit::close@0060ee50`. For comparison, `UnitData::vftable`'s slot is
   `Object::close`.
7. **`Unit::close(0, −1, 0.0)`**:
   - its death arm (`Random::get`, `GLOG_DEATHS`, `kill_guy`) sits under
     `param_1 != 0`, so a zero first argument **spends no draw**;
   - the population and `track_unit_type` arm is gated on
     `(field_0x68 & 1) == 0`, so a decoy hands back nothing (its
     population was handed back at its creation);
   - what is left is the squad relink (`+0x8e`/`+0x90`), the orders
     (`close_orders`), and `Object::close`'s hold.
8. **`DECOY_TIME`** is `rules.xml`'s `2500 frames`. `get_general_upgrade`
   is 0 here: 2,500 is the only life that fits 2499 standing on 14136 and
   gone on 14137.
