# Item 4018 — the camera, proven against the original's function

Opus 5.5 at `--effort medium`, lane B, base `4f5dde2aa`. One session. The first `render` item.

## What was booked, and what it turned out to be

The booking asked for a pure camera and projection in a new `render` crate. It was to be read from
`BaseCamera::update_transforms` and `Camera::update_transforms`, then checked against `MiscAccess::world_to_screen`
under unicorn on synthesized states, and on a packet already on disk. Two things in the reading changed the shape of
the check.

- **The projection matrix is not in the executable.** `graphsys` comes from a DLL: `GraphSys::init_system` calls
  `get_graphsy_object_ptr`, exported by the install's `d3dgl.dll`. That DLL ships its own `d3dgl.pdb` and map, so
  slots `0x198` and `0x19c` have names: `D3D11GLSys::set_projection` and `set_projection_parallel`. The oracle maps
  the DLL beside the executable and runs those two functions too. Without that, the sweep would have checked only
  `world_to_screen`'s last stage against a matrix this crate built itself.
- **The game's camera is orthographic.** run1484's packet holds `parallel = 1`, with `parallel_distance` equal to
  `zoom_distance / 2`. So the zoom scales the picture through `parallel_distance` alone.

## The reading, then the build

The reading is `docs/RENDERER.md` §3.4. The parts that cost a wrong guess were settled by the listing, not the
decompile:

- `world_to_console`'s true signature is `(x, y, z, w, h)`, with the outputs in `ecx` and `edx`.
- `Quat::set` puts the cosine in `w`, where the decompile reads it swapped.
- The negative-angle reduction is `359 − (−a mod 360)`.

The crate is `crates/render/src/camera.rs`, with no dependency. It follows the original's single-precision
operation order, so it lands on the original's pixel, not beside it.
