## The mechanism

`Roads::redo_changed_roads@0088e670` sets `flags = 0` only on the tiles of its list. Then, where `set_diags`
answers zero, it runs `mark_splits`, `mark_white_lines`, `mark_yellow_lines`, `mark_intersections` and
`fixup_lines`. `mark_intersections@00892390` only ORs. A tile joined E–W takes `E` (`0x10`) where its east
neighbour is not joined both ways, and `W` likewise. N–S works the same way. `mark_splits@00891d40` zeroes the low
byte of each neighbour it joins. A straight laid west to east keeps an `E` on every tile but the last, because
each tile is last redone while its successor is the run's end. The sim test asserts
`[0, 0x11, 0x10, 0x10, 0]` on five tiles. A once-only evaluation marks only the second tile's `W` and the fourth's
`E`.

`mark_splits`' own joins go into a local copy and are never written back, so the simulation's connections are
untouched.
