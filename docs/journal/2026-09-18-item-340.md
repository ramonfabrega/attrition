### What this did not establish

- **The `+0x1d` set's tie-break.** A `variant + 0x1d` that lands on `0x20`
  walks the host's chain for a scholar already playing `0x20` and takes
  `0x1d` instead; the decompiler's aliasing does not settle whether the
  walk starts at the head or its successor, and this crate tests "any
  other scholar in the chain". The falsifier is named: a `GUYS` window
  over a university holding **two or more** scholars. run98's holds one.
- **A scholar inside a *unit*.** `inside_up` points at a boat as readily as
  at a building; a boat's passengers are a filter here rather than an
  ordered chain, so the head cannot be told and this crate answers "not the
  head". No capture has a scholar aboard anything.
- **`guy_flags & 0x80` outliving a type change.** `Guy::init_real` sets it
  from the type the figure was *born* with and this crate reads the unit's
  current `Worker`. Nothing upgrades a scholar in any capture.
