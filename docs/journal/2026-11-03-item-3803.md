# Item 3803 — `TerrainGroups::place_all` on Great Sahara (2026-11-03, `att-3803`, Opus 5.5)

**Map generation's third phase agrees with the original on its first run.**
The port's `place_all` produces the original's own draw stream, site for site
and word for word, on **all 280 Great Sahara draw-site traces**: 267 at 2
players (1,238 draws) and 13 at 3 (1,324). Seed and parts 2, 5 and 6 agree at
`map.cpp` 7859 on all 105 files that carry it. `floors::MAPGEN` GreatSahara
moves 2 → 3 of 8. The `Mapgen:` line is the commander's to write:
`GreatSahara 3 of 8 phases`.

Two sessions did this work. The first ran four readings and wrote the port,
untested, and cleared at 332 k. The second wrote the install inputs and the
check, made every pin fail on purpose, and wrote the paperwork.

## How it was established

**The readings.** Four Opus 5.5 readers each wrote a specification in their
own words, outside the repo, and checked it against the listing:
- A: the ladder, the patterns, the rings, `scale_number`, the parsed table.
- B: `place_player_group` and its callees.
- C: `place_region_group`, its LFSR walk and the start tests.
- D: `add_doobers`, `treeify_mountains`, the land table and the templates.

`sim::terrain_groups` was written from the four readings. Every draw is
tagged with the return address the trace records for it (`site::*`).

**The no-float note came first, as the booking required.** Two Great Sahara
groups are pattern 2 (`world`). The weight runs only when `clumps > 6 &&
regions < 6`, which only the sprinkling group (n = 20) meets. The listing
(0x6a7e24..0x6a7f29) is all single precision and every step is correctly
rounded, so `sim::single` reproduces it under DECISIONS 19 (MAPGEN §11). On
one region the weight is `n` exactly.

**The inputs come from the install** (`rondata::mapgen`):
- `terrain_groups` uses `scale_number`, with `SCALE` = `max(1, (35 +
  xs·v)/70)` and 70 being `rules.xml`'s Standard map size, read from the
  file. Its output equals reader A's table row by row
  (`great_sahara_s_groups_parse_as_read`).
- `tileset_groups` reads GS_Desert's 60/50/40.
- `group_template` lays `rondata::mountains`' templates. That module's
  orientation (top row first) is already pinned against run144's packet, so
  the draft's first open risk was already closed on disk.
- `place_all_inputs` assembles all three.
- `place_all_draws` reads a trace's segment, from the first
  `randomize_mountains` draw to the first `nubify_forest` draw, and stops
  reading there. A whole sweep takes under five seconds.

**The check** is `great_sahara_s_place_all_is_the_traced_stream_and_7859`.
For each generator run in the archive it runs 3798's chain to 7854, then
`place_all`. A trace is Great Sahara's when a gamelog of its run names the
map. The first run keyed traces by their opening word alone, and six
traces "parted" at the ladder: Himalayas (run348, run464, run465, run1128), the
click-free Amazon (run1099) and Southwest Mesa (run1118). They enter
`place_all` on the same seed and read their own groups. The 2- and
3-player runs enter on one seed and one grid, so a trace passes when it
matches one run's stream whole, and both runs must be met. Then seed and
parts 2, 5 and 6 at 7859. The 7859 grids hold forest, mountain, rock and oil
cells: 151/134/42/10 at 2 players, 182/164/51/15 at 3.

**The pins fail when they should** (`tools/mutate.py`, each `held`):
1. LFSR tap 0x16800 → 0x16900: all 280 traces part at draw 23.
2. Rings randomized small, large, medium: all part at draw 0, on the site
   (`0x89cb34` against `0x89cae1`).
3. The fringe cell's FOREST bit dropped: no draw moves, and 7859 part 5
   parts.
4. A stray tile-mask bit (`|= 0x408`): no draw and no cell moves, and 7859
   part 6 parts alone.

## What it found beside the score

- **The region walk is a 16-bit LFSR, not 17-bit.** The listing XORs
  0x16800 before the shift, so the taps are 0xB400 and the period is 65,535
  (`the_region_walk_visits_every_state`, which failed first at 65,535
  against 131,071). MAPGEN §7 and the port's comment said 17-bit; both are
  amended. Reader C had it right. The draws were never affected.
- **The oil open risk is settled.** Oil cells are placed on both lobbies
  with `goods` 0, and part 5 agrees. The OIL object `set_oil_at` makes walks
  no part.
