The first reading was that the original's `gather_max` is zero until a
building starts. It fits the timing: the Mine is placed on 10583 and
starts on 10766, and the region count moves on the next sweep. But
`Build::init` and `find_gather_tiles` both write `+0x80` at placement, and

since the placement.

`count_gather_slots` has no completion test, and this crate's did.
