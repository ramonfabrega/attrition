## Step 3: the reading

Two functions write `WData.val` (`+0xc` of the 0x1c cell): `World::
compute_all_vals` at map build (and the constructor and the scenario loader), and **`City::fix_world_vals@00735aa0`**.
`City::init@00737050` calls `fix_world_vals` last and unconditionally,
so it runs on a founding and on a capture alike. `fix_world_vals` does
this:

- the centre is the city's cell;
- `r = min(get_radius, 0x40)` and `k = min((r + 3) / 4, 0x3d)`;
- for `i < circle_radius[k + 3]`, `val >>= 2` while `i < circle_radius[k]`,
  and `>>= 1` otherwise, each cell in bounds.

`City::close` gives nothing back. `docs/CITIES.md` §5.1 named the call
("AI site values"), and nothing here implemented it.

Norwich (54, 32) was founded after the frame-0 dump. (55, 25) is seven
cells from it, in the halved rings, so its `val` 4 became 2 and it
scores 1253. (51, 22) is ten cells away and keeps 3. The original's
spiral takes (55, 25), and so does ours now.
