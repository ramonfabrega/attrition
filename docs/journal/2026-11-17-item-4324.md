## The family

- **The writer.** `plan_strategy`'s step 12, read with `BitMask<44>::count` beside it: zero when `flags & 1` (the empty
  cache), otherwise the bit count of `size` (6) bytes from `ptr` (`+0x6da4`). The count rewrites `flags`: 0 when it
  found bits, 1 when it found none. The type record gives the layout as `bits` +0, `size` +4, `flags` +8, `ptr` +0xc.
  `docs/AI.md`'s "words from `+0x6da0`" was the `flags` word, amended in place.
- **The cache.** `BitMask<17>::clear@0047cd10` (the folded name) writes `flags = 1`. `Leader::gather@006ce280` copies
  `rare = rare_owned | rare_conquest` whole, `flags` included (`BitMask<44>::operator|` takes the left operand's).
  The crate does not carry `flags`, and the checksum folds `bits`, `size` and `ptr` without it. Counting the mask
  itself equals the original wherever the cache matches its bytes, and every writer the export shows keeps them
  matched.
- **The direction: the original acted.** Theirs' `+0x850` holds the count; ours wrote a constant. No mask writer was
  added.
