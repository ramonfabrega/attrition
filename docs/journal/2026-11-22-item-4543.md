- **The arithmetic.** With `ATTRITION` 48, a 24 means susceptibility 128
  in place of 256. The candidates are trader, air, peace, or strength
  doubled. `UnitData::get_attrition@00608fd0` ends with the aged-up arm:
  `ahead = ages[ground] − ages[victim]`, and if `ahead ≥ 0`, strength
  becomes `((ATTRITION_AGED_UP × ahead + 100) × strength + 99) / 100`.
  The age it reads is `*(leaders[p] +0x6eb8) +0xdc ^ 0x62766`, the
  leader's `ages` word.
- **The cause.** Ours computed `ahead` from `PlayerState.age`. No writer
  sets that field: `docs/ATTRITION.md` §3 carried it as a SEAM ("the
  aged-up arm is never taken here"). The crate already holds the same word
  as `PlayerTech::ages`: `fold_rates` and `unit_update_hits_view` read it,
  and the leaders line walks it (`LeaderDataEncrypt::walk_data`, the 60th
  of 62 words).

**Both sides' ages on the frame**, from theirs' leaders record on 6108
(the leaders line agrees to 6119, so ours holds the same): leader 0
