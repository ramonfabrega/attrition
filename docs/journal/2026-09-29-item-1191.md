## The reading

- `Unit::set_new_location@005f8d20`'s sea arm: `eject_contents(boat, 0, -1,
  1, 1)`, then, if `num_inside == 0`, `Object::die(boat, 0, -1, 0)` and
  `return 0`.
- `Object::die@00647080` calls the `+0x150` slot, `Unit::close@0060ee50`.
  With `param_1 == 0` its `kill_guy` loops are skipped; it frees `+0xb8`,
  closes the orders and calls `Object::close@00647160`, which removes the
  object from the world and the object list and clears `+0x8`. **Neither
  writes the guy array (`+0xf4`) or its counts (`+0xb5`, `+0xe8`).**
- `Unit::process@00610bc0` calls the think (`+0x188`) and then loops
  `Guy::process` over `0 .. +0xb5` and `+0x304 .. +0xe8` with no test
  between. So the dead boat's figure runs `Guy::move`, and its at-`des`,
  settled, walk-and-stopped arm calls `set_anim(CHAR_DEFAULT, 0, 1)`: the
  idle roll at `+0x19f`.
- **Ours** returned from `process_unit` as soon as the unit was dead after
  `work`, and [`Sim::disembark`] is the one death this crate takes inside a
  unit's own work.

## The build

- `Sim::process_unit` (`crates/sim/src/lib.rs`): a sea unit dead and off
  the map after its own `work` — the boat `disembark` just killed — runs
  `process_movement` before returning. A `SEAM` beside it names what is not
  modelled: a unit killed in its own work *with* a death animation (its
  figures go to `kill_guy` first), and the sixty-fourth-frame repaint of the
  dead boat's disc, which `coll_follow`/`coll_repaint` skip.
- `a_boat_that_steps_ashore_in_its_own_work_still_pays_its_arrival`
  (`crate::transport`): a barge a step from the shore, facing its waypoint,
  on the walk with `stopped` set; one `process_unit` puts its passenger out,
  kills it, and leaves its figure on `DEFAULT`. The fixture needed the
  boat's speed, a turn rate, and an average speed at its base (else
  `walk_for` gives `SLOG`, which is not the arm's `CHAR_WALK`).
