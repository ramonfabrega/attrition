A probe (`RON_PROBE_4535`, committed whole and reverted whole) printed
our arrow inputs at each shot. On 5968 two of the owner's Citizens were
inside, and on 5999 five (`0/3`, `0/4`, `0/1`, `0/28`, `0/6`; attack 40,
range 0, FOOT). Ours counted each `(4 + 1) / 2` = 2. On 5999 that is
`(10 + 8/2) / 8` = 1 arrow, so `recharge 30 / 1`.

`BuildData::get_garrison_arrows@0062e3d0` sums
`ObjectData::count_inside@0064dda0(COUNT_GARRISON_ARROWS)`. That case
takes a worker (`is_worker`) whose owner `has_tech` its tribe's militia
(`MILITIA` when `tribe_can_type` allows it, else the type's slot `+0x68`),
or a unit that `is(MILITIA)`. Such a figure adds `(attack / 10 + 2) / 3`
of the building owner's `current_upgrade(MILITIA)` type (`+0x1e8`), in
place of its own halved attack. COMBAT §8.6 had read the arm, and
`units/count_inside_view.rs` ports it unwired. `garrison_attack_sum` said
"the militia third is not modelled". With a militia third of 3 or more, five
Citizens give `(15 + 4) / 8` = 2 arrows, which is theirs' 15. Two give 1
arrow either way, which is why 5968 agreed.
