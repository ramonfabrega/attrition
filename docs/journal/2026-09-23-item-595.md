## The pivot, read to the listing

`Unit::set_attack@005fce70` aims figures `0 .. guy_mark` and nothing else.
That explains the horse's `ox −1`. For a type whose graphic piece has
`<RESTRICTION>` rows, a `max_range` and a live target, it answers with
the last of those figures' `Guy::set_all_pivots@005d8bc0`. `fight`
keeps `this->angle` when the answer is non-zero. COMBAT §8.2 had read that
answer as "wait for the pivot, skip the attack". The dump says the attack
goes ahead on that frame: `recharging 25` on 634, with no turn.

`set_all_pivots` read from `llvm-objdump 0x5d8bc0..0x5d8fa7`, since the
decompile drops both fastcall pairs:

- `find_angle`'s `ecx`/`edx` are the target minus **the unit's** point
  minus the node's rotated offset (`cvttss2si` of `get_position`'s
  vector).
- `angle_to_degrees`' `ecx` is that bearing minus the **figure's**
  `+0x18` angle.
- The four float constants are the PE's own: 180, 360, 45 and −45.

`angle_to_degrees@00a28e00` looked like rounding, and it isn't. A Python
check of the decompiled arithmetic against `round(a·360/2³²)` agreed on
two million random angles and disagreed on 1136 of the 58,000 points
within ±40 units of a half degree. So it is written as the steps it
takes.

The rows are `unit_graphics.xml`'s 81 `<RESTRICTION>`s, bound by `GRAPH`.
`String::operator==@00a1f140` ends in `_wcsicmp`, so `Mameluke` binds.
`CHARIOT` is node 4, −180..180. run145's `0/8` bears at −37°, inside ±45°.
