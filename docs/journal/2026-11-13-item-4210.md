The four cells are a radius-4 disc round fog (105, 47), the cells to r² 20 that no other disc lights.
Citizen `1/7` stands there: `TypeIndex` `0x32`, land (the probe's `TI` and domain), one of the
citizens `build_units` gives `1/2001`, the Woodcutter. It is born on the building's point at (105, 46), and its birth disc there is lit on both sides
((101, 48), (102, 49) and (103, 50) seen by both). `come_out` then steps it to (105, 47).

`Unit::come_out@00617c10`'s no-host arm (`get_inside` < 0) finds the spot and calls
`set_new_location(spot, 1, 1)` at `:518` without first storing the spot in `+0x10`/`+0x14`. The
inside arms do store it (`:506`). So `005f8d20`'s half-cell test compares the old fog cell with the
new one, sees a change, and calls vslot `+0x174` with `param_3 == 0`: `update_seen(0)`, the whole
disc at the new spot. `Sim::come_out_own_point` moved the unit through `Sim::set_new_location`,
which makes no reveal; the reveal is its callers' `moved_to`. It now ends in
`self.moved_to(u, from, false)`, the half-cell test and the whole disc.

Why the late ages: these lobbies start with `STARTING_RESOURCES 7`, eight more citizens, so more of
them step across a fog cell. Only where a new disc pokes outside the city's are cells lit that
nothing else lit.
