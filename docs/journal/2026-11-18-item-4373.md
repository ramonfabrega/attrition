*GPT-6, direct sequential Codex branch, 2026-11-18.*

Scoring a complete recording used the same named field records as a
diagnostic dump, although its caller consumes only bytes. On recordings A
and B, constructing the world ranges averaged 5.08 and 5.03 ms per sampled
state before the change. The detailed walk and byte walk now share their

The field walk still names every leaf and distinguishes unavailable data.
Neither path caches simulation state between frames.

**Allocation and time.** A counting allocator measured the normal public
checksum at states 0, 500, 1500 and 3000, counting allocations and
reallocations. Recording A took 211,305–211,604 calls before and
13,221–13,359 after. B took 210,425–210,685 before and 12,341–12,461
after. The reduction is 15.8–17.1× across the eight paired observations.
Requested allocation bytes fell about 64% on both games. Construction
time averaged 0.88 ms (A, 93 samples) and 0.85 ms (B, 114 samples)
afterwards, with samples 127 states apart. Hashing and ticking were
outside those construction measurements.

reversing the order on the second pair under the same one-CPU reservation.
Median elapsed process time, including startup, ticking and all 12 checks,
was A 99.85 → 55.44 s (1.80×) and B 118.86 → 66.83 s (1.78×). Each of the eight
runs compared the original trace through closing. The allocation ratio
above describes the world checksum alone.
