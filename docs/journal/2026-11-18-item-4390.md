## The shader, as the card binds it

The pixel shader is source in the install: `game/shaders/ps_global.hlsl`, with `shader_texture_data.h` for the stage
macros. `d3dgl.dll`'s strings hold the macro names it compiles with (`HAS_SCREEN_REFLECTIONS`, `TS1_COLORARG_1`, …).
Draw 162's options are `ENABLE_LIGHTING`, `SPEC_MODEL_TORRANCE`, `SCREEN_REFLECTIONS`,
`ENABLE_VERTEX_COLOR_DIFFUSE` and `TEX1_USE_UV1`. Under them its colour is
`pow(saturate(stage), 1.45) · (diffuse + ambient) + specular + reflection`, at the stage's alpha.

| input | what the card binds | its value on the strokes against beside them | lays the strokes |
|---|---|---|---|
| stage 0 | `SELECTARG2(TEXTURE, DIFFUSE)`, colour and alpha | the vertex colour | no: the texture is not selected |
| stage 1 | `SELECTARG2(DIFFUSE, CURRENT)`, alpha `SELECTARG2(~TEXTURE, CURRENT)` | the current | no: `fow_fade.tga` is not selected |
| normal | the stream | (0, 0, 1) on all 1,755 vertices, z 0 | no |
| specular | roughness 0.3, specular 40, light (−0.707, 0, −0.707), V = `−normalize(ViewPos)` | N·H 0.631–0.645 against 0.630–0.653; 2.3–6.9·10⁻⁶ against 2.1–13.5·10⁻⁶ (HDR) | no |
| reflection | t5/t6: the previous frame's depth and HDR colour at a quarter size | 0 hits of 290 sampled stroke pixels, 0 of 12,568 beside them, 0 of 1,143 on the body; closest step 0.0188 against the threshold 0.00075 | no |

**The stage arguments' order.** `RenderState` in rise.pdb holds `colorop` at `+0xac`, `colorarg1` at `+0xb4` and
`colorarg2` at `+0xbc`, which is the order `card_pass.py` reads. So `SEL2` takes the second argument, and the
textures do not enter.

**Where the reflection's textures come from**, by d3dgl's listing and its type records:
- `ReflectionManager` (`d3dgl.pdb`, sizeof 64) holds `mDepthTexture`, `mBackTexture`, their SRVs and RTVs,
  `mResample`, and `mBufferWidth`/`mBufferHeight` at +0x38/+0x3c.
- `createTextures` (d3dgl `0x10050f00`) makes both textures at the screen's width and height shifted right by 2.
  The back texture is `R16G16B16A16_FLOAT` (0xa), the depth texture `R32_FLOAT` (0x27), each bound as a shader
  resource and a render target (0x28).
- `D3D11GLGraphicCard::frame_ended` (d3dgl `0x1001d280`) runs `mResample` twice. The first copies `d3d_sys +0x1238`
  (`mHDRBuffer +1256`, `D3D11GLBuffer::mResource`) into `mBackRTV`. The second copies `d3d_sys +0xd04`
  (`mScreen +1220`, `mDepthSRV`) into `mDepthRTV`.
- `create_hdr_buffer` (d3dgl `0x10023f70`) sets `mHDRBuffer`'s `mDepthSurface` to `mScreen.get_dsv()`, so the world
  pass writes the screen's depth.
- `set_render_state` binds `mDepthResource` and `mBackResource` at slots 5 and 6 (`PSSetShaderResources(5, 2, …)`)
  when `enables & 0x200000`.

**Who writes the depth** (`set_depth_stencil_state`, d3dgl `0x1002b5f0`: `DepthWriteMask = (enables & 3) == 3`):

The HUD's quads (170 onward, z 0.0979) write depth too. None of them lies on the lake's strokes.

**The march** (`march.py`) follows the shader exactly:
- each water pixel is unprojected onto z 0 through the draw's own `gView` and `gProjection` (orthographic: w is the
  constant 10,430, and depth runs 0 to 1 over view z 6,589 to 36,262);
- V = `−normalize(ViewPos)`, and the ray is `−reflect(V, N)`;
- ten 10-unit steps, each compared with the pass's rasterised depth at full size (a quarter-size bilinear resample
  only smooths it).

No step comes within 0.0188 of a written depth. That is about 560 view units, the land layer under the water,
which agrees with item 4353's bound that a hit needs a bottom within 6.1 units of the surface.

**The value diff** is the table above: each input's value on the stroke pixels against the water beside them, in the
draw's own coordinates. None parts.
