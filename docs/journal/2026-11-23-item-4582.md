## The first act: the tone, read

**The original does not tone-map the minimap's background, and does not light it in HDR.**
- `WorldMapBackground::render` sets `GSGraphicCard::minimap_mode` (`+0x10`) for the whole draw. It is the only writer
  in the executable.
- The reader is in the graphics DLL. In `d3dgl.dll`'s listing (the lab's `d3dgl.asm` from item 4390), where
  `D3D11GLGraphicCard::set_shader_options` tests the card's `+0x10`, it appends option `0x1a`.
  `rondata::trace::card::OPTIONS[26]` is `NO_HDR`.
- `ps_global.hlsl` under `NO_HDR`:
  - the HDR multiplier is 1;
  - the lit branch is `c · (diffuse + ambient)`, with no 1.45 power and no specular;
  - the output is saturated.
- The draw goes into the background's own buffer and is copied into the texture with alpha forced to `0xff`. No tone
  map touches it.
- Before the terrain: a clear to black, then a pre-transformed quad over the texture in the tileset's
  `minimap_ocean` (top-left) to `minimap_ocean_bottom` (bottom-right), their mean on the other corners.
- `TerrainOut::render(1)` skips `render_ocean_floor` (it runs only under flag 0), and no water pass is called. So
  open water on the minimap is that gradient, not a lit lake.
- Then the mountains, the cliffs and the features: the trees, the rivers, the darken (`Snowy` alone) and the
  mountains.

**`render_feature`**, read: per cell whose flag word carries the feature's bit, the cell's 5 × 5 terrain mesh, its
vertex colours white at alpha 0 and then set per sub-tile.
- The trees set all of them to `tree_minimap_color` at `0xff`. The corner test compares map tile coordinates, so in
  practice it spares only the map's corners.
- The rivers take `river_minimap_color` opaque, where the tile's word has `0x800`.
- The mountains take alpha `0x77` where the tile's height class is 1 or 2, added (`mountain_feature_rs`, `dst_blend`
  ONE).
- `feature_rs` is source alpha over, with the vertex colour selected (the constructor).

**Arithmetic, why the tone was not built blind.** For flat land at the land's light, a texel of 0.7 in red:
- `NO_HDR`: 0.7 × 0.972 × 0.906 ≈ 0.62, so 157.
- Our HDR path at the clamp's white: the tone of 5 · 0.68^1.45 · 0.906 ≈ 0.74, so 188.

So `NO_HDR` alone darkens our land, which already sits near theirs: theirs (203, 188, 102) against ours (196, 180,
104) at cell (40, 20). That is unresolved until the ocean gradient, the dropped floor and the white are all built
together and measured (4582a).
