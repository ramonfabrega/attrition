## The value diff: the woodcutter's right edge

0/10 (piece 4576, window (1680, 404)..(1710, 452)) reads 0.383 before and after: ours 763, theirs 309, both 297.
I measured the three candidate causes the booking named before naming a cause:

- **Extent**: the sprite is `woodcutter_6.tga` (the `WOODCUTTER-DEFAULT-AGE5` `<BUILD>`), 173 × 201. Its last
  column is opaque on 40 rows, so the art reaches the image's edge.
- **Scale and offset**: the anchor projects to (1640, 438). The offset is (83, 178) and `s` = 13905/20858 =
  0.66665, so left = 1640 − (int)(83·s) = 1585 and top = 320. The right edge is 1585 + 173·s = **1700.3**.
  This is the placement item 4076 states (`render::draw::sprite_at`).
- **Alpha**: 829 of the image's pixels carry partial alpha, but **none** of them lie past column 155, which is
  screen x 1688. All 758 opaque pixels there have alpha 255, so the reader's half-alpha cut does not move this
  edge.
- **The photograph**: the traced building ends at x 1686 on row 404 and runs diagonally to 1660 on row 435.
  `edge-ov.png` and `side.png` set the photograph beside our sprite. The photograph shows a pine's foliage over
  the crate's lower right, from x 1660 to 1715 across rows 405–460, drawn in front of the building. On our
  draw the crate is uncovered, because the client draws no trees. `ages.png` composites all three woodcutter
  sprites at the same anchor: the tower matches `woodcutter_6`, so the age is right.
- **The count**: 307 of the woodcutter's pixels in 0/10's window lie outside its trace, and 99 of them are at

**The cause is a draw step we do not make**: the map's trees in front of the buildings they occlude. It is filed as
parked 4079a, with its values. The reader was not moved, and 0/10 is unmoved. The measurement is an assertion:
`client::silhouette::tests::the_woodcutter_s_right_edge_is_the_sprite_s_own` pins the anchor, the image size, the
offset, the placement, the two-level alpha past column 155, the 1700 edge, and the (307, 99) count. It went red
once, on its first draft, which claimed the whole image was two-level. The image has 829 partial pixels, so I
