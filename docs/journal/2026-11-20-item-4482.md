## 3. What the packet holds apart: the emitters

`GraphicPieces` is the word at `0x00C06214`. Its `particle_emitters` (`+0xadc`) and `stopped_particle_emitters`
(`+0xb14`) are `PtrArray<ParticleEmitter>`. Each emitter (`0x5c`) has `desc` (`ParticleSourceDesc`) at `+0x20`, whose
`who`, `o`, `node_num` and `system_type` are at `+0x1c`–`+0x22`. Its particle count is `particles`' length, at `+0x8`.
System 98 is the 99th `<PARTICLESYSTEM>` of `effects_graphics.xml`, `MOVE DUST`; the file and the packet each hold 146.

Every emitter stands at its tank's **3279** point, the position 4458 projected: the packet's renderer holds the
photograph's frame, as 4073 found. The three hazy tanks carry 7 to 10 particles. The control's earlier emitter stopped
with none left, and its fresh one holds a single particle. That single difference holds on every tank. The Sherman's
`<UNIT>` (piece 196) hangs `MOVE DUST` on `CHAR_SLOG`, `CHAR_WALK` and `CHAR_JOG`, from 75 to 9000 ms, on node 5, axis

burning slot's two plumes).

`[75, 9000)` window against an animation's own clock, and when that clock restarts, is the reading the build needs.

## For the Loop

When a render item puts a particle-shaped haze over an object, read the packet's `GraphicPieces` emitter lists keyed by
`(who, o)` before reading `UnitData`. It took one scratch script and one minute, and it answered what three readings of
masks could not.
