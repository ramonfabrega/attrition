## 4159r2c, the Citizen's attrition period: built; `attrition_stamp2` leaves

an empty order list. The crate had both pieces (`Resistance`, `foraging_1`, `UnitKind::idle`) and wired neither:
`territory.players[w].resistance` stayed `Resistance::BASE` (the seam item 4162 parked as 4162b), `foraging_1`

1024.0): the Citizen `1/6`'s computed period is four times the unwired 6, and `PEACE_ATTRITION`'s 8 wins. That is

`attrition_for` sets `idle` from the order list. Tests: `the_record_s_single_is_the_resistance` (the arithmetic)
and `the_anti_attrition_single_reaches_the_bleed` (the wiring, red first with the hand-over disabled).
