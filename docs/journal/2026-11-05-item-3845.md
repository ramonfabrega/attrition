## The build

`sim::map_rivers` gains, from the decompile (`make_rivers@00692fb0`, `make_riverlist@00693820`,
`astar_river@00686690`):

- **`make_riverlist`**: the spread draw and `SCALE(start + r)` rivers; the sources in a Galois LFSR's order over
  `1..=n` (8, 12 or 16 bits by the source count) from `World.seed`'s low bits `% n + 1`, stopping at its third
  return; the nation balance (a source whose nearest start city already ends more rivers than the least is passed);
  the end test against `river_flags` (water 1, border 2, inland 4), the two length floors, the three spline
  stretches `wide`/`med` cut the path into (their union is the path), and the marks — 0x1000 on each path cell,
  0x2 on the `circle_radius[valley]` disc round it within the path cell's `region` (`sim::ai_place::circle`).
- **`astar_river`**: not an A\* but a greedy best-first — a node's value is its own price and a pull toward
  `river_length`, never its parent's. The open list is the pathfinder's (value, newest first among equals); a cell
  enters once (a live refs entry or a closed one refuses it); the expansion's own rules (the bank, the neighbours'
  count, the ancestors, a terminal neighbour ending the expansion with it alone or with nothing) are the function's
  doc comment. One draw `% 20 + 10` per dry neighbour it prices.
- `rondata::mapgen::river_list` grows from a count to `River` rows; `make_rivers` takes a `Phase` (threshold, city
  radius, rows, Standard size, `World.seed`, nations) and the `Draws`, and its checkpoint is handed the stream.
  `Unported::RiverList` is gone. The Great Sahara and East Indies checks moved to the new signature unchanged.

**No float.** The spline (`RiverOut::add_spline_point`, `RiversOut::start_river`) is the renderer's: it takes
integer cell coordinates, and every record from 5012 to 5118 agrees without it, so it is not built.
