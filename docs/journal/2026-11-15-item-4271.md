Through ADDSIGNED that makes a stage colour of about (135, 113, 80). The tint takes it to (153, 131, 80). Undone
through the 1.45 power and the curve, the gap is about **(18, 16, 1) bytes of stage colour: the tint's (18, 18, 0)
to the byte.**

**What was read.**
- *The stage op.* The install's `ps_global.hlsl` takes the fixed-function stages through `do_texture_operation`
  (`shader_texture_data.h`), and ADDSIGNED is `arg1 + arg2 − 0.5` on `tex1 · vDiff`, then `saturate`. That is
  `fs_mountain`'s op exactly.
- *The tint in the code.* `TileSet::init@0087b370` calls `set_tint` on `mountain_base`. `Texture` begins with
  `ImageIO`'s 0x2c bytes (type record), so `set_tint` lands on `tint_color` and `use_tint`.
  `Texture::init@005245f0` reaches `ImageIO::load@00512560`, which calls `ImageIO::load_tga@00512ac0`. In the
  listing at `512d48`–`512d5e`, each row is tinted in place:
  - `ecx = Texture::get_data(0, row)`, `edx = this`, then `call 0x511aa0` (`ImageIO::tint`);
  - after that come `release_data(height)` and `post_load` (vtable `0xb22f18` slots +0x2c, +0x38 and +0x50, named
    from the PE against the index).
  The decompile had lost the arguments (`unaff_EDI`, `unaff_ESI`).
- *The tint on the packet* (run1559b, `~/ron-golden/run1559b/map-14/frame-snapshot.bin`):
  - `cur_tileset` is `0x160a3714`, and `mountain_base` at `+0x81c` holds `tint_color` `0xff001212` with `use_tint`
    1.
  - Its device record (`+0x30`, then `+0x18`) points at `+0x88` to `0x2c401250`. There, three rows of
    `t_mountain_plain` match the file plus (18, 18, 0) in RGBA order.
  - `mountain_alpha`'s record points the same way at `t_mountain_blend`, also tinted.
  - A raw level 0 of each texture, `t_mountainpeak`'s too, also sits in a 0x40000-aligned slab, with its tinted twin
    0x40000 after it. Nothing owns the raw copy: there is no pointer into `0x2d1f0000` at all. The slab holds no mip
    levels.

**So the hypothesis "theirs is untinted" is killed as a mechanism.** The original tints the bytes it holds, and the
upload follows the tint in the code. Untinted, our pipeline would score 94 of 106 and read the body at a mean ratio
of 0.986. But that is a fit the reading and the packet both contradict, so it is not drawn. Whether the card samples
the tinted bytes is not reachable from process memory: the device's GPU copy lives behind d3dgl and DXVK (parked
4271a).
