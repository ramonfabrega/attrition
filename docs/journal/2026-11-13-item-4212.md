## 2. The tint: read, drawn, and not the mechanism

grass goes (162.8, 144.2, 67.0) → (180.8, 162.2, 67.0), hue 48.3° → 50.2°, the hue row 17 → 16. The hypothesis the
item named is not the mechanism. It is kept, because it is the original's and the lit model needs it (§3).
