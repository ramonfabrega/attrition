# Item 430 — what a fix at 9382 would be worth, in frames (2026-09-19)

*Opus 5, on the AI lane. `docs/AI.md` §49.4.*

## The answer

**Eight frames.**

The two causes at Great Lakes 9382 are real, they are **locked**, and
correcting both of them moves the AI headline **9510 → 9518** — eight
frames of twenty-four thousand. Measured, not inferred, before either
successor item was written.

Each probe cost one release run and each answered something the one before

- **Probe 2 corrected a document.** Removing the wrap costs 2,727 frames,

- **Probe 1 measured its own bluntness.** Forcing *every* Scholar offer

  **A payoff probe must change only the frames under test**, because a
  probe wide enough to touch a frame that already agrees reports that
  frame's breakage as its result, and the number looks exactly like a
  finding.
- **Probe 3 found the second cause.** The ranking alone loses 128 frames.
- **Probe 4 priced the pair.** Both halves together gain eight.

## The pair is locked, and that is the structural fact

Correcting the valuation alone moves the word **down** by 128 frames.
Correcting the expiry alone would do the mirror image. So these are not a
queue of causes to peel one at a time — they are **a pair that has to fall
together**, and anyone who lands either on its own will watch the score
drop and reasonably conclude they broke something. They will not have.

That is why the success criterion for whatever comes next cannot be "the
word moves": it has to be **"9382 agrees under the probe"**, which makes
the probe part of the item rather than a scratch thing.

## Three separate calls, not one walk

The first attempt at probe 4's expiry half capped the walk *inside* one
`expire` call and changed nothing. The three `Leader::make_stuff+0x63d`
draws come from **three separate `expire` calls** — the slot loop calls it
once per bought slot — so the cap had to count across the frame.

That is a fact about the shape of the difference rather than about the
patch, and it survives whatever the successor turns out to be: **it is not
one over-long walk.**

## And the train rate is not a second finding

Under probe 4 the new parting at 9518 is this crate birthing the sixth
scholar **eight frames late**: queued on 9382 on both sides, the original's
job runs 128 frames and this crate's runs 136. That looked like an
unblocked defect worth more than eight frames, and it is not.

**The disk already said so, in an assertion that has been in the gate all
along.** `run97_s_build_queues_are_the_original_s` compares `(type,
job_counter)` for every live slot of every building over `[8030, 9349]` —
**1,319 blocks, 37,899 building-frames, zero divergences** — and that
window contains **three scholar jobs this crate runs today**, completing
on 9087, 9201 and 9322. The train rate agrees tick for tick on every job
both sides actually run.

So 136 against 128 is an **artefact of probe 4's forced purchase**, not a
defect. The train rate is not yet a finding, and the eight frames are the
honest next move.

**And the shape of that answer is worth more than the answer.** The
question was framed as a new measurement and it was already green in the
gate — the third time today the disk answered before a run did. The
generalisation: **before booking a measurement, ask whether an assertion
already in the suite covers it.** A passing test is evidence nobody reads,
and this suite has eight hundred of them. `run97`'s queue comparison had
been asserting the scholar train rate on every commit for weeks; nothing
had ever asked it a question.

One correction to my own read, recorded because it was wrong in a message
before it was right here: I argued the train-rate divergence was the
better next buy because it was "on disk and not blocked behind a probe".
That is true of the *record* and false of the *score* — 9518 only exists
under probe 4, so nothing there is reachable until the pair falls. The
commander caught it and the grep confirmed it.

## What this cost, and what it is worth

Five release runs, no capture, no reading, no screen. It priced a cause at
the AI headline that two items were about to be written on, and the price
is **eight frames**.

That number is the lane's most useful product today and it is not a
comfortable one: a day of two lanes and eight landings, and the
best-measured cause at the headline is worth eight frames of 24,000.
`CLAUDE.md` says the number is the stopping rule for the long middle. This
is the number.

**But eight frames is the toll, not the value.** Everything behind 9510 is
currently invisible: the scholar-birth divergence at 9518 is a probe
artefact, and so is whatever follows it, because the tree never gets
there. Landing the pair turns 9518 into an ordinary residue on the real
tree — measurable, diffable, and bookable by frame and draw delta like
anything else. So the successor buys eight frames **and a working
instrument past the blockage**, and the second half is the reason to do it
at this price.
