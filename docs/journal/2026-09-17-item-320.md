## 2026-09-17 — item 320: the scout's route round a city, and the world under it (Opus 5)

Great Lakes' word did **not** move. What moved instead is how much of block
8002 is still a question: four candidate causes went in and one came out,
and three of the eliminations are now diffs that keep checking rather than
paragraphs that do not.

**The brief's framing was right, for once.** Item 319 left the AI scout
`1/0` taking its `EXPLORETOORDER` to `(4344, 32760)` on block 8002 and
planning a five-entry stack where the original plans six, and named
`find_wpath`. The widening confirms it: the whole frame's diverging rows at
8002 are `1/0`'s stack, plus `1/23`'s order and `1/24`–`1/26`'s positions
and the group ids, all of which start hundreds of blocks earlier and are
this window's inherited residue. The scout's own record parts on **8002**
(the route) and **8014** (the position), exactly where 319's assertions
say.

The route, in cells, is `(5,38) → (4,38) → (3,39) → (3,40) → (3,41)` here
against `(5,38) → (4,38) → (3,38) → (2,39) → (2,40) → (3,41)` there. Cells
`(3,39)` and `(3,40)` are two of the four the human capital's 6 × 6
footprint stands on. **One step longer, and round the city.**

**Two forcings reproduce the original's stack entry for entry, and no
third.** Half-cells `(7,79)` and `(7,81)` — `div_3_table[to >> 7]` of the
two nodes, the exact points `calc_cost`'s fog test reads — forced *seen*;
or cells `(3,39)` and `(3,40)` forced invalid in `valid_wcoord`. Both were
measured, not argued: an env-switched hack, four variants each, the
diff's own `stack on 8002` line coming back `[]`. And the narrowing is what
made the rest of the day possible — with `(7,79)` alone one row still
parts, with `(7,81)` alone three do, so the question is *those two
half-cells* rather than "the fog".

**Then the disk answered a question `docs/VISION.md` had costed a capture
for.** §7 said "no dump on disk carries a *second* fog plane to diff
against" and priced the `[End Frame] WORLD=6` window that would fix it.
That is wrong twice over: a `DUMP_ALL` **window** prints the whole `WORLD`
scan on every block it covers, and *three* Great Lakes archives already
carry one mid-game — run13 at 95–104, run73 at 5564–5580 and **run93 at
7929–7936**, seventy frames under the word. `grep -c "seen2\[scan\]"` over
the Logs directory is what found them, in about a minute, after an hour
spent reasoning about what the fog *ought* to be.

What they say, at block 7932, whole map:

The fog is **exact** — 7,932 frames of every reveal `docs/VISION.md` §3–§6
makes, against the original's own scan, and §3–§6 were entirely
reading-backed until today. The 27 cells and 171 tiles are all in the AI's
own base (`x` 37–58, `y` 18–34), thirty cells east of the scout: the
`BUILDING` bit and a blocked count one or two short around finished
buildings. **None is in the western half of the map at all**, which is what
turns "the world is probably right" into "the world under this route is the
original's". All three are pinned in
`run93_s_block_7932_is_this_crate_s_world_cell_for_cell` — the fog at zero,
the other two by count *and* by place, so a fix has to lower them and a
regression has to raise them.

**With that, four things are eliminated rather than doubted**: the danger
grid (already diff-backed, and unread in the fog branch), `think_scout`
(item 319's), the cost arithmetic (every priced step of this crate's search
reproduces its exact number by hand, seen and unseen), and the search's
five mode flags (each forced on and off; three of them move the
*destination*, which is pinned).

**And the fog forcing is ruled out as a reveal.** `1/0` is the only owner-1
unit that has ever been west of cell 37 — every other one on block 8002 is
at `x ≥ 28992` — it enters on block 7893 from the east, and its `mylos` is
6 on every compared frame, so `update_seen`'s radius is 3 and its disc is
`dx² + dy² ≤ 13`. Its sweep centre over the whole approach runs `(15,81) →
… → (12,81) → … → (11,77) → (10,76) → (10,77) → (9,77)`. Half-cell
`(7,81)` needs a centre at `x ≤ 10` while `y` is 79–83; the nearest this
scout ever comes is 12, and there is no other candidate. So the plane is
the original's at 7932 and cannot have diverged by 8002.

**What is left is a measurement, and it is running.** run95 is a `callwin`
proxy over `calc_cost` and `astar_path` on sim-frame 8001 — the instrument
that answered item 125 in one reading, where "all seven [calls] that did
not [agree] were steps into the four cells under player 1's second city".
It logs every node the original priced and what it charged for it, which is
the one thing neither a dump nor a reading can give. `docs/PATHFINDER.md`
§20 holds the whole elimination; §20.3 holds two gaps in `invalid_loc` that
this found and that do **not** move this map's word — the land arm's
`cell flags & 0x70` refusal (world-grid-only, since only `valid_wcoord`
passes `p3` and `p6` together) and the fog shortcut `_fog_relax` names and
does not make.

**Three outcomes are live, not two.** run95 discriminates the two forcings
by construction, because a `valid_wcoord` refusal happens *upstream* of
`calc_cost`: the absence of a cost record for the step `(3456, 29568) →
(2688, 30336)` is itself the verdict that the predicate refused. But if the
record is there and reads 9, then validity passed **and** the arithmetic
agrees, and the cause is neither forcing — it is the search's own ordering,
which nothing here has yet had to consider. That is a live outcome and it
will be said plainly if it lands, not folded back into one of the two
hypotheses this item started with.

**And if it lands between them, the emulator is the next rung rather than
another capture.** `valid_wcoord` is not pure — it reads `worldc` (the tile
dimensions, the `WData` array and the tile-mask array), `leadersc`, and the
unit's own `path`, `who`, type and `unit_masks` — so it is
`docs/EMULATOR.md` §2's *synthesized-state* tier, an hour for one function,
not the free one. What earns that hour here is that the inputs are no
longer in question: run93 pins tile mask `0x6103` at `(14, 158)` and cell
flags `0x80`, both this crate's own. An emulated `invalid_loc(tile, p3 = 1,
p4, p5 = 0, p6 = 1, p7 = 0)` over those exact values therefore tests the
*reading of the branches* and nothing else, which is the one case the tier
pays for.

**Two lessons, both about order.**

- **Grep the disk one level wider than the question.** The fog planes were
  found by grepping *every* Great Lakes archive for `seen2[scan]`, not by
  grepping the one the item was about. run94 has the plane too — at block
  0, which is useless — and stopping there would have booked the capture
  `docs/VISION.md` already had written down.
- **A sufficiency is not a cause.** Forcing the fog reproduced the
  original's stack perfectly and was still the wrong answer; what killed it
  was asking *which sweep would have to have lit it* and finding that none
  can. The forcing told me where to look; the arithmetic of the disc told
  me what it was not.
