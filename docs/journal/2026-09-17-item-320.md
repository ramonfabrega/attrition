## 2026-09-17 — item 320: the scout's route round a city, and the world under it (Opus 5)

**The lesson first, because it transfers and the fog does not.** This item
ran a correct elimination to a wrong verdict by hopping over a premise it
never said out loud: *the fog has one writer*. The arithmetic that said no
`update_seen` disc of that scout could light the two half-cells was right
and is still right; what was missing is that `docs/VISION.md` §6 names a
**fourth** reveal which is not `update_seen` at all. `CLAUDE.md` has the
rule verbatim — **grep the writers of every field you call frozen** — and
an elimination is only ever as wide as the set it enumerates. Say what set
you are enumerating over, out loud, before you conclude from it.

Great Lakes' word did **not** move. What moved instead is how much of block
8002 is still a question: four candidate causes went in and one came out,
and three of the eliminations are now diffs that keep checking rather than
paragraphs that do not.

**The brief's framing was right, for once.** Item 319 left the AI scout
`1/0` taking its `EXPLORETOORDER` to `(4344, 32760)` on block 8002 and
planning a five-entry stack where the original plans six, and named
`find_wpath`. The widening confirms it: the whole frame's diverging rows at
8002 are `1/0`'s stack, plus `1/23`'s order and `1/24`–`1/26`'s positions
and the group ids, all of which start hundreds of blocks earlier and are
this window's inherited residue. The scout's own record parts on **8002**
(the route) and **8014** (the position), exactly where 319's assertions
say.

The route, in cells, is `(5,38) → (4,38) → (3,39) → (3,40) → (3,41)` here
against `(5,38) → (4,38) → (3,38) → (2,39) → (2,40) → (3,41)` there. Cells
`(3,39)` and `(3,40)` are two of the four the human capital's 6 × 6
footprint stands on. **One step longer, and round the city.**

**With that, four things are eliminated rather than doubted**: the danger

**And then run95 landed and overturned me.** The commander had written,
an hour before: "five of six items today had their named mechanism
overturned, and the sixth would be yours." It was.

The `callwin` proxy carries 83 `calc_cost` records on sim-frame 8001 — the
whole of `1/0`'s one search. The step from cell `(4, 38)` into `(3, 39)` is
**priced at 328** where this crate prices it 9, and the neighbouring step
into `(3, 38)` at **1**, the unseen price, where this crate also says 1. So
the step is *priced, not refused* — and since a `valid_wcoord` refusal
happens upstream of `calc_cost`, the record's presence is itself the
verdict that the predicate passed. The validity forcing is dead and **the
fog forcing was right all along**.

What survives of the elimination is its premise, not its verdict. run95's
own `DUMP_ALL` window carries the plane at the block the search runs on,
and **fourteen half-cells of 14,400 part**: a 3 × 5 block at `x` 6–8,
`y` 78–82 — two `circle_radius[1]` discs centred on `(7, 79)` and
`(7, 81)`, which are the `2c + 1` half-cells of cells `(3, 39)` and

cannot make any of it: the centres are right, the radius is right, and the
shape is not a disc of any radius around any position that scout ever had.
It is a *second* reveal, and `docs/VISION.md` §6 has named it since
2026-08-31 — `Unit::update_local_seen@0060e410`, which lights
`circle_radius[type->x_size]` points around an object's own half-cell with
`ObjectData::visible` as the **mask** rather than the owner's bit, so a
human object can light the AI's plane. **"Not modelled; nothing in this
simulation sets `visible`" is the score.** A document's own *not
established* line was worth a hundred frames of Great Lakes, which is an
argument for writing those lines as precisely as the modelled ones: they
are not disclaimers, they are the backlog.

The reveal lands between blocks **7937 and 7998** — run93's 7936 has all
fourteen dark, run95's 7999 has them lit, and the planes are identical at

**What the mistake was, exactly.** Not the measurement: the fog forcing
reproduced the original's stack entry for entry, and the disc arithmetic
that said no sweep could make it was correct and is still correct. The
mistake was the step from "no *unit's disc* can make this" to "so it is not
the fog" — a hop over an unstated premise, that the fog has one writer.
`docs/VISION.md` §6 lists three callers of `update_seen` and then a fourth
reveal that is not `update_seen` at all, and I had read that paragraph
twice the same afternoon. An elimination is only as wide as the set it
enumerates, and I did not say out loud what set I was enumerating over.

**Three outcomes were live, not two.** run95 discriminates the two forcings
by construction, because a `valid_wcoord` refusal happens *upstream* of
`calc_cost`: the absence of a cost record for the step `(3456, 29568) →
(2688, 30336)` is itself the verdict that the predicate refused. But if the
record is there and reads 9, then validity passed **and** the arithmetic
agrees, and the cause is neither forcing — it is the search's own ordering,
which nothing here has yet had to consider. That is a live outcome and it
will be said plainly if it lands, not folded back into one of the two
hypotheses this item started with.

**And if it lands between them, the emulator is the next rung rather than
another capture.** `valid_wcoord` is not pure — it reads `worldc` (the tile
dimensions, the `WData` array and the tile-mask array), `leadersc`, and the
unit's own `path`, `who`, type and `unit_masks` — so it is
`docs/EMULATOR.md` §2's *synthesized-state* tier, an hour for one function,
not the free one. What earns that hour here is that the inputs are no
longer in question: run93 pins tile mask `0x6103` at `(14, 158)` and cell
flags `0x80`, both this crate's own. An emulated `invalid_loc(tile, p3 = 1,
p4, p5 = 0, p6 = 1, p7 = 0)` over those exact values therefore tests the
*reading of the branches* and nothing else, which is the one case the tier
pays for.

**Two lessons, both about order.**

- **Grep the disk one level wider than the question.** The fog planes were
  found by grepping *every* Great Lakes archive for `seen2[scan]`, not by
  grepping the one the item was about. run94 has the plane too — at block
  0, which is useless — and stopping there would have booked the capture
  `docs/VISION.md` already had written down.
- **A check that answers 0 for the wrong reason cannot fail, and that is
  twice today.** run95's stanza asked `grep -c calc_cost` of the *trace*,
  which is a binary log carrying no symbol names, so a capture whose
  proxies worked perfectly reported 0 and the queue called the run failed.
  Item 317's sequence check had the same shape — a symbol compared against
  a raw hex string. Both are the same mistake one level below the
  assertion: the check was well-formed and looking in the wrong encoding.
  A check is only made-to-fail if it has been made to fail **on data that
  should pass**, not merely on data that should not.
- **A sufficiency is not a cause.** Forcing the fog reproduced the
  original's stack perfectly and was still the wrong answer; what killed it
  was asking *which sweep would have to have lit it* and finding that none
  can. The forcing told me where to look; the arithmetic of the disc told
  me what it was not.
