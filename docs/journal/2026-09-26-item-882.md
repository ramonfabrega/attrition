## Step 1: the listing, and the "human arm"

**The booking's "human arm" was 877's hypothesis, and the listing killed
it.** `618900`..`6189aa` gate the push on four tests:
- the unit is a captain (vslot `0xe8`, `o_up >> 15`);
- the host index is ≥ 0;
- the host's vslot `0x20` answers, which is `Buffer::is_pending_load` in
  `Build::vftable`: a building, not a transport;
- the type's `uber_size` (`+0x308`) is over 1.

Then comes `Group::add(o, who, 0, 0)`, which adds the captain and walks
its `o_down` chain, and then `push_group(who, g, 1)`. **There is no
owner test.** A computer's trained squad takes a slot the same way. That
is East Indies' one-block birth rows on 17363 and 17575, parked 689.
