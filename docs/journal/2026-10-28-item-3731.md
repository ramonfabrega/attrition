# Item 3731 — the `obj` sweeps become one driver and a row table (2026-10-28, `att-3731`, Opus 5.5)

This is a sweep-kind lane of the sixty-second tranche, from base `42ba0316`. It moved no word and took no
run. It renamed park slot VZ, which now holds three rows (3731a–c).

## What a script was

Of the 1,699 `tools/emu/sweep_*.py`, 1,109 were `tools/sweep_gen.py obj` with windows, and they held 641 k of
the 679 k lines. Each was the `OBJ_WIN_SCRIPT` template: a docstring, a block of about sixty literal constants,
and a body of about 600 lines.

Stripped of docstring and constants, the bodies came in 754 distinct versions, mostly ten scripts a batch. The
template grew additively behind constants left empty, and lanes hand-edited bodies. Only 70 bodies were the
current template's word for word. So I could not read the extraction off the code; the rows had to prove it.

**The rows are the constants, not the docstring's prose.** Each script's constant block is the machine form of
what its docstring says. `ast.literal_eval` reads it exactly, which no prose parse could, and every one of the
1,109 parsed. The seed (`random.Random(<item>)`) and row count (`range(N)`) are the body's only literals, and
they become `SEED` and `ROWS`. The row keeps the constants that differ from the driver's defaults. Older
scripts' `SEQ` pairs and `DEREF` triples were widened with the `None` the generator writes today (112 rows).

## The driver, and the proof before any deletion

`tools/emu/sweep_obj.py` is the current template's body over its defaults. It takes `<exe> <snake> [<row>]`.
The Rust side (`sweep/support.rs`, `emu_obj`) hands it the row literal as an argument. The cache key already
holds the arguments, so an edited row is a miss for that sweep alone, and neither `support.rs`'s key text nor
`emu_receipts.py`'s rebuild of it changed. The driver keeps the `sweep_` prefix: every other `tools/emu/*.py`
is a shared module hashed into every key, and a new one would have dropped the hand-written scripts' entries
too.

**The proof was rows, byte for byte**, before a file was touched. 840 old scripts' rows came out of the cache
under their own keys. The other 269 (over the 16 MiB cap, or never cached) ran fresh. Every row ran through the
driver, six at a time, in two passes (scratch only, `proof.py`):

- 1,076 of 1,109 identical.
- 31 part on row 1. An older template drew a stub's answer or an argument in another order; one has a 4-member
  `ALIAS`, and one faults. They stay scripts (parked 3731b).
- 2 of the 33 non-identical rows fell in the replaced groups, below, and went with them.

The converted rows' 915 tests now call `emu_obj("<snake>")`, and their 915 scripts were deleted. The full `sweep::`
count runs in the gate, quoted at the end.
