## The reading

`execute_at_cursor` calls `issue_spell` from two sites, `:1681` and `:1996`:

- **The Scout-line arm**, selection `COUNT_TYPE 0x45`: Counterintelligence on an enemy Spy. With a Commando
  (`0x49`): Sabotage on a building or a vehicle (`has_objmask(0x200000)`), else the Sniper when `is_valid_target`
  passes.
- **The Spy arm**, selection `0x3a`: Counterintelligence on an enemy Spy. On a unit, the Informer on a citizen, else
  Bribe when it is valid. On a building, Bribe on our own ground, else the Informer.

The decompile hides the `is` and `has_objmask` arguments, which the listing gives (`push 0x3a`, `push 0x200000`).
The vtable slot names come from the PDB's `SubObjectData`/`ObjectData` field lists, because `vtables.txt` folds
them to `Window::get_button`. An arm that has taken the object sets `local_34` whether or not it issues, and the
dispatch returns at `7c9ee9`. The full arms are in `docs/CLIENT_INPUT.md` §37.2.
