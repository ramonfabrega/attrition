## The first act: what theirs adds

`~/ron-data/pages-standard/` (run1579 to run1593, run1600, run1652, run1653) holds 36 citizens' standard panels,
eleven adds each, plus `TOARMSSPELL` on slot 1 at 13000 and 14400: 398 adds. The PDB's `OptionType`
(`llvm-pdbutil dump --types`, `LF_ENUMERATE`) names them: 0 to 2 the page buttons, 3 `OPTION_REPAIR`, 27
`OPTION_ALARM_PEASANT`, 4 `OPTION_SPELL`, 26 `OPTION_GARRISON`, 25 `OPTION_HALT`, 14 `OPTION_TRANSPORT` (greyed on
all 36), 9 `OPTION_MOVE_TO`, 10 `OPTION_ATTACK_TO`, 33 `OPTION_AUTO_EXPLORE`. `UnitOut::make_options@00615fc0`
adds them in that order.

Each one's press was read in `Options::exec@007188c0`'s second switch and its `do_*`:

- `do_halt@0071c580` and `do_alarm@0071c3f0` write the selection's `group` and a one-byte command (`0x0c`,
  `0x1b`). `rondata::commands` already codes both.
- `do_repair@0071ccb0` and `do_move_to@0071d260` arm a pick. `picked_spot@00721c40` turns its spot into
  `execute_at_cursor(0, 3 or 1)` or the repair arm.
- `do_transport@0071c500` writes `set_transport(can_transport == 0)`.

`python3 tools/ctx.py` after the first act read 163 k. The build went on.

- `Event::Unbuilt(option)` covers `OPTION_REPAIR`, `OPTION_MOVE_TO`, `OPTION_ATTACK_TO` and `OPTION_TRANSPORT`. It is

  whole word, so an oracle run needs the byte's current value first, or a byte poke.

  recording on disk was checked for them, and no playback ran one.
