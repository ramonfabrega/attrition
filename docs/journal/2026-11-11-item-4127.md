## What was read

The base layer of a `BASELAND` tile, from the listing and the decompile
(RENDERER §3.6, "The ground's base layer (item 4127)", committed before
its count in `6dfa88f97`):

- **The choice.** `TerrainOut::generate_land_lists@0085f6a0` →
  `TerrainList::add_new_coord_info@0084ba10` →
  `TileSet::convert_land_tex_to_linear@0087ace0`. On land 0 the bucket is
  the textures of the bases before `land_sub`, plus `(cx mod 3) + 3·(cy mod 3)`.
  The `0x55555555` idiom was checked numerically, 0 misses for x < 300. The
  land pass binds `get_land_textures_by_index@0087a9a0` of the bucket.
  `land_sub` is the crate's own (`TerrainScene::land_sub`, the generator's
  `fill_fertile`), so **kill (b) does not apply**.
- **The sub-tile.** `TextureEffect::load_as_supertile@00542640`: a 3 × 3
  cut, with sub-tile `k` at column `k mod 3`, row `k / 3`, `w/3` square
  (170 px on the 512 files). `load_land_data@008767d0` sets
  `supertile_discrete_mip` 1, which takes the same offsets.
  `get_supertile_texture@0087a770` has no caller and no stored pointer
  (byte scan of `.text` and of the whole file).
- **How it lies.** `generate_land@0085f8d0` at `85fb9a`–`85fc7e`: the
  corner texcoord is `(x & 3)/4 + (x >> 2)`, the same in y. That is one
  sub-tile a cell, with u along world x and v along world y. `smooth_tcoord`
  smooths `+0x4a4`, not a texcoord.
- **What multiplies it.** `determine_land_height_color@0086c1e0` writes
  `0xffffffff` on every path. The light and the `_mtrl` stage are not drawn.
