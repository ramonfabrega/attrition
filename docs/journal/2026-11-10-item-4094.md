## The order of work

1. **The record, from the stream.** A scratch decoder split the 519 builds rows by call site into fifteen
   buildings and named every byte from the type records (`SubObjectData`, `ObjectData`, `WallData`,
   `BuildData`, `QueueItem`, `ArrayBaseMaster`). The walkers (`BuildData::walk_data`, `WallData::walk_data`,
   `Object::walk_data`, `SubObject::walk_data`, `BuildQueue::walk_data`, `Array<TCoordData>::walk_data`, the
   gather list's) named the locals. §7 had the order wrong in four places: `founder`/`max_age` first,
   four `must_walk` bytes rather than a pair, `orig_type` last, and `queue_size` slots rather than the live
   entries. The table is `docs/CHECKSUM.md` §14.
2. **The port, with every field's best source**, then the test decoding both sides — ours by our range
   names, theirs by each row's call site, so a misplaced range of ours shows as a walk mismatch rather than
   agreeing with itself. `gpiece` is art: the test hands in `rondata::free::placed_buildings`' piece.
3. **The red**: 18 ranges parted. `uid` on 13 buildings (ours 0), the British farm's `frame_started`
   (−1|17) and `build_masks` (0|1024), the Library's two entries' `type` (550|551, 564|565), both camps'
   `recharging` (83|90, 67|89) and the British camp's `up_who` (−1|1).
4. **What each parting was.** `uid`: `Object::init`'s per-player counter at `Objects +0x1d4`, one writer,
   carried. `frame_started`: `Wall::start`'s frame, carried. `0x400`: `Wall::process`'s latch of `helpers`,
   carried beside this crate's `helpers = 0`. The tech type: the tree has no entry for the goody box's
   0x21f, so a tech's tree id is one below its `TypeIndex` (`ids.rs`'s doc said otherwise; amended).
   `recharging`: `Guy::move@005d9240` bumps a gather building on a carrying walk, and this crate ports
   only `do_gather`'s and `do_non_flat_gather`'s bumps. `up_who`: the object chain's leftover, which needs
   buildings threaded on the chain.
5. **The probe** (scratch, not kept): ticking our walk to 91 and counting the frames on which a camp's
   gatherer moved with a carry bit and nothing bumped the camp gives **7** (frames 4..10) and **22**
   (4..15, 17..26) — the two deficits exactly.
6. **Held out**, not carried: `recharging` has readers (`Wall::inc_time`, `units/think/work_gate.rs`), so
   the arm is a mechanic with floors of its own; `up_who` needs the chain to hold buildings.
