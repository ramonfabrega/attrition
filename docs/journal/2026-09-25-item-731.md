## Step 1: the issuer under the emulator

- **`CommandManager::issue_attack@009415e0(group, ox, whom, ignore,
  queued)` appends 26 bytes** for three captains: the 9-byte `group` and
  a 17-byte `attack`, type `0x04`, `[ox][whom][ignore][queued]`.
- **It writes** the package and the selection caches, and nothing else.
  The same selection again appends the 3-byte reuse. `ignore 7` and each
  queue ride through as passed; a non-captain is dropped.
- **A negative `ox` or `whom` appends nothing** and writes nothing: the
  issuer tests both before `check_accept_issue`. `use_mp_playback`,
  `semaphore & 0x10` and `semaphore & 4` each append nothing.

  never a point. A ctrl+right-click on ground is `issue_move_to` with
  `ATTACK_TO` (`WorldMap::on_right_up@008c7050:199`), and the emulator
  appends its 22-byte `move_to` with `orders` 2.
- **What the emulator could not reach**: `process_attack` →
  `action_attack` → `add_attack_order`, and `process_move_to` →
  `action_move_near` → `add_group_move_order`.
- The scratch script ran in the job's tmp dir.

## Step 2: the chapter, before the capture (`eff8b57`)

**The reading killed half the booked premise before the run.** No issuer
builds a `GroupAttackOrder` (`docs/ORDERS.md` §7.9: nothing asks
`get_obj(GROUP_ATTACK)`). A player's attack on a unit is one
`AttackOrder` a member, `mandatory` 1. The `GroupAttackToOrder` is the
attack-move's, `issue_move_to` with kind 2 through
`add_group_move_order`. So the chapter measures one absence and one
presence, and the ground point is a second issuer.

- **The cast**: two Hoplite squads (captains `0/6`, `0/9`) and a who=1
  Chariot `1/6` about 3,840 north.
- **The staging had a constraint the reading found.** The first scratch
  walk had the attack die on its first step: `fight`'s `valid_target`
  drops an unseen target, and a click can only name a seen one
  (`execute_at_cursor`'s `FILTER_SEEN`). A Hoplite sees about 1,150 and
  searches 2,304; the Chariot sees about 1,730. No ground lets who=0 see
  it with nobody engaging. So the group walks in under a right-click
  `@move` through the Chariot's spot, whose action bit keeps it from
  answering fire, and `@attack` comes on 734, fifteen frames after this
  crate first sees `1/6`.
- **A second Chariot for the attack-move was tried and dropped.** The
  scratch walk had it kill three Hoplites and split the group's orders
  by `unit_masks & 4`. The en-route engagement is already the patrol's
  and the armies'; the chapter keeps one lever per command.
- **The DLL**: `@attack <who> <ox> <whom> <o>…` (`ignore` 0, QUEUE_NEW)
  and `@amove`, `@move` with `ATTACK_TO`. Both compile `-Werror`, plain
  and under `RON_AUTOSTART`.
- **The harness** parses both verbs. `@amove` runs through
  `input::group_move_to` with `orders` 2, which the crate already took.
  `@attack` is skipped as a named seam. `input::group_attack` is built
  and left unwired until the floor.
- **The pool's level.** `GroupData::log_data@0045e1d0` sets no logger
  type, so the pool inherits whatever `dump_units` left. The capture runs
  run178's `GUYS=4` with no `DEATHS` or `AMMO`, the one line whose pool
  printed.
- **A side finding for the parser**: `GroupAttackOrder::log_data
  @00485140` prints its header as `GroupAttackOrder`, mixed case.
  `rondata::gamelog` keys type 20 on `GROUPATTACKORDER`, a spelling no
  dump carries. The killer greps both.
