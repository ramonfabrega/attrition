## What would kill each reading, written before the build

One confession first: this stanza was written after one pass over
`ObjectData::valid_target_const@006472c0`'s decompile, and before its
listing, the build or any diff. Three readings of why the original's
Bomber `1/6` takes no attack on 616 where ours takes one on the Fighter
`0/6`:

1. **The searcher is refused**: an aircraft with no base, or at
   `air_alt` 0, never reaches the idle search. **Killed if** the original's
   Fighter or Bomber ever holds an `ATTACK` in run168: every `UNITDATA`
   order list for `0/6` from 611 and for `1/6` from 616, to 899. The
   first place it could fire is block 616's `1/6` order list.
2. **The target is refused**: `valid_target_const`'s air ladder. A
   searcher that is not `ANTI_AIR` and whose type has `FLY_HIGH` 0 cannot
   take an aircraft that `UnitData::is_flying_high@0060a310` calls high.
   An aircraft with no order is high, because `is_flying_low@0060a140`
   returns 0 on `NONE`. **Killed if** the Bomber's `FLY_HIGH` is not 0,
   or it carries `ANTI_AIR` (obj-mask `6`), or the Fighter holds an order
   on 615 or 616. Those are the rules row and the Fighter's order list on
   block 616. It would also be killed if ours, with the ladder built,
   still spends 25 draws on 616.
3. **The reach gate** (COMBAT §60.2): the Bomber must reach what it takes.
   **Killed if** the Bomber is not a `local_24` searcher. That holds if
   its `stance` is not STAND_GROUND (block 616 prints `stance 0`), it is
   not entrenched, and its type does not pack. And if it is one, the reading
   is killed if its range covers 8 tiles. The rules row says `1-3rng`, so it
   does not.

## What each reading met

1. **The searcher is refused.** It was not killed on its own terms: no
   aircraft holds an attack in run168. But it was not needed. Nothing in
   `Unit::think` or `think_attack@005f5a80` asks about the air domain, and
   both aircraft reach the search.
2. **The target is refused.** It survived. The rules row has the Bomber
   at `FLY_HIGH 0`, `FLY_LOW 10%` and `OBJ_MASK BSX3T`, with no `6`. The
   Fighter holds no order on 615 or 616. With the ladder built, ours spends
   24 draws on 616 and the word went to 635. **But the ladder was not the
   whole of it.** On 635 the Fighter's periodic think (`(6 + 634) & 31 ==
   0`) took the Bomber. The ladder passes an `ANTI_AIR` aircraft straight
   through, and the original's Fighter never takes it.
3. **The reach gate.** Killed. The Bomber prints `stance 0`, does not pack
   and is not entrenched, so it is not a `local_24` searcher. Its range is
   three tiles either way.

The second half of the target reading is `check_target`'s tail.
`poor_target@0064a270` opens on the target's first figure's `guy_flags &
0x40`. `Guy::init_real` sets that bit on every figure of an `is_plane`
type. The arm refuses a plane outright to a searcher without `ANTI_AIR`,
and to one with it that is further than its own `max_range` tiles. The
Fighter reaches seven and the Bomber is eight away. This crate had the
arm as a stated seam (COMBAT §37.3), and its ring search never called
`poor_target` at all.

The listing settled two things the decompile left open:

- the ladder's searcher/target reads (`0xc0ab84` against `0xc0aec0`);
- that `+0xf4` is `guys.list`, so the byte is a figure's `guy_flags` and
  not `UnitTypeData +0x9a`, as §30.5 had it. That is struck in place, and
  §30.5's owed capture is struck with it.

`is_flying_low`'s slot `0xfc` is overridden only by the three air-order
classes, so in a crate without player air orders every plane is high.
