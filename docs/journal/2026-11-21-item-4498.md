## The reading

`Group::action_build@00707510` calls `GroupData::validate_build@00708620`,
and each pass of its loop calls `BuildTypeData::snap_center(.., who)`
(`00707834`) before `blocked_site`. `snap_center@00636190`'s second arm,
from the listing: `is(0x1b0)` (the Dock), or `who >= 0` (`636304`) and
`is(0x1a3)` or `is(0x1a2)` (the Mine and the Woodcutter's Camp,
`636312`–`636328`). Then `blocked_site` runs at the clicked tile's centre.
When it refuses, the arm walks `move_x/move_y[1..=80]`, bounds-checking only
the row, and takes the first tile it clears. `docs/CITIES.md` §2.2 had read
this all along. This crate built the Dock's half for the harness's `add`
(item 803), and `place.rs` carried the Mine and Camp half as a SEAM, "none
reached by a capture on file". The sample reaches it.

**Built**:

- `crates/sim/src/cities/place.rs`: `Sim::snap_center_placed` takes the
  gatherer arm when `who` is a player. The SEAM is narrowed to the oil snap
