## The reading and the build

`Unit::update_speed@006055c0` has an arm after the French siege arms and before the Spy's. For a type whose
`+0x218 == 2` (air), when the leader's `0x6da7 & 1` or `0x6dcf & 1` is set, it computes `speed =
(ALUMINUM_AIR_SPEED + 100) × speed / 100`.
- That byte is the Whales arm's: Aluminum (TI 30) is mask bit 24, byte 3 bit 0, and Whales (31) is the bit beside it.
- `rules.xml` ships `ALUMINUM_AIR_SPEED` at 25 %.
- `Leader::calc_unit_stats` already re-speeds every unit when a rare is gained, so no new caller is needed.

Built in `crates/sim/src/rares.rs` (`speed_of`) and `crates/sim/src/tuning.rs` (`aluminum_air_speed`, the
`ALUMINUM_AIR_SPEED` slot). The test is `rares::tests::aluminum_speeds_the_aircraft_and_nothing_else`.
