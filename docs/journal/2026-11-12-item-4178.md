## 4. The red, read further (4174a)

**The specular.** `ps_global.hlsl`'s Torrance specular is the material's 40 × D·F·G / (4 N·L N·V), on top of
the HDR multiplier. At roughness 0.3 it swings from 0.05 to about 2.4 in HDR. It depends on which way `V`
points, and on an orthographic camera the shader takes `V` as `−normalize(ViewPos)`. That is not settled here.

**The screen reflection.** It marches ten 10-unit steps along the reflected ray. On a hit within 0.00075 of the
depth of what was drawn before the water, it adds `N·V × BackTex`. On the pinned camera that threshold is about
22 view units. So it can hit in the shallows and add the sand's own colour, but not over the floor 283 units
below.

**The photograph's lake** fits 0.6 × the lit model plus a grey of about 94, in tone space. A neutral specular
cannot lower the blue, so this mix points away from the specular alone. None of it is counted.
