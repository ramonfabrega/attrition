# Item 478 — the word was a farm dying, and the field was never parsed

*2026-09-21, worker lane, Opus 5. `docs/ORDERS.md` §19, `docs/ROADS.md` §1.2.*

The brief was the AI headline's own parting: `calc_road_cost` draws, ours
0 and theirs 198, on Great Lakes 10234. Item 475 had killed the
tie-break reading and left two questions — which of `find_road`'s three
callers runs, and why this crate runs none — with run117 reserved and
approved to answer the first, because "no capture on disk carries a
proxied call there, and the trace's `cover` was off for run53".

Both halves of that sentence were wrong, and finding out cost thirteen
seconds.

## The disk had it, four times over

**run53 was taken with `cover=1`.** `docs/RUNS.md` says so in its own
run53 stanza and again in run77's, and `report.py … functions` prints a
first-entry frame for 6,936 of them. `Caravan::process@0073e000` is not
in the list: it never runs in 24,001 frames. That killed one of the three
callers in the time it took to grep.

**And the caller is in the dump, not the trace.** The draw chain stops
three frames deep at `find_road`, so no trace at any depth on disk names
the caller — but `WallData::build_masks` is printed for every building of
every block from `BUILDS=1`, and its `0x100` is "my roads want
replanning". Streaming run100 for blocks 10180–10250 takes three seconds
and prints the whole answer: the bit goes on for all six of the human's
buildings between 10230 and 10231, and comes off one at a time, `0/2006`
across 10234 and `0/2005` across 10235. Two buildings, two frames, each
on the `(frame + o) % 16` slot `docs/ROADS.md` §1 has described since it
was written. Not one search carried over, which is what §18.1 had said.

A second pass over the same blocks, diffing the whole roster, named the
cause: `0/2004`, the human's farm, is gone between 10230 and 10231, and
`0/2003`'s `city_down` goes `2004 → 2005` — the linked-list splice at the
top of `Build::remove_from_city`, whose fourth statement is
`City::regen_roads`.

So run117 was not taken. The lane's screen is free, and the rule that
produced that is CLAUDE.md's own, one level up: grep the disk before
booking the capture somebody else already approved.

## The fix was one line, and the document already knew

`docs/ROADS.md` §1 has named both of `City::regen_roads`' callers from
the day it was written. This crate had one. `Sim::remove_from_city`
unlinked the building and stopped, and `crates/sim/src/city.rs` carried
a comment on the *other* call saying it "is the only writer a traced game
reaches" — a claim nobody had tested, and one that a diff of the field
would have failed on any capture.

Which brings the actual finding.

## `build_masks` was never parsed

`BuildDump::build_masks` has been in the reader since the `BUILDDATA`
block existed. It was read with `b.int("build_masks")`. The record writes
it at **`WALLDATA`**'s indent, between `ever_seen_completed` and
`helpers` — one level in. So it was `None` on every capture ever taken.

`crate::ledger` had it listed as uncompared and had done for weeks. The
ledger exists precisely to be the queue of cheap widenings, and this row
sat on it while four items chased the frame it explains.

It is a `WALLDATA` field now, `build_masks_is_the_wall_s_field` pins the
indent with a decoy at the outer one, and the replan bit is compared on
every linked building-frame of every capture — 63,000 more comparisons on
run57 alone, none of them wrong, which is its own small evidence that the
flag's life agrees on the second map too.

With the call in: 10234 spends 204 draws against 204 and 10235 spends 78
against 78, node for node. The word goes 10234 → 10237 on the sequence
and 10244 on the count, and the widening's parted keys go 328 → 297.

## Two things left for somebody else

**The sequence word is a label, not a divergence.** 10237 and 10242 both
spend the same number of draws on both sides and part at index 5 and 6 on
`678cb9` — `Ammo::do_damage+0xc59`, an address `trace::SITES` does not
carry — against this crate's unattributed `projectiles` phase label.
Until that site is named the sequence cannot pass 10237 however the
simulation behaves, which is why `LONG_WORD_GREAT_LAKES` is pinned at the
lower of the two numbers. It belongs with `docs/COMBAT.md` and lane 479
owns that document, so it is reported and not taken.

**476 and 477 are untouched.** f10234's three value rows and f10235's
`1/28 pos` stand exactly as minted; the widening now pins 10235's three
by name, since the headline has walked past them.

## What this cost and what it bought

Under two hours, no capture, one line of simulation and one of parser.
The expensive part was re-pinning: nine tests across three families — two
endpoints, two ladder rungs, three `build_compared` totals, both halves
of the ledger — all of them moved by a field that started being compared,
which is the tax a widening always pays and the reason it is worth
paying.
