## 2. The rule, then the count

**Why before the tone map.** The client's ground is the tone map's output (§3.14 keeps it near the texel). The
original darkens its HDR target and then runs `GSToneMapper`'s filmic curve. Blending in display space put ours
at 0.79 of theirs (the median of ours/theirs luminance over the row's tiles). Taking the keep map through
`T(T⁻¹(g)·keep)` puts the tree tiles at 0.93. The curve's constants are the live ones on the packet (§3.14), so
nothing here is fitted. The CPU compose with the rule's own nearest texels reads 65 against the GPU's 66. So the
GPU's pass is the arithmetic the rule assumes.
