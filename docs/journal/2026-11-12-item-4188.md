## What was built

- **The instrument on all 108 packets**: `--stream --values` on each, every one exiting 0 with its
  stream's Adler equal to the original's on all fifteen lines. The output is outside git, under
  `~/ron-data/lab-experiments/2026-11-12-item-4188/<run>/` (`RON_PACKETS_DIR`).
- **`rondata::diff::checksum_lines::packets`** (`docs/CHECKSUM.md` §23), generalised from frame 91's
  comparison. It uses the same `parting`, `leader_leaf_rows`, `leader_held_name` and `build_pieces`
  (now `build_pieces_with`, the data layer read once a walk), and the same stream reader
  (`stream_lines`, which `stream` now calls). Each game's test walks to its packets' frames, ascending,
  and holds the twelve lines range by range there, with the leaders line leaf by leaf:
  - 59 word tests use `third::stand_up_from`, factored out of `walk_from`, so the word's walk and
    the packet's walk start from one function;
  - 6 chapter tests use `golden::walk_script_to`, whose stand-up is factored out of `walk_script`;
  - 5 melee tests use the word's stand-up inside the melee's staged script, `seat_walk`'s walk;
  - `minted_packet` uses the frame-91 pair's own walk.
- **The census**, `every_packet_is_wired_to_its_game_or_named`. The score is
  `the_packets_score_is_the_pins`. The probe is `probe_one_packet` (ignored). The red check is
  `a_packet_s_comparison_can_part`.
- **Two corrections to the leaders line** (`crates/sim/src/checksum/leaders.rs`): `mil_trainers`'
  elements are the trainers' object numbers, and a `SimpleArray<int>`'s size is its capacity in
  tens (below).
- `testkit::GROUND_INEXACT` gains seven rows. They are the corners each word's walk meets before its
  last packet, a subset of what its own word's walk already meets. `ledger::NOT_A_DIFFER` names
  `packets.rs`.

## What it found

**The boundary.** A packet at logger frame `n` is our walk at `sim.frame == n` on a word's stand-up,
as on the minted walk. On run1161 (frame 4) frame 4 agrees on 4 of 9 counted lines, with units
parting on 535 ranges and builds on 13. Frame 3 agrees on 3, with 2,772 and 53 parting, and frame 5
agrees on 4, with 2,765 and 15.

**147 of 1,058 counted lines agree on 107 packets** (`docs/CHECKSUM.md` §23.4 has the table by
line). The minted packet agrees on 8 of 9 lines, as frame 91's own pins say. The chapters agree on 28
of 68: groups, cities, items and goods agree on all seven. The melees agree on 23 of 127 and the words
on 88 of 854. Units, builds, guys and world agree only on the minted packet. The world line's
`start_x.num` reads ours 0 against theirs 2 or 3 on all 106 others, because a dump-seated start
carries no start arrays. Ammo (56 packets) and deaths (39) have no emitter in the crate. No packet
walks a wall.

**The leaders line: no leaf released.** Every leaf of `LEADERS_HELD_OUT` but `tech.ptr` is unheld on
all 226 walked leaders, so it has no value to agree, and `tech.ptr` parts on all 226. 129 §5 releases
nothing until 4187 gives the unprinted leaves a value. Five of them, `incoming*`, `score_explored`,
`score_pop`, `tribute_received` and `tribute_sent`, are zero on every packet, so the packets cannot
release them even once held. `LEADERS_HELD_OUT` stays at 61. No leaf meets 128 §3 newly: no new dump.
