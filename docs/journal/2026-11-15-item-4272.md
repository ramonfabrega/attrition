textures and over every template's `MAIN_ALPHA_TEX` and `RING_ALPHA_TEX` (stage 1). In the original:
- `TileSet::init@0087b370` calls `set_tint` on `mountain_base` and `mountain_alpha` (stage 0).
- A range's alphas load through `MountainRange::init@008998b0`'s plain `Texture::init@005245f0` calls. That copies
  the texture's own tint, which `Texture::Texture@00524170` leaves black.
- No `set_tint` caller touches `MountainRange`.
