## 2. What the haze is not

Each claim below was read off our walk on 3279/3280, the frame the client draws:

- **Not dying.** All five tanks have `health` 210 and `damage_frac` 0.
- **Not a decoy.** `GuyOut::draw@005dc490` sets `transparent` to `0x1f` for `unit_masks & 1` (the decoy bit,
  `docs/ARMY.md`) when the viewer owns it, is allied with it, or detects it. On our walk, no unit on the frame has
  `decoy` set.
- **Not cloaked by `unit_masks2 & 0x8000`.** The tanks' `unit_masks2` is 0. `GuyOut::draw`'s other arm pulses
  `transparent` at `cloak_pulse_rate` for `is_cloaked`: `unit_masks & 0x800`, the type's `+0x2b4 & 0x4000`,
  `unit_masks2 & 0x8000`, or `+0x2b4 & 0x40000` with no order. Two of its terms are open. The sim does not carry
  `unit_masks & 0x800` (`docs/GOLDEN.md`). The tank's `+0x2b4` was not read.
- **Not movement.** Over 3280 → 3283, `1/40` stands still and is hazy. `1/15` moves (6024, 31128) → (5956, 31062) and
  is crisp. `1/27` and `1/16` move.

**One candidate is consistent with all of this, and it is unproven: the attrition underlay.**
`UnitOut::suffer_graphic_attrition@00608e20` is called from `Unit::suffer_attrition@005e1a10` alone. It calls
`Underlays::add` at the unit's point with the type's `+0x234 × 0x60` and a random 0..360 turn. All five tanks stand in
cells our territory gives player 0: (5, 41), (8, 40), (7, 40), (8, 42). Health 210 with `damage_frac` 0 does not fit
attrition that has bitten, though. And an underlay explains the base, not the haze over the hull.
