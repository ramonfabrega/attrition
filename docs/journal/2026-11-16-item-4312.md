## What was read

The reading is in `docs/RENDERER.md` §3.24, "The `Oil` good's billboard". In short:
`draw_good_underlays` → `GoodOut::draw_underlays` → `GoodTypeOut::draw`. That chain keeps an `Oil` good only on a
cell whose `WData::land` is 1 or 2 and whose `flags & 0x100` is clear. Then `GraphicPieces::draw_good` translates
the sprite by 192 × the `offset`, and `ZSpriteAccumMethod::execute` lays one upright quad, the texture at
`fixed_zooms[6] / zoom_distance`. The first build drew every oil good. Plain fell 666 → 663 and `ROCK` rose 9 → 16,
from oil on land. The land predicate brought both back to their pins.

- **4312b. The other `BillBoard` underlays are not drawn.** Salt, Amber, Silver, Cotton, Copper and Diamonds are
  `model="BillBoard" underlay="1"` in `effects_graphics.xml`. Their `GoodTypeOut::draw` has no land test, and
  Silver and Diamonds carry a particle. `client::goods` lays only `Oil`.
- **4312c. The billboard's predicates are not ported.** `GoodOut::draw_underlays` asks
  `LeaderData::type_avail(viewer, OIL, 1)` (or the semaphore's bit 8) and skips an ocean oil good under a
  building. `draw_good` greys the vertex where the cell is not seen. The client draws every live oil good on an
  allowed cell, white. All three hold on run1564's frame, because the card draws it.
