# Item 391 — the hit belongs to the captain, and the group was innocent

Rules track, Opus 5, its own worktree. The brief named two things left at the
golden record's frame 617: a `GROUP_ATTACK_TO` lifting `1/6` off its seat, and
`0/7`'s retaliation striking one frame early. **They are one defect, it is
neither of the two mechanisms the brief named, and the word moved: 617 → 618**,
value diff 618 → 619.

## What the frame was actually short of

Two draws, and the trace prints them plainly once you ask for the whole frame
rather than for the mechanism you came with:

```

f617 ours    6× Farms
```

`Unit::fight+0x9b0` is §12.4's one-in-five re-search roll, captain-gated.
`Guy::set_anim+0xf2f < Guy::move+0x166` is `SITE_ATTACK_STAND` — the swing

## Re-measuring first, which is what found it

A backtrace on the group order — three minutes — said the caller was not
`Group::target_opportunity` and not the group at all:

```
group_action_move_to ← group_action_siege_attack_to ← army_tick
                     ← armies_emergency ← do_damage ← fight_pub
```

`Armies::emergency(1)`, from `0/7` striking `1/6` during 617. And
`Armies::emergency` is **not** gated by `ai off` — the cheat's only readers in
the simulation are `Unit::think`, `Leader::production_ai` and
`Leader::diplomacy`. So the question stopped being "why does the group move"
and became "why does `0/7` strike on 617".

## The answer, out of the dump

`Unit::target_opportunity@005fffc0` opens with a `while (true)` that ends
`if (is_captain(this)) break; this = objects[who][this->o_up]`. **Every test
below it runs on the head of the victim's squad.** The golden dump prints the
consequence in five fields: at the end of 616 the ATTACKORDER on `1/6` is
carried by **`0/6`** — `0/7`'s captain, the only one of who=0's three with
`o_up -1` — while `0/7`, which took the damage, carries nothing; `0/7` and
`0/8` get theirs at the end of 617; `0/7` strikes on 618.

This crate retaliated on the victim. One `squad_captain` call, a function
`orders.rs` already had for `ungroup_move_order`, and the chain unwound: `0/6`
takes the order on 616 and spends 617 in `fight` with `recharging 0`, which is
the frame's first missing draw; `0/7` has no order until the end of 617, so
nothing hits `1/6` during 617, so no emergency, so `1/6` keeps its seat and
pays its swing — the second.

The group branch of the same loop is guarded by `type +0x2c8 & 0x10000 == 0`,
so a combat-role unit never takes it. `Group::target_opportunity` was never in
this frame.

## What is pinned, and what is not

Three assertions, each made to fail on purpose first:

- `fight::tests::the_hit_is_answered_by_the_victim_s_captain` — a two-figure
  squad, the subordinate hit; the old code gives `(None, Some(foe))`.
- `chapter_one_s_hit_is_answered_by_the_victim_s_captain` — the golden dump's
  own frame 617, both halves: the captain holds the attacker and **neither**
  member holds anything.
- `GOLDEN_WORD_CHAPTER_ONE = 618`.

**Not established:** whether this crate's `Army::process` would still walk the
squad off if the emergency were reached on the right frame. It is not reached
again in chapter one's 901 frames, so no capture on disk can say. The original
reaches it on 618 and does not move the squad.

## What stands at 618, and whose it is

One draw: a second `Unit::fight+0x9b0`, `0/6`'s. The original's `0/6` spends
617 in §17's ring — `find_attack_pos` answers `(1080, 8280)` and the chase
re-entry plans the five-node path the dump prints at block 618 — so from 618
its current order is the move and `do_attack` is not reached again until 650.
This crate's `find_attack_pos` answers nothing, falls back to the target's own
point, loses the move inside the frame that ordered it, and rolls again on 618,
620 and 621. That is `docs/COMBAT.md` §17's ring, and it is the next item.

The one after it is already visible at the same place: who=0's `0/7` and `0/8`
take the captain's order a frame after the captain, and this crate gives them
none at all — the mirror of `Group::target_opportunity`'s "only the captain
searches", which `docs/GROUPS.md` §13 still owes.

## The lesson, which is the standing warning again

Six booked mechanisms in two days were wrong with the frame right; this is the
seventh. Both of the brief's named suspects were innocent, and what found the
cause was widening to *every* draw on the frame and then backtracing the one
order the harness issued that the original does not. Twenty minutes. The
reading came after, to name what the measurement had already cornered.
