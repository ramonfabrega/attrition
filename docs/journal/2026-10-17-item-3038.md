- The decompiler's `::ignore_always` is **not** one global: each error call site has its own flag byte (`0xee121c`,
  `0xee1232`, `0xee1244`, `0xee1245`); the cmp in the listing names it. `--cell-win` of such a VA writes a *pointer* there
  (its low byte is the flag), which passed one sweep by luck; use `--at VA:SIZE` with `--wbias c0+0:1` instead.
- `--regen` keeps the old line's flags; changing a flag kind needs `--regen --fresh` with the whole line.
- A 16-entry `--argrange` is inclusive (`0:0:16` can reach 16 against a 64-byte window).
- zsh does not word-split `$VAR`; `--wbias 'p0+*:…'` needs quoting in zsh.

for the Loop: `sweep_gen.py` could take `--flag-byte VA` (a muted-error call site's byte) as a named option so `--cell-win`
on such a VA is refused (it passed `remove_hold_doobers` on a pointer's low byte until the next draw), and `mutate.py` could
decode `\n` in a batch row so a multi-line break is writable.
