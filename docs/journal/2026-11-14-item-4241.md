## 2. The reading, and the value diff

- **The order.** `ps_tonemap.hlsl`: `color = tonemap(gExposure · src) · whiteScale`, written to the target with no
  clamp in the shader, so the target clamps after the white. The client held its frame at the floor's white, clamped
  to 1, and scaled it after (§3.21): at run1559b's scale, 0.898, no channel passed 229.
  - Pixel (1175, 460), ours' brightest by slot 8: (229, 229, 229) → (255, 255, 239) → with the bloom (255, 255, 240).
  - The photograph's brightest there, (1176, 466): (255, 255, 249). Ours there: (206, 151, 81) → (220, 169, 92).
- **Whether the bloom runs.** `Scene::render@008b3270` calls the tone mapper's `do_run` (vtable +4, `rise.pdb`'s
  `GSToneMapper` method list) with `section_scales[22] == 2`. `RenderSections` names 22 `DRAW_SECTION_BLOOM`, and
  `SectionGrade` names 2 `FULL_SECTION`. On both packets (`graphsys` +0xe0 `scalability`, +0x14 `section_scales`),
  all 25 sections are 2.
- **The chain** (`d3dgl.dll`, `sbl/d3dgl.map`'s names):
  - `do_run` (`0x10044a30`) calls `do_luminance` and `do_tonemap`, then, with the flag, `do_blur` and `do_blend`.
  - `create_ping_pong_buffers` (`0x10043910`) makes two `DXGI_FORMAT_R10G10B10A2_UNORM` targets. The first is half
    the screen (views +0xa8 and +0xac), the second the whole screen (+0xb0 and +0xb4).
  - `do_blur` (`0x10044de0`) sets a viewport of half the screen. It writes `BlurData` (`update_blur_data`, cbuffer
    4: `gUVScale`, `gDirection`) as (1, 1, 1, 0), then draws from +0xb4 into +0xa8. It writes (1, 1, 0, 1), then
    draws from +0xac into +0xb0.
  - `ps_blend_screen.hlsl` samples the bloom at `uv · 0.5`: the full target's quarter, which the second pass wrote.
  - `gScreenSize` is the whole screen's (`vs_global.hlsl` places interface pixels by it), so a tap is `(i − 4) · i/2
    · 3` screen pixels.
- **Not read:** the sampler, which `create_state_objects` does not make (it makes a blend, a rasterizer and a depth
  state). It is taken linear and clamped.

- **4241b. The drag per render step.** `Particle::pass_time` runs from `GraphicPieces::inc_time` once a render, with
  the milliseconds since the last. `Particle::init` steps a birth by its whole age in one call, so a catch-up birth
  takes its drag once. Ours steps every 67 ms. The cadence is the capture's (4240a). On run1570, four frames from the
  photograph, the packet's particles through our pass stand 15 and 28 rows tall on slots 8 and 9, against the
  photograph's 13 and 27, and ours 11 and 26. No row separates a cadence.
- **4241c. The bloom of a pixel no plume covers.** The client's frame is clamped at the floor's white before the
  expose pass, so a pixel past it that no plume covers (a white wall, a lit roof) is scaled at 1 and blooms less than
  the original's. Only a plume's pixel is composed again unclamped. A scene held in HDR to the expose pass would
  answer it; that changes every pass's target.
- **4241d. `back_calc`'s thinning by frame rate.** `ParticleEmitter::back_calc@008dc5f0` keeps one birth in two or
  four when `30000 / scene._padding_` is under 15 or 10, unless the system's flag at +0xcc is set. The member is
  unnamed, and whether the captures ran under the thinning is not read.
