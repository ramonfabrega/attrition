## Stage 2: the pass

`TerrainOut::render`'s `showtrans` block works per (alpha, `landindex`) batch:

- stage 0 takes the patch's base land texture;
- stage 1 takes the alpha;
- the state is the `RenderState` at `TerrainOut +0x442c`, filled in `TerrainOut::one_time_init`.

Its fields are named by the type record (`RenderState`, size 0x168), and its values by the PDB's enums
(`TextureOp`, `TextureArg`, `BlendMode`, `MIPFilter`, `TexAddress`):

| field | stage 0 | stage 1 |
|---|---|---|
| colorop | MODULATE (TEXTURE, DIFFUSE) | SELECTARG2 (CURRENT) |
| alphaop | SELECTARG2 (DIFFUSE) | MODULATE (TEXTURE, CURRENT) |
| address u, v | CLAMP | CLAMP |
| mag, min, mip filter | POINT | POINT |
| mipmap_bias | — | −1.5 |

The blend is `src_blend` SRCALPHA and `dst_blend` INVSRCALPHA, so the output is the patch's base × A plus the frame
× (1 − A), drawn after the base. 4132's assumption 1 is read, and kept.

The count, from our choice on the packet's cells, each read against (a)–(d) (`RUN1559B_3279_PATCH_READS`):

| read | wins of 503 |

is corrected: the filter is point, clamped, not bilinear.
