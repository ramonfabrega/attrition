## Step 3: the port

- `GroupState::role` holds `Group::find_role`'s word. `Sim::group_find_role` computes it from the
  members: the OR of the in-use members' `UnitCols::role`, or 0 for a building group, which has no
  unit list. It is written in these places:
  - `Sim::seat_normalize`, after its prune, through `Sim::seat_normalize_tail`;
  - `Sim::group_set_speed`, the speed tail of every normalize-opening site;
  - `Army::normalize`'s seat tail;
  - both arms of `Sim::groups_process`.

  `GroupState::emptied` zeroes it, and `copied` keeps the previous occupant's. Nothing else in the
  simulation reads it.
- `Sim::checksum_groups` (`crates/sim/src/checksum/groups.rs`) emits the line from the pool in walk
  order. It takes the army's record while that army has members, else the `Pushed` entry, else
  `Groups::clear`'s image. `disband` and `priority` are 0; `who` is 0 on a cleared, empty record;
  `buildings` is 1 when the group holds buildings; `last_group` is `who·64 + s`.
- Documents:
  - `docs/GROUPS.md` §3.4 (new): `role` and the record's constant words.
  - `docs/CHECKSUM.md` §19 (new); §4, §7 (the member order, the eight ints named) and §10 amended in
    place.
- No float entered the simulation.
