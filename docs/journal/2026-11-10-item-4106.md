## 1. The value, on the listing and the packet

**The listing.** `GraphicPieces::prep_unit_lighting@00905c10`:

- It hands the card a material (`gs_card` vtable +0x1c): `GraphicPieces::unit_material`'s ambient, diffuse,
  specular and emissive. The r, g and b of each are scaled by one percent; the alphas are kept.
- It writes `lights[0]`'s fields at +0x130 and +0x140 to (1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0). The PDB's field list for `Light`
  (`LF_FIELDLIST 0x14A44`; the export pads it) names them **`cur_ambient`** and **`cur_diffuse`**.

So **the light is white.** `GraphicPieces::init_lighting@00904a30` loads `unit_material`'s ambient and diffuse from
the unit-graphics XML.

**The packet**, through the type records (scratch script over `tools/recomp/snapshot.py`, outside the repo):

- **The addresses.** `graphic_pieces` is `0003:2635480` = `0xe896d8`, and `unit_material` is at +0xff0
  (`types.txt`, `Material`, 0x58). `lights` is `0003:20468` = `0xc0aff4` (`PtrArray<Light>`).
- **`unit_material`.** Ambient **(0.600, 0.716, 0.745)**, `ambient_a` 0.558. Diffuse **(1, 1, 1)**, `diffuse_a`
  2.5. Specular and emissive are 0.
- **`land_material`**, for the record. Ambient (0.01, 0.03, 0.18), `a` 2.0. Diffuse (1, 1, 1), `a` 1.36.
- **`lights`.** The count is 1. `lights[0]`: type 2, `cur_on` 1, **`cur_dir` (−0.7071, 0, −0.7071)**,
  `cur_ambient` (1, 1, 1, 1), `cur_diffuse` (1, 1, 1, 1), `cur_specular` 0.
- `light_units` (+0xaa8) is 1.

**What the packet cannot give.** The card is `d3dgl.dll`: the packet's `gs_card` vtable is `0x786352ec`, outside
the image, and the DLL ships no PDB. Its renderer is the Extended Edition's, compiled from the install's own
`shaders/`, which I read as a reading tool; none of it enters the repository. Paraphrased:

- **It is not the fixed-function light.** The lit branch of `ps_global.hlsl` (l. 238–388) raises the texture
  stage's colour to the power **1.45**. It multiplies that by a diffuse term and adds an ambient term, both scaled
  for HDR. The diffuse term is the material diffuse × the light's diffuse × N·L (× 5/π); the ambient term is the
  material ambient × a per-scene ambient light (× 5).
- **The tonemap follows** (`ps_tonemap.hlsl`): a fixed filmic curve on exposure × colour, a white scale from the
  frame's mean luminance, a lerp to grey by a saturation, and a tint.
- **Not in the packet:** the scene ambient, the exposure, the white point, the saturation and the tint. They are
  held by the DLL.

So on a figure the diffuse is grey: a white material under a white light. The ambient is coloured, and its weight
against the diffuse turns with N·L. The power and the tone curve act per channel. Whether the net is coloured is
the photograph's to say.
