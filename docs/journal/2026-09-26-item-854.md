## Step 1: the bit's writers, and the disk

`types.txt` names `WallData +0x60 build_masks` (a `short`). The
decompiler names it only in three functions. Every other store goes
through `field_0x60` or `(ushort *)(x + 0x60)`, so the grep had to cover
both spellings. The writers of `0x80`:
- **`Build::init@00629740:280`**: `can_carry(AIR)` → `|= 0x88`. The
  hangar bit and repeat air are set together, at placement. `Wall::init`
  zeroes the word first.
- **`Group::action_buildmask@006fc9a0`**: flips any bit that
  `WallData::valid_buildmask` passes (`0x80` needs `can_carry(AIR)`).
  Its caller is `CommandPackage::process_buildmask`, from
  `Options::set_air_repeat@0071c740`. That is the player's repeat-orders
  toggle on a selected building. On a unit selection it sends the unit
  mask `0x200000` instead.
- `ScenarioFuncSet::repeat_orders_enable`/`_disable` and
  `UnitBalance::next`: out of v1.
- `Wall::swap_team` copies the whole word.

**The disk (checklist row two).** I read every `BUILDDATA` record in all
201 dumps (the kept logs and every golden run) and took `build_masks &
0x88` against `orig_type`:

## Step 2: `land_plane` and what the plane does next

`Unit::land_plane@005e9950`, from the decompile. The branches are plain
here, so the listing was not needed:
1. The current `AirOrder`'s `returning` is set to 0 (`piVar7[6] = 0`).
2. A `StrafeOrder` whose target is not live (`ox` or `whom` < 0) goes:
   `clear_orders` if mandatory, else `kill_current_order`. That sets
   `bVar2`.
3. `005e9a43`: the home's vslot `0xf0`. If it is 0: `unit_masks &=
   ~0x4000000`, path 0, `close_orders`, `clear_partial_path`,
   `update_action`. Otherwise, **only if the strafe did not go**:
   `update_order(this)->flags &= ~4`.
4. `add_spec_anim_order` to the base, then `work` (§33.5).

**What the plane does next.** It is inside, and `Unit::process` runs no
`work` for it. Its patrol sits on the stack. `Object::do_launch`'s
predicate is `has_repeat_air() || flags & 4` (line 113 of the
decompile). At a full tank (`mana_burn` 0) the patrol is launched, its
`returning` cleared, and the plane flies it again. It is not killed. The
tank refills 2 a frame from 400 on 1385, so it would launch near 1585,
past run265's end.

**The frame each row first shows**: 1385 for `0/6` (1384: the patrol
with `returning 1`, outside) and 1489 for `0/7` (1488: likewise).
