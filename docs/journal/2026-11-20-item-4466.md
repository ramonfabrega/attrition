## The first act: the writers of `0x400000`

The export has one writer of `leader_flags |= 0x400000`:
`Cities::capture_city@00733380:833`. It sets the bit on `leaders[param_3]`,
the loser, in the first-capital arm of the plunder. That arm is entered
unless the bit is already set, the city is not a capital, the Persians'
`find_capital` names this city (`local_88`), or `team_style == 3`. Nothing
clears the bit (`0xffbfffff` appears in no leader function). So yes: 3887's
change of owner is what raises it.

Ours already kept this state, as `city_tally[o].capital_plundered`, set in
`plunder_on_capture`'s first-capital arm. `leader_flag_words` just never put
it on the line.

## The make list's city

The leaders image wrote a `MakeObject`'s city as the city's rank among the

which 4463 gave the builds and cities walks.

## The epoch: the members close before the city

`epochs_queued` counts the 28 library techs, so the queued entry belonged to
the captured city's library. `Build::swap_team@00628210` leaves a library's
queue alone (`is(0x1b3)`), so the queue is answered when the old library
closes. `Build::close@00628980:468`'s arm passes a first library's queue to
the owner's next library, or cleans it with a refund when there is none.
Its third gate is `[esp+0x14]`, which the decompile prints as `pLStack_38`
because it reuses the slot. The listing settles it: `0x628a2d` calls
`get_first_library` and `0x628a47` stores `this->o == first`, at entry,
behind the `+8 & 1` alive test. Ours computes the same `was_first_library`.

Probes (commits `a699e529b`, `770093fac` and `580d6bd96`, reverted whole as
`015792ada`, `5bcda783b` and `f96b5febe`):

- `close f3887 b24 who4 firstNone was_firstfalse q1 cityNone silenttrue`.
  Ours' library `b24` had no city at the close's entry, so it was no first
  library and the arm never ran.
- At `capture_city`'s entry, and again after its head, `b24`'s city was
  still `Some(3)`. The close list was `[19, 20, …, 24, …]`, with the old
  centre `b19` first. Closing `b19` ran `close_city(3)`, which cleared every
  member's `city` before `b24`'s turn.

In the original, the close loop's first test is `(flags(+8) & 0x20) == 0`,
so it **skips the city centre**. The members close while the old city is
still open. Then come `lost_capital` and `recapture_capital`, then
`City::close` (`:1157`), and only then `+0x72 = -1` and the centre's
`close(5)` (`:1170`–`:1172`). The library closes as its owner's first
library. Player 4 has no other library, so `clean_queue(1)` refunds the
entry and takes down `num_queued` and `epochs_queued`. That refund is
what raised buckets 0 and 4 in theirs.
