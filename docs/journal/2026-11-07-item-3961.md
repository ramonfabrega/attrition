## The booking's hypothesis, and what it took

"Red where the crate has no field": the first full `rondata` run had 20 widenings red on `healing` rows. Most were
planes reading ours 0 against theirs 1. A probe (`RON_PROBE_HEAL`, committed alone and reverted whole) showed ours at
heal level 0, period 20. Theirs heals at the all-tech level 3. My first `heal_level` indexed the tech tree by
`TypeIndex`, which it is not; a second probe showed `types.get(0x2ef)` was `None`. Reading the rows off `TECHBONUSES`
closed 14 of the 20. The six left each follow either a hit-point parting that already stood, or an unbuilt heal arm
on the block its hits part. All six are re-pinned with their deltas and parked as 3961a.

I made one mistake along the way. The first probe commit used `commit -a` and swept up uncommitted tests. It was
never pushed; I undid it with a soft reset and removed the probe by hand. The second probe was committed on its own
file and reverted whole (`317378d77`, `1b77bbb14`).
