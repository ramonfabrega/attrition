## What the reading found

- **`Console::execute_at_cursor@007c6630`'s gather arm.** With a building selected, `GroupData::count`'s
  `COUNT_GATHER_POINT` must be nonzero. Then two looks, `find_target(SEARCH_ALL)` and `find_target(SEARCH_FRIENDLY)`.
  If neither finds an object, the arm calls `GroupOut::issue_gather_point(drag_x2, drag_y2, 0, shift)`. On a plain
  press that is `gather_point(x, y, 0, 0)`, the form of 153 of the 177 recorded rally points.
- **`COUNT_GATHER_POINT`** (arm 23 of `GroupData::count@00711720`) admits a building that has a garrison limit, or is a
  training building, or has one of a chain of type abilities.
- **`action_gather_point`'s "inside" arm**: a point on a member's own footprint is stored as (−1, −1). The first point
  chosen fell there, and the Citizen stayed inside the capital. Our walk showed it before any capture.
