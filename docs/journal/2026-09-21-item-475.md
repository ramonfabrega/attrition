## The hour I lost, and it is worth more than the finding

Between the second measurement and the third I told the commander something
false: that the draw count at 10234 sat inside an unaligned stream and could
not mean anything. I had read `rondata::diff::FLOORS[1].word` — `1850` for
Great Lakes — as the map's draw word.

It is not. `FLOORS` is the **scored** captures' scoreboard, those runs are
1,850 frames long, and `first_count` is computed with `unwrap_or(last)`, so
`1850` there means *never parted inside the capture*. The long captures'
words are pinned separately. `run53_s_24000_frames_put_the_ceiling_where_
run33_did`, on my own tip:

```
run53: word parts at 10234, sequence at 10234 of 24000
  frame 10234: ours 6 theirs 204 — at 4,
    ours   Guy::set_anim+0x97a < Guy::inc_time+0x271
    theirs PathFinder::calc_road_cost+0x46
```

Count *and* sequence agree on all 10,234 frames below. The 204 is a true
measurement on an aligned stream. I retracted it in the next message; the
commander had already passed it upward and corrected that in turn.

The trap is in §18.2 now, because the shape of it is general: **two
constants with the same name mean different things on different captures,
and `unwrap_or(last)` makes "never happened" indistinguishable from "happened
at the end".** A number that reads as a sentinel should be checked against
what produced it before it is read as a measurement.

A missing behaviour rather than a divergent one — and item 334 closed the

## The audit

The commander asked, in a thirty-minute box, which items **changed code** on
the strength of the false attribution as against merely citing it. The answer
is clean: **none changed code, and it selected exactly one item — this one.**
464 cited it in an accounting paragraph; 465 cited it and explicitly steered
away from it ("not the pathfinder's draw count, which is a much larger and
older hole"); 471 cited it and built §17.6 on it, while its own change rests

care whose draws they are. The table is §18.4.

The lesson is narrower than "a false frame steered the loop", and better for
being narrow: **a draw count names a site, and a site names a call chain —
read the chain before naming the caller.** `report.py … sites` prints it
beside every fold and it had been there, unread, since the number was first
written down on item 464.
