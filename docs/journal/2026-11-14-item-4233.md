## 3. The reading

- **`Particle::draw@008da6b0`: the look.** Life runs through three phases on `fade[0..2]`. The alpha is `hold_alpha
  · t`, then held, then faded out. The size runs over keys 0 to 3, and the quad's half extent is 1.5 × the size.
- **`Particle::pass_time@008db470`: the motion.** The acceleration is gravity plus the wind × `wind_use`, the wind
  only from `min_wind_height` over the ground. Drag multiplies the velocity each step.
- **`Particle::init@008db7c0`: the start.** The velocity is `desc.vel · start_speed` plus a noise of ±⌊|v|·noise⌋/340;
  the point is the anchor ± `pos_noise`; the angle is random.
- **`emit_build_particles@00909590`.** `desc.vel` is `get_position`'s second vector.
- **The wind, `Scene::render@008b3270`.** The listing at `0x008b33ca` to `0x008b350c` and the constants read from
  the PE give `(4, 4, 3)/67000`, turned through ⌊22.5·(sin(t/0.8) + cos(t/1.2))⌋° clamped to ±45. `wind_scalar`
  (0.8, 1.2) is read off the packet.
- **The render states, off the packet.** `enables` 0x20085 and `src_blend` 5. The smoke has `dst_blend` 6 and
  `colorop` 4, which `rise.pdb`'s `TextureOp` field list names `TOP_MODULATE2X`. The fire has `dst_blend` 2 and
  `colorop` 3.

  (`left_over` and `counter` are not read).
