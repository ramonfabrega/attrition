## The reading

`Objects::find_free@0065ad60` reuses a number below the mark only when
the object is dead (`flags & 1` clear), **its `hold_frames` is zero**,
and, for a unit, its `o_up` is negative. This crate tested the first
condition only.

Two writers keep the hold up while a death object lives.
`DeathObj::inc_time@008d5240` adds one to the dead slot's `hold_frames`
as its first statement, every frame, from `Objects::inc_time`.
`Objects::process_all@0065dce0` takes one off a dead slot at its visit.

record's number reads at least 1 when the cheat's `add` looks for it.

has no `UnitTypeData` vtable, and the PDB's field list names the slot

hold at 30.

the bump never landed. It had no decrement at all.
