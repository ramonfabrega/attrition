## 3215: a Marine's idle wrap

1. **The draws.** On 3215 theirs spends four `Guy::inc_time` wraps, at indices 6–9 (callers `+0x3e, +0x3e, +0x6e,
   +0x3e`). Ours spends three: `1/57`, `1/95` and `9/8`. So theirs' extra wrap is a `+0x3e` by a who=1 figure.
2. **The capture.** No dump on disk printed `cur_anim` or `cur_time` before 3215, so I booked run1325 with `GUYS=4`.
   Ours' first candidate was the Cuirassier `1/47`. Theirs' clocks show it agreeing, so it was the wrong figure.
3. **The comparison.** Every who=1 figure's `(cur_anim, cur_time, end_time)` on block 3214 parts on four Marines:
   `1/40`, `1/49`, `1/55` and `1/56`. In theirs they play idle variant 0; in ours they play 1 or 2. `1/55` is at t68
   of 70, so it wraps on 3215 in theirs alone.
4. **The mechanism.** The record that says why is `unit_masks2` `0x20800`: the marines are in their own trench.
   `Unit::check_idle@006032c0:33–41` digs in a unit when three things hold:
   - it carries `unit_masks2 & 0x800` (`set_type`'s `is(0x77)` mark);
   - its idle is at least `americans_marine_entrench` as loaded: `x − x/15 + 2` = 7
     (`Constants::init@00569a90:1825`);
   - it is not yet entrenched.

   Digging in sets `unit_masks |= 0x2000000`, `unit_masks2 |= 0x20000` and `+0x5c = +0x50`. `Guy::set_anim`'s
   `005daba0` then sends the trenched figure to anim 0.

**Built** (`f8afc01ec`):
- the two bits;
- the trench in `check_idle`, and its clear in `unit_step` (`move_step@005faf30:60–65`);
- the set_anim fallback;
- `combat::State::trench_angle`;
- `Sim::hard_trench` at the three readers that exempt the marines (`fight`, `find_melee_target` and
  `find_nearby_target`). The crate's `find_melee_target` had an AND where the original has
  `!(hard(this) && hard(captain))`; it now follows the original.

The harness compares both bits. **3215 → 5583.**
