## Stage 1: the masks from the cells (the second session)

The second session opened by re-running the lane gate on `5553ce5d1`. It went red on a different
guard: `docs/MAPGEN.md has no unbuilt constants; delete its UNBUILT row`. The port of
`check_for_diagonal` carries MAPGEN's four pinned words (`0x1c0`, `0x801`, `0xe07`, `0xfc0`), which
the guard's own comment says a transitions port would bank. The row is deleted.

Read: `mark_nodes`, `mark_trans_flags`, `check_for_tendrils`, `neighbor_count`, `set_edge_x`,
`set_edge_y`, `create_nodes`, `generate_transitions`, `ArrayBase<int>::add_unique`,
`Terrain::spot_update_land` and `spot_update_land_batch`. The rule is in RENDERER §3.8, step 2.
Three findings change what the draft assumed:

- `add_unique` returns −1 for a cell already queued, so only the cell's *first* lower orthogonal
  side marks.
- Setting the block field clears the same bits of the connect field.
- `neighbor_count`'s result is dropped.

The edge diff, ours marked on the packet's cells against the packet's, every allocated edge:

| step | x edges | y edges |
|---|---|---|
| first reading (diagonal-only case writes two `edge_mask_y` words) | 3,266 of 3,600 | 3,034 of 3,600 |

| every allocated edge (61 × 60 each) | **3,402 of 3,660** | **3,375 of 3,660** |

The arrays are `(w + 1)·h` and `(h + 1)·w`. The packet's row of y edges at x = 60 reads past the
allocation (values such as `7667736`).

The value diff of the first parting edges (axis, ours → theirs, hex): (50, 5) x `139` → `039`;
(51, 5) y `000` → `038`; (53, 5) x `1c0` → `038`; (48, 6) y `178` → `078`; (51, 6) x `139` → `119`.
Marked from our own cells instead, 603 edges part. By Chebyshev distance to the nearest moved cell
that is 50 at 0, 163 at 1 and 60 at 2. The rest are mostly whole blocks of bare `038`, for example
x 4–9, y 24–30, far from any moved cell.

That is `spot_update_land`'s shape. It sets each updated cell's four edges to `0x38`, empties its
patch and re-runs `mark_trans_flags`. The queue is never cleared, so a cell already queued skips its
sides. A region updated with no net change keeps bare connect words. The history is not on the
packet.

**The score, on our masks from the packet's cells:** `[1512, 1512, 113, 1185]` of
`[1512, 1512, 113, 1185]`, with nothing parting. The three cells that part on the packet's own

way: the patches were laid on fresh masks, and the masks went stale afterwards.
