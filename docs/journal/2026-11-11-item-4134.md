## Stage 2: the pick, on the packet's masks

Read: `build_trans_flags`, `check_blocks`, `check_connects`, `check_fills`, `check_for_diagonal`,
the corner closing in `create_transition_renderables`, `Transitions::find_map`, `Transitions::init`'s
eight orientations, `TileSet::is_combo_acceptable`, `mark_nodes` and `create_nodes`. Of
`spot_update_land`, only the mask reset and the patch emptying were read. One reading went to the
listing: `check_connects` really does `and 0xe00; not; or`, a whole-word complement, and it is ported
as such.

The first run gave 1,509 matching alphas, but 406 cells were picked that the packet leaves bare. The
queue was missing: `create_transition_renderables` visits only the cells `mark_trans_flags` added, those
with a neighbour one step below. With the queue added:

| drawn patch, our alpha | **1,509** of 1,512 |
| emptied patch, ours none | **113** of 113 |
| no patch, ours none | **1,185** of 1,185 |
| drawn patch, another alpha | 3 |

Alpha 13 is the third of three candidates, and frac 21 and 37 pick index 0. No reading of the pick
reaches it on these masks.

The hypothesis is that these patches were laid under earlier masks. `spot_update_land` resets the masks
of the cells around a land change to `0x38` and re-marks them; a neighbour outside that list keeps the
patch it had. This is not confirmed (parked 4134a).

**The 113** are exactly that emptying: counts, bucket, `landindex` and texture zeroed by `spot_update_land`
on a cell that was not re-queued.

## Stage 3: the texcoords

`create_transition_renderables`' quadrant is `⌊frac · 0.04⌋` in single precision. `rotate_tex_coords`
takes its turn from the orientation: 2, 3, 0, 1, 2, 1, 0, 3. The decompile hides the input point,
so I read the listing.

- The input is the cell's `trans_texcoords0`: `(u, 1 − v)` in four blocks of the switch, and `(1 − u, 1 − v)`
  in the other four.
- The jump table at `0x8638f0` sends cases 0–3 to the `(1 − u, 1 − v)` blocks and cases 4–7 to the others.
- `0xb69514` reads 1.0 and `0xb694c0` reads 0.5.

The counts, in order:

| texcoord rule | exact of 1,509 |
|---|---|
| input `(u, v)` | 350 |
| the flips, by the jump table | 799 |
| the turn's sign, `(u, v) → (v, 1 − u)` | **1,509**, all 25 vertices |

The sign was taken from the packet; `Transform::rotate_z` and `Point::rotate` are not read.
