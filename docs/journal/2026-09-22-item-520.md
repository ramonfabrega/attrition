## The fix, judged out of reach

The Merchant offer needs three things this item did not read.

- **The writer of `reg_known_rares`.** `Leader::plan_strategy@006b9620`
  counts each of the leader's rares into its region through a per-tile
  explored-bit test, territory ownership and a treaty check.
- **The offer's value.** 909,090, 227,272 and 869,565 are 1,000,000
  divided by 1.1, 4.4 and 1.15. This crate's arm would say a flat
  1,000,000.
- **Why the re-offer empties the old slot.**

The offer has been missing since block 8181, so adding it changes every
rotation after that. It is a mechanic, not a patch. run123 prints
`known_rares` and `reg_known_rares[scan]` on all 700 blocks, so the
successor's oracle is already on disk.
