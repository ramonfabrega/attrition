## What was built

- **`render::silhouette`** (new): `rasterize`, a CPU triangle rasterizer into a buffer of object keys. It uses the
  client shader's `to_screen_matrix`, the divide by `w`, the viewport, and the depth test (`render_matrix`'s third
  column, less wins). `footprints` and `Ground` read the original's footprint off a photograph by the ring's colours;
  `READING` holds the reading constants and `score` counts the objects that reach the threshold. Unit tests:
  `a_silhouette_agrees_where_the_photograph_has_it_and_not_elsewhere`, which shifts the photograph's object by one
  window and sees the overlap fall from 1.0 to under 0.05, and `the_nearer_object_takes_the_pixel`.
- **`client::silhouette`** (new): `INTERFACE` (the photograph's eleven interface rectangles, by eye),
  `score_frame`, `explain`, the pinned test, and the `--silhouette OUT.bmp` mode.
- **`client::world`**: `objects` returns each drawn object's placed triangles as a `Drawing`, and `figures` now
  builds its soup from them. The pixels drawn are the same.
- **Documents**: `docs/RENDERER.md` §5.3 amended in place with the first row. Parked 4038a–c under slot DV.
