## The mechanism, in the listing

`detect_unit_collision@00617060` sets `0x100000` at `006177f3`, reached
only by falling out of the nine-cell loop (`cmpl $0x9` / `jl`). A hard
candidate returns 1 from inside the loop. So the one-shot belongs to a
sweep that *ended* soft. This crate's `name_collider` set it on any
sweep that *saw* a soft candidate, and `1/37`'s sweep of 11804 saw a
group-mate on its way to `1/64`. COLLISION §4.3 had the loop exit right
and its gloss ("whatever the scan found") wrong. The gloss is struck
through in place, and its address `00617817` is corrected to `006177f3`.

The fix is one clause: `soft && hard.is_none()`.
