## What the takes found

- **`Console::execute_at_cursor`'s visibility arm.** run1044's first right-click on `1/6` (714) hit the Chariot,
  which was out of the selection's sight. The game answered "You are cheating--you can't really see this target!
  Moving instead." and issued a move to the target's spot: `process_move_to 3045 12306 2 …` on 717, type 2, at
  (15.9, 64.1) tiles, the Chariot's square. Twenty frames later the same click was an attack. The `@attack` lines
  never show this, since they skip the UI. The predicate is GOLDEN §144.3's "UI predicate" the `@` route cannot check.
- **The console's `select` and `camera` reach the screen a few frames late.** run1045's shot on 633, after `630
  select`/`camera`, still showed the start view and the Small City. Its shot on 645 showed the Fighter. So run1046's
  Small City at 620 was this lag, not a failed `select`. run1055 selected by a click anyway.
- **A first launch that dies before its window** (run1048, 18:28: `waitwin: riseofnations.exe died before its
  window`) archives nothing, and a relaunch of the same stanza ran clean.

## What went wrong, and what it cost

My first launch (16:27) refused run1044, run1045, run1048 and run1049 before launch. The install's `rontrace.dll` was
another tree's build (`b8d80339…`) and those stanzas keep `@` lines. The tool's refusal names the fix (`build.sh`).
My wait looked only at run1044's driver file, for its first shot, and missed the refusal: it grepped `refus` against
a log that prints `REFUSED`. So run1046 and run1047, with no `@` line, launched, waited at their first shot for a
click that never came, and ran unattended to frame 3000: 1 h 38 min of lane 1, while att-3437 waited behind it. The
commander caught it. `zsh tools/trace/build.sh ~/code/fun/attrition/game` rebuilt the DLL from this tree
(`d6ef5de1…`). The pool never loads that file (it builds its own per run), so att-3437 was not disturbed. The relaunch
ran each stanza in turn with a per-run wait that also breaks on the log's `REFUSED`, `no window` and summary lines.
Second-launch minutes waited on another lane: none. att-3437 held off its run1052 until my line at 19:00.

## For the Loop

A wait on a driven take keys on the run's own screenshot. If the take refuses, no screenshot ever comes, and the
queue moves on to the next stanza. That stanza then waits for a click no one sends, and runs to its `frames:` cap. A
driven queue-lane wait must also break on the runner's per-stanza verdicts: `REFUSED`, `no window`, `CAPTURE FAILED`
and the summary. Better still, `handdriver.sh` could end a game whose cmd file gets no line within N minutes. run1046
and run1047 cost 98 minutes of lane 1 to this.
