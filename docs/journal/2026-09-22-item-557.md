## The pool, and 11424

The pool dump said what the pointer could not. Group 66 is
`{60, 61, 64}` from block 11425: **the squad's tail alone**. `1/62` and
`1/63` point at 66 and are not in it. So the brief's "why 11420–11426 as
well?" has a plain answer. **554 was right to want it, and right about
what it would show**: the `come_out` push on 11423 (`Unit::come_out`,

the sort on 11512 has nothing to sort.

Reading `Group::add` against the list: it opens every step with
`get_num`, and `Group::get_num@00714700` is `normalize` whole for a group
of fewer than four. The squad's pointers still name 69, so each step's
normalize drops the figure the step before appended. GROUPS §4.1 had the
walk right and never mentioned the `get_num`. `Unit::set_group` then
climbs to the captain and points all three at 66.

On 11512, `action_stance` walks `{60, 61, 64, 65}`, which is exactly the
reach the dump shows. `Group::sort` then finds `64` under a captain that
is not the last one seen. It `kill`s it, clearing its pointer because
the pointer names 66, and re-`add`s it with `const 1`. With no `get_num`
this time, the squad comes back whole at the end, and no pointer is
written. `1/64`'s own turn then hits `do_group_move`'s first line,
`+0x80 == −1`, and ungroups. **(559)'s hypothesis, that the step of 12
was the group cap, is dead**: the step of 12 was this crate's
in-formation follower, and the original's `1/64` was never in formation.

## The fix

554 judged it out of reach, because it means modelling the pointer.
The changes:

- **`Unit::group_ptr`**, with the original's writers: `set_group`,
  `push_group`, the eviction, `kill`, and the follower's not-listed
  clear.
- **The pointer's readers**: `seat_of` → `group_of` and the cap,
  `army_of` as `Object::get_army` (pointer **and** list),
  `pool_group_of`, `pool_members`, `same_group_soft`, and the follower's
  "in my group" test.
- **`seat_add`/`seat_kill`/`seat_normalize`/`seat_sort`**, operating on a
  seated group's own list, so `army_add_unit` joins through the real
  `add`. `Group::sort` runs at `categorize`'s head in the layout, and
  a member the sort brings in is decided by the order loop.
- **`army_normalize` and `groups_process` prune by the pointer.**

Two things broke on the way, and both were the model being honest:

- The first test run failed two ungroup fixtures. They set `o_up` and
  not `combat.captain`, while I had walked `captain_of`. The original
  walks `+0x8e`/`+0x90` throughout, so `top_captain` does now, and the
  fresh-army path builds a stack group through `group_add`.
- run29's scene stands an army up from a dump and never seated a
  pointer. It now seats each unit's from the record's own `group`.
