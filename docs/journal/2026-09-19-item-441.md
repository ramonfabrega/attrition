# Item 441 — the widening, and the finding I had to withdraw

*2026-09-19, Opus 5, `worktree-att-365`. No capture; run113 still unspent.
No score moved. `docs/COMBAT.md` §29, a correction to §26 and
`docs/GOLDEN.md` §6, and a rewritten test.*

Four items chased a radius and the bracket did not move. `CLAUDE.md` says
what to do then, and the commander said it again: widen every record on
the frame, the whole cast, and name the cause before any mechanism. It
took one probe over `crate::diff::harness::compare` and it produced two
results, one of which is mine to take back.

## What the widening says

Frames 612–640, every unit of both real players, every field, both
directions:

- **612 to 615: nothing.** Not one divergence in any field.
- **616: three.** who=1's `1/6`, `1/7`, `1/8` — order list **ours 1,
  theirs 0**.
- **617: the same three at ours 2, theirs 0.**
- **618: nine** — the positions part and a path appears.
- **619 onward: nine a frame**, the same three, walking.

**who=0's nine units never diverge at all.** Nor does anything else the
dump carries. The entire divergence of chapter two is three units of one
squad, beginning exactly at the word.

## The finding I withdrew

Item 415 walked to 622, found who=1's squad 140 units west of the dump on
all three figures, and called it `find_nearby_spot`'s ring failing on
clear ground. I reported it as the best result of that item and the
commander booked it as the first suspect for 426.

The seating is **exact** at 616 and 617. The positions part from 618, and
from there this crate's squad walks west at 28 units a frame while the
original's never moves. 622 − 617 is five frames; 5 × 28 is 140.

The measurement was right and the mechanism was invented. That is the
third time on this frame — visibility, the remembered-target arm, and now
the ring — and all three have the same shape: **a number that fits,
explained before it was bracketed.** The difference is that this one I
turned into an assertion and shipped, and it sat in three documents for
four items.

The test is rewritten rather than deleted. It walks to 617 and asserts all
six units are on the dump's own cells; then it steps five frames and
asserts the drift is exactly `5 × 28` in x and zero in y. Both halves are
true, neither invents a cause, and the second one is the arithmetic that
killed the first.

## The cause, from the data

At 616 an `ATTACK`; at 617 a `MOVE_TO` pushed in front of it — the chase;
at 618 a path; then the march. The original's hoplites hold no order at
all through 634. **One order, and everything after it downstream** — the
second order, the path, the 140, the draw.

And it is narrower than any of the seven accounts §28.4 lists, because
every one of those asked *why the search found something*. The widening
does not establish that a search ran. Item 435 showed **635** is a search,
because `near_o` turns from −1 to 10 on that frame. On 616 `near_o` stays
−1 **on both sides** — so whatever produces this crate's order at 616 is
not the path that writes `near_o`.

That retires the question four items were answering. It is not "what
radius reaches 7.25 tiles"; it is "which producer of an attack order runs
on a cheat-spawned captain's birth frame and does not write `near_o`".
The radius arithmetic in §26–§28 is not wrong, but it rests on 616 being a
search, and §29.3 now doubts that.

## What I would do differently

The commander's brief said the radius may not be the cause and that four
items had assumed the order comes from the search. That was right, and I
could have checked it at any point in those four items with the grep I
finally ran: `near_o` on 616, on both sides. It costs one line. The rule
that would have caught it is the one already in `CLAUDE.md` — widen the
record before booking a mechanism — and I booked four mechanisms first.
