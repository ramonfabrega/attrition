## What landed: the two trunks joined

`diff/harness/trunk.rs` alone (test-only). A trunk's key is `Root`: the frame-0 print less the guys of
frames no word is installed on, `sim.roster.art.lengths` and the notes, with the count of the stand-up's
notes beside it. A rung is a `Rung` (the state and its stand-up's note count), and `Rung::carry` gives a
taker of the other stand-up its own guys, lengths and stand-up notes, the rung's ticked notes after them.
`RON_TRUNK_LOG` gains a sixth column (joined) and the resume line says "(the game's other stand-up)". One
new unit test, `the_game_s_other_stand_up_rides_the_trunk_and_carries_its_own_fields`, which also holds
that guys on a seeded frame stay in the key. `docs/TEST_PERFORMANCE.md`, "The group unit": the "two
trunks" paragraph amended in place.

**Why the three fields**: `Built::tick` reads `frame_guys` only inside the word's install
(`setup.rs`, the `frame_seeds` branch), so the guys of an unseeded frame are never read; the notes are
read by no tick (two tests read them); the lengths are written only by `build_sim` and **read by a tick**
(`anim.rs`, `Art::length`). So the join is the verifier's, not construction's: a length one stand-up holds
and the other does not, read before the rung's frame, turns it red.

**Seen red**: with `Rung::carry` keeping the rung's lengths instead of the taker's, the new test fails
under the verifier: `the trunk's rung on 512 did not walk to the state a fresh walk reaches on 600`.
