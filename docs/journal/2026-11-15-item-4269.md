not the land's: `calculate_norms`' normal at the same corner is a mean of 0.25 away. They are
`RenderHelper::generate_normals@00847f90`'s:
- for each face `(a, b, c)`, the cross product of the unit edges `(b − a) × (c − a)` is added to all three

- a face is weighted by its angle at `a`;
- per tile the packet's faces are `(a, b, d)` then `(d, b, c)`.
