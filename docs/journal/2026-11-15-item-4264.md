## What the reading found

- **The button**: `Options::do_spell@0071d7a0` checks `can_activate` and `get_spellcaster`. For To Arms it also
  checks `has_tech(MILITIA)`, then `get_valid_spellcaster`. An untargeted craft (`spell_flags & 0xe` clear) is issued
  at once as `target_spell(t, −1, −1, 0, 0)`; a targeted one sets `selecting_spot`.
- **The pick**: `Options::picked_spot@00721c40`'s `OPTION_SPELL` arm issues `target_spell(t, −1, −1, x, y)` for a
  ground craft (`& 8`). Otherwise it is `SpellTypeData::find_target`'s `(ox, whom)` at `(x, y)`, the pressed point.
  Then `target_spell@0071dda0` → `validate_spell` → `CommandManager::issue_spell@00941b80`.
- **The recordings**: 17, all the 2017 sample's, slot 3's. Six are Forced March (`0x27c`) at `(−1, −1, 0, 0)`: the
  button's. Eleven are targeted at (0, 0): Bribe ×5, Informer on a building, Sabotage ×2, Sniper ×3. The pick writes
  the point, so these come from the right press (`Console::execute_at_cursor@007c6630`'s `GroupOut::issue_spell`
  with (0, 0)).
- **The empty groups**: `CommandPackage::add_group@0094bb60` writes a count of zero when `who`, the objects and
  their uids equal the last group sent. Thirteen of the 17 spells carry one. This answers 4261a.
- **The minted game's slot 0** (a scratch probe on our walk of the city-gathered file, not kept): six Citizens, a
  Scout and three Slingers, and the same 18 techs from 6720 to 12,000. Its buildings are nine, and none of them
  researches Militia (`group_queue_up(0x42)` answers 0 at each). To Arms on a Citizen left it a Citizen at every
  200-frame step to 8000.
