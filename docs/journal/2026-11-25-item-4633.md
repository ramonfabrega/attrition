## The reading

`Unit::same_damage@005f9400` (export, read whole) is a squad walk. This crate's version was a one-line form under its
own SEAM ("the original walks both squads and spreads the damage figure by figure"):

- The receiver climbs from `this` down `o_down` while the next figure's `+8 & 1` holds: the squad's last live figure.
- It takes two fractions, each `total_damage << 8` over hits (vslot `+0x11c(0)`, `myhits` at `+0x20` on a unit): the
  receiver's own squad and the host's. It keeps the larger.
- Owed = (receiver's hits × fraction, signed `>> 8`) − the receiver squad's `total_damage`, floored at 0.
- The walk starts at the tail and steps up through `+0x8e` (`o_up`, read at `this_00[1].field_0x3e`, `Object` being 0x50
  bytes). A member owed less than `hits / uber_size` has that written as its `damage` (`+0x24`). A member owed at least
  that dies (vslot `+0x158`, `Object::die(f, 0, −1, 0)`) and passes the remainder on. The captain is written `min(owed,
  hits − total_damage − 1)`.

`UnitData::total_damage@00609c00` (read whole) climbs to the captain. It sums `+0x24` down the live `o_down` chain, adds
`(uber_size − curr_uber_size(1)) × hits / uber_size` for lost figures, and adds the chain's summed `+0x3b` sixteenths
`>> 4` once they pass 15.

## The build

`Sim::same_damage(to, from)` is that walk, and `Sim::total_damage(u)` is the sum. In this crate a figure's `damage` is
`max_health − health` and its sixteenths are `damage_frac`; `curr_uber_size` and `die_quietly` already existed. Both
directions use it: unloading (`eject_riders`, `boat_same_damage`) and boarding (`cast_transport`). On boarding, the boat
now takes the whole squad's damage rather than the captain's alone.
