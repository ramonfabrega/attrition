## The readings, and what would kill each

These were written before ours' values were read.

- **R1: the posts go out in birth order, and the births come in a
  different order.** Killed if the three are born in the same order on
  both sides. `action_guard` walks `this->list` (bound: `this->num`,
  `field_0xc`, 4 here) and appends through `Group::add@00714350`. That
  function only appends; it never sorts. So birth order reaches the
  slots only through the group's list, and R1 becomes R3.
- **R2: the slot list itself is in a different order.** Killed if ours'
  `slots.off`, read by index, is the original's `(0, 264), (−144, 264),
  (+144, 264)` for local indices 0, 1, 2. It would first fire in
  `form_dests` on 15094 (the guard is issued on that frame and logged in
  block 15095).
- **R3: the walk is over a group whose member order differs.** Killed if
  group 67's list agrees on 15094. The widening already compares every
  player-1 pool list in order, every block from 11800 (`pool:67`). No
  `pool:` row first-parts anywhere under the word. **Killed by the
  pinned widening** before any reading. Ours' local list will be read
  anyway, as a check.
- **R4 (mine): the mirror.** `Group::compute_form@00707c80` toggles the
  group's `facing` when the leader's heading, less its slot byte, is more
  than 90° off the formation angle. For a guard that angle is 0.
  `Form::compute` negates every `x` under `facing`. A mirror flips
  exactly one sign: the `dx` of the two flanks. Killed if ours' `reverse`
  equals the original's. The original's leader is `1/76` (first captain
  of the lowest category, `GroupData::find_leader@0070ccb0`), heading
  0x55555555, with a cleared slot byte (`Group::clear@00713e80` zeroes
  `+0x48`). 0x55555555 lies in (0x3fffffff, 0xc0000001), so the
  original's `facing` is **1**. The semaphore bit (`game +…[1] & 8`)
  that zeroes it is a seam this reading does not settle.

## The cause: the target's own mirror

`Unit::do_guard@005e5c70` negates `dx` when its **target** carries
`unit_masks & 2` (`5e5fed`–`5e5ff6`). `docs/ORDERS.md` §24 had named
this as a seam: "not carried — run133's wagon has it clear". The dump
prints the Despot's `unit_masks` as 0x4000A, so here it is set. Mirrored,
the arithmetic gives the original's three posts to the unit, including
`1/78`'s 22392, which comes from the heading's `sinx` term.

The decompiler shows no writer of bit 2. `llvm-objdump` finds two, and
three readers:

- **Writers**:
  - `Unit::set_angle@00605400` (`xorl $0x2, 0x68(%esi)` at `605424`)
    flips it on a `reversing` turn. That happens for every unit, before
    the leader test that flips the group's `facing`.
  - `Unit::kill_current_order@005e2cb0` sets or clears it (`5e3087`,
    `5e308d`) with the value it hands the group, again before the leader
    test.
- **Readers**: `do_guard`, `Unit::set_new_location` (`5f9290`, a crew
  offset pair) and `Guy::set_anim` (`5dafad`).

The Despot's history in run192 is the first writer's. It is born
0x40000 on 14983. On 14985 its angle goes 0x55555555 → −756678656, a
reversing turn, and the mask becomes 0x4000E.

**Built**: `Movement::mirror`. Every port of `set_angle` flips it
(`unit_set_angle`, `set_heading`, `set_facing`), `hand_back_facing`
writes it, and `do_guard` reads it (`docs/GROUPS.md` §25). Two unit
tests use the original's numbers:

- `a_guard_s_offset_is_mirrored_by_its_target_s_own_flag` takes run196's
  Despot and gives the three posts of block 15095. Unmirrored, the flanks
  trade.
- `a_unit_s_own_mirror_flips_on_a_reversing_turn_leader_or_not` takes the
  Despot's 14985 turn and both writers.
