change was not needed, and I spent the door's change on two other things, each found by a probe against the dump.

1. **A figure's height.** `Scene` carried only `UnitScene::z`, the tile height under the unit. Measured against
   3,842 dumped figures:

   RENDERER §7.1's open line. `units` now holds only living units.
