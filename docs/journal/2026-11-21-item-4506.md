age 208, velocity (0.16203, 0.16203, 0.12963), which is 0.208 · 0.779 · (1, 1, 0.8)·10⁻³·10³. That is one
`pass_time` call over the particle's whole age. `pass_time@008db470` applies `drag` once per call. Both the run's
spacing and the overshoot it leaves behind are `back_calc`'s:
