## The rule, fixed before the first count

- **The footprint** comes from geometry and never from a colour. A pixel is in it when its centre lies in a
  triangle of `World::border_meshes`, laid with the owner grid unmoved. Each triangle is projected through the
  still's own `to_screen_matrix` exactly as `vs_patch` does. The strip's vertex alpha, interpolated
  perspective-correct, must also pass the card's alpha test: above 4 of 255, from draws 135 and 136 of run1567's
  card pass (item 4379). Every arm uses the same footprint. The perturbed arm is the moved one, and its geometry
  is not used for the footprint.
- **Near:** `|Δr| + |Δg| + |Δb| ≤ 30` against the photograph, ten sRGB steps a channel.
