## 0. The route

- **Wine's `+d3d` is blind here.** wined3d gets no GL context from `winemac.drv` (`docs/ORACLE.md`, "Off
  CrossOver"), so a take without DXVK draws nothing.
- **So the route is the engine's own setters.** The executable never calls Direct3D. It calls `graphsys`, which is
  `d3dgl.dll`'s `D3D11GLSys`. That DLL turns the fixed-function state into a shader permutation and four constant
  buffers, and its PDB and map ship in `sbl/`.
- **The packet on disk was grepped first.** It holds `render_states[]` and the team colours, but no draw's options,
  stages or buffers.
- **The probe** is `tools/trace/card.h` (`RON_CARD_PROBE`). At the first `do_frame` it asks the loader for d3dgl's
  base, checks each prologue against the DLL's listing, and hooks these sites:
  - `D3D11GLSys::render`, indexed (DLL `0x10023ac0`) and plain (`0x10023d30`);
  - `begin_drawing` (`0x10020f10`) and `end_drawing` (`0x10022f40`), which bound a pass;
  - `D3D11GLGraphicCard::get_internal_stream` (`0x1002c970`) and `release_internal_stream` (`0x1002caa0`), where the
    streamed vertices are whole.
- **What each draw writes:**
  - `GraphSys::current_state` (`+0x190`, the `RenderState`);
  - `D3D11Context`'s `PerScene`, `PerObject`, `PerMaterial` and `LightData` (`+0x4f0 +0x70`);
  - `mShaderOptions` (`+0x390`, `ShaderCompilationOptions`);
  - the card's arrays;
  - three of the executable's return addresses;
  - each stage's texture name, through `D3D11GLTexture +0x18 → +0x10 → {chars, length}`.

  The layouts are `sbl/d3dgl.pdb`'s type records (`llvm-pdbutil dump --types`).
- **run1566** read the names one dereference short (a `String` object, length 62), and the arguments off a copy.
  **run1566b** fixed both: every draw's arguments are read where the call left them (`args_checked`, asserted). It
  also added the streams and the frames.

**The pass is the photograph's frame, and 4073a's reason is found.** Under the take's `!ffwd` the original draws
one frame in sixteen. The window 3260–3300 holds exactly two passes, drawn after 3263 and 3279, and the take pauses
on 3294 with 3279's picture on screen.

## 2. The colour, and the figure that parts

The install's `ps_global.hlsl` lit branch was read again against these buffers; nothing of it is copied here.

- The ambient is five times the material's times the scene's. The light's own ambient does not enter.
- The diffuse is 5/π times the material's diffuse, the light's diffuse and N·L.
- The specular is the material's, 0.
- The texture stage's result is saturated, then raised to the power 1.45.

**One blend no draw of ours makes (4165b).** Stage 1's alpha is `MODULATE4X(TEXTURE, TFACTOR)` on the same texture,
and blending is on (`enables` 0x287, `SRCALPHA`/`INVSRCALPHA`). So a lit pixel is `A·C + (1 − A)·behind`, where
`A = min(1, 4·α·a)`. That touches 319 of the scored figures' 23,316 own pixels. Most are on 0/0, whose tfactor alpha
