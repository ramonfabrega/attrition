## The port

`sim::mapgen` gains:
- `Regions`, with `clear_all`, `find_all`, `find` and the overflow eviction;
- `MapGrid::fix_diag_land`, `fix_lakes` and `make_coastlines`;
- `Fractal::init`'s byte grid, `fertile_partitions` and `MapGrid::fill_fertile`.

`rondata::mapgen` gains `StartGrid::seed` (the `WORLD` block's `seed`) and `fertility`, which reads the
tileset's `CLUMP_FACTOR`, its `BASE` count and the `TILESET_DATA` frequencies from the install. A scratch Python
prototype of the `Fractal` and the fertility map hit 969,480,808 before any Rust was written. The Rust then
agreed on all **105 Great Sahara files at all four checkpoints**, first try.

Three things the reading fixed that §6's prose had wrong or left open:
- **The first sea region is 0x41, not 0x40.** `clear_all` sets the counter to 0x40, and `find_all` increments
  it before using it.
- **The adoption arm.** Before opening a region, an unlabelled cell takes the label of its first neighbour with
  the same wetness.
- **The `Fractal` takes no float.** It is an integer midpoint displacement on its own `Random`, seeded with
  `World.seed` (7236). Its doubles feed only `get_height`.

The partitions' `(int)(f/100f·255f)` has a test, and its killer fired on the first sweep. The single-precision
product equals `f·255/100` only for |f| < 6,420, and parts at ±6,420. The test now states that bound
(parked 3798d).

## A second check the booking did not name: every start grid's labels

Great Sahara's 7835 and 7846 grids are uniform (every cell is region 1), so they never exercise label order,
sea numbering or eviction. A start dump's `region` is the 7937 pair's, so `find_all_relabels_every_start_grid`
clears it and relabels the dumped cells. **494 of 496** come back whole across five styles (GS 105, GL 185,
EI 202, styles 9 and 22). The two that part are the fuzz lobby's East Indies, with land regions 1 and 2
swapped on 106 cells. (31, 2)..(33, 2) carry HALFLAND, so they were water at 7937, and their `region` is
`Terrain::mark_halfwater`'s later write. Clearing 0x100 just moves the swap to (31, 3), because the dump cannot
say which half-land cells were land. Those two files are named (`HALF_LAND_REORDERS`, parked 3798b); any other
file that parts fails.

## What no diff reaches
