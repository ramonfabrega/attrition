## The route

The brief named two routes, and I took the cheaper one: a tracer line. The tracer had no `@sell`, so I added two
leader verbs, `@sell <who> <good> <flags>` and `@buy …`, in `tools/trace/tracer.c`.

The Market's buttons call `LeaderOut::issue_sell@006d3eb0` and `issue_buy@006d3ef0` on
`leaders.list + console->who`, with the good and the flags (`Options::exec@007188c0:1149`, `:1162`). The new verbs
make that same call. The leader pointer is at `0xc061e0`, the stride is `0x6eec`, and `who` sits at +8. All three are
off the listing at `719f8a`–`719fce` and `946988`–`946993`.

Calling the leader issuer rather than `CommandManager::issue_sell` directly means both issuers are entered.
`LeaderOut::issue_sell` is the button's own call, so neither issuer goes to `ENTERED_BY_THE_DLL_ONLY`. The difference
from `issue_stance` (3424) is that the image references both (`report.py refs` is not needed: the listing's `call
0x6d3eb0` at `719fce` and `call 0x942a80` at `6d3ee1` are the references).

The verbs live outside the group verbs. They take no group, so they have their own path, `market_line`, ahead of
`issue_line`'s verb table. That leaves `ISSUER_RVA` and `ISSUE_VERBS` alone, which 3425 is extending. The prologue
guard is the shared `issuer_is_shipped`, fed eleven bytes off the listing: `a1 e8 61 c0 00 56 8b f1 f6 80 20`, the
same in both issuers. Under `cover=1` the five-byte `mov eax, [0xc061e8]` is the displaced instruction.
