## Step 4: the cheap piles

**`light`**: writers by both nets. The export's `->light` names `WData::WData` (`this->light = '\0'`)
and `WData::log_data` (a read). The listing's register-relative byte stores at `+0xe` number 29 in the
whole image: of those in a `WData` context, `WData::WData` (`movb $0x0, 0xe(%ecx)`) and `World::wipe`
(`movb $0x0, 0xe(%edx)`), both zero; `TerrainOut::caravan_step`'s two `orb` at `+0xe` are into the
terrain's render cell (`+0x6ab0`, a 16-byte stride), not a `WData`. The third pass, every store whose
bytes cover `+0xe` in the `idx · 0x1c` form (`k(%reg,%reg,4)` after `lea (,%eax,8); sub`), finds none
in a `WData` context (the fifteen are `VoidArray`, `Leader::gain_tech`, `Options::add`, `TerrainOut`'s
normals, `WorldMap::add_blip` and drawing code). So `light` is 0 on every cell once the world is
wiped: 3,600 cells 0 on run 1562's frame and on run1100's. It agreed by default before it was read.

**`danger` rows 2–7**: `World::init` mallocs eight rows of `reg_size` ints and `memset`s each to 0;
`World::wipe` zeroes them again. `GameDaemon::calc_danger` opens by clearing each row whose leader has
`leader_flags & 2`, and its `do_danger` loop adds to a viewer's row only for a viewer with `& 2`. An
unseated slot holds `0x2000000` alone (§20), so its row is `World::init`'s zeros for the whole game.
The crate's rows exist only for its seated viewers; the emitter writes zeros for a slot past
`territory.players`. Run1100's rows 3–7 (three seated) are zero too. It agreed by default before it
was read.

**`oil_x`, `oil_y`**: the export names them only in `World::walk_data`, `World::init`,
`World::close` and `WorldData::log_data`; `init` and `close` free the list and zero `num`, `size` and
the flags. The listing's references at `+0xfc..+0x134` in the world's classes (`wd4131.py`) are the
same two and the walker; the absolute addresses (`0xc098e4`, `0xc09900`) only `WorldData`'s
constructor and destructor. So each is its zero count, on both frames.

**`nuke_hits`**: the export's `->nuke_hits` names only `TerrainData`'s constructor and destructor; the
listing's references to its offsets in any class's frame (`hl4131.py`, and a scan of every function)
are those two, `TerrainOut::close` (empties), `TerrainOut::rebuild_nuke_craters` (reads the list; it
is reached from `WalkDataGame::walk_data`, a restore) and the two walkers. No store appends. Its zero
count.

**The start arrays**: the listing's references at `+0x80..+0xf0` in the world's classes: map
generation's `World::add_starting_location` (`leal 0x80`) and `Map::place_start_in_region`, the
empties in `World::init`, `close` and `wipe`, and readers (`Setup::build_cities`, `build_units`). The
composed chain's `MapGrid::starts` is what the built start already reads for `build_cities`; it is now
kept on `World::record` (`crates/sim/src/world.rs`), set in `start::built_from_lobby`'s map arm. It
agreed on its first emission: (55, 4), (21, 40), (55, 54, 55, 54, 4, 3, 4, 3), (21, 21, 20, 20, 40, 40,
39, 39), size 4 and 8, increment −1, flags 0.

**The half-land lists**: the listing's references to the four lists (`TerrainData +0x4b40..`,
`TerrainOut +0x4b80..`): `TerrainData`'s constructor and destructor, `TerrainOut::close`, and
`Terrain::mark_halfwater`'s two `leal`s (its appends). The export: `mark_halfwater` is reached from
`check_and_set_halfland_wcoord` alone, which is reached from `Terrain::check_and_set_halfland`
(`Terrain::init`) and the scenario editor. The composed chain already computed them at 720 and handed
only their sums; `LakesMap::halfland` now keeps them, and `World::record` carries them. They agree on
frame 91, 692 coordinates, types and subtypes each, size 1,024 — through the game's first 91 frames.

**`map`, `seed`**: `MAP_STYLE` 14 and the chain's `game_seed` 7,236 (`Setup::build_game`'s re-seed,
`docs/START.md` §2 step 3), on `World::record`. Run1100's frame holds 14 and 7,236 too.
