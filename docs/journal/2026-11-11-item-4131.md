## Step 4: the cheap piles

**`light`**: writers by both nets. The export's `->light` names `WData::WData` (`this->light = '\0'`)
and `WData::log_data` (a read). The listing's register-relative byte stores at `+0xe` number 29 in the
whole image: of those in a `WData` context, `WData::WData` (`movb $0x0, 0xe(%ecx)`) and `World::wipe`
(`movb $0x0, 0xe(%edx)`), both zero; `TerrainOut::caravan_step`'s two `orb` at `+0xe` are into the
terrain's render cell (`+0x6ab0`, a 16-byte stride), not a `WData`. The third pass, every store whose
bytes cover `+0xe` in the `idx · 0x1c` form (`k(%reg,%reg,4)` after `lea (,%eax,8); sub`), finds none
in a `WData` context (the fifteen are `VoidArray`, `Leader::gain_tech`, `Options::add`, `TerrainOut`'s
normals, `WorldMap::add_blip` and drawing code). So `light` is 0 on every cell once the world is
wiped: 3,600 cells 0 on run 1562's frame and on run1100's. It agreed by default before it was read.
