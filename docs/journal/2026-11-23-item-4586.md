- It selects cells with `WData.flags & 0x50` (`cell::MOUNTAIN | cell::CLIFF`).
- It lays each one again through `TerrainOut::get_indexed_mesh`, 25 vertices, all `0x00ffffff`.
- For each of the 16 tiles whose `TData.mask & 3` is 2 or 1 (`tile::OBJECT_MOUNTAIN`, `OBJECT_CLIFF`), it writes
  `0x77000000 | mountain_minimap_color` to that tile's four corners (vertex index, +1, +5, +6).

- `src_blend` `SRCALPHA`, `dst_blend` `ONE`.

added. `<MOUNTAINS minimap_color>` on `early_autumn` is (133, 127, 105) (`artdata::minimap_colors`, already pinned).

linearly along a diagonal that is not read. Recorded in §3.32.
