## What the reading found

`docs/ORDERS.md` §4.4 step 2 already named the arm. This crate's
`do_move` carried a SEAM line that called it dormant: "a `GATHER` action
within `vector_dist < 0x120` parks the unit". The decompile of
`Unit::do_move@005f7b30` gives the cadence, `(elapsed + 2) & 0x80000007 ==
0`. It prints the distance as `vector_dist(unaff_EDI, unaff_ESI)`, the
fastcall trap, so I read the listing. The pair is `|mo.x − x|` and
`|mo.y − y|`, the **move order's** point against the unit's decoded
position. `avoid_x/y` takes the move's point, not the unit's, which
ORDERS had written wrong and is amended. For `1/43`: elapsed 6, and
`vector_dist(96, 240) = 259 < 288`.
