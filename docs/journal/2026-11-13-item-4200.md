## 2. The water's tone: what the evidence killed

The card's water and floor draws were read on run1567's trace (pass after 3279).

**The water draw (162):**
- view and projection: orthographic, `w` 10,429.4;
- water material: ambient 0.2, diffuse (1, 1, 1.5), **specular 40**, roughness 0.3;
- its light: diffuse 1, direction (−0.707, 0, −0.707).

**The floor draw (36):** white material, light diffuse 1.36, ambient material 1.

**Specular, killed.** The water's normal is (0, 0, 1). In view space it is (0, 0.866, −0.5), and L is (0.5, 0.362, −0.787).
At the screen's centre V is (0, 0, −1), so N·H = 0.639. Beckmann's D at roughness 0.3 is then 2.4e-6, and
Fresnel is 0.021. The specular is about 7e-6 HDR at `gSpecular` 40. V varies by ±12° across the screen, which is
far from the 27° the specular would need.

**The screen reflection, killed in the deep.** The reflected ray in view space is (0, 0.866, 0.5).
- Each 10-unit step brings it 10 view-z nearer the camera than the water at the same pixel.
- The threshold is 0.00075 × 10,429.4 / 0.35148 = 22.3 view units.
- A bottom h below the water lies 2h behind it in view z.
- So a hit needs h ≤ 6 world units.

**The floor's texture, killed.** `corr2.py` high-passes both images (σ 2 minus σ 30). The photograph against our
still, over 33,178 pixels of our floor polygon, correlates at |r| ≤ 0.063 at zero shift. The best over ±60 px is
0.083 at (−54, 48).

**Read off the packet** (`coral_probe.py`, scratch): `scene_coral` (terrain `+0x1870`) holds 4,928 vertices. Their
positions, texcoords and colours equal the sand layer's 4,928, rgb 252, alpha 0–255, mean 122.4. Draw 131's
stage-0 alpha is `SELECTARG2(TEXTURE, DIFFUSE | COMPLEMENT)`.

**The tone's shape, measured and not drawn.**
- Lit water over the coral in HDR, toned at the clamp, gives the deep water (10, 133, 191).
- The photograph reads (47, 113, 158) on `OCEAN` and (49, 111, 155) on the deep sand.
- Desaturated at 0.6, the model gives (47, 121, 156).
- `run1564-tonemap.txt` (item 4168) holds `saturation 0x3f800000` and `cb_saturation 0x3f800000`, both 1.0. So this
  names no mechanism. The red is open, and nothing is fitted.
