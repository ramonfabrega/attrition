## 1. The tone mapper, off the packets (§3.14)

- **The disk was grepped first.** 4114 had written "the packet does not hold the DLL's data", because the card's
  vtable lies outside the image. That is true of `d3dgl.dll`'s image, but the packet keeps the heap. The
  executable's `graphsys` (`0x00e335a0`, `sbl/rise_z.map`) points at a heap `D3D11GLSys`. Its `mToneMapper`
  (+4976) and `mAntiAlias` (+4980) are heap objects too (`d3dgl.pdb`, field list `0x71DE`).
- **Typed by their vtables.** On both packets the tone mapper's vtable is `0x78634fc4` and the anti-alias's is
  `0x786344ac`. Those are `??_7D3D11ToneMapper@@6B@` (`0x100e4fc4`) and `??_7D3D11AntiAlias@@6B@` (`0x100e44ac`)
  under one relocation, `0x68550000`. `tools/render/tonemap_packet.py` refuses a packet where the two relocations
  differ.
- **The value diff**, frame 3294 on run1559b's and run1564's packets, identical on both. Each value is the
  member's, then the constant buffer's shadow (`D3D11ToneMapper::mData`, `ToneMapData`), then ours:

| field | `GSToneMapper` | `mData` | ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| exposure (+ adjustment) | `0x3f800000` 1.0 (+0) | 1.0 | `EXPOSURE` 1.0 |
| white point | `0x40600000` 3.5 | 3.5 | `WHITE_POINT` 3.5 |
| minimum luminance | `0x3fb33333` 1.4 | 1.4 | `MIN_LUMINANCE` 1.4 |
| saturation | 1.0 | 1.0 | `SATURATION` 1.0 (new) |
| threshold | `0x3f19999a` 0.6 | 0.6 | `BLOOM_THRESHOLD` (new) |
| tightness | `0x41480000` 12.5 | 12.5 | `BLOOM_TIGHTNESS` (new) |
| strength | `0x40c00000` 6.0 | 6.0 | `BLOOM_STRENGTH` (new) |
| tint | bytes (238, 193, 14, 0) | (0.933, 0.757, 0.055, **0**) | `TINT_ALPHA` 0 (new) |
| `mDirty` | 0 | | |
| `mAAEnabled` | 1 | | FXAA not drawn |

- **One correction to 4114's reading.** The tint's rgb is set to (238, 193, 14), not 0. Its alpha is 0, and at
  alpha 0 the shader's lerp is the identity, so nothing changes.
- `render::silhouette::tests::the_tone_mapper_on_both_packets` asserts every row bit for bit. I flipped
  `BLOOM_STRENGTH` to 6.5 once: the test went red (exit 101, `run1559b: bloom_strength is 0x40c00000/6.0 against
  6.5`), and I restored it.

**Why the row cannot move through them.** The reader scores chromaticity. A tint at alpha 0 and a saturation of 1
are identities, and the exposure and white point are the candidate's. Only the frame's mean is left: it is the
luminance target's mip 10, which lives on the card and is in no packet. It scales every channel alike, so it moves a
hue only where a channel clips at 255.
