## What was booked, and what it turned out to be

The booking's hypothesis: the door carries `orig_type`, `rondata` resolves the piece through the loader's name,
and the building is anchored at its position, "a TGA, not a mesh at its point". Three parts held, one did not:

- **The anchor is not the point.** `WallOut::draw@00641930` projects `x_internal − 0x60·h`, `y_internal − 0x60·h`,
  `z_internal` with `w = h = −1` (the listing, `00642033`–`0064207d`: the decompiler printed the arguments in the
  wrong order; `SubObjectData` keeps `z` at `+0xc`, `x` at `+0x10`, `y` at `+0x14`). `h` is
  `BuildRDataStruct::build_half_tcoord`, which `init_build_data@008f2f20` reads from the `<BUILD>`'s attribute
  at `int_str_array + 0x12d18`. That is `internal_strings.xml`'s `halfoffset`, between `spriteyoff` and
  `Billboard`, and its neighbour `constructsize` is the `build_construction` written beside it. It is 0 or 1, and
  constant within a `GRAPH`, so the anchor depends on the type and not the piece.
- **The piece is the founder's art.** `orig_type` alone does not name a piece. `Wall::update_gpiece@00643070`
  walks `get_build_gpiece@0090bce0` from the *current* type, the **founder**'s nation (`BuildData +0x7f`) and
  `max_age`: down the ages in `BUILD_CONTINENT`, then `BACKUP_BUILD_CONTINENT`, then default.
  `artdata::BuildLook` is that walk over the `<BUILD>` entries that load. The door carries the founder's tribe
  and the owner's ages (`max_age` is not kept: parked 4035b).
- **A building's height is not its tile's.** See the value diff.
- **Not every building is drawn.** `WallOut::draw` fades an unfinished building in from 0.84 of
  `job_counter / construct_time(0)` and inverts that for the farm. 1,279 of the 4,564 dumped records are drawn by
  neither side (finished farms, sites under 0.84).
