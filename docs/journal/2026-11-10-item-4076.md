## 2. Which art, where, at what scale: the values

- **The art.** The capital's `<BUILD>` names `texture=".\art\village_6t.tga"`, a 523×348 run-length 32-bit TGA. It
  shows the same five buildings and fountain as the photograph. Its alpha is near two-level: 89,011 pixels at 0,
  88,934 at 255 and 4,059 between.
- **Where.** `WallOut::draw@00641930` projects the anchor into `loc2dx`/`loc2dy`, and
  `GraphicPieces::draw_build@008e7610` copies them to `build_x`/`build_y`. `SpriteMethod::execute@0091ca30` then
  draws at `build_x − (int)(spritexoff·s)`, `build_y − (int)(spriteyoff·s)`. The offsets are `init_build_data`'s
  strings 3852 and 3853, which `internal_strings_uwp.xml` names `spritexoff` and `spriteyoff`.
- **The scale.** `s = (int) fixed_zooms[6] · game_sprite_scale / (int) zoom_distance`, read off the listing at
  `8e7a10`–`8e7a3a`. **run1559b's packet** (`frame-snapshot.bin`, read through `tools/recomp/snapshot.py`) holds:
  - camera `0xc11d48`, `zoom_distance` 20858.805;
  - `fixed_zooms` = (173823.4, 139058.7, 55623.5, 41717.6, 27811.7, 20858.8, 13905.87);
  - `game_sprite_scale` 1.0.

  So `s` = 13905 / 20858 = **0.66665**. The sprites are authored at the nearest zoom.
- **The value diff on 3279**, our anchor through the original's arithmetic:

| building | anchor (draw list, in register) | offset | sprite on screen | photograph |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0/2000 | (1127, 505) | (290, 247) | (934, 341)..(1283, 573) | buildings (952..1275, 325..562) |
| 0/2001 | (1640, 438) | (83, 178) | (1585, 320)..(1700, 454) | (1585..1680, 315..450) |

The sprite's alpha edge was drawn over the photograph (`ov_cap.png`, `ov_wood.png` in the same directory). Both sit
on the photograph's buildings within a pixel or two by eye. That check is by eye and gates nothing. The woodcutter's
crane boom is outside the outline: it is a `<BUILD>` `EXISTEVENT` addon, not the sprite (parked 4076c).

## 3. The build

- `rondata::artdata::BuildArt::sprite_offset`: `spritexoff` and `spriteyoff`, default −1 as the loader has it.
- `render::draw::{SpriteAt, sprite_at}`: the arithmetic above.
- `render::silhouette::{Stamp, rasterize_with}`: a sprite keyed where its alpha is half or more, sampled nearest,
  at the anchor's camera depth.
- `client::world`: `World::sprites` (on by default; `--zbuffer` turns it off), `sprite_image`, `sprite_drawings`.
  The three types drawn as a model at the point keep their model.
- `client::gpu`: the client's first textured pipeline. Each sprite is a screen-pixel quad, alpha-tested at half, and
  blitted after the terrain with depth compare always, writing the anchor's depth. The figures are drawn after it, so
  a nearer figure draws over the sprite. A still of 3279 is `still.png` in the lab directory.
- `client::PINNED_SPRITE_ZOOM` = (13905.87, 1.0), the packet's values beside `pinned_camera`.
