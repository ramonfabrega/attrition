## The build

Every `add_unique` now goes onto `RoadMesh::order`, one list per side, in push order: the simulation's
(`add_unique`) and the line passes' (`tex_push`), each tile once where it was first pushed. `RoadMesh::redo` keeps
the simulation's membership. `redo_changed_roads_steps` walks `order`. A tile the simulation did not push takes
the texture variant of each step, in its place:
- pass 1: no `clear_support`;
- pass 2: lines, intersections and joins cleared, with no trim;
- pass 3: `leech_lines` and the marks on a refilled cache, with `set_diags` as zero;
- pass 4: `fixup_lines`, `delete_straglers` and the pick.

Each texture step saves the mesh's cursor and caches and restores them after (`SimRedo::on_tex`). So a simulation
tile's steps read exactly what they read before, and the 20 `movement::mesh::` tests pass unchanged.

## The kill: why 482 is not reached

A trace (not kept) printed every redo batch holding any of (16..19, 163): its list, each tile's position and
origin, and the lines before and after. The first two batches hold (17, 163) and (18, 163) together, with
(19, 163) ahead of both, but they run while row 164 still stands, so the yellow is re-marked there rightly.

After row 164's clear, every batch holds one of the pair:
- (17, 163) alone on one list, then (18, 163) alone on the other.
- Each one's redo leeches the yellow back from the other (`10` ↔ `02`).
- Its `fixup_lines` turn pushes the other onto the opposite list.

(19, 163)'s loops break because (19, 163) now shares a batch with (18, 163). In the round after (19, 163)'s own
redo for (19, 164)'s clear, the batch holds five tiles: (18, 163) at position 0, pushed by a line pass, and
(19, 163) at position 3, the simulation's. Both are cleared before either leeches. Lines after that batch:
(18, 163) `023100`, (19, 163) `00cf00`, the packet's 4500cf04. Push order is the original's rule, but
it cannot join two tiles that never sit on one list. What would put them on one list is the original's pushes
during row 164's clear: `add_roads`' remove arm, its marks, and which list each push names. That is a reading
(4772a).
