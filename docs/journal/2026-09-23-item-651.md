## The premise's killer, read before the run

Committed as `b098e75`, in `chapter6b.cmd`'s header. The premise as booked
was "a based aircraft moves where an unbased one did not". Its killer:

- **An aircraft moves only on an air order**, or on an attack order's walk.
  run168's had an empty stack.
- **The base's three writers of an air order act only on aircraft inside
  it.** They are `Object::do_launch`, `Object::attempt_launch` and
  `Build::train`'s gather arm. Each walks the base's `inside_down` chain.
  `do_launch`'s whole body sits under `-1 < inside_down`, so an empty base
  spends nothing.
- **What fills the chain is `Unit::go_inside`, and `add` never calls it.**
  `Build::train` seats its trainee inside the base right after
  `init_unit`. `add` is `find_nearby_spot` and `init_unit` and nothing
  after that. I grepped every caller of `go_inside`, and none is reachable
  from a channel line under `!ai off`.
- **The loops cited:** `do_launch`'s computer arm is bounded to leaders
  `0..7` (`local_34 < 0xe0`, and `local_20 < 0xe71ef8`). The chain walks
  end on a negative head, so an empty base runs zero iterations.

So the base-link half was predicted dead by construction. **The staging
also named a second, open arm.** who=1's Bomber is AI-driven, so its idle
search reaches 24 tiles, and who=0's Airbase was 10.8 tiles away. The
order stack would tell the two arms apart. Three more falsifiers could
fire against the reading:

- the seats;
- an empty tank acting, on 1010 and 1215;
- an empty base drawing, or who=1's Airbase disbanded by
  `check_orphaned_buildings`.
