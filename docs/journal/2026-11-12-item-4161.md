`Clock: ceilings on GreatLakes lobby, 600 frames off-screen from frame 0 · frame zero 1.5 s · p99 20 ms · slowest 400 ms (frame 1, first sight) · peak 320 MiB`

`rondata::diff::floors::clock_line` prints this line from `render::clock::CLOCK`. The test
`the_handoff_s_clock_line_is_the_pinned_ceilings` is on `release_gate.py`'s `COMMANDERS_LINES`, and
it is red on this branch until the driver writes the line at the merge: "it says None".

from frame 3 a frame takes about 4 ms. Frame 1 decodes every piece's model, textures and sprite it
shows (`scene`, 36 to 57 ms, the longest phase in six of eight sessions). It also builds the ground's
meshes (`ground`, 14 to 27 ms) and uploads both (`gpu` and `wait`, 36 to 144 ms).

## What is not established

- Seconds to lobby (DECISIONS 114 §4): the client has no lobby screen.
- The windowed session: its frames wait on vsync. A window in a gate could take a capture lane's
  clicks, so none was opened.
- Whether the lobby's ground art is right. The art is the pair's start's (run1428's tileset and
  seed); run53 reads `game_random seed 12345`. That the two agree is read off the picture, not
  checked.
- The ceilings are set under shared load on one machine. A gate at eight threads with three lanes
  beside it has not been measured against them.
