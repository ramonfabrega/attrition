- **The reading's one surprise.** No `PatrolOrder` is ever built alone.
  `add_patrol_order` asks for `GROUP_PATROL`, and `process_group` forces
  `push_group`, so a lone chariot leads a group of one.

  `Group::action_move_to(QUEUE_FIRST, ATTACK_TO)`. A group's

  with its own prologue check. It compiles `-Werror` both plain and under
  `RON_AUTOSTART`. `golden.rs` parses the verb.
- **The staging.** The chariot keeps chapter nine's seat, cell (4, 9). The
  squad moves east of the sand strip, to cell (14, 10). The patrols run
  five cells south, and the window is `[605, 1250)`.
