# Item 435 — the dump refuted my belief in one grep

*2026-09-19, Opus 5, `worktree-att-365`. A reading. `docs/COMBAT.md` §27,
landed as a belief; §26.4's belief struck through and pointed at it. No
capture, no score moved.*

The brief was my own belief from 434: the 6.85-tile lower bound belongs to
`think`'s cheap remembered-target arm rather than to the search, which
would vacate the bound and let one constant explain everything.

**It is wrong, and the dump said so before the decompile was opened.**

## Fetch rather than assume, twice, and both times it mattered

The type record says `ObjectData +0x34` and `+0x36` are **`near_o` and
`near_who`** — the remembered-target pair the cheap arm reads. **The dump
prints them.** On who=1's hoplite captain they are −1 from 616 through 634
and become `10` / `0` at 635, the frame the attack order appears. No
remembered target existed, so the cheap arm cannot have run, and 635 is the
search's own result. The lower bound stands.

That took one `track.py` line. I had been about to read
`is_in_range@006486b0` to find out.

The second fetch closed the last non-reading alternative. The dump also
prints `idle` (`UnitData +0xb0`) and `stance`, and **both captains carry
`idle 1, stance 0` on their birth frame** — the hoplite at 616, the slinger
at 621. So they reach the search by the same arm of the same gate, and the
difference between them is only which *range* they search with. Had idle
differed, the whole reading would have been unnecessary.

## What the read found instead, and it is structural

`Unit::think_attack@005f5a80` does not call the search with −1. It computes
`~(unit_masks >> bit 18) & 1`, ORs in a leader flag, and passes **−1 or 0**.
This crate passes −1 always: **the branch does not exist here.**

And the zero path is not the same function with a different number in it —
it is three radii on two frame grids, sweeping 128 tiles once in 1024
frames, 64 once in 256, and narrow between. None of that is modelled.

That is a better answer than the constant I was chasing, because it is a
shape rather than a value, and shapes are what this crate gets wrong.

## The arithmetic, and the hole I am not papering over

`unit_respond_range` = 7 with the −1 branch and no AI-driven arm fits every
measured datum — hoplite 7 tiles (6.85 in, 7.25 out), slinger 9 (8.6 in),
bowmen 13 — and 7 is the only integer that does. The hole is the AI-driven
arm, which would raise the hoplite to 14 tiles. Its word is not misread:
`UnitData +0x68` is `unit_masks` by the type record and the dump prints
262144 on who=1's hoplites against 0 on who=0's. The zero path's own narrow
arm has the same shape and no integer fits it either — 3 gives 6 tiles, 4
gives 8, and the bracket is [6.85, 7.25).

So §27.4 states the belief with the hole named rather than smoothed: these
units take the zero path under a leader flag **this dump does not print**,
and §27.6 says the `LEADERS=9` window is the one question here that a
capture answers and a reading cannot. I did not take it — the brief said
not to capture without asking, and the AI lane may want the screen.

## The slingers, again

Invariant under every value and every branch the section ranges over:
who=0's units carry `unit_masks` 0, so `uVar12` is 1 unconditionally, they
take the −1 branch, their own term dominates at nine tiles against the 8.6
they engage at, and the AI-driven arm can never apply to them. That is now
the third account in a row that has had to clear this bar, and the second
that failed it silently until I checked.

## The habit that did the work

434's lesson was "fetch rather than assume"; 435 is the same lesson with a
sharper edge — **the dump already holds the field your reading is about**.
`near_o`, `near_who`, `idle` and `stance` were all printed, all along, in a
capture taken two items ago for something else. Grepping the dump before
booking a reading is `CLAUDE.md`'s rule and it saved the read that was the
whole of this item's brief.
