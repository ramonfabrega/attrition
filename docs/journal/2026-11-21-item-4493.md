## The fix

`Sim::group_refresh_order` (`groups/group/query.rs`) runs `seat_normalize` on a seated group and reads the slot from
the pruned list. A group with no seat keeps the speed tail. `group_move_follower`'s `group_rewrite_leader`, which
follows the refresh, reads the group again. `docs/GROUPS.md` §23.4 lists the refresh among the pointer prunes. It
also names the sites still on the speed tail alone: `kill_group_move`, the `is_*` queries and `redo_patrol_order`
(4493a below).

**Test.** `row_85_s_refresh_prunes_the_member_its_sort_unpointed_on_run1751_s_ranges` (`diff::held_out`) compares
every groups range on 3830 and 3831, ours against theirs, byte for byte, and names theirs' record 68 `num`: 2 on
3830, 3 on 3831. **Mutation**: `tools/mutate.py --edit crates/sim/src/groups/group/query.rs 'self.seat_normalize(seat);'
→ 'self.seat_normalize_tail(seat);'` (the prune taken out, the speed tail kept). It held: exit 101, and the one failed
test was that one, at `groups range 86 on 3831, record 68 id..march`.
