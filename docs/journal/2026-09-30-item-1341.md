## The bump loop, as the listing reads

`get_cost` takes the loop only when **this type's availability bit**
(`leader + 0x6c18`) is set. That is the train arm, and the loop never runs
beside the research arm.

1. **The candidates.** The loop takes every unit type `p` from 50 to below
   `0x192` whose `num_queued[p]` is not zero (`cmpw $0x0` over `+0x5a86`,
   which is `+0x5a22 + 2p`). A queued research of `p` raises it as much as
   a queued unit.
2. **The test.** `p` is a hit in either of two ways:
   - `p`'s `FROM` (`+0x3c`, through `tribe_can_type` or vtable `+0x68`)
     is this type;
   - or `p` lies on this type's `JUMP` chain (`+0x48`). The chain's first
     link goes through `LeaderData::get_graft`, and every later link goes
     through `tribe_can_type` or `+0x68`.
3. **The bump.** Each hit adds `max(0, p.costs[r] − this.costs[r])` to the
   unscaled base (`006646b9`–`006646c9`).
4. **After the loop**, `× UNIT_COST_FACTOR` (`006645ae`) multiplies the
   bumped base, so the ramp's ceiling is measured against it too.

The research refit, which item 545 built, is the same walk with its two
ends swapped.
