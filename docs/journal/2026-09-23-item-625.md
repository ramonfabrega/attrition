## The reading

`Guy::move@005d9240`, the listing `5d9565`–`5d958a`:

```
5d9565  testb $0x40, 0x9a(%ebx)      ; guy_flags & 0x40, a plane
5d956c  jne   5d958f                 ; skip the walk
5d9571  testb $0x4, 0x2b8(type)      ; unit_flags2 & 4, a type that packs
5d9578  je    5d9583                 ; does not pack: ask for it
5d957a  testl $0x80000, 0x68(unit)   ; unit_masks & 0x80000, packed
5d9581  je    5d958f                 ; unpacked: skip the walk
5d958a  call  Guy::set_anim(walk, 0, 1)
```

So a packer's figure that moves while unpacked keeps whatever slot it
has. The catapult cannot move unpacked. What moves is the crew, pulled
round by figure 0's turn. Their slot stays the idle, and
`Guy::inc_time`'s mirror (the crew's first arm) copies figure 0's
`TURN_LEFT` and time into them on the same frame.

This crate's `Sim::guy_follow_anim` asked for the walk on the moving arm
for every figure, gated only by the turn slots. On 781 the crew took
`WALK` (3 frames), stepped their own clock, wrapped on 782 and rolled
the idle twice.
