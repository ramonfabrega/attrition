## The cause

Piece 168 is restricted: it has a turret on node 4, and ours' pivot gate holds its 4496 release. But
`sim::pivot::RELEASES` had no row for it. `guy_release_events` therefore fell through to `launch::launch_point`,
whose unmeasured branch is the unit's own square.

**Built** (`crates/sim/src/pivot.rs`). From run1164's packet, `get_position.py … entries 168` gives `NODES` (168, 4,
(0, 1.79)) and `RDATA_SCALE` (168, 19/20), which is `RData +0x88` 0.95. It also gives four `RELEASES` rows:
`CHAR_ATTACKWALK` 1, `CHAR_ATTACK1` 1, `CHAR_ATTACK2` 11 and `CHAR_ATTACK3` 12. Their twelve `float_lands` cells
come from the unicorn sweep of the pivot branch (`get_position.py … pivot 168 0 <anim> <time> 1`, 92,416 cells a
row). The test is `pivot::tests::the_original_s_release_agrees_on_every_cell_for_piece_168`, gated on
`RON_RELEASE_TABLE_168` (the tables are outside git). With the rows in, ours' round leaves from (9412, 31822) at
`t 1/6`, which is the packet's launch.
