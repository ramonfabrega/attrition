## The cause

While a record lives, the hold stays level. `process_all` takes one off, `DeathObj::inc_time@008d5240` adds one
back, and the `blocks_while_dead` raise only floors it at 30. So theirs' 2 after tick 1538 means 31 at the
record's end, where ours held 30. `Objects::inc_time@0065db70` runs every `Ammo::inc_time` and then every live
record. The Carrier was sunk by a round, so its record took a bump on its own frame: `Object::close`'s 30, plus
one. This crate folded both bumps into one pass ahead of `process_projectiles`, with the comment "no capture can
see the order". A packet sees it. The fix splits the pass: `Sim::hold_frames_tick` keeps the dead-shooter bump
ahead of the walk, and the new `Sim::death_objs_inc_time` runs the records' bump and cull after it.
