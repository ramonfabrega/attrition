The brief was a widening with no hypothesis to inherit, and an explicit
question attached: does the cause sit inside run97, or past 9349? **It sits
inside — at 8982 and 8985.** No frame past 9349 was wanted at any point, and
none is owed for this item.

### Where forty wealth comes from in three frames

It cannot come from income: player 1 earns about seventeen wealth per two
hundred frames all through this window. It comes from the market, and
`docs/AI.md` §40 had already ruled that out — for a reason that turned out
to be a predicate error of exactly the kind the audit README keeps naming.

§40 reads `use_market`'s once-flag correctly: it clears when a candidate
passes the stock test, not when a sale goes through, so *"nothing here is
sellable" is the case that spends one draw and stops*. True. The
inference drawn from it — **one draw therefore means nothing was sold** —
is not, because the outer loop is

```c
do { if (uVar5 != 0) break; uVar5 = 1; … } while (bucket[g] < need[g]);
```

and a sale that carries the bucket over `need` exits on the `while`
**before the once-flag is ever consulted**. One draw has two meanings. On
8582 and 8585 it is §40's; on 8982 it is the other one — timber stands at
141 against a need of 0 and clears `need ≤ stock − 100` comfortably.

So the item became the mechanic `docs/ECONOMY.md` had been naming as its
own last open seam for three weeks: the trade.

### Three functions, and they are small

- `LeaderData::calc_market_prices@006dc2a0` — `sell = price + flux`,
  `buy = 2·price + flux`, one spread bonus (Nubian, else **Amber**, never
  both), then `sell ≥ 1`, `buy ≥ MARKET_BASEMENT·2`, `buy ≥ sell + 10`.
- `Leader::do_sell@006cfc60` — a hundred of the good out, the sell price
  into wealth, the good's escrow released, the price down by
  `MARKET_SUPPLY_DEMAND` with a floor of zero.
- `Leader::do_buy@006cfbd0` — the buy price out of wealth, a hundred of
  the good in, **wealth's** escrow released, the price up with no ceiling.

Six constants joined `Tuning` and are re-derived from the install by
`rondata` like the other 289. `AMBER` is `TypeIndex` 17: the decompile's
`rare.ptr[1] & 8` is byte 1 bit 3, bit `17 − BASE_RARE`, and
`resourcerules.xml`'s eighteenth `RESOURCE` is Amber — the same byte
arithmetic `TOBACCO` and `GEMS` are already pinned by.

`use_market` then became the original's own loop rather than a draw-site
stub: branch, draw, rotation, sale, re-check.

### The probe that graduated

`RON_DEBUG_LEADER=<lo>-<hi>` joins `RON_DEBUG_UNIT`, `RON_DEBUG_ARMIES`
and `RON_DEBUG_BUILDS` in the harness: every computer leader's step
machine, make list, stockpile, escrow and Commerce level over a window.
It is the fourth of that family and the one a *leader* divergence asks
for, since `use_market` and both expiry walks are functions of the make
list alone. run97 carries `LEADERDATA` at `LEADERS=1` only, so this line
is read against the **draws** rather than against a dumped list —
`docs/AI.md` §41 names the capture that would print the other side.

### What this does not establish

- **The Supercollider's clamp** (`has_wonder(0x21d)`, building 541) and
  **CtW's per-leader bonus** are stated, not modelled.
  `PlayerTech::wonders` has no writer anywhere in this crate, so the
  predicate could only be a constant `false`. A capture reaching the
  Information age with that wonder standing would refuse it; nothing on
  disk is past the Classical age.
- **The hundred.** Both trades move exactly a hundred units. No capture on
  disk shows a bucket step that is not a multiple of it, and a `LEADERS=9`
  window over 9182 — where the original spends **three** market draws and
  this crate one — is the run that would say.
- **The rotation past the first sale.** 8982 sells once and stops because
  the sale covers the need; "every qualifying rotation position sells"
  rests on the decompile alone.
- **The third scholar** (`docs/AI.md` §41). The offers are right — the
  head expiry draws three times on both sides — and the wealth is there;
  what blocks it is that step 4 clears every duplicate before step 6 can
  read it. Three readings fit and the run that separates them is a
  `LEADERS=9` window over `[8980, 8990]`, which no capture covers: run19's
  is `[8174, 8192]`.
- **9182.** Above the word now, still three market draws against one, and
  still queuing a citizen at `1/2007` the original does not. It is the
  next market question and it is where a `LEADERS=9` run would pay twice.
