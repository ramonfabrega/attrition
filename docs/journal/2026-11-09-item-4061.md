**The arithmetic, each line checked against the listing:**

- *Share*: `(float)(u32)job_counter / (float)(u32)WallData::construct_time` (vtable `+0x18c` returns `constr_time`;
  `divss` at `00641907`, passed in `xmm3`). The decompiler printed `0.0`. It is `PlacedBuilding::share`.
- *Foundation point*: `(x − 0x60·b, y − 0x60·b, z)`, where `b` is `build_half_tcoord` (`halfoffset`) or, when
  `build_construction` (`constructsize`, the internal string three before `halfoffset`; `init_build_data@008f2f20`
  reads it at `0x12cdc`) is not 0, `BuildTypeData +0x234` (x footprint) − `constructsize`. Angle 270.
- *Foundation slot* (`get_construct_gpiece`): domain (`+0x218`) 1 → 9; `is(0x1b6)` (Senate) → 10; else
  `constructsize`, or `+0x238` (y footprint) when 0, less 1. These name the eleven `<CONSTRUCTION>` entries of
  `building_graphics.xml` in order (1x1..7x7, the 8x8 repeat, 9x5, dock, Senate 3x6): TGA sprites, the dock's with a
  `.bh3`.
- *Scaffold table*: `scaffold_timing[y_size − 1]`, or `[9]` for `is(0x1b6)`; none for the farm or domain 1. Loaded
  by `init_scaffold_timing@008ed6c0` from `effects_graphics.xml`'s ten `<SCAFFOLD_TIMING>` (the first empty), each
  `<TIMING name start_percent end_percent x y z angle>`; x, y, z ×3.0 (`0x40400000`). All x, y, z and angles in the
  install are integers.
- *Scaffold slot drawn* when `start ≤ share ≤ end`; its point is `(x − h + 3tx, y − h + 3ty, z + 3tz)` with `h` the
  **raw** `build_half_tcoord` (`subl %esi` at `009075ed`, no ×0x60): one world unit, not the sprite's half tile.
- *Scaffold piece*: `first_ammo_piece + index of name in ammo_names` (the first `<SCAFFOLD>` of that name; no
  `<AMMO>`/`<ENTRENCH>` shares one, which the reader refuses) `+ 4 · bracket`, the bracket of the owner's age
  (`LeaderDataEncrypt +0xdc ^ 0x62766`): 0 for ages 0–2, 1 for 3–4, 2 from 5. `num_scaffold_pieces_per_age = 4`
  (`init_ammo_piece_ranges@008f6140`). The absolute piece numbers are not computed (no reader knows
  `first_ammo_piece`, and no dump prints a scaffold piece); an entry is named by its `<SCAFFOLD>` element instead.
- *Gates*: `is_force_construction@0063e220` is the fog gate and is 0 for an observer, so both passes run on the
  observer's view, as the sprites do.

**Settled in the second session:**

- `init_scaffold_timing` keeps a slot only when its `name` is in `ammo_names`, and the match is
  `String::operator==@00a1f140`: the insensitive hash or `_wcsicmp`. So the eight `Crane` timings find `crane`; a
  case-sensitive reader would have dropped them.
- `constructsize` is read from the **piece's** `BuildRData +0x28` (`get_construct_gpiece`, `draw_underlays`), and
  differs between ages of one graph (the Asian, Arab and East Indian cities: 0 and 6). The look keys it by piece;
  `halfoffset` (`+0x2c`) stays by type.
- The foundation's farm gate is the exact type (`+4 == 0x1a1` at `00641546`), and a finished building reads share 1.0
  (vtable `+0x4c`). A farm's own alpha branch (wheat or not) is reached but never drawn.
- The started gate cannot be seen in these dumps: every placed record has share 0, and the least `start_percent` in
  the install is 0.05.
