**How the stores were found.** The export names a `LeaderData` field `field_0x<off>` on a `Leader *` (the
`Leader` record has no fields of its own in the type stream, and `LeaderData` sits at its `+0`), so
`b4124/finder4124.py` lists every `field_0x<off>` and `->name` of a leaf's element offsets on a line of a
function that names a `Leader`. That misses aliased stores: `Object::take_damage`'s `units_killed` is
`local_8[0x20d].field_0x33ee` in the decompile. So the stores were taken from the executable's listing
(`b4124/disp4124.py`, outside git): `leaders.list` is at `0xe3a390` (stride `0x6eec`), so a store by an
index register is `0x<0xe3a390 + off>(%reg)`, and in the `Leader` classes a store through `this` is
`0x<off>(%reg)`, not `%esp` or `%ebp`. That listing still misses a `Leaders *` walked by another class:
`GameDaemon::process_victory`'s `local_30->list[0].field_0x44c` (`wonderwin_timer`) and
`Game::init_teams`' `chat_status` loop came from the export and from reading. For the never-reached
verdicts a third net ran: every register-relative store at the leaf's offset in any function
(`b4124/relall4124.py`); each hit outside the `Leader` classes was read and is another record's (an
indexed array of `Constants`, `Game`, a unit type).

  crate's records of one owner, in order, are that list (`Sim::init_city` reuses the owner's first dead
