## 3. Why there is no draw

Both draws were read before building (the decompile export).
- **`DeathObjOut::draw`** places each record at its own x, y and `center_z`, turned by its angle. It plays the body
  on `cur_anim` at `age × 200 / 3` ms, held at the action's end. The body holds for 105 frames past the action
  (`0x69`) and then fades to `body_fade_end`. A skeleton piece fades in beside it. `sim::combat::Death` stores
  `who`, `o`, `first_frame`, `cur_anim`, `life` and the figures' lives and pieces, and **no position or angle**. So
  the door cannot carry one until the simulation keeps it. That is a state change in `crates/sim`, not a door.
- **`AmmoOut::draw`** interpolates x and y from start to end at `(cur_time × 200 / 3 + graphic_dt) / 66.67 /
  total_time`. It takes z by `traj`: 0 linear, 1 `sz + v1z·t + GRAV_Z·t²/2` and pitched along the arc, 2 the
  missile's path table. A round not in flight (`flags & 2` clear) fades over 200 frames. It is drawn only where
  `world_to_console` and the fog allow. `sim::combat::Projectile` holds `sz`, `ez`, `v1z` and `spline`. The door's
  `ProjectileScene` carries `launch`, `landing`, `angle`, the times, `air` and `gpiece`, and drops the rest.
