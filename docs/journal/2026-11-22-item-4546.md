## The build

`ai_host::lap(base, mark, cursor)` resets a cursor at or past `mark`, or under zero, to `base`, then laps from
`cursor + 1`, wrapping to `base`. `lap_units` and `lap_builds` both call it. A builds cursor in `0..2000` keeps
starting at 2000; the original would walk unit slots there. No counter this crate writes holds such a value, and
the doc comment says so.
