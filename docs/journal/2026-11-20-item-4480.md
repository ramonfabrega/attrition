## The reading

- `Wall::init@0063e9b0` calls `Object::init@00647750`. That function ends with `has_objmask(0x2000000)` at vslot
  `+0x148`, and on an answer sets `field_0x8 |= 0x40`. `0x2000000` is `combat::mask::DETECT`.
- On `Build::vftable`, slot `+0x148` is `BuildData::has_objmask@0062e210`. It returns the type's mask (type slot
  `0xf0`). Its city arm (`flags & 0x20`, `city >= 0`, the Gunpowder or tech-bit tests) only adds `0x40` and strips
  `0x20000`. So `DETECT` on a building comes from its type alone.
- `Object::update_seen@00651b80:107`: with `field_0x8 & 0x40` set and the circle (not ring) case, the cutoff is
  `circle_radius[r]`, the range's own end. A building's sweep is always the whole circle, so a `DETECT` building
  lights `seen3` over its whole disc.

## The fix

`Sim::update_seen_build` (`crates/sim/src/vision.rs`) passes `detect` to `write_sweep`. `detect` is the building
type's `combat.obj_masks & DETECT`. The units' arm already did this (`docs/GOLDEN.md` §101).
