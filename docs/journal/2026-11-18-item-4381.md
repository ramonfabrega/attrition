  - `KeyMap::keymap_options` is the S_GDATA32 at 0002:141168, two shorts a row. Row 392 is (3, −1), OPTION_REPAIR.
    The same reading gives row 177 as (17, 0), row 149 as (26, −1) and row 89 as (153, −1). That matches item 4378's
    stance row and its garrison and pause options.

  `ScenarioFuncSet::damage_object@009f5bd0` takes a 1-based player, the object, and a percent below 0x65. The
  Barracks' `damage` is 480 from 690 to 1000.
