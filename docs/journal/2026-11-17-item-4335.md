## The tool

`tools/render/card_pass.py <rontrace.log> [--frame F] [--draws LO-HI] [--over X0,Y0,X1,Y1] [--verts]` reads the
card probe's records in Python, the same parse as `rondata::trace::card`. It prints one row per draw of the pass:
- the caller and both stages' files;
- the blend, the alpha test and the address modes;
- the filters (`RenderState +0xec`, `+0xf4`, `+0xfc`, `MIPFilter` from `rise.pdb`'s enumerators) and
  `mipmap_bias` (`+0x104`), which no reader had printed before;
- each stage's colour and alpha op, the shader options, the material's and light's diffuse, and `gTLAlpha`;
- the stream's screen box, when the take streamed one.

It was run once on run1567's pass after 3279: 210 draws in 0.25 s, exit 0. Draws 121–131 and 155–169 read as
`the_lake_s_layers_at_the_card` asserts them. It is the scratch test of items 4310, 4327, 4329 and 4330,
graduated (DECISIONS 139 §1). run1567 streams no vertices, so `--over` keeps every draw there.
