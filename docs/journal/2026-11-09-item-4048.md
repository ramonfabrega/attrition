  test (`PACKET_3294_FARMS`).

  building's terraform, for the **owner's** farms within eight tiles. `1/2003`'s corner went 182 → 179 by run1428's
  frame 37, and `1/2002`'s `[0][2]` went 196 → 221 by 3294. `sim` kept no heights (`SimFarmsHost::terrain_height` was

  `Farms::add` and by the new `Sim::farms_recalc_heights`. It changes no sync behaviour, since the heights are art and
