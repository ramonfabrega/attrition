## The piece is derivable

`get_unit_gpiece@0090c030`, the branch `param_1 − 0x192 < 0xc`, computes `first_bird_piece +
3·(type − 0x192) + v`. The variant `v` starts at `(seed + o) % 3` and is walked **down** while
`data_pieces[piece]` is null. `first_bird_piece` is `init_piece_ranges`' constant `0xea8d`
(FORMATS, item 4028). The install ships these variants:

- `-TYPE0` only: the whales and the three birds.
- `-TYPE0` and `-TYPE1`: the pig and the chicken.
- all three: every other herd.

So every whale is 60075 whatever its `o`, and that is what run1148's dump prints for all of them.

Built:

- `artdata::gaia_tables` reads `GaiaVariants`, `(TypeIndex, v) → the shipped variant`. The lengths
  it reads now walk down too. They used to fall back to `-TYPE0`, and the walk down changes that
  for one case only: a pig's or chicken's `-TYPE2` is its `-TYPE1`.
- `Loaded::gaia_variants` and `Art::gaia_variants`, installed by `install_art`.
- `Sim::gaia_piece`. `piece_of` takes it wherever the dump names no piece.
- The checksum emitter's `guy_gpiece` reads it too, so the walk down is shared.
