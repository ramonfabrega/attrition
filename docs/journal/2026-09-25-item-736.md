## The cause

`Group::action_siege_attack_to@0070d830` builds its sub-group on the
stack. It calls `Group::clear(-1)`, then copies the parent's `id` and
`army` and sets `stamp` to 0. `clear` zeroes `facing`.

This crate keys a group's record by its seat. The sub-group carried
`army: g.army`, so `gstate(&sub)` was army 3's record, and army 3's
`facing` was 1 on 15350. On 15095 it was 0, which is why the first issue
agreed.

The fix was in fenced `group.rs`, so I asked the commander first. They
granted `group_action_siege_attack_to` alone, on condition that the fix be
a real cleared record and not a save-and-restore of the flag. The sub-group
now lays out on `GroupState::default()`, which is `Group::clear`'s, plus
the carried pool id and `stamp 0`. The parent's record is set aside for
that one call and put back.

`the_anchor_s_sub_group_lays_out_on_its_own_cleared_facing` fails
without the fix (`Some(true)`), and it also checks that the army's
record is untouched.

A flag-only prototype, measured before the ask, moved the word to the
same place.
