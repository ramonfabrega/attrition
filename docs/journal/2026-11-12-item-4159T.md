## What was built

`crates/rondata/src/diff/transfer.rs` (test-only). It runs no simulation:

- **The corpus.** Every `gamelog-*.txt` under the kept dumps' directory except the held-out battery's
  (`diff::battery::BATTERY`'s `start` and `long`, DECISIONS 61 §6). 910 of them print the level-9
  `LEADERDATA` record. A streaming line reader keeps, per frame and leader, the first record's words
  the steps read (the thirteen rates, `ages_get()`, `leader_flags`, the six bins, `supply_mark`,
  `num_queued[scan]`'s unit records) and the units' own `supply` records by `(who, o, uid)`. The game's
  closing record is labelled the last frame plus one. The extraction is cached outside the repository in
  `~/Library/Caches/attrition/transfer-v1/`, keyed by name, size and modification time. It holds the
  original's own values and never ours.
- **The pairs.** Consecutive records of one leader in one dump. A pair whose frames do not advance, or
  that touches a frame label printed twice, is not a step's pair and is not counted (864, run27's and
  run29's 15101 among them: the first "15101" there is still the state before 15100's tick).
- **The steps, all the crate's:**
  - The bins: `Sim::armed_queued_bins` (new, factored out of `Sim::step_armed_queued`, so the routing
    is the one the sim steps by) and `leaders::marks::step_bin`, run on each unit record's `num_queued`
    move across the pair, one `±1` at a time. `track_queued` steps `num_queued[t]` and the bins by the
    same `d` in one call, so the array's move is the bins' input, printed.
  - `supply_mark`: `territory::supply::SupplyList` rebuilt from the first frame's supply records (its
    length must be the printed mark, or the pair is unprinted). Each record a unit gives up is closed,
    each new holder is registered, and the list's length is compared.
  - The averages: `leaders::rates::Rates::fold` on the first record's averages, `frame_battle` and
    `ages_get()`. The window's count the fold reads is taken from each `*_fifteen_seconds` step across
    the pair (step 1 adds that count to the total). A pair with no fold tick between its records holds
    the averages still. A pair spanning two folds is unprinted.
- **Pinned** (`PINNED`): per leaf, the pairs, the moving pairs and the partings. `RELEASED` asserts the
  five each transfer, are emitted (not in `RATES_OFF_THE_LINE`) and are gone from `LEADERS_HELD_OUT`.
- **Red, kept as a check** (`the_bent_steps_part_on_a_named_pair`): the bins' increment doubled parts
  `barracks_queued` on 693 pairs (first run1001's who 1, 13985 → 13986, ours 2 theirs 1). The empty
  fold's decay at `·6 >> 3` parts `average_kill_rate` on 1,295 pairs (first run1001's who 1,
  13900 → 13901, ours 37 theirs 43). A newborn supply record appended past the mark parts
  `supply_mark` on all 82 moving pairs (first run1013b's who 1, 9741 → 9757, ours 2 theirs 1).

The five released leaves are emitted on the leaders line: `Sim::leader_long` gains `supply_mark` and
the stable, factory and air bins beside `dock_queued`, and `RATES_OFF_THE_LINE` loses
`average_kill_rate` (12 → 11). Each joins `LEADERS_BENT`.

## Made to fail on the crate's own step

With `leaders::rates::fold_average`'s empty decay bent to `(a * 6) >> 3` in `crates/sim` and nothing
else changed, the pinned test went red: `average_kill_rate` parted on 1,295 of 476,892 pairs. The first
was run1001's who 1, 13900 → 13901, ours 37 theirs 43: the printed 50 before the fold, `50·7 >> 3 = 43`.
`average_death_rate` parted on 1,191, `average_hit_rate` on 334 and `average_damage_rate` on 498, and
every bin and `supply_mark` held. Then the source was restored. The kept check bends the bins' increment
and the supply take in the harness, and both part on named pairs (above). The red-first order was
kept for the harness: it ran against an empty `PINNED` before any leaf was released.
