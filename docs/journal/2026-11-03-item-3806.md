any target. When the ammo is not `missile="1"`, it takes `calc_nuke_spline` ahead of the Bomber's fall and the
straight line. The fourth argument is `is(0x13a)`; the decompile shows it as the float 4.4e-43, whose bits are 314.

The Cruise Missile arm lays the launch and the launch 250 up. It then lays one point per 768 of distance at the
ground plus 250 (+750 or +1250 by the tile word), the landing 250 up, and the landing. The resolution is the
polygon over **96**, a constant the decompile hid: the listing's `movss 0xb696a8, %xmm1` at `913de7`. The knot
weights are all 30 and nothing caps the resolution.
