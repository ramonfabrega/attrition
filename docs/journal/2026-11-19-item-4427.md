## The dependency, and why it is earned

`ab_glyph`'s stack (`ab_glyph`, `owned_ttf_parser`, `ab_glyph_rasterizer`) has no TrueType interpreter, and the
DLL's glyphs are hinted (`FT_Load_Glyph(face, index, 0)`, `FT::FontFace::RenderGlyph@10009e40`, item 4426).
`skrifa` 0.44's interpreter is the fontations project's port of FreeType's, tested outline for outline against it;
its `SmoothMode::Normal` target is `FT_LOAD_TARGET_NORMAL` under v40. Named to the driver before it was added; the
driver's yes held three conditions, each kept: it moved the row (below); `crates/client` only, nothing reaches
`crates/sim` (`cargo tree -p sim -e normal | grep -c skrifa` 0) and the face is still read from the install at run
time with nothing derived from it committed; and the lock adds exactly `skrifa`, `read-fonts` and `font-types`
(`bytemuck` and `once_cell` were in it). `ab_glyph_rasterizer` becomes a direct dependency of `crates/client`; it
was already in the lock under `ab_glyph`.

## What was read

- **The DLL's FreeType**: `strings d3dgl.dll` carries `FREETYPE_PROPERTIES` (the environment property, FreeType
  2.7.1 and later, whose default interpreter is v40) and `interpreter-version`; no version string. So v40 is an
  inference, borne out by the row and by the widths: v40 makes no horizontal moves in backward-compatibility mode,
  and every hinted cell keeps the packet's `pix_width`.
- **The bitmap's box**: `FT_Render_Glyph` sizes the bitmap from the outline's control box floored and ceiled; the
  cell takes the hinted outline's box the same way (`PixelPath::cbox`, every point and control).
