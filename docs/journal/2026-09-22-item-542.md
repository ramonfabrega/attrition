## The draw named the least of it

The item was booked on two draws, `Ammo::init+0xcd9` and `+0xd0b`. The
listing puts both on the landing scatter at `0x67c8c9`/`0x67c8fb`,
`rand(0, 0xffff) % accuracy` once per axis, which is on every shot's
path. So the delta says only that the original's round launched on 621.
It names no ship-only branch, and there was none to find in `Ammo::init`.

The dump said where to look before any reading did. `1/6` prints
`recharging 36` on block 621 and the round is first in the air on 622,
so the reload is not what gates the launch. The launch is the
animation's release event (§9.0). The three rounds of each volley land on
blocks +0, +4 and +12, on both ships and every volley in the capture. The
Trireme's three `<RELEASEEVENT>`s are 400, 666 and 1200 ms, and this
crate's `× 3 / 200` puts them at 6, 9 and 18, three and twelve apart.
That reading could not be right.

## The divisor

`GraphicPieces::init_unit_events` stores the milliseconds raw, and it
was the wrong loader. The list `execute_game_events` walks is
`GraphicEvents::init_unit_events@008e2520`'s, which divides `starttime`
by 67 with `imul 0x7a44c6b; sar edx, 1` and floors the result at 1. That
gives 5, 9 and 17, and the spacing is four and twelve. The element and
attribute names came from `internal_strings.xml` by index, the method §22
used; a regex count first gave an off-by-several table, and the XML
parser fixed it.

Every release measured before run127 gives the same frame under either
divisor, which is why §9.0's four-shot table could not have told them
apart. 356 of the install's 2,593 events separate the two, and the Trireme
is the first a capture reached.

## The keel

The release points came straight from `AMMO`'s `sx, sy` less the hull's
position: (+37, −25), (−12, +7), (−56, +35). They are the same three on
every round of both ships. Node 0 walks the keel as the swing plays,
from bow to aft. `sim::launch` already had a measured table of these,
so the precedent was item 396's and no art was loaded. I solved the
three rows through the crate's own `sin_component` with a scratch test,
since deleted. The first row is exactly dead ahead. For the other two,
no stern bearing reproduces the integers at this facing, and the rows are
the nearest solution to the keel.

## The water, which only the widening could have found

With the first two causes fixed the word moved to 664. I then widened
the `AMMO` comparison to the rest of the record this crate carries:
`sz`, `ez`, `angle`, `v1z` and `rolling`. It failed on its first run.
Every trireme round launched at `sz −185` against the dump's 88. §46.1's
launch height is the node over `find_data_z`, which is the lake bed under
a ship. `Guy::update_z@005d9950` gives a sea figure `z = 0`. That fix did
not move the word, because `v1z` touches only a rolled shot, but it is
now diff-backed to the last printed digit on all seven rounds under the
word.

Both fixes were made to fail once. Restoring `× 3 / 200` sends the word
back to 621 with `1/6 ammo[0]` on 622. Restoring the lake bed brings
`sz`/`v1z` back under the word.
