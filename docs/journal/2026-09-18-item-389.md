# Item 389 — the arrow is the animation's, and Great Lakes goes 9415 → 9451

The item was booked as "9415 is `1/29`'s", with `1/29` named as one of item
368's eight off-position units from 8442. The brief's own first instruction
was to re-measure before diagnosing. That instruction was the whole item.

## The premise was wrong, and twenty minutes said so

`track.py UNITDATA x_internal,y_internal --where who=1,o=29` over run97's
window, against this crate's own `RON_DEBUG_UNIT=1/29@8400-9420`, with the
dump's `BEGIN FRAME n` read as the end of sim-frame `n−1`:

> **Zero mismatches on 949 frames**, 8400 through 9349.

`1/29` is not off its position and has not been since 8442. It is on the
original's own coordinates on every frame of the window the residue floor
covers. That is the seventh booked mechanism in two days to be wrong with
the frame right (parked 377), and the cheapest refutation any of them has
had.

## What the two draws actually were

`attributed_sites` prints the frame as

```text
ours    unit 1/29 · unit 1/29 · Guy::set_anim+0x104b · Guy::set_anim+0x104b · Farms::inc_time+0x1ae
theirs  Guy::set_anim+0x104b · Farms::inc_time+0x1ae
```

and the label `unit 1/29` is not a site at all: it is `process_unit`'s own
enclosing mark, which `mark_sites` hands to any draw taken inside a unit's
turn without an inner site label. So the brief's "a site `trace::SITES` does
not name" was two **unlabelled** draws of ours, not an unnamed address of
theirs.

A probe in `fire_ammo` named them in one run: at frame 9415 our `1/29` fires
at the building `0/2004` with `s = 192`, which is `combat::scatter_point`'s
two draws. The third extra draw — a second `Guy::set_anim+0x104b` for the
bird `9/0` — is downstream of the shifted word, not a second fault.

So the question was never `1/29`'s position. It was: **why does the original
not shoot on 9415?**

## The original does not shoot on 9415, and the trace says who does

`report.py when Ammo::init 1 9000` answers first: the original's earliest
`Ammo::init` draws in the whole 24,000-frame capture are on **9425**. And
`report.py functions` puts the first `Objects::add_ammo` there too, with
`Unit::set_attack`, `Unit::set_attacking` and `UnitData::recharge` all
first-entered on **9415**. So on 9415 `Unit::fight` ran, set the swing and
the reload — and launched nothing.

The ebp chain on 9425 is the finding:

```text
Ammo::init+0xcd9 < Objects::add_ammo+0x119 < GraphicEvents::execute_game_events+0x40d
```

`Object::fire_ammo` is not in it. **A unit's arrow is added by its attack
animation's own event track**, walked once a frame by
`GraphicEvents::execute_game_events@008e48e0` under
`Objects::inc_time`'s per-object `+0xa0` / `+0x154` pairing — the clocks,
then the events. `docs/COMBAT.md` §9.0 has the gate, the target's
provenance (the current `UnitOrder`, not the guy) and the seams.

## The times are open data, and the conversion is measured

`unit_graphics.xml` carries the event track in plain XML —
`<RELEASEEVENT starttime="733" anim="CHAR_ATTACK3" .../>` — and
`rondata::artdata` already read that file for §3.1's lengths. The only
thing to decide was the millisecond-to-frame conversion, and the capture
decides it rather than a reading, because all four shipped products land
at remainder 198 or 199 of 200 and so separate truncation from rounding
on every single shot:

| shooter | slot | `starttime` | truncated | rounded | fired on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `1/29` | `CHAR_ATTACK3` | 733 | **10** | 11 | **9425** |
| `1/28` | `CHAR_ATTACK2` | 333 | **4** | 5 | **9426** |
| `1/29` | `CHAR_ATTACK3` | 1666 | **24** | 25 | **9439** |
| `1/28` | `CHAR_ATTACK2` | 1533 | **22** | 23 | **9444** |

Four for four on `ms × 3 / 200` truncated; nought for four on the rounding
`game_frames` uses for a *length*. The dump gives each guy's animation
clock frame by frame, so each row is read off the capture rather than
fitted.

No float anywhere: a `u32` multiply and an integer divide, at load.

**Great Lakes' word moves 9415 → 9451, +36**, and the count-word with it.
Every frame from 9415 to 9450 now agrees draw for draw — both archers'
first arrows, both their second, and the thirty frames of reload between.

**The value diff, on the frame it stops.** No record on disk prints an
`Ammo`, so the value this bought is read where the arrow lands: run100's
`BUILDDATA who=0 o=2004` is `damage 0` through block 9451 and **`damage 1`

launched at 9425, landing on sim-frame **9451**. That is the new word's own
frame, and the two draws this crate is missing there are
`Object::take_damage+0xe1`, twice.

**The negative, pinned.** `run53_s_24000_frames_put_the_ceiling_where_

arrow before 9425 fails on that fact alone, whatever its draw count says.
Made to fail on purpose: putting `fire_ammo` back in `fight`'s ranged arm
sends the word straight back to **9415** and the assertion prints
`left: [9415, 9422, 9425, 9426, 9439, 9444, 9445, 9449]` against
`right: [9425, 9426, 9439, 9444]` — the four the original takes, with four
of `fight`'s own in among them.

**The endpoint moved, in the good direction on the column that counts.**

— eleven positions closer and nine more of the roster unlinked, all
thirteen consecutive (`1/68`–`1/80`), 14,550 frames past the word.

`great_lakes_scholars_sit_on_their_universities` fired, which is what item

## What is not established

- **`UnitType +0x2cc`**, the type's fire-projectile graphic and the one
  other route into `Object::fire_ammo` for a unit. Longbowmen reach
  neither it nor the merchant arm beside it, and nothing on this disk
  reaches the field at all. This crate defers exactly when the install's
  `<RELEASEEVENT>` table knows the piece and keeps the immediate launch
  otherwise — a **seam**, chosen so that every sim built from tables alone
  (the unit tests, the soak) still means what it meant.
- **The launch point.** The original offsets the package by the event
  node's own position through `GraphicPieces::get_position`, a float and a
  graphic one; this crate launches from the unit's position.
- **`Guy::execute_events`' `uid` test.** This crate requires the target to
  be active; the original also requires the stored `uid`.
- **Melee.** `fight`'s `do_damage` arm is untouched. No capture here has a
  melee unit in a fight — run53's first `Object::do_damage` is 9451, and
  it is an arrow landing.
