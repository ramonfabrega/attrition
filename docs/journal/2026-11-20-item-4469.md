## The reading

`World::compute_reg_territory@006b0bb0:425` loads the city's `race`
(`CityData +0x5f`, a `char`) and tests it against the list's slot
(`local_3c`). When they are equal, the civic, flat and temple bonuses are
the slot's own (`local_70`, `*local_7c`, `*local_80`). When they differ,
the bonuses come from the arrays indexed by `race` (`aiStack_150`,
`local_b0`, `local_f0`). The limit (`:413`) reads the slot's alone.
`:467` and `:481` write the winner and the runner-up as `0xfffffffe`
unless `race == local_3c`.

`City::capture` gives an unassimilated city the loser's `race`
(`docs/CITIES.md` §7.3). Ours already kept `race` and the hand-over's rule.
`territory::Source` already had `owner_agrees`, and `ATTRITION.md` had the
−2 rule since its second reading. But `sync_territory` built every city
with `owner_agrees: true` and the owner's bonuses. `City::assimilate@00738e90`
sets `race` and calls `Region::fix_borders`, and ours does too
(`SimAssimilate::fix_borders`), so the −2 ends where theirs does.
