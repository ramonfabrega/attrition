## 1. What the original does, cheapest rung first

- **The card** (run1566b's card trace, the pass after 3279; now `the_trees_draw_on_the_card`). The trees are seven
  draws, one per texture: `tree_t_bark.tga` and the six `tree_tea_*` leaf layers of `#EARLY_AUTUMN_TREE_TEXTURES0`.
  Each draw has stage 0 `ADDSIGNED(TEXTURE, CURRENT)` (op 7: the shader header's macro order, which puts 12 at
  `BLENDTEXTUREALPHA`, as `card.rs` already pins), stage 1 `MODULATE(CURRENT, TFACTOR)` with tfactor 255 × 4, and
  alpha `MODULATE(TEXTURE, CURRENT)` then `SELECTARG2`. Enables are 0x20087, `alpha_func` 4 and `alpha_ref` 0. The
  options are `ALPHA_TEST VERTEX_COLOR_DIFFUSE TL_FAKE_LIGHT`, with no `LIGHTING` and no `NO_HDR`. A shadow draw
  (119, `tree_t_shadows.tga`) comes earlier in the pass.
- **The vertices** (run1559b's packet). `tools/render/tree_points.py` has a new `vdiff` row. All 12 cache entries
  print only `ff808080` (315 to 356 vertices each). Each vertex is eight dwords: xyz, the diffuse, uv0 and a zero
  uv1. The output is `run1559b-trees.txt` in the lab directory. Its first 347 rows match 4082's file byte for byte;
  after those, 4085's `rstate` rows stand where 4082's `--sweep` rows stood.
- **The shader** (`ps_global.hlsl`, read). Without `LIGHTING`, `TL_FAKE_LIGHT` computes `pow(color, 1.9) × 5`, and
  the frame's tone map follows (§3.14). d3dgl's argument table (`0x100fe0c8`, built at `0x10002730`; read with
  `llvm-objdump`) writes `TA_CURRENT` as `COLOR_CURRENT` on every stage (`0x1003ffa3` writes `TS1_COLORARG_2`), and
  the shader starts `outColor` at 1. So the shader read literally adds 0.5 to every texel. Direct3D's stage-0
  `CURRENT` is the diffuse, 0x80, which makes `ADDSIGNED` the texel itself. Both readings went into the count.

## 2. The rule, then the count (RENDERER §3.7, "The trees' own colour")

The rule was committed in `d20f0fc99`. A tree is scored when its own pixels in the key buffer number 40 off the
`INTERFACE`. Those are the pixels `TreePixels::Scene` keys after the leaves' alpha test and cull, minus the pixels a
silhouette takes. Each own pixel is read against the photograph's pixel under it; no footprint is needed, because the
trees stand in register. There are four candidates. The drawn one is the candidate the colour reader passes most
trees under; a tie goes to the luminance count, and a second tie keeps Texel.

| candidate | colour | luminance |

| Texel, `t + 0x80 − 127.5` | 21 | 56 |
| **FakeLight**, Texel through `fake_light` | **37** | **54** |
| Literal, `t + 127.5`, through `fake_light` | 0 | 0 |
| FakeLight at run1441's camera | 0 of 57 | 9 of 57 |

## 3. The binding, and what it moved

- **The painter.** `client::trees::DRAWN` is FakeLight. `paint_nearer` now colours the nearer tree as it is drawn.
  `paint_nearer_as(…, None)` keeps the raw texel for the_lit_pixel's leaf, whose 514 / 1,946 of 3,136 counts read
  the raw texture.
- **The client.** `World::tree_textured` gives one textured mesh per texture, on its one level
  (`TreeMeshes::leaf_mips`). `client::gpu` has a `fs_tree` pass: the texture sampled at level 0, bilinear and
  clamped, discarded at alpha 0, then the painter's arithmetic on the bytes (4171's plain `Unorm` target). The pass
  uses LessEqual with depth written and runs after the figures' soup and before the guys pass, whose depth test needs
  the trees. A face with no texture stays in the flat soup. `the_tree_pass_is_the_painter_s` asserts the shader text.
  My first version handed the result back decoded for an sRGB target. On 4171's plain target that read
  `_COLOUR_STILL` 18, an artefact, and it is fixed.
- **`RUN1559B_3279_COLOUR` 18 → 17, named** (`commander`'s word: a named regression). 0/1's 159 behind pixels agree
  81 → 66 over the drawn leaf, and 80 are needed. 0/6 goes 97 → 94 and stays in; 0/9 holds at 101. A probe that
  composed the silhouette before the tone map read 11; it was removed before the commit. `_COLOUR_LIT` goes 17 → 16
  (0/1), and `the_stored_normals`' pair goes (18, 16) → (17, 15). The 4172 rule's `lc.0 < 18` still holds.
- **The still** (4171's harness, `Before Rgba8Unorm`). The trees row reads 37 and 54, the painter's, and every tree
  agrees within 5 % of its painted count. `_COLOUR_STILL` holds at 14: 0/1 goes 30 → 64 (the painter's 66, all 213
  of its pixels within 8), and 0/5, 0/6 and 1/34 remain 4171b's. `_TRACED_STILL` goes **31 → 32**: 0/1's overlap
  rises 0.159 → 0.850 (ours 68 → 378, both 66 → 364), because its ring no longer holds a silhouette's red over flat
  green (4171a). Among the four readings, after-sRGB goes 32 → 33; every colour count holds.
- **The `Clock:` ceilings**: the client's `clock` test passes with the tree pass in (67 client tests green).

## 4. What this establishes, and what it does not

Diff-backed: the card's tree state (`the_trees_draw_on_the_card`), the row and the still's agreement with it. Read
only: the shader's `TL_FAKE_LIGHT` arithmetic, which the row's 37 against Texel's 21 supports but a reading alone
states, and the vertex diffuse, a packet row with no test. Not established: the blend (4173a), why 0/1 prefers the
raw texel (4173b), the shadows (4173c), and the two families of models (4173d).
