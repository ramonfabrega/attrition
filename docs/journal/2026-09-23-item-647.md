`1/2007` stands at (36960, 23904): 7 × 7, tiles 189–195 × 121–127,

`docs/VISION.md` §6.1 named the writer as a seam: *"`Wall::start`'s own
direct `seen2` write over its footprint (the owner's bit) is not made; it
is the owner's own bit over ground the owner's own line of sight
covers."* `Wall::start@0063e810` has the loop, between `mask_me` and
`check_ever_seen`. It walks `x_size × y_size`, not grown, and for every
tile on the map it ors `1 << who` into `seen2` at `tile >> 1` and into the
cell's `WData +0x14`. It is a raw byte write, so no `set_seen` side
effect follows. The premise fails here because the AI places a city out
of its own sight.
