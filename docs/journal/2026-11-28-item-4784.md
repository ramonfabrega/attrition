## The reading

`Object::do_damage@0064a480:953`–`:1050`, read from the decompile and checked against the listing (`64bc2b`–`64bec5`).
`docs/COMBAT.md` §7.4 has it whole. The predicate in brief:

- the attacker's `is(0x83)` (`FLAMETHROWER`);
- the target's vslot `0x18` answers 0, so it is a building;
- `!can_carry(AIR)`;
- `n = num_inside(1)`.

Then come `eject_contents(1, -1, 0, 1)` (on a building, just the 0x4000) and, with `n` non-zero and fewer than
`x_size >> 1` scorched bodies in the death pool, `x_size >> 1` Citizens. Each is `init_unit` on the building's point,
then `go_inside`, then `come_out`, and each is closed through vslot `0x150` (`Unit::close`, per `vtables.txt`) with
`(4, -1, 0)`.

`init_unit` and `go_inside` are passed the same register as the owner, the target's `param_2`. The `0x800000` bit is
`Unit::init:578`'s and `Unit::close:79`'s, and `Leader::compute_score` clears it. Ours already sets it in `add_unit`,
so it follows. The pool's `+0x20` is `gpiece` (`Objects::add_death@00653b60` writes `+0x18`/`+0x1c` who and o).
