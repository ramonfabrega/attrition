## How it was found

- **The item's shapes do not stand on one state.** A scratch probe printed three stand-ups of run1429's game
  (`GreatLakesAztecModern`) whole. The closing state's and the word walk's are equal, byte for byte. A window's
  is 143 KB shorter: `frame_guys` (22,344 printed lines: the guys of frames no seed is installed on),
  `sim.roster.art.lengths` (204 lines, 51 lengths the capture's later frames show) and one note. The window
  reads the capture's replay setup; the other two parse the whole log. Ticked side by side to frame 3389 the
  two are equal less those three fields on every hundredth frame and on the last. So a game has two trunks,
  and the key being the state keeps them apart without a rule that says so.
- **Every stand-up carries the phase trace.** `walk_word` sets `trace_phases`, and the first cut cleaned its
  rungs of the trace before leaving them. The log said the state was already traced at frame 0 (473 marks):
  `build_sim` turns it on. The word walk is the plain walk with a reader beside it, and its rungs are its
  states as they are.
- **What a rung costs** (run1429's game, a quiet moment): the print 10.5 ms for 6.2 MB, a clone at frame 0
  0.5 ms, the 3,389 ticks 487 ms, the stand-up about 0.4 s of which the install's load is 0.25 s.
- **Seen red.** The verifier, on a rung with one bit of its stream flipped: `the trunk's rung on 256 did not
  walk to the state a fresh walk reaches on 300` (`the_verifier_refuses_a_rung_that_is_not_the_plain_walk_s_state`).
  And by accident: the first run of the unit tests had one red, a test finding another test's rungs, because
  two tests used one seed. The store is keyed by content and did what it says.
- **The harness the draft commits has run**: `cargo test --release -p rondata --lib -- harness::trunk
  ledger::tests`, cargo exit 0, 9 of 9 (the trunk's six and the ledger's three).

## Where this departs from the item's list, and why

The commander's note: the list is a design, not an order.

- **Not "the whole-walk shapes on the group's kept walk".** The kept walk is keyed by input names, and a
  closing state names none: 193 of them are written out inline. Keying by the state needed one edit in
  `walk_to_close_quitting` and serves every caller, and it cannot share between two stand-ups that differ.
- **The word walk and the audit resume nothing.** Each compares or observes every frame; sharing those frames
  would share a comparison. The word walk leaves rungs; the audit walks through `ReplaySession` and was left
  alone.
- **Takes as one game, the coverage and ages drivers, the pins as rows: not started.**

## Parked

- **4220a. A rung holds every frame's sites.** Every stand-up traces its phases, so `Built::frame_sites`
  grows a row a frame on every walk and a rung clones it. Nine groups peak over 1.5 times their old number
  (`game:run1256` 1,717 to 3,689 MiB). Whether a walk that reads no sites could run untraced was not tried: it
  would change the state every test stands on.
- **4220b. 140 rungs is fewer than the closing states.** 112 closing states run after their module's word
  walk in item 4219's gate; the tally's 140 covers them, the packets and the windows' approaches together.
  Which closing states found no rung was not listed.
- **4220c. `HeadSerial` prints as its name.** A unit's head-order serial is identity, compared for change
  inside a tick and never for value; two states that differ only in it print equal. A rung is a clone of a
  state reached by ticks, so it carries the serial its walk had.
