## What was booked, and what it turned out to be

The booking: find who reads the `<FARM>` element, `farm_wheat_1.tga` and the stalk colours, then the farm's
condition 1, for K +1. The reader turned up, and condition 1 holds. K +1 did not hold, because the farm's
condition 2 had only held vacuously.

- **The reader is `Farms::init@008d9310`**, called from `Game::init@0058c480`, not `GraphicPieces`. It walks
  `building_graphics.xml`'s `Root/Buildings/Farm` (internal strings 400, 401 and 2673, names compared without
  case). It reads the four `stalk_*` (2674–2677) into bytes at `farms + 0x40`, and `min_height`/`max_height`
  (2678, 2679) × 3.0 into `+0x50`/`+0x4c`. The string offsets are `int_str_array + 20·n`, counting the
  self-closing `<STRING/>` entries; a first count that skipped them was eight off.
- **`farm_wheat_1.tga` is not the farm's `texture=`.** The loader reads that attribute into the scratch `String`
  `msg` (`0xed6730`; the listing, `008d93c8`–`008d93e9`) and drops it. `FarmsOut::draw@008d75f0` binds stage 0
  to `&tilesets.cur_tileset->wheat_texture` (`008d84b1`–`008d84c5`). `TileSet::init@0087b370` fills that from
  the tileset's `FARMTEX/TEXTURE file=`. The seventeen tilesets name three images.
- **A farm is not a mesh at a point.** `FarmsOut::draw` builds a wheat field over the footprint every frame:
  stalks of the `<FARM>` colour, between its two heights, on the farm's `FarmStruct` corner heights. The type is
  `0x1a1` (417), the one `Build::init@00629740` hands to `Farms::add@008d8a40`.
- **The lobby's tileset is `early_autumn`.** Great Lakes draws one of thirteen. The seed that run1428 prints
  after `map.cpp` 7805 is 2,726,892,157, the one `mapgen`'s own test pins, and it draws `early_autumn`. My first
  reading took the seed printed *before* the record (87,628,868, which draws `Dirty`), and the pin caught it.
  Both name `farm_wheat_1.tga`.
