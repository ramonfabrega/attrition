## Step 1: the issuers under the emulator, and the readers

A scratch script on `command_oracle.py`'s fixture synthesized a `Build`
on `Build::vftable`, its `BuildTypeData`, the type list and the leader's
bitmask. The table it prints is derived from the user's executable and
stays out of git.

**The issuers**:
- `issue_queue_up(group [b], type, num)` appends 14 bytes: the group,
  then `18 [type][num]`.
- `issue_buildmask(…, 0x40, set)` writes `set` 1, as in §32.

**`valid_buildmask(0x40)` answers 1 only on a training building with a
train job queued.** A train job is a unit type with its bit set. An
empty queue, a research entry, a tech entry, or a building that does not
train each answer 0.

**`action_buildmask(0x40)` toggles**: 4104 ↔ 4168, whatever `set` says,
and 4296 → 4232 (the `0x80` bit is left alone). With only a research
entry queued, or an empty queue, it writes nothing.

**`unqueue(0, 0)` on a one-entry queue clears the bit** (4168 → 4104); on
a two-entry queue the bit stays.

`do_queue`'s arm was read from the listing:
- `61ec24` reads the word **before** the `unqueue(i, 0)` at `61ec35`;
- `61ec4f` clears the bit;
- `61ec59` calls `queue_up(type, 0)`;
- `61ec66` sets the bit back on success.

**The readers of `+0x60 & 0x40`**, grepped by offset in every spelling:
- in the simulation: `do_queue`, `unqueue`, `clean_queue`,
  `action_unqueue`, and the out-of-v1 scenario toggle;
- the interface: `IFaceSelected`, `IFaceOptions`, `GroupData`, and
  `GroupOut::issue_queue_up`'s message;
- **none is the AI's.**

The `& 0xffbf` stores in `come_out`, `action_alarm`, `start_me` and
`mask_me` are a city flag or a tile mask.

**The brief's claim holds**: `0x40` is repeat production. The emulator
