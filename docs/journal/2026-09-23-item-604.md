## What the loader said, and why this was not a generator reading

The booking expected `MountainRange::init` to load an authored list. It does
not. It loads the template's `TEMPLATE_TEX` as a 32-bit scratch image and
derives both lists from its **alpha**. A tile is the range's when its four
corner pixels are set. A cell is solid when more than 15 of 25 samples over
its 16-pixel block are set. The Python prototype matched all three packet
templates on its first run, once the TGA's bottom-left origin was
honoured. With the file's own row order, all three failed.

The placements did not need a map-generator reading either.
`GameLog::dump_mountains` prints them in every `DUMP_ALL` head, and both
maps' harnesses already borrow one: run38 for East Indies, run3 and run12
for Great Lakes. run38's eighteen matched the packet's list. So the
brief's stop condition ("if it needs a much larger map-generator reading")
never applied.

## What would have killed each reading, written before the run

- **The alpha rule** would be killed if any of the three templates
  disagreed with the packet's lists, in order. None did, with 107 cells.
- **The placement frame** (`4 · loc + off` for tiles) would be killed if
  the union of the placed tiles differed from the start dump's
  `OBJECT_MOUNTAIN` tiles, on either map. It is exact on both: 1,956 tiles
  on East Indies. The Great Lakes union also needs no packet.
- **The index** would be killed if `find_nearest`'s placed index differed
  from run80's `mtn`. It is 6 and 0, both right.
