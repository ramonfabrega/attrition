## Stage 1: the layer

`crates/client/src/input.rs` is new. It holds `Event`, `View`, `Input::event`, `ground_at` and `footprint`, and it is
tested without a window. Every test's name carries `minted`.

The order door is `FreeRunner::issue`. It calls `Stream::push`, which encodes and decodes the commands as a
recording stores them, into a `Stream::pump_empty` stream. The script moved from the test-only `diff::minted` into
`rondata::script`, so the client can name it. `diff::minted` now imports it, and `start_walk`'s `LOBBY_DUMP` is the
script's.

The original's click inverse was not opened. The footprint is coarser than the script's coordinate, but a rule read
there could not make an arbitrary integer reachable at that zoom, so the reading could not move the count (parked
4145a).

One defect was found and fixed. At ×8 many pixels hold no integer point, and `ground_at` returned `None` for them;
the chosen building was dropped with it. Now the rounded point stands for such a pixel, and the choice is kept until
a placement lands. `minted_a_fine_pixel_still_names_the_ground` covers this.
