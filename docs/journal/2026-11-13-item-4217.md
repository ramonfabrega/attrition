## 1. The first act: the card's normals on draws 0–35

run1567's card trace holds no stream for any land draw: the land's buffers are static, and the probe dumps only
`get_internal_stream`'s. What the card does show for draws 0–35: `NORMAL_MAP SPEC_MAP GLOSS_MAP ENABLE_LIGHTING
ENABLE_VERTEX_COLOR_DIFFUSE`, `gView` orthonormal, `gWorld` and `gWorldInverseTranspose` the identity. So a normal
(0, 0, 1) lights at 0.7071 after `vs_global.hlsl`'s `transformNormal` and the shader's `L · gView`.

The normals themselves are on the packet. `TerrainOut` `0x00C0B0F0` `+0x3f4` holds the normals, `+0x410` the colours
and `+0x4a4` the heights, a 241 × 241 grid.

The vertex colour's constants are the tileset's `<LIGHTING>`. A least-squares fit over the packet's unsaturated
channels gave slope 1.360 and intercepts (0.008, 0.028, 0.178) in r, g, b, residual at most one byte. That is (0.01,
0.03, 0.18) truncated. `TileSet::init` reads the `<COLOR>`s positionally; `GraphicPieces::init_lighting@00904a30`
reorders them to r, g, b, a; d3dgl's `DIFFUSE` is `R8G8B8A8_UNORM` (`0x100dce34`). So red is the low byte, and no swap.
