## The fix: a rider is counted by its container

`Leader::plan_strategy@006b9620`'s unit census, read from the decompile
(`docs/AI.md` §58.1), has three parts that bear on a rider.

- **The region.** A unit whose `inside_up ≥ 0` takes its region from the
  outermost container's tile. `ObjectData::get_inside` walks the chain
  through units.
- **The citizen branch, off the map**, has two arms:
  - the oil platform;
  - a container that is a unit, sea-domain, with a current order that
    answers `is_move` (vslot `+0x14`; every `MoveOrder` class does). Here
    the region of the cell under `MoveOrder::x/y` goes into the sweep's
    region local, and it counts `reg_xport_peasants[r]++` and
    `xport_peasants++`. The rider is never counted free.
- **The shared local.** The `reg_active` family after the worker branch
  reads that same local. So the rider is active in region 5, the barge's
  destination, and not in the ocean it sails on. The dump's
  `reg_active[5] = 1` is that.

This crate checked only `inside` (a building), so it counted the rider on
the map where it boarded.

- `census_unit_region` takes a boat's tile;
- `census_citizen` sends a rider to the off-map arm;
- `census_citizen_inside` has the boat arm, and returns the move point's
  region, which `census_units` carries into `reg_active`.

The unit test `a_rider_is_an_xport_peasant_where_its_boat_is_going` was
made to fail on purpose by sending the rider down the on-map arm.
