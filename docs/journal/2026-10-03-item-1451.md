## Arithmetic first, then the listing

A temporary print in `create_buildings`, `building_value` and
`gather_value` (removed) gave the crate's offer: base 170000, multiplier
198, then ×4/5 (no free peasants in the region) and ÷2 (one farm queued),
which makes 52593. Holding the base fixed, the original's 45156 needs a
multiplier of exactly 170. Walking the chain back, 170 is 384 after the
two ×2/3 steps where ours had 448. That fits `(k + X) × m0 / k` with
X = 2 where the crate has 3. I wrote that down before opening the listing
(`notes.md`).

The listing of `Leader::create_buildings@006c1be0` has `max(3 −
GoodTypeData::largest_gather, ter)` in both arms. The field was named
from the type record (`+0x2ec`), and the third reading
(`create-buildings.md` §3.2) has the same formula. The crate's seam list
stood in 0 for it. `GoodType::compute_largest_gather@0066e920` writes the
field at load, clamped to `[1, 2]`, so it is never 0. On the shipped land
table it is 1 for all six goods. `crate::world::largest_gather` now
computes it from `LANDS`. The payoff probe gave **45156** exactly.
