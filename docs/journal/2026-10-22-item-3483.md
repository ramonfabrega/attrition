# Item 3483 — where a dumped frame's seconds go (2026-10-22, `att-3483`, Opus 5.5)

A `coverage` lane of the fifty-first tranche, on the click-free pool, base `de10ec3c`. It is a timing probe, with no
simulation change and nothing pinned. `blind::` does not move.

**The result: neither the writer nor the walkers. `OutputDebugStringW` is 95 % of a dumped frame.** Every line the
gamelog takes is also rendered a second time and handed to the debugger, and that costs about 47 µs a line under
Wine. The file's whole path is about 0.1 s of a 2.6 s frame. Turning the mirror off with a pref takes a dumped frame
from 3.82 s to 0.073 s on the same blocks. The gamelog stays the same outside its header's timing lines.

## The split, in seconds and bytes a dumped frame

Exclusive seconds (a site's own time, with its timed callees taken off), averaged over each take's timed dumped
frames. Bytes are the gamelog's ` BEGIN FRAME` blocks, and they match the bytes the CRT handed `WriteFile`. The
three booked takes ran side by side on lanes 1–3 under the commander's gate sweeps, so their absolute seconds run
about 2× run1101j's quiet frames (2.6 s). The shares hold on every take.

- **The three columns the booking named**: `Log::say`'s own code is about 1 %. The CRT's two imports
  (`__stdio_common_vfwprintf` and `fputwc`, the format and the wide-to-narrow text conversion into the stream's
  buffer) are about 2 %. The per-category `fflush(NULL)` is under a millisecond, three calls a frame. The walkers
  (`full_dump` and `dump_units`/`dump_leaders` with every object's `log_data`) are 0.4 %. `NetDaemon::process_all`
  is under a millisecond. The syscall (`WriteFile`, 1,084 calls of about 1.4 KB) is 0.2–0.5 %.
- **Bytes**: a `UNITS=3,LEADERS=9` block is 1.531 MB and a `LEADERS=9` block 1.450 MB. On the golden start the
  units add 81 KB and 4,857 lines. `LEADERS=9` is the frame. `fputwc` is called 166,786 times a frame, for the
  indent, one space a call.
- **The stride** (run1112): the kept blocks cost what run1101's do, and the skipped frames cost 0.004–0.007 s, the
  same as an undumped frame. A frame outside the window costs 0.004 s at `cover=1`. So nearly all of a pool take's
  minute is the dumped frames' mirror.
- **The probe's own cost** (parked 3483b): 21–26 ns a timed call by the calibration loop, about 0.01 s a dumped frame.
  run1101d's untimed frames sit within 0.005 s of its timed ones. run1101's `dumptime=2` comparison (6.93 s untimed
  against 6.21 s timed) is the three-take load's noise. The instrument is "the same stubs with the clock off": the
  off frames still enter every stub, and `dt_enter` returns at once.

## Where the mirror comes from

`Log::say@00a3ab00` (and its `ulong` and `float` twins), after the `fwprintf`: `if (flags & 2)` → `String::printf`
→ `(*kernel32_OutputDebugStringW_Ptr)(line)`. `Log::init@00a3baa0` sets flag 2 when `Prefs::get` of
`AllowLogs_ToConsole` (the wide string at 0xb1abb8) is non-zero, and its default is 1. Neither the pool's `rise.ini`
nor its `rise2.ini` carries the key. The pointer is filled by the game after the tracer attaches, so the clock swaps
it at the first frame. Under Wine `OutputDebugStringW` raises a debug-print exception to an absent debugger, and
each call costs about 47 µs.

**For 3484** (parked 3483a): its hook point is none of the three. A byte-identical writer at `Log::say`, the CRT
imports or `fflush` could save at most the file path's ~0.1 s of a quiet 2.6 s frame. The 95 % is the pref, which a
writer of the same bytes leaves running. So 3484 as booked is not worth an item. There are two zero-code shapes.
The pool can stage `--console-log 0` (added here; it writes the pref into the lane's `rise.ini`, which the runner
restores). Or 3484's `fastlog=1` can become that pref. Either is checked as 3484 books, by comparing the gamelog
with an unhooked twin on the three lobbies, masking the header's load timings and `Sys Memory`, which differ between
any two takes. run1101j and run1101k already show it on the golden start to 40.

## How it was built, and the two scratch failures

`tools/trace/dumptime.h`, included by `tracer.c` before `is_hook_site`:
- **Code sites** displace their prologue into a stub. Coverage leaves them alone (`dt_is_site`).
- **Imports** (the executable's IAT slots at 0xac5634, 0xac55d4, 0xac55c8, 0xac55cc, 0xac5610; ucrtbase's own
  `WriteFile` slot, found by walking its import table) swap a pointer.
- **Every stub** calls `dt_enter` with the caller's return slot. On do_frame's thread, with the clock on,
  `dt_enter` pushes the entry on a shadow stack and points the slot at the site's exit thunk. The thunk stamps the
  exit and returns to the saved address. No convention is assumed and no argument is read except `WriteFile`'s
  count. `rdtsc` (1 GHz under Rosetta by the calibration) is read twice a call; QPC once a frame.

The bisection cost ten scratch takes to frame 40 (`~/ron-golden/run1101a`…`k`, not filed) and one dead take at the
item's own number:
- **The first run1101, run1101a and run1101b died on frame 0** (`0xc0000008` and a read at 0x1e in `Log::begin`).
  The exit thunk is 25 bytes, and at +40 of a 64-byte slot its `ret` sat on the next site's first byte, which that
  site's stub then overwrote with `push eax`. run1101c, `WriteFile` alone, survived only because the calibration
  site's `push eax` let it fall through into a working stub. Its numbers were thrown away. The slot is now 80 bytes,
  with the thunk at +48.
- **run1101e, f, h and i stalled before frame 0's record** on lanes 1 and 2, after run1101g (the two new code sites
  alone) passed. The stall was the `VirtualProtect` of the `.data` page holding `kernel32_OutputDebugStringW_Ptr`,
  at the first frame with the game's threads up. The swap is now a plain aligned store. `.data` is writable.
- **The first run1101's directory** is moved, not deleted, to `~/ron-golden/run1101-dead-frame0`.

The cursor pin (`--cursor 1400,1000`, Loop 3432) is clean against an unpinned twin on all three booked takes:
`rngcmp.py` against run1084 gives 607 in common, 0 differing and 607 identical on each.
