Ten never-backed simulation functions with a body, packet-free, no subagents, **all ten generated** by
`tools/sweep_gen.py obj`. **`Census:` 556 → 566 of 3,611** (`tools/census.py --layers` on the merged tree, after
`ccc update` to `bd5eb772`, which brought a PARKED amendment and moved nothing in the census);
`census::SIMULATION_BACKED` re-pinned. No parting: all ten agree on every row on the first run. Three were made to
fail on purpose and did: `Map::calc_bonus` with `0x3d` read as `0x3e`, `WorldData::is_tocean_slow` with land 1
read as 3, and `tile_corner_of` with the odd-size snap at a whole tile. A fourth break did **not** fail —
`tile_corner_of` with `size >> 1` written `size / 2` — because the sizes are held to `0..=9`; the sweep is blind to
the `sar` on a negative size, which no footprint has.

**The generator** (parked 2123, Loop 2003's eleventh reach; its test fails on the tree before): seven of the ten
read a table through a pointer, so `obj` gained `--place LOC:EXPR:SIZE` — the word at LOC (`m+OFF`, `cN+OFF`,
`pN+OFF` or an address expression) written as `buffer − EXPR` over the row's arguments and windows, a new `p`
window; the shape 2112 wrote by hand three times (2113) and the world cell array 2046 asked for, closed both.
Beside it: `--wbias` (a window's word held every row, `*` every word), `--fill` (a table laid by expression — the

(2045's two candidates; `--name` because `WorldData::get_land` at 0x6b4730 snakes to 1993's module for 0x46f9d0 —
the first generation overwrote that sweep and was restored from git before anything was committed), and
`--slotbase OFF:ADDR` (a stubbed slot laid as its base address half the rows, a flag column after the answers).
