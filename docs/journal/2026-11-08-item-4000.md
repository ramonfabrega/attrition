## The mechanism (read off the decompile, and confirmed by the diff)

1. **`Army::add_unit@006f9f40` takes the push arm only when the army lists no group** (`+0x96 < 1 || list[0] < 0`).
   `Group::kill` empties and clears the record (`Group::clear(-1)`), but nothing writes the army's `list[0]`. So
   theirs' 642 join (`think_attack_join_army` → `add_to_army` → `add_unit(6)`) goes through the record's own
   `Group::add`. On 642 theirs enters no `push_group`, `get_open_slot` or `copy_group`.
   - At every step of that squad walk, `get_num`'s normalize prunes the figure added the step before, because its
     `+0x80` still names 66. Only `8` stays listed.
   - `set_group` then points all three at 64.
   - Ours had item 518's `|| !live` arm, which pushes the whole chain `[6 7 8]`.
2. **`Group::sort` runs inside `compute_form`, before the mirror test** (`compute_form@00707c80`: `find_leader` at
   `:37`, `Form::categorize` → `Group::sort` at `:72`, the `facing` toggle test at `:103`, `Form::compute` after it).
   - The list `[8 6 7]` has a follower first, so the sort kills the squad whole (`Group::kill` → `clear(-1)`). That
     zeroes `facing` (`+0x48`), and every back-pointer that named 64 goes to −1.
   - The sort then re-adds the squad with `Group::add`, which never writes `+0x80`.
   - The layout's mirror is therefore 0, and so is the orders' `facing` (`Form +0xe88`, `Form::compute@0072e8e0:16`).
3. Ours read `facing` and `reverse` before its sort, and its `seat_kill` never cleared a record it emptied.
