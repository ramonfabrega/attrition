- `create_buildings`' wonder arm, listing `6c2bf0`–`6c2cac`, multiplies
  by `buildtypes[t]->vslot(+0x118)()`. The export calls `BuildData`'s
  `+0x118` `health_level`; the call is on a type. Off the PE: the vtable
  at `0xb428d4` is `.?AVBuildTypeData@@` by its RTTI, and its `+0x118`
  holds `0x661ba0`, `ObjectTypeData::get_wonder_value`. That returns
  `wonder_val` (`+0x2d0`), which `BuildType::init` reads from
  `WONDER_VAL`. Tikal's is 2.
- The product also carries `(team + 1)(ev + 1)`. `get_team_wonder_value`
  sums `get_wonder_value` over the team. That function walks the wonder
  list under `wonder_mark`, each wonder's type at `+0x118` again
  (`Build`'s `+0x14c` is `0x46cf20`, read off the PE). The dump's block
  14784 holds one wonder in the whole game: who=1's Pyramids
  (`orig_type 526`, `wonder 0`), worth 1.
- `2 × (1 + 1) = 4`. This crate read the factor as 1 and every term of
  the bookkeeping as empty (parked 798).
