# Item 3495 — a patrol's waypoints under a held Shift (2026-10-22, `att-3495`, Opus 5.5)

A `coverage` lane of the fifty-first tranche, on base `47ebd6e2`. Booked as a capture: chapter ten's lobby at
`cover=1`, `622 select 6`, and a new `@hold 16 640` tracer verb, so that `UnitOut::draw_orders@006075f0` runs its
order loop under Shift and calls the patrol's +0x5c, the vtordisp thunk 0x48387c, whose `jmp` reaches
`PatrolOrder::get_patrol_order` (0x4843e0, a `NEVER` row).

**The result: no capture, and no verb. The row is unreachable, and it moves to `blind::RESIDUE` (open 68 → 67,
`NEVER` stays 250).** I checked the vtable slot before building the hook, and the booking's premise failed on the
image's own bytes.

## What killed it

- Chapter ten's patrol is not a `PatrolOrder`. `add_patrol_order` asks for `GroupPatrolOrder` (type 22), as
  `tools/gamelog/golden/chapter10.cmd` and `docs/GOLDEN.md` §18 say, and run184's block 622 printed it on disk.
  `GroupPatrolOrder::vftable` (0xb48498) +0x5c reads **0x482dac** in the shipped image (`2b 49 fc e9 0c 00 00 00`:
  `sub ecx, [ecx-4]; jmp 0x482dc0`), and 0x482dc0 is `lea eax, [ecx-0x5c]; ret` (the export folds it under
  `FormOrder::get_form_order`). `AirPatrolOrder::vftable` (0xb48c50) +0x5c is 0x4843bc, a thunk to 0x4843d0.
  Only `PatrolOrder::vftable` (0xb48aec) holds 0x48387c, at +0x5c and +0xc8.
- `report.py <exe> refs`: 0x4843e0's one reference is the thunk's `jmp` (0x48387f). The thunk's only references
  are those two slots. `PatrolOrder::vftable`'s references are the constructor `+0x30` and `~PatrolOrder+0x2a`.
  The constructor (0x483720) is called only from `GroupPatrolOrder::GroupPatrolOrder+0x4f` and
  `AirPatrolOrder::AirPatrolOrder+0x4f`. Each of them then re-points the vptr at its own vftable
  (`GroupPatrolOrder@00481fe0.c:30`). `get_new_order@00730550` builds only the two derived kinds.
- Neither the constructor nor the destructor (the only windows in which the vptr is `PatrolOrder::vftable`)
  dispatches a virtual: their bodies only write the vptr and the two `SimpleArray<Coord>`s, and call `clear`
  directly.

So no run of the original can enter 0x4843e0, with or without Shift. This follows the precedent of the
`GroupAttackOrder::log_data@00485140` row: "reached only through its vtordisp thunk's jmp". Per CLAUDE.md's rule
that a hypothesis the floor kills is not built by the item that killed it, `@hold` stays unbuilt and its design
parks under 3442a's own scores (parked 3495a). A held-Shift take of this lobby would enter 0x482dac/0x482dc0,
which no document cites, so it would move no row.
