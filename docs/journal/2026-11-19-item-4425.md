`form_member_active` keeps the record's old entry, negated when the layout
is `flipped`. `form_member_active` becomes `pub(crate)`. The test
`a_member_off_the_map_at_a_layout_keeps_its_record_offset`
(`groups/group/tests.rs`) lays out three members, then takes the third off
the map and moves the group again. When the move is aligned, the third keeps its
entry. When it is opposed, the third takes the entry's negation.
