## 3. The seed (4233c)

- **The listing** (`0x008db840` to `0x008db88e`), SSE single precision: `seed = cvttss2si(frame · S · k + S) + k`,
  with `S = (pos.y + pos.x) + pos.z` and `k = desc.random · counter · 47`. `emit_build_particles` sets `random` 1.
  On frame 3280, `S` 34,452 and `k` 7,285 put the product near 8e11, and past `i32` `cvttss2si` gives
  `0x80000000`. Every birth past the first few frames therefore seeds `0x80000000 + 47 · counter`. The frame and
  the anchor drop out, and the four slots' noise is the same for the same count.
- **`Random::get@00a39d70`**: `seed · 0x19660d + 0x3c6ef35f`, then `lo + ((seed & 0xffff) · (hi − lo) >> 16)`. `hi` is
  never drawn, so the smoke's velocity noise is −1 or 0 over 340, never +1.
- **The value diff**: `seedpos.py`, and `client::particle::tests::the_starts_are_the_packets`. The particles are
  numbered newest first from `counter − 1` (`ParticleEmitter +0x50`: smoke 156 on run1559b, 165 on run1570).
  - Slot 8 on run1559b, `time_left` 6087 → 82: the packet's z position noise is 9, 6, 3, 0, −3, −6, −9, −12, −16,
    −19, −22, −25, −28, −31, −34, −37, −40, −44.
  - The draws for counts 155 → 138 give the same.
  - The velocity's z noise is exactly −1/340 or 0 on each (`seedz.py`).
- **Tally.** 141 of 144 smoke particles stand on the packet's z within half a unit, and the count shifted by ±1
  lands 0. The three that miss are slot 10's oldest on run1559b, residual −7.4 to −13.9 over 340. The ground
  (`find_data_z`) kept them out of the wind for about a second, which our motion models.
- **The draws sweep.** Successive counts step by a near-constant amount on each axis, so a life's starts trace a
  line rather than a scatter.

**What the seed cannot give: the count** (4240a). The packets' smoke births (by `time_left`, slot 8, run1559b) are
gaps of 400, 400, 400, 58, 400, 363, 400, 400, 315, 400, 400, 400, 90, 400, 379, 400, 400: 18 live where `freq`
gives 15.6.
- `prep@008dc520` calls `back_calc@008dc5f0` each render while in view. Births run from `left_over +
  max(last_view_time, now − life)`, a `freq` apart, to `now`, and the overshoot is carried. Out of view the
  particles are cleared.
- `births.py`, from the emitters' `start_time` 166,361 (frame 2483) with a `prep` every game frame, gives counter
  148 (the packet's 156), gaps of 334 to 400, and 17 live. The fire gives 250 (the packet's 239). One to eight
  calls a frame give the same.
- The runs of exactly 400 are catch-ups after a gap between renders or a clear, which is the capture's render cadence
  and view.
- The particles' own clock lags the game's too: run1559b's newest smoke birth, at 219,616, is 144 ms old on the
  packet (frame 3280's clock), where `end_time` 220,698 says frame 3293.

So ours births on each multiple of `freq`, with the count `born / freq`.
