## Why

`Guy::move:203` is `GuyData::get_speed@005de410`. Its listing calls
the unit's vslot `+0x17c` with the **figure's** `x`/`y` and 1. That is
`UnitData::get_speed@00608720`, layer 3, so the step takes the order
scale. The `+9` is behind `guy_num < squad_size` (`5de473`, `jge`), and
`squad_size` is 1.

`1/67`'s action is GUARD, and its `unit_masks` is `0x848008`, which
carries `0x40000`. So the speed is ×10/8: 42 → 52, and × 11/8 → **71**.
The crate stepped every tracked crew figure on `movement.speed`, the
type's base: 42 × 11/8 = 57.

This is parked **1333** exactly: "ours 57 against 71 on run517's 9222
and run511's 9328". It is Great Sahara Toughest's Senator `1/80`.
