## The reading

`Object::close@00647160` skips `remove_from_world` when `param_1` is non-zero and the type's vslot `+0x120`
(`blocks_while_dead`) answers. `Unit::close@0060ee50` hands `Object::close` its own `param_1` (`:602`). But at `:592`
it **zeroes** it on every path where the death-animation arm did not run (`:460`–`:591`). The arm needs `local_1c`
clear (the unpacked merchant's arm sets it), vslot `+8` and vslot `+0xbc` (on the map), and `unit_masks & 1` clear (no
decoy). So a decoy's close always takes `remove_from_world`, and draws no death record.

Ours' `close_unit` already took that gate for the draw (item 4488's merchant clause, the decoy and `on_map` tests). But
`keeps_disc` asked only `dtype != 0`, `on_map` and the flag. So the decoy Tank was kept as a wreck, on the chain and
with its disc. It had no death record, so no cull would ever have cleared it.
