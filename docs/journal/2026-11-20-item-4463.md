   `flags & 0xbf` and `+0x40`. Ours now copies `damage_marked` (`0x10`),
   `attacking` (`0x80`) and `visible`.

   slot in place, and `init_city` reuses the first dead one. The builds
   and cities walks now use `Sim::city_index_in_owner`, which counts dead
   slots, where they used the rank among live cities.
5. **`traded_with`**: `City::capture@00736c40:77`–`84` copies the eight
   words to the new record.
6. **The caravan**: `City::close@00737550:202` calls `Unit::repath` on every
   caravan linked to the closed city. `repath` pops its move, `do_trade`
   finds the home closed and kills the `TRADE_ROUTE`, and
   `kill_current_order@005e2cb0:119`–`120` ends the route inside the kill.
   Ours had no repath arm and no trade arm in `kill_order`, so it ended
   the route a frame late in `caravan_work`. `end_trade_route` now also
   clears the order's `loaded` and recounts `trade_val` at each live end,
