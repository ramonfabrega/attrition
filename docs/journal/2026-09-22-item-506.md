## Why the fix is the mechanism and not a coincidence

Twenty lines, and then the probe that matters. With the passes in, this
crate buys on the original's own frame — and still buys the wrong good,
timber at 94 rather than food at 128. Probed inside the pass on 10576,
its own price for food is **128**: the number the original paid, to the
unit. What differs is the **purse**, 124 against 128, and the buy pass
walks goods in index order, so food is asked first on both sides and four
wealth decide which good the AI comes away with.

So the frame, the function, the quantity and the price are all measured;
what is left is `1/income[2:wealth]`, 960 here against 992 on all 245
blocks, which run107 says was not there at 9199. That is the successor,
and it is a different mechanic.
