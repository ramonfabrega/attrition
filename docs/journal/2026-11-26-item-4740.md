2. **The fields.** I decoded the guy record by `GUY_FIELDS`: `y`, `angle`, `last_angle`, `des_y`, `des_angle` and

3. **The unit.** `RON_DEBUG_UNIT=2/116@3770-3791` showed the Jet Fighter, its `Strafe` at `Unit(104)`, and the
   strafe's path node sliding 47 a frame west. `RON_DEBUG_UNIT=*/**@3787-3790` (through `env`, because zsh read
   the quoted assignment as a command) named the target, `1/34`, an Attack Helicopter.
4. **The reading.** In `bank_plane_as`, a stored bank of −0.0 makes the step's sign the sign handed in.
   `do_air_physics@005e86d0` hands `bank_aircraft@005e9520` `local_20`, not `returning`. The listing at
   `0x5e8a0c`–`0x5e8b6a` sets `local_20` to 1 when all of these hold: not returning (`+0x18` of the air order),
   `is_attack` (vslot `+0x18`), the plane is not a helicopter, the target type's `+0x218 == 2`, and
   `valid_target` (`0x648ba0`) takes the target. It also needs `vector_dist(|dx|, |dy|) < min_range · 0xc0`
   (`lea; shl 6`, `jge` out) and `fold(find_angle(dx, dy) − heading) < 0x40000000` (`cmovb`). `bank_aircraft`
   replaces only a 0 `param_3` with the sign of `des − heading`. The doubled want is the order's own `returning`,
   read through vslot `0xfc`, which ours already had right.
5. **The geometry.** `1/34` is 106 off and 7.6° off the nose. The Jet Fighter's `min_range` is 1, so the radius
   is 192. On 3788 the helicopter was 287 off, outside it, which is why the arm first fires on 3789.
