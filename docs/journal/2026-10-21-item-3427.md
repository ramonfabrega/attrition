## The instrument: a driver told where to click

Every earlier driver (`clickdriver.sh`, `echelon.sh`) clicks blind at fixed points. A command card's buttons and a
unit's drawn position are known only from a screenshot of the run itself. So `tools/gamelog/handdriver.sh` is a
`longtrace.sh` `DRIVER` that reads its clicks, one a line, from `/tmp/ron-runs/hand-<run>/cmd` while the game runs.
It runs under RonDriver with the capture, so its `cliclick` and `screencapture` carry the app's grants. A caller only
appends lines and reads the screenshots and `done.log` that come back. The verbs are `wait F`, `focus`, `m`, `c`,
`rc`, `sc`, `key`, `shot`, `sleep` and `stop`. Two operational findings from it:

- **The game menu (1899, 11) pauses the game**, and **Quit Game (960, 694) returns to the main menu with no
  confirmation**. The dump then stops growing, `longtrace.sh` settles on it and archives, so a driven take ends when
  its clicks are done rather than at its `!quit` frame. The three takes ran 5–8 minutes each instead of the ~20 a
  3,000-frame live game would cost.
- The queue lane runs live at about two frames a second at these details.

`runqueue.sh` gains a `misc:` key (`DETAIL_MISC` in `longtrace.sh`), so a stanza can print `[Misc Logging]
COMMANDMANAGER=1`, the `CM` field the owed table names. It had only `end:` and `start:`.
