## The cause

`calc_resource_caps@006ce900` adds the wonders' flat terms per slot,
between Diamonds and the republic tier. The Pyramids' is
`PYRAMIDS_COMMERCE`, 50, on food and wealth. `rules.xml` and
`Constants::init` read it plain. who=1, British at commerce level 3, is
`200 × 125 / 100 = 250`, plus 50, times 16: **4800**. This crate's
`commerce_cap` had no wonder term, and `PlayerTech::wonders`, the one
`has_wonder` it had, has no writer. **R1 holds; R2 was killed on the
dump.**

`Sim::wonders_held` reads `has_wonder` off the buildings: activated,
alive, of the type, and in a city unless it is the Red Fort. It goes into
`Holdings::wonders` every frame beside the commerce level, because the
caps are computed every frame. `economy::wonder_commerce` carries the
listing's seven wonder terms, and the seven constants are pinned in the
slot table.

With the cap alone, the word went 17099 → 17128. The cap and the re-deal
went, and `1/3`, `1/5`, `1/70` agree. who=1's food `income` still parted
on 17088, 1600 here against **1920** there. That is `PYRAMIDS_FOOD`'s
fifth, paid in `calc_resource_bonuses@006db030`, the last step of
`calc_gather`, on who=1's dirty reassembly at sim-frame 17087. It is the
same activation's second term, so I built the function's wonder terms as
`economy::resource_bonuses`, in the listing's order. Russian oil, Virtual
Reality and CtW are seams. It took the leader's rows off and left the
word at 17128.

Both unit tests were made to fail on the old rule first:
`the_pyramids_add_fifty_to_food_and_wealth_only` (4000 against 4800) and
`the_pyramids_pay_a_fifth_more_food`. ECONOMY §15 has the tables.
