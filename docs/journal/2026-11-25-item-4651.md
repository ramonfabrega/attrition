## The lister

`artdata::tests::every_release_of_a_bay_piece_s_family_has_its_row` walks `Loaded::piece_releases`, which is every
`<RELEASEEVENT>` of every unit piece the install names. It takes each key the way `Sim::guy_release_events` resolves it:
`pivot::release_node`, else `launch::bay`, else `launch::node`. A piece is in a held family when its type (`piece % 352`)
or its type's `GRAPH` is that of a piece `BAYS` or `GUNS` holds. That covers every art set, age, gender and crew of the
type, plus the elites and other types playing the same model. A key is dropped when its animation is not in the piece's
slot list (`piece_lengths`), because it never plays. There are 77 of those in the families: every `-PACKED` entry carries
one `CHAR_ATTACK1` event and has no `CHAR_ATTACK1` animation, and the bowmen carry a `CHAR_GROUP_IDLE1` arrow and have no
such animation. The arm (pivot or bay) is `node & 3` against the type's `<RESTRICTION>` count.

On the base the lister was red with 683 keys: 528 on the bay arm of a held type, 148 on the bay arm of a held `GRAPH`, and
7 on the pivot arm. Outside every family it counts 532 bay keys, 263 pivot keys and 16 slotless keys (4651a).

## The measurement

There was no new reading of the executable. Every key was measured the way items 4618 and 4630 measured theirs.
`tools/recomp/sweep_keys.py` is `get_position.py`'s `Oracle` driven over a key list. It uses a fresh machine for each
piece, `init_position` with axis 3 for each key the packet does not store, then a sweep of 361 headings. 675 keys ran on
run1579's packet in 34 s, 659 distinct. `init_position(584, 1, 10, 14)` faults on run1579 (the Arab Flak Gun's walk is not
resident there), so that key was swept on run1816's packet instead.

Each sweep was then tested against every `BAYS`/`GUNS` row at its key, using `fit_bay.py`'s arithmetic (the crate's
`COS_DEG`). The scratch scripts are kept beside the sweeps under `~/ron-data/lab-experiments/2026-11-25-item-4651/`.

- **79 pieces read another's rows** (`launch::SAME_AS`). For each one, a single held piece's rows reproduce every one of
  its keys at 361 of 361 headings. That is 561 keys. Same-type sources are preferred. The others agree to the unit
  through a shared model: the Heavy Machine Gun's four unheld sets (75, 427, 1483, 1835) release as the Machine Gun's 73
  does, and the four `INFANTRY`-`GRAPH` types 380–383 release as 52 and 57 do. A piece in the table holds no row of its
  own (`a_piece_that_reads_another_s_bays_holds_none`), and no `SAME_AS` piece has a `MEASURED` row, so nothing that
  resolved before resolves differently.
- **19 pieces are fitted**, 66 `BAYS` rows. These are keys no held row reproduces. Each row is `fit_bay.py`'s centroid,
  every centroid lies inside its region, and the smallest region is 9 points. The American Bomber (958, ten keys), the Asian
  Dromon (1330, nine) and the Partisans' sets are other models. The Partisans' 1426 and 1778 read 18's fitted rows.
- **The Fighter's NA and India sets** (1647 and 1999) give one point per node on all eight `CHAR_ATTACK2` frames. Two nodes
  fire on each frame, which `BAYS`' frame key cannot hold, so they are four `GUNS` rows, as 239's are.
- **The pivot keys.** `unit_graphics.xml` gives `FLAKGUN-ARAB-AGE0` and `AVENGER-ARAB-AGE0` the default block line for line
  except the texture. `get_position`'s pivot arm, after `init_position` of nodes 4 and 0, answers all 92,416 cells of every
  node-0 key as 232's and 234's do: 584's three attacks on run1579 and its walk on run1816, and 586's two attacks.
  `pivot::SAME_AS` takes (584, 232) and (586, 234). 586's walk is 234's by the art alone, because no packet on disk holds
  234's walk animation. Their node-1 keys are bay keys, `launch::SAME_AS`'s.
