## What moved

`release` now runs two lanes. The first is the isolated library. The second runs every other binary in Cargo's
order, one at a time, at the gate's width and reservations. It starts once the library's head test is running,
so the coverage walk is admitted first. Each binary's output goes to its own log, which the runner prints whole
after the library's block. A zero-exit binary without one verdict is 125, and so is a built executable with no
exit. The lanes' spans go to `isolated/lanes.json` and to a printed `Release lanes:` line, so the booking gate
measures the overlap. `docs/TEST_PERFORMANCE.md`, "The release step's lanes (Loop 4042)", has the detail. The
4072 section's last sentence is struck there and points at it.
