## How it is built

A height is held only where it differs from the ground. Before a placement's terraform, every unit's stored `z` and
every figure's `(z, last_z)` is snapshot; after it, each that the new ground would move is held at the old value;
then the walk drops the holds of the chained land units it refreshes (the unit's and its figures' `z`; their
`last_z` stays held). A unit's hold is dropped by `Unit::set_new_location` (every call past the shore arm). A figure's
`z` hold is read only while its body stands on the point it was held at; `Guy::move`'s head copies it into the
`last_z` hold; a `Guy::set_new_location(…, 1)` seating drops both.
