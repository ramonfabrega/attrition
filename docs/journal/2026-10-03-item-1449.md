## Cause and change

`Build::process@0061edf0` runs `Wall::process`, the tower and then
`Build::do_queue`. The vslot `+0x1b4` was named from the Build vtable
rather than guessed. `Objects::process_all` runs it per building, in
object order. The tick ran every building's `Wall::process` part first and
every queue after, and its own comment called that split "still ours".
City `1/2008` comes before wonder `1/2022`, so in the original the
newborn is on the map when the recruiter looks.

The change runs `process_queue(b)` right after `process_building(b)`.
`process_queues` stays only for its one unit test. The tower pass and the
gather region pass are unchanged and stated as unestablished.
