# Item 362 — the market at 9182, and the field it turned out to be

*2026-09-18, Opus 5, worker on `worktree-att-362`.*

Booked as "Great Lakes 9182 is the market's, and 358 left it there".
It is not the market's. What it is, is one struct field this crate
computed four times and never wrote down.

## Measuring first

The brief said no widening was owed because the frame is named, so the
first hour went on the frame itself. The original's ten draws at 9182
are three `Leader::use_market+0x1ed`, two `Leader::make_stuff+0x221`,
two `Guy::set_anim+0x97a` and three `Guy::set_anim+0x104b`. This crate
spent nine: one market, **three** head-expiry, two slot-expiry, two
`+0x97a`, one bird coin.

`RON_DEBUG_MARKET`, a throwaway probe inside `use_market`, printed what
the market actually saw:

```
market who1 need [0, 0, 76, 0, 0, 0] bucket [73, 84, 73, 111, 71, 0]
  g2 sell=true
    cand g2=0 sellable=false stock 73 need 0
    cand g2=1 sellable=false stock 84 need 0
    cand g2=3 sellable=false stock 111 need 0     (knowledge — excluded)
    cand g2=4 sellable=false stock 71 need 0
```

One short good, nothing sellable, one draw. Then the arithmetic that
makes the frame decidable: `use_market`'s outer `while (bucket[g] <
need[g])` can only go round again when a candidate passed the sell
test, and **a sale raises wealth, never the short good**. So three
draws is three short goods, or one good with two sales under it — and
this ledger has neither, because nothing but knowledge is over a
hundred. The market's own arithmetic is the decompile's line for line;
the divergence is upstream, in a value.

## The turn

The upstream value was §41's: the AI's University queues three scholars
on 8985 and this crate queued two. §41 offered three readings, all of
them about `make_stuff`'s expiry walk, and said the run that would
settle it was a `LEADERS=9` window over `[8980, 8990]` that nothing on
disk covers.

The draw stream settled it instead, in one command. 8985 costs **three**
`make_stuff+0x221` and **zero** `make_stuff+0x63d`, *on both sides*. A
step-6 purchase can never cost zero expiry draws — the walk starts at
the slot it just bought, and that slot holds the type. So the original's
three scholars are **one** `make_this`, and the quantity is the make
list slot's own `num`.

`create_units@006c40a0` keeps it in `TVar24` and every arm sets it. This
crate's `Sim::civilian_value` computed it — `k` for the scholar, 3/2/1
for the caravan, `min(deficit, room)` for the citizen — and returned
three fields that did not include it. Every civilian the AI has ever
offered itself went out as a batch of one.

Five lines: `&mut num` through `civilian_value` and `citizen_value`, and
`*num_out = …` in the three arms that had one.

## What this says about the method

The item named a mechanism and the mechanism was innocent. Two things
found the real one, and neither was a capture:

1. **Reading the loop for what a count can mean.** "Three draws" is not
   "three passes with sales" — it is a disjunction, and writing the
   disjunction down is what ruled the market out in twenty minutes.
2. **Asking the draw stream about a frame nobody had asked about.**
   8985's *zero* `+0x63d` draws is a fact that had been sitting in
   `rontrace-run53.log` since 31 August, and it falsifies all three of
   §41's readings at once. §41 had booked a capture to settle what the
   trace already said. Grep the dump before booking a reading; grep the
   **trace** before booking a capture.
