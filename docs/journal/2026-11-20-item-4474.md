- **The direction: one side walked more.** On 2743 both sides hand over order type 0x11 (`AIR_PATROL`) and its

- **The field list on 2743**, record 91 after the fix: every units and guys range agrees.

**The reading.** `AirPatrolOrder::walk_data@00483ed0`. `AirPatrolOrder` is `PatrolOrder` at +0 and `AirOrder`
at +0x40 over the virtual `Order`, size 0x68. The PDB field list puts the bases at offsets 0 and 64, and
`this adjust` is 96. The walk hands over the flags byte, `waypoint` (+0x3c), `x_pos` (+0x4) and `y_pos` (+0x20)
by `SimpleArray<Coord>::walk_data@00483cc0`, the flags byte again, then `AirOrder` +0x44..+0x5c. A
`SimpleArray` hands over its length and, when non-empty, its size, increment, flags and elements in one range.
The size is the capacity. `PatrolOrder::clear@00483ac0` keeps it when at least 1. At increment −1,
`make_valid@0046edd0` with `increase_size@00434630` grows it by the capacity when that suffices. So a fresh
order holds 1, 2, 4, 8.

## The second parting, on 2744: the exit's turn

With the walk built, record 91 parted on 2744 on two fields. `unit_masks` read ours 0x40000, theirs 0x40002
(`mirror`, bit 2). The figure's `last_angle` read ours 0x55555555, theirs 0. `Unit::do_spec_anim@005e5880`'s
EXIT at an airbase (`:74`) calls `Unit::set_angle(this, 0, ·, 1)`. `set_angle@00605400` XORs `unit_masks` with 2
on a turn in [0x40000000, 0xc0000000], and 0 − 0x55555555 is one. It then calls `Guy::set_angle(guy 0, 0, 1)`,

`heading`, `facing` and `frame_facing` directly. It now calls `unit_set_angle_as(u, 0, true)` and keeps its
`des_angle` 0. Record 91 agrees on both lines from 2743 to 2746.
