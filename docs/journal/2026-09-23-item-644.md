## The cause: the `library` handler skipped `gain_tech`'s tail

`ConsoleWin::run_cmd`'s `library` case is `Leader::set_epoch` on four lines,
then `Leader::set_age`. Both raise their level through a **whole
`Leader::gain_tech` per step** (`gain_tech(this, t, 0, 0, ·, 1)`,
`006d26f0`/`006d25a0`). `gain_tech`'s goods loop pays `bucket_add(g,
starting[g])` for a good whose first prerequisite is the tech just gained,
when the leader lacked it before. That makes the Classical age worth 100
knowledge and 100 metal. This crate models that grant
(`Sim::pay_arriving_goods`) but only on `Sim::gain_tech`.
`set_leader_age` and `set_leader_epoch` raised the levels in the tree
alone. So after `library who=1 2`, who=1 held knowledge 0 and metal 0
where the original held 100 of each. That was the standing first-block
rows, and it carried through to 1018 and then to 1176.

Chapter seven stood on the same seam for who=0: its "two leader buckets"
standing rows are these two. It never parted a word, because the human's
buckets decide nothing its record reaches.

## The build

- **`Sim::set_leader_levels`**: the shared body of `set_age` and
  `set_epoch`.
  - It loses every level at or above `n`, highest first.
  - It raises the rest through `Sim::gain_tech`, one call per level. Each
    call carries the grant, the unit upgrades, the Science re-pricing, the
    age snap, `check_transport` and `apply_gained`.
  - Then it runs `drop_unfollowed` and `apply_gained`.
- **`Sim::gain_tech` raises the economy flag** (`*this |= 0x2000000`,
  `gain_tech:319`), on every gain. The two research paths already raised it
  after the call.
- **The caps read the Commerce level live.** `Leader::gather@006ce280:58`
  runs `calc_resource_caps` on every frame, outside the reassembly gate.
  That function reads `LeaderDataEncrypt + 0xf0` directly. This crate read
  `holdings.commerce`, which is written only at a reassembly, so a level
  raised between two reassemblies lagged. **That was parked 633**: run157's
  605 is 2992 on the original's side, and this crate's showed 1392 until
  its next reassembly.
- **The target-liveness test is the uid test.** `Unit::work@0060d180:319`
  compares the action's target uid with the object's `+0x30`, and a closed
  building keeps its uid until `Objects::find_free` hands its number on.
  This crate killed the action on the alive bit instead. So with the camp
  bought and destroyed, ours dropped `1/7`'s walk and `BUILDORDER` on 1176,
  where the original kept both to the end of the capture. The test is now
  `Sim::building_slot_reused`, meaning a later building carries the same
  owner and number. `do_build`'s and `do_gather`'s own `exists` tests
  still end the order on arrival. This moved the word 1177 → 1187 on top
  of the grant.
