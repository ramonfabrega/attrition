## Step 2: the packet's own patch, whole

The tool is `tools/render/patches.py` (new). It reads `TerrainOut + 0x4974` for `Transitions`,
`+0x6ab0` for the cell table, each `CoordInfo`'s `PatchInfo`, and `TerrainData::texture_fractal`.

`uv run --locked --project tools/emu python tools/render/patches.py ~/ron-golden/run1559b/map-14/frame-snapshot.bin`
exits 0 and prints 2,064 patches. Its output is outside the repository, at
`~/ron-data/lab-experiments/2026-11-11-item-4132/run1559b-patches.txt`.

- **The table**: 14 `AlphaInfo`s, buckets 0 to 13, their textures the 14 `.tga`s in name order.
  Their `bitcode` rows are the name's code and its seven reorderings. Example: `001110011100.tga`
  gives `39c 873 70e ce1 873 70e ce1 39c`.
- **The patched land-0 cells**: 1,512 in all.
  - 113 have no alpha geometry (`trans_texcoords1` empty), so `add_patch` skips them.
  - The other 1,399 lay the base of `land_sub − 1` on the packet's own `land_sub`, every one:
    sub 1 → 0 on 129 cells, 2 → 1 on 732, 3 → 2 on 651. They carry these alphas:

| alpha | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cells | 16 | 2 | 118 | 15 | 31 | 3 | 1 | 298 | 51 | 553 | 5 | 74 | 345 |

- **Is the map a function?** Key each cell by its eight neighbours' `land_sub` against its own
  (lower, equal, higher, or off the map). Of 471 distinct codes, 470 give one alpha. The other,
  `==><>---`, gives alphas 10 and 9 on one cell each. That fits `find_map`'s pick among candidates by
  the fractal.
- **The texcoords**: every patch's `trans_texcoords1` is a 5 × 5 grid spanning one quadrant of the alpha,
  turned or mirrored. The grid's corners at vertex 0 fall in all nine positions of {0, ½, 1}².
  The vertex order is `j·5 + i` with `i` along world x. The packet's own `trans_texcoords0` shows it
  (`(0, 0), (0.25, 0), …`), and so do the verts (x 1536, 1728, … at y 0).

## Step 3: what is read

- **The alpha files**. `Transitions::init` loads each as a `PF_DXT3` texture with 4 mips. The
  decode: type 10, 32-bit, 128 × 128, descriptor `0x08`. R, G and B are 255 on every texel of all 14,
  and A runs 0 to 255. Recorded in `docs/FORMATS.md`, "The transition alphas".
- **The choice**. These are read as far as RENDERER §3.6 "The transition patch" states, not whole:
  - `create_transition_renderables` builds three codes from `build_trans_flags`, runs the four checks
    and closes the corners;
  - `find_map` filters candidates and makes the fractal pick;
  - the texcoords come from `rotate_tex_coords` by `selected_bitcode`, halved and offset into a quadrant
    by the fractal.

  Not read: `mark_trans_flags`, which sets the edge masks with `check_for_tendrils` (655 decompiled
  lines) and `is_combo_acceptable`. Also unread are the four checks' bodies, `rotate_tex_coords`, and the
  pass's blend state.
- **The fractal**. `texture_fractal` is already ported, as `sim::mapgen::Fractal::init(xs + 1, ys + 1, 0, 2,
  seed)` (`map_heights::build_coastal_data`). The packet's header line: `fractal 61 61 flags 2 parts0 -1`.

## Step 5, from the packet's choice: the read alpha against the controls

Candidate (e) lays the patch's base over ours at weight A/255. A is the alpha's channel at the packet's
`trans_texcoords1`, sampled at the pixel's place in the cell, bilinear on level 0. Candidate (f) is the
same alpha with the cell turned a quarter turn, `(u, v) → (v, 1 − u)`.

| pool | (a) | (b) | (c) | (d) | (e) or (f) | none above |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a)–(d) | 204 | 148 | 31 | 115 | | 5 |
| (a)–(e) | 15 | 41 | 5 | 42 | **(e) 395** | 5 |
| (a)–(d), (f) | 66 | 102 | 22 | 108 | (f) 201 | 4 |

The mean r of (e) is **0.716**, against 0.519, 0.484, 0.609 and 0.618 for (a) to (d), and 0.614 for (f).
(e) wins 159 of sub 2's (−1), all 120 of sub 2's (−2) and 116 of sub 3's.

Pinned as `RUN1559B_3279_GROUND_BLEND_PACKET` (395; [15, 41, 5, 42]; 201; of 503). The test asserts that
(e) beats every control and its quarter turn.

**This is not the landing's row.** The choice is the packet's, so it measures the draw's ceiling under
the read alpha, not a rule of ours. Two assumptions are named, not read: that A weights the patch's base
and not ours, and the level. That makes it a reading with a count, the shape kill (c) would need.
It is not a kill: the blend wins 395 against a best control of 204.
