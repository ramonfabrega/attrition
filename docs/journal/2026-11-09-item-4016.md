## How, and why no statement moved

A scratch script (`split.py`, not committed) did each group in two steps. First it cut the fields' chunks out of `pub
struct Sim`, wrote the struct, and gathered their `Sim::new` initialisers into a nested literal where the last of them
stood. Then it looped `cargo check --workspace --all-targets --message-format=json`. Every `E0609` (`no field X on type
Sim`) naming a moved field, and every `E0615` (`players` is also a `Sim` method), got `<state>.` inserted at the
compiler's own span. The loop ended when no such error was left, in two or three rounds per group. That covers every
crate and target: the sim's modules and tests, the sweeps under `crates/sim/tests`, and `rondata`, whose direct reads
moved the same way (48 files, 52 distinct field paths). This is the minimal rewrite the brief asked for; 4017 is the
door. Inserting a path segment cannot reorder an evaluation or a draw. The `Sim::new` literal is constructors only, with
no rng, so nesting it moved nothing either.

**Checked as text.** I compared all 260 changed files with the base after deleting `<state>.` wherever it precedes one
of that state's moved fields, ignoring whitespace and trailing commas:
- 204 are equal.
- 34 more are equal once both sides go through rustfmt at `max_width = 2000`. The difference was rustfmt's rewraps and
  the braces it adds to closures it wraps.
- The remaining 22 are the 13 `mod.rs` files that gained a struct, `lib.rs`, `floors.rs` (the re-pin), and seven I read
  hunk by hunk: `rondata/src/diff/world.rs`, `ai/ai_host.rs`, `air/mod.rs`, `cities/city.rs`, `economy/holdings.rs`,
  `groups/group/actions.rs`, `units/garrison.rs`. Every hunk is a path insertion plus a rewrap. The checker trips on a
  doubled name split across a line break (`.cities\n.cities`).

## The whole-`Sim` readers

The brief asked for every reader that iterates, hashes, serializes or `Debug`-prints `Sim`:
- **The soak digest** (`soak.rs`, `sample`) hashes named fields in its own order (`units`, then `cities`, `ledgers`,
  …). Those reads gained a segment (`sim.cities.cities`, `sim.economy.ledgers`), so the inputs and their order are the
  base's. The struct's declaration order is never read.
- **Clone**: `Sim` is `#[derive(Clone, Debug)]` and so is every new state. The debug viewer's replay checkpoints
  (`rondata::diff::harness::checkpoint::ReplaySession`, derived `Clone`) clone the whole replay, and a derived clone of
  nested structs copies the same values.
- **Debug**: the derived output nests now. Nothing compares it. No test or tool prints a whole `Sim` with `{:?}`
  (grepped across `crates`; the hits print a unit's orders or `phase_marks`).
- **Serialization**: none. `Sim` has no serde and no hand-written byte writer.
- **`rondata`'s 56 direct field reads**: rewritten by path in the same commits, as above.
