## The reading

**"Lookup 10" was never a lookup.** `on_redraw` calls `FontFamily::get@00522810(fonts, 10, -1)`, the integer
overload. It scans `fonts` (the `FontSpec` list: `+0x2c` size, `+0x30` style) for the point size, with the style
first exact and then under looser masks. A style of −1 takes `def_style`. The string overload (`@00522050`) is the
one that reads `<LOOKUPS>` by tag. `def_style` is set to 1 (bold) by `System::init_graphics@00598ac0` and by
`OptionsWinGraphics::change_resolution` after `FontFamily::init`. So the figures' face is size 10, bold.
At both 1280 and 1024 that is `fonts.xml`'s `arialbd.ttf` 10 `B`, not `city_name_size`'s Georgia. The sans the
photograph reads is right.

- `draw_build_name@00820c30`: `get(fonts, 12, 1)`, which is `arialbd` at 12, the clock's face. Then `write_cent_l`
  in `name`, white over a black shadow.
- `draw_hitpoints@00821150`: `get(fonts, 10, 0)`, which is `arial.ttf` at 10, white, and `COLOR_NONE` for the
  shadow.
