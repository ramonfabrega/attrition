00b42174`): a `Build`'s `+8` is `0041bff0` (`xor eax, eax`), and its

`is_gather_type` (CITIES.md §1.5). `+0x70` is `BuildData::gather_down`
and `+0x92` is `UnitData::gather_down`: **the gatherer chain**
(ORDERS.md §6.1), not a garrison. The distance test's `unaff_` pair is
