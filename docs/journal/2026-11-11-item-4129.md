## Step 4: what the tables name, read whole — the blend (ii)

Step 3 agrees, and step 2 puts the losses beside another `land_sub`, so this is reading (ii). The live path is
`TerrainOut::create_transition_renderables`, called from `Terrain::init` and `Terrain::spot_update_land`. The dead path
is `generate_transitions`, the row in `rondata::blind::RESIDUE`.

For each cell on its list it does this:

- It builds the neighbours' codes with `build_trans_flags` at 6, 3 and 0, and runs `check_blocks`, `check_connects`,
  `check_fills` and `check_for_diagonal`.
- It picks an `AlphaInfo` with `Transitions::find_map`.
- It gives the cell one `PatchInfo`: `bucket` is the alpha's, `tex` the land texture, and `landindex` is
  `convert_land_tex_to_linear(land, land_sub − 1, cx, cy)`. A non-zero `land_sub` is decremented through the
  `local_2c[-1].field_0x1b` idiom, `−0x1c + 0x1b`.

**So the patch lays the next base down over the cell.** On the packet, by (rule's base is the best, the patch's
base less the rule's, best is the next base down), over the 1,003 plain tiles:

| | no patch | patch two down | patch one down, best not one down | patch one down, best one down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the rule wins (759) | 499 | 120 | 140 | 0 |
| the rule loses (244) | 1 | 7 | 6 | **230** |

The losses are the patch. The weight, the alpha the bitcode picks and how `selected_bitcode` lays it, is unread. So no
blend is drawn: a weight chosen by its count is ruled out. Parked 4129a, with the 244 as its ceiling. The light and the
`_mtrl` stage are not named, because the losses are on patched cells, not everywhere.
