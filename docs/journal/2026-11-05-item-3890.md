## What the original does (read off the decompile and the listing)

`Terrain::init` calls `Cliffs::init@008a8020` after `adjust_for_mountains`, just before the 894 checksum
(`Terrain/init@00850f70.c` l. 978–983). In order:
1. each queued cliff (`MapGrid::cliffs`, `(x, y, size t, index i within size t)`) goes through
   `place_cliff@008a7ea0`: over the `index_to_size[t]`² vertex box (`index_to_size@00af2fd0` = `[4, 8, 16, 32,
   64]`, PE-read) at `offtable[t]` cells, a vertex whose cell is on the map, has no 0x10, is not dry-skipped, has no
   0x1000 and whose `CoordInfo.flags` (`terrain+0x6ab0` is `CoordInfo*` per cell; `+0x1c` is `flags`, `& 4` is the
   port's `coord::HALF`) lacks 4 takes the template's height added (`movss` template, `addss` z). The template index
   counts every box vertex, skipped or not (listing 0x8a7fe7). Then the template's cells take `CoordInfo
   ::cliff_group_index` (+0x6a, −1 from the constructor) = the queue index;
2. `assess_wcoord@008a6a40` on every cell, y outer: on a 0x40 cell, each tile (y outer) of a cell without flag 4
   or `land` 1/2 and not a mountain tile (`& 3 == 2`) goes to `has_plus_slope`; a pass marks mask +0x5e4, calls
   `set_blocked_at(tile, 1)` and then rewrites the mask `& 0xfffd | 1` (read after the call, 0x8a6b8e); each of its
   eight neighbours (`move[1..9]`) on the map marks mask +0x5f0, is blocked and rewritten the same way, marks its
   cell in +0x5fc, gets `move_goody` on its cell, and its cell inherits the group index when −1. After the 16: any
   pass → `move_goody` on the cell, none → the cell loses 0x40;
3. a `Cliff` per +0x5fc cell; `Cliff::init@008a5190` returns the count of the cell's tiles that are not a
   mountain's and sit in +0x5e4 or +0x5f0 (listing 0x8a5304..0x8a5372); under a group ≥ 0 and a non-zero count,
   those tiles join the group's mining list;
4. `set_edge_flags@008a85e0`: each mining tile with `WorldData::has_gather_access(.., −1, 0, 1)` takes 0x8000.

`has_plus_slope@00869790`: `xmm3` = 25.0 (`[0xb69658]`), `· 0x3c8efa35`, `tanf`, `· 192` = 89.531075
