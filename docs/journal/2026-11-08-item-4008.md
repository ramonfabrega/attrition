The queue says `test-tails 65k · sweep-lines 149.5k … largest 14223`. Three differences, each a counting convention:
- **sweep-lines** and **largest**: the inventory counted lines with `split('\n')`, one more than `wc -l` for every

  item's own guard tests raised the count to 65,186, which a count that may only fall cannot carry.
