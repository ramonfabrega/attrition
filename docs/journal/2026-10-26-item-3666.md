## The regression that was not the mechanism

The draft reported that the crew-loop build moved fourteen other lobbies' `first_parting` floors down (Roman
Medieval 5979 → 3390 and thirteen more). It said the build "must not land as is", and it named three hypotheses
about `des_angle`. They were all wrong. **The draft bound guy 0's point as `let from = unit.movement.body.pos;`.
That shadowed `unit_step`'s own `from` (`unit.pos`, bound at the function's head), which five later lines read**:
the waypoint deltas, the tile test, the snapped arrival and `moved_to`.

How it was found:
1. The probe's `RON_PROBE_3666_OFF` switch was meant to measure the draft without the write. It still gave Roman
   Medieval 3390: the shadow sat outside the switch.
2. A detached worktree at the base read Roman Medieval 5979. Its first run passed every test in 0.00 s, because
   `install_root` resolves `../../game` from the manifest and finds nothing under the job's tmp. With `RON_INSTALL`
   set, it walked for real.
3. Bisecting the three changed files narrowed it to `orders.rs`. A probe allocation and an env read alone moved
   nothing, which left the binding.

Under the name `guy0`, every one of the 80 `first_parting` tests holds both ways except the Korean pair's.
