## The first half: what a held-out row's test fails on

`held_verdict(want, got)` gives back `(fails, drift)`:

- **Fails**: the states compared; the set of whole lines, in either direction (a line agrees on every state and
  parts nowhere); the first parting, meaning its state and the lines that part on it, in either direction. A row
  coming whole fails too, because it moves both.
- **Drift**: a parted line's agreeing count, a line's first parting later than the row's, the Adlers on the first
  parting, and ours' end. Each is printed as `HELD OUT DRIFT row N runM: …` and not held.

The `HELD OUT row …` line still prints the whole score, so the pin can be refreshed at a booking. The sample's
`SAMPLE_OURS_HELD` works the same way: the count of frames with no wall, shot or death (3450) now prints as
`SAMPLE HELD DRIFT`, and the first held frame (2929) still fails. `SAMPLE_LINES` and the take's shape are unchanged.

## What this gives up

As DECISIONS 154 §5 says: a landing that changes a game only after a first parting no longer turns a test red.
Its measure is the drift it prints, and that drift is quoted in the landing's journal. A booking that re-pins still
reads the `HELD OUT row` line.

For the Loop: a lane re-pins a held-out row only when its whole lines or first parting moved. Drift is re-pinned
at a booking, or not at all.
