## The first act

- **The issuer.** `Options::exec` case `0x21` calls `do_auto_explore(option->object)`. `UnitOut::make_options`
  adds `OPTION_AUTO_EXPLORE` with object −1. It refuses a plane (vslot `+0xc0`, bar the type's `0x20`) and, without
  `prefs & 2`, a caravan. The key map names `OPTION_AUTO_EXPLORE` too.
- **`do_auto_explore`**: `can_activate`, a clear byte at the selection's `+1`, the flash, then
  `issue_unitmask(0x100, object)`; the two sounds read `get_auto_explore`.
- **The handler.** `process_unitmask` → `action_unitmask(mask, set)` on the package's group. It toggles the bit on
  the first live member, and a member after one that cleared is cleared too. For `0x100` each member also takes
  `flags |= 0x10`, `unit_masks &= ~0x4000000`, `+0xc0 = 0`, `close_orders(0)`, `clear_partial_path` and
  `update_action`.
- **The bit's readers** (each grepped, `0x68 & 0x100` and `0x69 & 1`; the `0x1000` and `0x100000` hits are other
  bits):
  - `think`: the head skips `near_o` and the auto-attack; the human block calls `think_scout(this, 0)`;
  - `think_scout`: the city loop for any unit, and the plain-human arms (`0x40000` clear: step 1, cap 12 then 8, the
    `+0x4c` mark) our port asserts unreachable;
  - `think_civilian_transport`: `EXPLORE_TO` for `MOVE_TO`, `think_scout(this, 1)`;
  - `reveal_fog`: a revealed goody box is taken;
  - `work`: the clear under order flag `4`;
  - `action_move_near`: the bit carried across its halt.
- **Our walk**: `Stream::pumped` skips `unitmask` as "not mapped". `crates/sim` has no field for the bit
  (`groups/halt_view.rs` says so), and `orders/think.rs` has no human-block `think_scout`.
