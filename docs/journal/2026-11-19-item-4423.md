*Opus 5.5, one session (lane-4423), no subagents, no capture (run1715 not taken). **The first act lands**: the city
plate's font advances are read off every packet on disk, and the client's table goes from the 4 characters `Napata`

## What was read

- **The font object**, `sbl/d3dgl.pdb`'s type records (`llvm-pdbutil dump --types`): `D3D11GLFont` (field list
  `0x75D0`) `mCharacterInfo` +340, a `PtrArray<CharacterInfo>`, whose `ArrayBaseMaster` (`0x16CB`) gives `length`
  +4 and `ArrayBase` (`0x735E`) `list` +16; `mFontHeight` +380, `mStyle` +396, `mPointSize` +400. `CharacterInfo`
  (`0x7241`, 24 bytes): `tex_x`, `tex_y`, `tex_width`, `tex_height` (floats), `c` (`wchar_t`) +16, `advance`
  (`char`) +18, `pix_width`, `pix_height`, `abs_a`, `texture`.
- **The route on the heap**: `fonts` (`?fonts@@3VFontFamily@@A`, `rise_z.map`), `FontFamily::lookups` (+0x20, an
  `ObjectArray<FontLookup>`), entry 36 (`FontLookup::font` +0x1c), the `Font`'s first word (`FontBase::gs_font`).
  On run1559b's packet that is `0x239470c`, vtable `0x7863596c` (`d3dgl.map`'s `??_7D3D11GLFont@@6B@`
  `0x100e596c` relocated by `0x68550000`): height 16, point 14, style 1, the font item 4403 named.
- **The entries are filled lazily**: of the 128 slots, run1559b's font holds 68. A heap scan for every
  `D3D11GLFont` showed the same shape in all of them (fonts at other sizes hold 0 to 74). So one packet cannot give
  every character, and a union across packets can, provided no two packets disagree.
