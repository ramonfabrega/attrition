## The reading, and what the crate lacked

- `Build::start@006273a0` calls `+0x164` for a wonder right after
  `Wall::start`. The crate's `start_building` did not.
- `Wall::update_local_seen`'s wonder branch writes **both** planes
  whether or not the wonder is started (`param_4 = uStack_2c ^ 1`), and
  it writes mask `0xff` only once started. The crate wrote `seen2` only.
  §6.1's table had put `is_started` in the plane test, so it is amended
  in place.
- `GameDaemon::update_all_seen`'s not-active arm relights a started
  wonder through `+0x164` and lights nothing for an unfinished ordinary
  site. The crate lit every building's disc.
- `Wall::init`'s `check_ever_seen(0)` was cited in `init_build`'s
  comment and never made. run610's new site `1/2024` showed it: 0 here
  for three blocks, 2 in the original.
