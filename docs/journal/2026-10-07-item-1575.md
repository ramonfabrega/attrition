### Findings, to park with their frames

- **`cosx`/`sinx` at distance `i32::MIN`** (`movement.rs` `sin_component`'s
  `d = -d`): the port panics, the original's `neg` wraps to 0 or −32768.
  No frame reaches it — no distance in play is near `i32::MIN`.
- **`Farms::grow` on a cut cell decayed k ≥ 1 times**: the original holds
  `(1.0f − k·0.01f) + 0.005f`, the port's `201 − 2k` adds land a few ulps
  lower; the port's one caller (`orders.rs`) grows only growing or empty
  cells, so no frame reaches it today. Whether a regrown cut cell ripens an
  add apart is `inc_time`'s question, untested.
- **A class: the port panics where the original wraps** — `WCoord` past
  2²⁹, `corner_tile` past ±10⁷, `mana_left`'s `MANA − mana_burn` overflow,
  `cosx` above. Each sweep kept to the domain a game reaches; a frame that
  leaves it would end our run, not part it.
- **Needs a packet**: `ObjectData::is_in_range` (the `objects` table at
  `0x006486d6`), `UnitData::get_speed`'s land arm and group cap
  (`div_3_table` null in the image at `0x0060ab40`, `worldc`, `groups`).
- **A port inconsistency, no parting**: `Sim::unit_speed` reads the domain
  from the type, `get_speed_at` from `unit.kind.domain` (Sonnet's note).
