## What was built

`rondata::stores` (`crates/rondata/src/stores.rs`; tests in `stores/tests.rs`) is a scanner of the
executable's stores to a field of `LeaderData`. It does not judge by displacement alone, because
displacement does not tell records apart:

| displacement | stores in the listing | of them through a leader |
|---|---|---|
| `+0x270` (`dow`) | 724 | 3 |
| `+0x1c` (`score_explored`) | 1,453 | 4 |
| `+0x34` (`score_pop`) | 660 | 4 |
| `+0x2f4..+0x314` (`last_spoke`) | 315 | 1 |

The brief's kill (a displacement under `+0x200`) applies to every field at these counts, `dow`
included. So the scan tells stores apart by the **base register's provenance**. It reads
`llvm-objdump`'s listing of `.text`, cuts it at the linker map's (`sbl/rise_z.map`) 54,255 function
starts, and runs a forward dataflow to a fixpoint in each function. No function is left unconverged;
that is pinned. The scan takes 1.2 to 3.7 s in release, plus objdump's 4.5 s.

A register is a leader pointer when it comes from one of these:
- `this` on entry to a `Leader`, `LeaderData`, `LeaderOut` or `Leaders` method;
- an address in `leaders.list` (the map's `?leaders@@3VLeaders@@A`, `0xe3a390`);
- a load of `GameAccess::leaders` or `GameAccessConst::leadersc`;
- the return of the two map functions whose decoration returns a leader.

A load from the heap or a global is **not** a leader. No record type in `types.txt` has a field typed
as a pointer to any of the four classes. The relocation table rebases only two static words into
`leaders.list`, the two references, and that is pinned. Stack loads and entry registers are a
caller's value (`V::Arg`). Those count only in functions a leader can arrive in: callees of an
escape, decorated names that mention a leader type, and functions that spill one off `esp`. The
`ebp` frame's slots are tracked. A loop's walking pointer is widened at the back edge and charged to
the element it starts at.

Choice of home: a crate module with its tests, not a `tools/` script. The analysis is the test's
subject, and the next frozen-leaf release calls `scan_install` with its own fields. The listing
comes from Homebrew LLVM's `llvm-objdump` (`$LLVM_OBJDUMP`, else `PATH`, else the keg). A machine
without it, or without the install, skips and says so.

## What is not established

- **The scan is a census under the program's types, not a proof.** It cannot see a leader pointer
  laundered through an integer or `void *` field, a function the map does not name, an `esp` spill
  (it widens scope instead), a loop walking past the array it starts in or past `SPAN_CAP`, or code
  reached only through an indirect jump with no readable table.
- **Untyped callees are judged by the callee, not scanned.** The callee's extent is not computed.
  `_memset`'s length is not read, and a `DataWalk` pair's extent is taken from the two pointers it
  is handed.
- **Typed escapes are trusted to their type.** An interior pointer passed as another class's `this`
  or as a typed parameter writes only inside that type's object.
- **Reading-only:** the claim that no record type holds a leader-typed pointer field rests on one
  grep of `types.txt`. The test does not re-derive it; the two static words are pinned.

## Parked

- **4159sa. `esp`-relative spills are not tracked.** One reached unresolved site stays because of
  it (`IFaceAirGarrison` at `+0x4b4`). Tracking `esp` needs each callee's stack cleanup, which is
  `ret N` from a first pass over the listing. That would clear it, and would narrow `V::Arg`'s
  scope.
- **4159sb. Untyped callees' extents.** The pushed `_memset` length and the `DataWalk` end pointer
  could bound each bulk call instead of "everything above". No release here needs it.
- **4159sc. The loop-walk charge.** A walking pointer's store is charged to the element it starts
  at, on the assumption that the walk stays in its array. A trip count read off the loop's compare
