  passes one figure's share (`+0x24 < local_c`). run989d: who 1's Cataphracts 1/72 and 1/74 on 17388 and 1/79 on
  17397, in melee, read ours 0 / theirs 1.

**A** folds the rows into `firsts`. It needed 398 re-pins (`repin.py --write` took 311, which I committed and then
reverted), 87 by hand: about 60 golden "what parts under the word" lists that pin `[]`, and six of `harness.rs`'s
