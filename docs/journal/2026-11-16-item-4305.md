## The oracle

`BuildOut::make_options@00629ed0` builds a selected building's panel through `Options::add@0072c290`. It runs on a
packet's own state under `tools/recomp/step4.py`:

- `--entry 629ed0 --this <building>`;
- `--poke` the console's `who` (`Console +0x298`) to the owner, because a building of another slot is offered only
  `OPTION_EJECT_ALL`;
- `--hook 72c290`, with the six arguments read at the add's entry.

The packet holds `options->mode` 0 and `selecting_spot` 0, which is the standard panel. The first run, on run1640's
slot 1 Barracks, returned in 1.5 s and 1.56 M instructions.

`tools/recomp/panels.py` now does this for every building of every slot. It reads `objects` and `MiscAccess::console`
off `sbl/rise_z.map` and writes one TSV row per add under `~/ron-data/panels/`:

- run1591 at 6537: 100 rows, every run returned;
- run1640 at 6079: 384 rows, every run returned.

`step4.py`'s hook now prints six stack words instead of four. The two new words come after the old four, and the one
test that reads the line (`test_recomp_flags.py`, `[esp+4]`) does not change.

The option numbers come off the data. `OPTION_TRAIN` is 47: the train arm's adds return to `0x62a742`.
`OPTION_RESEARCH` is 48: the Library's 35 research buttons on run1591.
