## The leaf, and its direction

`compare_target` entered on the city alone, with `--watch` on its locals. The raid arm at `0064ea57` needs three
terms: `-0x24` (vslot `+0x4c`), `-0x8` (`bVar17`, the raid stance) and `-0xc` (vslot `+0x20`). The reads were:
- `+0x20` 1;
- `bVar17` 1;
- **`+0x4c` 0**.

Vslot `+0x4c` on the BuildData vtable `0xb42174` is `WallData::is_active@00472350`, `flags & 4`, the finished bit. The
city's flags are `0x7eb0223`; the well's are `0x7f00213`. Both are unfinished. So theirs skips `/20` and takes the threat
formula:
- 80 × 80 × 100 / 873 = 733;
- × 10 = 7330 (the `ebx` the hook at `64eabd` printed);
- × damage 22 = 161,260;
- `(161,260 + 99) / 100` = **1613**.

The other gate at `64ea51` (`-0x1c`) is the target's `has_objmask(0x80000000)`, ANTI_AIR. Ours carries it as `!aa`.

**The direction**: ours took the `/20` arm and theirs did not. Ours' predicate was `is_build && raiding &&
self.active(target)`. [`Sim::active`] is alive-with-hit-points, not the finished bit. `docs/COMBAT.md` §68.1 already
said "active (`+0x4c`)". The code was the half that was wrong.

**The fix**: `raiding && target is a Build proper (index < WALL_BASE) && buildings[b].active`. The `+0x20` term is
`Buffer::is_pending_load`'s `return 1` for a `Build` and 0 for a wall, which ours had folded into `is_build`.
