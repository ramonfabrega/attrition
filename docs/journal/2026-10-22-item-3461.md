## For the Loop

A pool lane's restore can refuse on a sibling's launch. `unattended_capture.py`'s cleanup calls `require_closed`,
and that counts a game whose prefix `lsof` cannot yet read (a launch in its first second) as a human's. So a lane
that exits just as another lane launches is left staged, with `success: false` on a whole capture. Candidate: on an
unattributed pid, the cleanup retries `game_prefix` for a few seconds before it refuses.
