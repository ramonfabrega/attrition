## Step 1: under the emulator, and the listing

I wrote a scratch script on `tools/emu/callfn.py`'s machine (out of git).
It builds real `Build::vftable` and `Group::vftable` objects in who=0's
registry, points `GameAccess::objects` at the real `objects` global, and
adapts the imported `memcpy` as `command_oracle.py` does.
`Build::queue_up`, `LeaderData::researching` and the type record's `is`
are stubbed and recorded. `Group::add`, `push_group`, `equals_group`,
`get_open_slot` and `copy_group` run as shipped.

**The listing first.** The sort is `6fdc6b`..`6fdd86`. It compares the
pivot's `+0x82` with each later member's, unsigned, and swaps only on
strictly less (`jbe` at `6fdd4b`). A pivot that is not alive and finished
gets no inner loop. The passes are `6fdf20`..`6fe06f`. The answer of the
call at `6fe056` is not read. After the sort, vslot `0x14` is
`Group::action_begin`, which writes `+0x28` and nothing else.

**What the emulator showed** (the table is in §37):
- two empty members, `num` 3: 2007, 2008, 2007, 2008, 2007, 2008;
- `[q2, q0]`: 2008 first;
- equal queues keep the list order;
- a member that is not finished is never called, but it can be swapped
  forward;
- `num` 0 and −1 lay nothing;
- a refused call does not stop the passes;
- the research arm lays once;
- `action_buildmask(0x40)` on `[on, off]` gives `[off, off]`;
- the push of `[2007, 2008]` makes one record in that order and writes
  nothing on a building. The same group again writes nothing. The
  reversed group takes a new seat. A duplicate is dropped.

**The crate already had the sort, the passes and the toggle rule.** The
only piece missing was the seat: `push_command_buildings`' `if let [b]`
guard. **Checked against the listing's loops** (910): `process_group`
adds every live listed object, then pushes once at `0x94a6cf`.
`add_group`'s reuse test is an ordered `memcmp`.

**The counts** (823, 869):

  `new_library` and the constructor.

**What the emulator could not reach**: prices and room (the crate's,
