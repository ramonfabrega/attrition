- the killer's leader is `local_10 = leaders[param_8]` (`param_8 × 0x6eec`), and it must have tribe bonus 0;

**The pay** (`Unit::plunder:219`–`222`) is `((bucket +0xdc ^ 0x62766) + 1) × AZTEC_SLAY_BONUS`, capped at
`AZTEC_SLAY_CAP`. I grepped every reader of `+0xdc` across the export's `0x6eb8` bucket pointer. The field is
`LeaderDataEncrypt::ages` (`types.txt`; `walk_data@006d9900` writes it), the same word the Dutch HP and attack
bonuses read. So the crate's "slay count" was a misreading of the leader's age. `rules.xml` gives 15 and 60, and
45 = (2 + 1) × 15.
