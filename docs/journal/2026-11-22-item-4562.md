## The reading

`Object::do_launch@0064f3b0`, from the decompile and then the listing. The sortie scores three lists for each
leader L that is alive and not at peace with the owner:

- L's cities, `city_flags & 3 == 3`. Ours has this loop.
- When `LeaderData::is_enemy(L, owner)`, L's forts below `fort_mark` (`+6 & 1`). The value is
  `(damage / 100 + 1000) / (d / 0x300 + 1)`, never doubled.
- Also when L is an enemy, L's wonders below `wonder_mark` (`+0xc & 1`), skipping `is(0x213)`. The value is
  `damage / 1000 + 1000`, ×10 when `get_team_wonder_value < get_enemy_wonder_value`, ×100 when `get_diff > 2` and
  `wonder_winning` names a non-ally. Then `/ (d / 0x300 + 1)`.

replaces the best. Ours had the forts' and wonders' loops as a `SEAM` ("no list"). `silo_strike` has since made
`forts_of`, `wonders_of`, `team_wonder_points`, `enemy_wonder_points` and `wonder_winning`, so I reused them.
**The wonder's divisor, by the listing**: `0x10624dd3`, `sar 6` at `64fc9e`, is a thousandth. The silo's wonder
term at `6503c6` is `0x51eb851f`, `sar 5`, a hundredth, as `silo_strike` has it. The two arms differ, and the
