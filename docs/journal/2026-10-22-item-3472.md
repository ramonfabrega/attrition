- `camera 186,190` streamed new terrain on 5407.
- The click on 5410 went `on_left_up` → `left_click` → `find_target` → `MouseOver::set_good`.
- On 5411 `IFaceSelected::draw_good_name` and `GoodOut::draw_overlays` ran. That is herd 9's Good 37 at (185, 189),

calls +0x48 itself. So the animals fail one of `sort_all`'s gates before that call:
