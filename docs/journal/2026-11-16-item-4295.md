## The reading

- `Console::down@007cd8f0` puts the press's ground point in `drag_x`/`drag_y`, and `drag_form`/`drag_form_mod`
  from the selection.
- `Console::on_mouse_move@007cef20` clears `clicking` once the screen distance passes `click_drag_right_sensitive`.
  The constructor sets that to 6.
- `Console::on_right_up@007cd3c0` converts the release into `drag_x2`/`drag_y2`. It calls `find_angle` with `ecx`
  = `drag_x2 − drag_x` and `edx` = `drag_y2 − drag_y`. The decompiler printed `find_angle(unaff_EDI, unaff_ESI)`, and
  the listing at `7cd6e6` settled it. It then issues `move_to(drag_x, drag_y, queue, 1, angle, MOVE_TO, drag_form,
  drag_form_mod, 0)`.
  - The queue is the minimap's. Ctrl is not read.
  - A Caravan or a Merchant Fleet looks for a market first. A plane makes it a patrol.
- `find_angle@0092d130` masks with `& 0xffffc000` and shifts left by 2. That is why every recorded angle's low
  sixteen bits are 0. It is already `sim::movement::find_angle`, which the emulator's sweep diffs. One step of the
  ratio moves the result by less than the mask, so every multiple of `0x10000` is in its image. The census's "every
  angle in the image" is therefore exactly "the low sixteen bits are 0".
