**The chain**, read in the decompile and settled on the listing where the decompile mangles it:
- `adjust_for_mountains@008703c0` marks the cell that holds a range's peak tile:
  - flag `0x200`;
  - `snowcap_offsets`, the tile's place in its 4 × 4 cell;
  - but only when the template's `mountain_size` is not 1, and `snowcap_size` is 6 only for sizes 2 and 3.
  The `MountainRange` offsets sit 4 past the type record's, so +0xb0 is `mountain_size`, and +0xc4 and +0xe0 are the
  data pointers of `peak_tx` and `peak_ty`.
- `MountainRange::init@008998b0` takes the peak as the template's first vertex of greatest height, rows outer, every
  fourth pixel, at `(px − w/2) / 4` tiles from the range's origin.
- `CoordInfo::add_geometry@0084bef0` calls `add_weather_underlay@00874d00` on 6 × 6 tiles starting 3 tiles before
  the peak on each axis, clamped to the map.
- `add_weather_underlay` builds the mesh:
  - an odd corner steps back one tile and its side grows to even;
  - UV1 is (offset / side) · ½ plus a quadrant offset, and the quadrant is `random_map_pool[(4W + 1)·x + y] · 4 /
    100` at the unaligned corner, at most 3;
  - UV0 is (UV1 − quadrant offset) · 2, which spans the whole texture;
  - only the first vertex takes alpha 127, the vertex-alpha write's index never advancing (`875180`–`87518e`);
  - the constants 255, ½, 2, 100 and ⅓ are read from the PE.
