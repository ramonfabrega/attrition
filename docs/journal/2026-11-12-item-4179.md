## The dispatch, read

`Console::execute_at_cursor@007c6630` is what a right press reaches. `Console::right_click@007c4e70` calls it with
`(0, 0)` and the mouse move with `(1, 0)`, which only sets the cursor. The reader read the arms for one Citizen, no
modifier held, and the default profile; its report is outside the repository. I checked its three call sites in the
decompile myself:

- **an unfinished own building** draws `issue_swarm_around(ox, whom, queue, BUILD_AT)` (lines 885–897 and 1237);
- **a finished gather building with room** draws `issue_gather(ox, queue)` (931–945);
- **a finished, damaged building** draws the same `swarm_around` with `REPAIR` (1225–1237).

`BUILD_AT` is 6 and `REPAIR` 13, which are `sim::ids::order`'s. The rest is `docs/CLIENT_INPUT.md` §6.1.

Three findings differ from what `docs/GOLDEN.md` and the item supposed:

- **A gather building with room draws `gather` even when damaged.** The repair comes only when it is full or not a
  gather type.
- **A Scout on an enemy draws `follow`**, not attack, because its `ATTACK` is 0.
- **The garrison is not a default right press.** It needs the profile's `0x200`; without it, a Citizen on its own
  undamaged Village draws nothing at all.

So the queue's "a garrison where a unit enters" has no default-profile arm to mint (parked 4179c).
