## What was done

1. **The disk.** No Colonial Powers setup trace was on disk (§22.2), so no 7805..7945 existed for style 17.
2. **The size and listing compare, before any port.** In `INDEX.tsv` 0x697dd0 runs to 0x6984d0 (1,792 bytes),
   against Great Sahara's 368. It also has three unwind funclets, which Great Sahara lacks. `llvm-objdump -d` gives
   539 lines with one `make_region`, one `grow_region`, `check_player_land` and `eliminate_pools`. So it is not a
   dispatch. The decompile reads as two continents, opened half a turn apart on the ring `3·(m/2)/4`. The starts
   stand on the arc of their own continent. Region 2 grows with `avoid_center` raised to `7r/9`, and a short growth
   restarts the generation. The mask ends with `check_player_land` and `eliminate_pools(EDGES_ONLY)`. The listing
   settled what the decompile hid: `project`'s centre in `ecx`/`edx`, the spot and start outputs, the count and step
   arrays, and `push 1` for `EDGES_ONLY`.
3. **The take.** `df -g ~` read 82 GiB free and the lane was free. §22.2's line ran at `--map 17` through
   `viadriver.sh golden_capture.sh ~/ron-golden/run1534 … --cover cover=0`, waited on with `waitrun.sh` in the
   background, and filed with `filerun.py run1534 colonial-mapgen-start`. It took 26.3 s on pool lane 1: 37 frames,
   style 17, seed 12345, `TEAM_STYLE 1`. Minutes waited on another lane: none.
4. **What the shared code lacked.** `eliminate_pools` had only its `ENTIRE_WORLD` arm. `EDGES_ONLY` lists only the
   water regions a border cell holds, so an inland pool stays water. The new arm is
   `map_lakes::eliminate_pools_in(grid, edges_only)`, and the old name is its `false`. `check_player_land` with the
   guard on is East Meets West's call, so `map_east_west::check_player_land` became `pub(crate)` and is reused.
5. **The port**: `sim::map_colonial`. In the walk it is `MaskPort::Colonial`, `colonial_part` and the
   `COLONIAL_POWERS` row. The mask writes no `start_dist`, so the walk's `start_dist` check became per-port
   (`Option`).
6. **First walk: every phase agreed.** That covers 7807 and the whole 7,344-draw mask stream, 7835..7945 with 6,690
   draws, 734..815 (the half-land path, 720 among them), 5012..5118 (no river: an empty `RIVERLIST`), 894, and all
   58,081 start-dump vertices. The one red was the floors row, not yet pinned. No shared phase parted, and no shared
   fix was taken.
7. **Every other style's pins held**: `--lib mapgen`, 100 green, East Meets West and Himalayas among them. The one red
   is the queue's `Mapgen:` line.
8. **Made to fail** (MAPGEN §32.3). Unit tests came first: the arcs exactly, on four lobbies; the clamp on 24×24; the
   restart on 28×28 seed 99 (two stalls, a self-pin); and each border of `EDGES_ONLY`. One of those was wrong on its
   first run, because I had the row-major order of two border pools backwards; the expectation was fixed. Then
   `mutate.py --batch --timeout 900` ran 27 breaks, of which 22 held. Of the five that failed nothing, two were the
   restart and two the clamp; a probe found small maps that reach both (the probe was reverted and became the two
   tests above). The fifth was `max(4)` at 5, which changes nothing on run1534. A second batch of 8 all held,
   including `max` at 12 and at 20 (the latter hung and counts as held). So 34 of 35 held. The only break not run is
   the restore after the growth loop, which is an equivalent mutant. The border predicate is a disjunction: it was
   broken whole and in each of its four terms, and every break held. `EDGES_ONLY` → `ENTIRE_WORLD` also fails the
   walk alone (7807 and 7835), since run1534 holds an inland pool.

**Report: Colonial Powers 8 of 8 phases.**
