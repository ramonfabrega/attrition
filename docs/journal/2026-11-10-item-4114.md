## 2. What the install fixes

The install's files are a reading tool. What follows is paraphrase; nothing was copied.

**The lit branch** is `shaders/ps_global.hlsl` l. 238–388:

- The texture stage's colour comes first. That includes the team blend (`do_texture_operation`, l. 214), then
  saturate.
- That colour goes to the power 1.45. It is then multiplied by (diffuse + ambient) and specular is added.
- Diffuse is 5/π × the material diffuse × the light's diffuse × N·L, clamped at 0.
- Ambient is 5 × the material ambient × the scene ambient (`gAmbientLight`, PerScene `+0xc0`).

**The tone map** is `shaders/ps_tonemap.hlsl`:

- Hable's filmic curve, at its published parameters, on the exposure × the colour.
- That is divided by the curve at the white point × the frame's mean luminance. The mean is mip 10 of the
  luminance pass (`ps_luminance.hlsl`: weights 0.3, 0.59, 0.11), floored at the minimum luminance.
- Then a lerp from grey by the saturation, then a tint.
- Bloom is screen-blended after (`ps_blend_screen.hlsl`). It is not modelled; it acts over a threshold of 0.6 at a
  power of 12.5.

**The constants, by source** (`d3dgl.dll`, `sbl/d3dgl.pdb`, `sbl/d3dgl.map`; `llvm-objdump` on the DLL):

- **The tone mapper.** `GSToneMapper`'s fields are in both PDBs (`mExposure` +0x8 … `mTint` +0x28).
  - `D3D11ToneMapper::D3D11ToneMapper@10042c40` writes exposure 1.0, adjustment 0, white point 3.5, threshold 0.6,
    minimum luminance 1.4, saturation 1.0, bloom strength 6.0, tightness 12.5, and tint 0.
  - `update_params@10044860` sends exposure + adjustment and the tint bytes divided by 255.
  - In the executable, `Scene::render@008b3270` only calls `update_params` (slot 8) and `do_run` (slot 4) through
    `graphsys`'s `get_tonemapper` (slot 0xbc). No function names a field, the setters are non-virtual, and no INI or
    XML in the install names an exposure. So the defaults hold.
- **The scene ambient.** `Game::init_light@00590ef0` reads `unit_graphics.xml`'s `UNITS` attributes `ambientr`, `g`,
  `b` and `i` (internal strings 2707–2710). At 0x5912dc it hands them to `graphsys`'s `set_ambient` (slot 0x14c) as
  (0.6, 0.716, 0.745, 0.558). `D3D11GLSys::set_ambient@1001d860` stores them raw at `+0x620`.
- **The material.** `prep_unit_lighting` scales by a percent of 1.0 (`0xb69514` in `.rdata`, read off the PE).
  `D3D11Context::set_material@1001ab00` multiplies each term's r, g, b by its alpha and stores alpha 1. So the
  ambient is (0.6, 0.716, 0.745) × 0.558 and the diffuse is (1, 1, 1) × 2.5.
- **The light's diffuse** is (1, 1, 1, 1), per 4106.
- **The frame's mean luminance** is a function of the whole HDR frame before the interface, floored at 1.4. The packet
  does not hold the DLL's data: the card's vtable `0x786352ec` lies outside the image.
