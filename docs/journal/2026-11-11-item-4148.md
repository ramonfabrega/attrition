## Step 2: the writers, by both nets

**`was_seen` (`WData +0x14`).** The export's net (`wnet4148.py 14 was_seen`) has these stores in
a `WData` context:

- `World::set_seen` and `World::set_seen2`, on both arms of `param_4`.
- `World::set_was_seen`, outlined.
- `Wall::start`, the footprint walk: `tile >> 2` beside `seen2` at `tile >> 1`.
- `Object::update_seen_ally`.
- `WData::WData` (writes 0).

The listing's net: 19 functions name the world and hold a byte store at `+0x14`. Five of those
stores are the ones above: `orb %bl, 0x14(%eax,%ecx,4)` in `set_seen` and `set_seen2`, and
`movb %al, 0x14(...)` in `set_was_seen`, `Wall::start` and `update_seen_ally`. The others
(`MapConquest`, `MapScenario`, `Terrain::init`, `TerrainOil`, the scenario editor and reader) are
the `SimpleArray` reset pattern: `+0x10` freed, `+0x8`, `+0x4` and the flags byte `+0x14`
zeroed. The rest (`Roads`, `River::clear`, `Mountains::init`, `TerrainMap`, `TerrainWin`) name no
`wdata`.

The wider stores that cover `+0x14`: `World::wipe`'s `movw %di, 0x13(%edx)` (it zeroes `solid`
and `was_seen`), and among word and long stores at `0x11..0x14` nothing else in a cell context.
`set_was_seen` has no caller in the listing. In every writer, each `seen2` write is paired with
its `was_seen` write. The exception is `GameDaemon::update_all_seen`'s frame-0 ally arm, a raw
byte loop over `seen2` that runs only with allies and that this crate does not carry.

**`wcoord_seen` (`World +0x168`).** The export's net has `World::set_seen` (always) and
`World::set_seen2` (the `param_4 == 0` arm, the same arm that writes `seen`) as its or-ers.
`World::clear_seen` zeroes it with `seen`, by `memset` over `size`. `World::init` allocates and
zeroes it, `World::close` frees it, and `World::walk_data` and `WorldData::log_data` read it. Its
one reader in a game is `WorldData::is_seen_wcoord`.

The listing's net (`load4148.py 0x168`, loads of the pointer in a world-naming function) finds
`set_seen`, `set_seen2` and `walk_data`. In `World` methods without the global, it finds
`close`, `is_seen_wcoord` and `log_data`. `clear_seen`'s `pushl 0x168(%ebx)` into `memset` is the
one push-form reference. The pointer's own stores are in `Game::close`,
`Game::run_oos_recovery` and `ScenarioRead::import`.

**The callers** (the listing's `call`s):

- `set_seen`: `Object::update_seen`, `update_all_seen` (the scenario reveal points), and
  `ScenarioFuncSet::set_seen` and `set_explored`.
- `set_seen2`: `Object::update_seen` (the `visible` mask, `param_4` 0), `Unit::update_local_seen`
  (0), `Wall::update_local_seen` (`!wonder`), and `Ammo::do_damage` (0xff, 0).
- `clear_seen`: `update_all_seen` and `World::wipe`.
- `clear_seen2`: `World::wipe` alone.

**What clears each word.** `was_seen`: `WData::WData` and `World::wipe` only, never in a running
game. `wcoord_seen`: every resync. No cheat, reveal or departing leader writes either word.

**Not read:**

## Step 3: the port

`crates/sim/src/world.rs`:

- Two new fields, `was_seen` and `wcoord_seen`, one byte per cell. `World::set_fog` (the fog
  grid's allocation, `World::init`'s) allocates both zeroed.
- `World::set_seen` ors the mask into both words, at the cell holding the fog cell.
- `World::set_seen2_only` ors it into `was_seen`.

`update_local_seen_unit`, `update_local_seen_build` on both arms, `nuke_reveal`, `Wall::start`'s
footprint (`cities/city.rs`) and `update_seen_ally_build`. Integers only.

values (`05 cell.was_seen` 3,600 unheld, then `07 wcoord_seen` 3,600 unheld), and then green.
Two seams close: `update_local_seen_unit`'s and `nuke_reveal`'s, each of which named these two
writes.
