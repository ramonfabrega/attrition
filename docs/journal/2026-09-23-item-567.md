## The reading, and what it found

The chain was short once the frame named it. `Army::process`, on the
tick, calls `Group::action_siege_attack_to`. For an AI army with no siege
that function takes the first supply wagon as the anchor, sends it on,
and calls `Group::action_guard(anchor, who, QUEUE_NEW, 1)` on the rest.
`crates/sim/src/group.rs` stopped at that call with the comment "GUARD
is not an order the simulation has". `docs/ORDERS.md` §1.7 listed
`GUARDORDER` as a kind this crate could not spell, and the review in
`Unit::work` carried the GUARD arm as a seam. Nothing in the chain was
wrong. One link had never been built, which is what 552's five defects
looked like too.

Built: `Group::action_guard` for a unit target, `add_guard_order`,
`update_guard_order`, `do_guard`, the review's sixty-four-frame arm, and
the harness's parse and comparison of `GUARDORDER`'s own row
(`order:guard.*`). `docs/ORDERS.md` §24 has the reading. Three things in
it were read from the listing, not the decompile, and one corrected an
earlier document:

- **The post's four fastcall pairs.** `sinx(a, dy)` and `cosx(a, dx)` go
  on x, and `−cosx(a, dy)` and `+sinx(a, dx)` on y, with `a` the target's
  heading. The decompile prints `unaff_EDI/ESI` for every one.
- **The idle facing is `find_angle(post − target)`**: outward, away from
  what is guarded.
- **The reposition timer counts world cells (768), not tiles.** §7.5 said
  tiles, and it is corrected in place. run133's 419 and 389 settle it:
  30 × 14 − 1 and 30 × 13 − 1.
