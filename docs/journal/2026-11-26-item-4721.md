## The cause

`compute_site_stats` has one exit that zeroes every site of a leader at once: the distance loop `return`s on a dead
city slot (`006cd9e3`, `docs/AI.md` §2.13 step 12). A second probe printed the city records on each sweep: on 3751
record 5, who=2's city at (16, 34), is `owner 2, alive false`. It was taken on 3644 and re-founded for who=1 as record
8. Ours walked every record with `owner == who`, so it reached the dead one.

The original walks `cities.list[who][i]` for `i < LeaderData +0x408`. That length is the list's, not a count of live
cities. Its writers:

- `Cities::init_city@007352c0` reuses the first dead slot inside the length, else appends, and sets the length to one
  past the slot it filled.
- `Cities::close_city@00733310`, after `City::close`, pops trailing slots whose `flags & 1` is clear.
- `Cities::capture_city@00733380:1157`–`:1166` does the same pop inline after its own `City::close`.

`City::close` itself has no other caller, so the length is always one past the last live slot. who=2's list is
records 2, 3, 5, and 5 is at the tail, so the capture cut it off. A dead slot ahead of a live one stays inside the
length, and the walk still returns on it.

## The build

`Sim::city_list(who)` is who's records in order (a dead record keeps its owner, and `init_city` already reuses the
owner's first dead slot), cut after the last live one. `compute_site_stats`' distance loop walks it.

The killer: `ai::ai_sites::tests::a_trailing_dead_city_slot_is_off_the_list_and_a_middle_one_is_not`, on three
cities of who=1 around a site at (16, 16). Three live cities score the site (`dist` 1922). With the third dead,
`city_list` counts 2, the site still scores, and the score is the one it gets with the record given to nobody. With
the first dead instead, the list keeps 3 and the site scores 0. The mutation (`tools/mutate.py`, `self.city_list(who)`
→ the old `self.cities.cities.iter().filter(|c| c.owner == who)`) held: `zsh exited 101`, the killer failed on the
trailing-slot assert.
