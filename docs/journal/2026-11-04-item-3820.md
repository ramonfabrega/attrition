## The reading the port needed

The decompile gave the shape. The listing settled four things the decompiler blurred:
- **The ring's centre is `(xs/2, ys/2)`.** It is passed in ecx and edx at `006977be`; the decompile prints
  them as `unaff_`.
- **The two passes' `max_dist` is half the first radius**, not the fallen one: `-0x10(%ebp)` at `0069788c`
  and `006978df`, written at `0069774f` before the ring loop. The pass sizes are `A/3` (the `0x55555556`
  multiply) and `A`.
- **Both passes' failures jump back to `00697590`**, the top: `World::wipe` and `Regions::clear_all`, then
  the angle drawn again. `wipe` zeroes the four start arrays' counts (+0x84, +0xa0, +0xbc, +0xd8), so a
  restart starts them empty. `avoid_modify` is a `Map` field and carries across.
- **`grow_region` returns non-zero exactly when it ends short** with `param_6` 0: 1 for an empty record, or
  the count on either stall. Great Lakes' port had dropped the return. It now returns the bool, and Great
  Lakes ignores it.

Also from the reading:
- The player records take flags `|= 2`, and the further islands' take 5, 5 and 1. `grow_valid` and `point`
  test bit 1 only, so neither set of flags changes a grower.
- There is no invert, no `eliminate_pools` and no `check_player_land`. So the islands' region numbers stand
  in the cells at 7807's part 5.
- `avoid_continent` takes `max(4, …)` here, against Great Lakes' `max(6, …)`.
