## 1. What the original hands the skeleton (the listing)

- **`GuyOut::draw@005dc490`.** The time is `cur_time · 200 / 3 + graphic_dt`, clamped to
  `local_14 = animmgr.times[action_ids[slot]]`. A unit with `+0x6c & 0x10` instead takes `· 200 / 3 + 0x42` for
  both times. `cur_anim` is the guy's slot. Two branches overwrite them: entering or exiting a building gives slot
  8 at `order+0xc · 0x43`, and the `0x15f`-category branch gives slots 11 and 12 on `trench_angle`'s clock.
- **`GraphicPieces::draw_unit@008e7da0`.** Its decompile fails, so this is from the listing.
  - **At `8e7e0b` it may remap the slot.** It does so when `graphsys->scalability.mem_grade` (+0xe4) ≥ 3 or
    `section_render[11]` (+0x13c → +0xb) is 0. Slots 0–6 then become 0, slots 7–9 become 8, and a slot whose
    `UnitAnimCat` is 12 becomes 12. The time becomes `(Game::frame · 200 / 3) mod get_anim_time(slot)`, a clock
    shared by every guy.
  - **At `8e8be6`** it writes `cur_anim` and `cur_time` to the piece's `AnimationPacket` (`RData +0x54`:
    `cur_action` +0x8, `cur_time` +0xc). Then it calls the piece's render.
- **The transform method** (`VShaderTransformMethod::execute`, listing `924730`–`9247a4`). It takes
  `AnimationPacket::get_animobj@00917170`, which is `animmgr.actions[action_ids[cur_action]]`, and `cur_time`. It
  looks for a cached pose of the same type and action with a time in `[t, t + graphic_error)`, `graphic_error`
  being 2 on the packet. Otherwise it calls `HierObj::set_to_time` (vtable `+0x14`) on the piece's `HierObj`
  (`RData +0x48 → +8`) and then `update_all_transforms@00536db0`.
- **`HierObj::set_to_time@00536e30`** wraps the time by the node's `anim_time` and finds the bracketing pair. It
  blends by `key_flags & 3`, and leaves `iKey1`/`iKey2` (+0xac/+0xae) on the node.
- **The one direct caller of `set_to_time`** is `GuyOut::graph_inc_frame@005dd200`. That is the footprints'
  pose, not the draw's.

**What the process keeps.** One `AnimationPacket` and one `HierObj` per piece, which the last guy drawn sets. On
the packet that is `cur_action 13`, `cur_time 1400`: none of the five, since no guy of the five is in slot 13. So
the process keeps no pose per figure, and the photograph's own pose of 1/20 is not on the packet.
