## 2. The two causes, each with its record

**(a) The colour space.** The client drew to `Rgba8UnormSrgb` and `Bgra8UnormSrgb` and uploaded every texture as
`Rgba8UnormSrgb`. So the card decoded each texel to linear, took every blend in linear light and encoded the
result. Three kinds of arithmetic were wrong:

- **The vertex colours.** The owner's colour went in as bytes over 255 and was encoded on the way out. The flat
  tree's (0.25, 0.45, 0.2)·lit came out as (127, 166, 115), which is the pass's box.
- **`ADDSIGNED`.** `t0 + t1 − 0.5` on linearised texels falls to near zero. That is the mountain's (22, 18, 6).
- **The patch and the water's alpha.** Both blend in linear light.

The original's fixed-function stages and the painter take bytes. The fix: a texture's format follows the target's,
and the client's targets are plain `Unorm`. With only this changed, the run1559b ground is the painter's tile for
tile: 1,097 of 1,109, under a mountain 105 of 106, no crossing. The mountain's box sums 352 (photograph 392); it
summed 130 before.

**(b) The silhouette's place.** §5.3's reading (4085: list 1 is `render_states[1]` and then `[0]`) has the
silhouette drawn before the lit pass. The client drew it after. Its depth test, Greater, then passed on a figure's
far side behind its own near side. That is the wash: on the plain target with the silhouette after, colour reads 2.

With the silhouette moved before, colour reads 13. Two blends were still left, and the value diff shows them. 0/1's
pixel (1720, 387) was (206, 26, 11) on the still against the painter's (180, 47, 24). That equals 0.75·(255, 0, 0) +
0.25·(59, 104, 44): the closed mesh's near and far sides both pass behind the pine, so the colour is laid twice. The
painter's rule is one blend, `c·a + n·(1 − a)` (4103). A stencil (`ONCE`: Equal 0, then increment) keeps the first
blend. 0/1's pixel (1689, 390) is now (156, 51, 23), 0.5·(255, 0, 0) + 0.5 of our flat green. **Colour 14.**

## Parked

- **4171a. A silhouette in a ring that the painter's keys miss.** 0/1's ring on the still holds 91 of 1,104
  pixels of silhouette red, at pixels where `frame_scene`'s keys are 0. So either the client draws a figure there
  that the painter's `Drawing`s do not, or it draws a silhouette over a surface the painter's `behind` does not
  take. Which one is unread. Settled by a key pass on the card, or by `figure_meshes` diffed against
  `World::objects` on 3280. Scored by `RUN1559B_3279_TRACED_STILL`.
- **4171b. The card's mip level against the painter's.** The card picks a level per pixel from the derivatives;
  the painter picks one per triangle from the area ratio. 0/5, 0/6 and 1/34 part from the painter here. Which one
  the photograph agrees with is not read: the original's card picks per pixel too, but its chain is
  `GraphicPieces`' own. Settled by the original's level per draw, read off the card's setters
  (`RON_CARD_PROBE`, §3.14). Scored by `RUN1559B_3279_COLOUR_STILL`.
- **4171c. The original's cull on the figure states.** 4085's packet rows give cull mode 2 on both `[0]` and
  `[1]`. The client culls nothing; the stencil blends a silhouette once, where the original's cull would do it on
  a closed mesh. Where a mesh folds on itself (two front-facing layers), the original blends twice and ours once.
  Not drawn; no row tells the two apart on this frame.
