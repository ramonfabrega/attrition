## The reading the port needed

§5 had the reading in kind. The decompile and the listing gave the rest:
- **The lakes are land, grown on water and inverted.** `World::wipe` leaves every cell water. Each lake is a
  *land* region (`make_region` and `point` write `land` 0), and `invert_land` swaps the two at the end.
- **`grow_valid`'s first refusal is distance from the map centre, not from a region.** The `vector_dist`
  arguments at `0069d0ae` are `|x − xs/2|` and `|y − ys/2|`. Any other live region and the point within
  `avoid_center` refuses. This happens before the edge draws.
- **A size is drawn after every attempt**, whether it opened a lake or not.
- **`point` returns false only on a `grow_valid` refusal.** A cell off the grid or already taken is a
  success, and the stamps' short-circuits read exactly that.
- **`grow_region`'s draw sites are four**, by listing: `0069c860` is the `count/8` gate, `0069c8ae` the
  newest-eighth jitter, `0069c9d0` the ring pick (`local_1c + r % n`), and `0069ca04` the whole-region pick.
- **The starts walk in from outside the map.** They start at radius `3·xs/2` from `(xs/2, xs/2)` (the
  centre is `xs >> 1` in both registers) and step in by 3 to the first land cell two in from every edge.
- **`check_player_land` is called with `ecx = 1` and `edx = avoid_continent`** (listing `0069a60f`). Its
  ring is unit type 349's `max_range` quartered and rounded up. The type dump prints 24, so the ring is 6.
  This closes §14's "+0x1fc" question. After `invert_land` every `region` is 0, so its neighbour searches
  adopt or protect nothing. It makes land of the whole disc round each start-city cell.
- **`base_edge`** is `Map::make`'s `r % 4`, the draw that left the seed at 7805. It is recovered from that
  seed (`rondata::mapgen::base_edge_before`).
- **`avoid_continent`** goes through `scale_land_area` unchanged at or under `get_player_overage`'s
  allowance. Past it, the original divides in floats; the port refuses that case.

No float on the path. `land_dist` is rings of the circle table. `grow_valid`'s and `grow_region`'s distances
are `world::vector_dist`. The spacing's root is the function's own integer Newton loop
(`isqrt`, checked against `r² ≤ n < (r+1)²` for n < 5,000).
