*Opus 5.5, one session (lane-4293), no subagents. The census came first: all 200 orders carry `QUEUE_LAST`, the
shift alone, and are of four kinds the layer already issues (`move_to` 186, `build` 6, `gather` 4, `swarm_around` 4).
So the item's stop was not reached. Then the build: the layer takes the modifiers from the window, and every arm
writes its own issuer's queue. `INPUT_WHOLE_ORDERS` 927 · 994 · 508 → **927 · 1,190 · 312** of 2,429.
`INPUT_WHOLE` holds at 42 of 50. This branch does not touch `crates/sim`.*

## The reading

Every issuer reads its own modifiers, and they differ on alt and on ctrl (§33.1):

- `execute_at_cursor`: shift, then `alt ^ 1`, with alt only under `prefs & 0x400` clear. Ctrl with a unit selection
  is the attack-move, asked first.
- `WorldMap::on_right_up` and `do_formation`: shift → `QUEUE_LAST`, alt → `QUEUE_FIRST`. On the minimap, ctrl gives
  `ATTACK_TO`.
- `picked_spot`: the same as the minimap. Shift or ctrl keeps the placement armed, unless the type is a wonder
  (vtable `+0x1c` of `TypeData`, `is_wonder_type`, settled by `vtables.txt`). Ctrl's queue is `QUEUE_FIRST`, and
  shift's `QUEUE_LAST` overrules it.
- The ground's gather point: `add_to_end` from ctrl or shift.
- The garrison's queue is a literal `QUEUE_NEW`, so §23 stands.
