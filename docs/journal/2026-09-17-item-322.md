## 2026-09-17 — item 322: the building that tells the enemy where it is (Opus 5)

**The answer was printed on every record of the dump, and had been for
four months.** `BUILDDATA` writes `ever_seen` and `ever_seen_completed`
from `BUILDS=1`; nothing in this crate had ever parsed them. At run95's
block 8002, exactly two of player 0's seven buildings read **3** — the
Small City `0/2000` at half-cell `(8, 80)` and `0/2001` at `(11, 74)` —
where the other five read `1`. Those two are the centres of the two
patches that item 320 spent a session failing to attribute. The whole of
it cost one `grep` of a block that was already on disk.

Great Lakes' word: **8031 → 8186**, measured by
`run53_s_24000_frames_put_the_ceiling_where_run33_did` on the tip after my
last `ccc update`. The sequence went with it, to 8182.

### The offset search, which did work

The brief's open step was to find the writer of `World +0x160` and
`ObjectData +0x40` **by offset**, not by name, and the offset search was
right on its first pass: grepping `field_0x160` over the decompile turns
up four writers a `seen2` name grep does not — `World::set_was_seen`
(no callers at all), `Object::update_seen_ally` (setup only:
`Setup::build_cities`, `ScenarioRead`, CtW), `Wall::start` (the owner's
own bit over its own footprint), and `GameDaemon::update_all_seen`'s
`frame == 0` reveal-map arm. All four are eliminable in a line, and none
is it.

What was **not** the same search is where it actually landed.
`ObjectData +0x40` is `visible`, its writers are `Unit::set_attacking` and
`Unit::do_cast` (`|= 1 << who`, cleared by `Unit::work`) — so `visible` is
the rule that *a unit which attacks you becomes visible to you*, and it
has no building path. It is real, it is unmodelled, and it is not this.

The thing that found the answer was grepping the **vtable slot**.
`update_local_seen` is `+0x164`, and `(**(code **)(*(int *)this + 0x164))()`
has **ten** call sites: `Wall::check_ever_seen`, `Build::process`,
`Build::do_attack`, `Build::do_missile_launch`, `Build::check_capture`,
`Build::start`, `Object::update_seen`, `Unit::set_attacking`,
`Unit::do_cast`, `GameDaemon::update_all_seen`. Item 320's "reached only
from `update_seen(0)` and `update_all_seen`" was an elimination over two
of ten. `CLAUDE.md` has the rule verbatim — *grep the callers of every
function you call once-only* — and 320's own journal says the same thing
about the same function; the failure was to treat "grep the callers" as
"grep the name", when the callers of a virtual are the callers of the
**slot**.

### What the mechanic is

`Wall::check_ever_seen@0063ce70` scans the building's footprint against
`World +0x15c` — `seen`, the **current** plane, not `seen2` — and ors what
it finds into `ever_seen`. Its tail calls `update_local_seen` once, the
first time a leader that is not the owner appears there.
`Wall::update_local_seen@0063ed50` then ors `ever_seen | visible |
(1 << who)` into `seen2` over the footprint **grown by one tile on every
side**. The cadence is `Wall::process`'s very first statement: `frame != 0
&& (frame & 7) == who` — every eighth frame, on the one whose low three
bits are the owner's player number. `docs/VISION.md` §6.1 has all of it,
including the wonder branch (mask `0xff`, every player at once) and the
`param_4` that keeps the write off the `seen` plane — which is what stops
one building's reveal from convincing the building next door that it has
been spotted.

**The geometry needed the dump twice.** `docs/PATHFINDER.md` §20 had the
capital as "6 × 6, tiles 13–18 × 157–162", and on that reading the
rectangle is half-cells 6–9 × 78–81 — which cannot make the row at
`y` 82 that actually parts. A Small City is **7 × 7**: `X_SIZE 7` in the
crate's own loader test, and the dump's `mylos 15` is `LOS 12 + 7/2`,
which is `docs/VISION.md` §2.1's tail confirming it from the original's
own byte. The 6 × 6 is the *blocked* region `BuildType::mask_me`'s
template paints inside the 7 × 7, and run95's `WORLD` scan says so: cells
`(3,39)`, `(3,40)`, `(4,39)`, `(4,40)` all carry `blocked 9`, which a 7 × 7
cannot produce and a 6 × 6 at 13–18 × 157–162 produces exactly.
`update_local_seen` walks the type's rectangle, not the mask's: tiles
12–20 × 156–164, half-cells **6–10 × 78–82**. The columns at 9 and 10 and
the three cells `(8, 78..80)` were already lit by the scout's own disc.
Twelve remained; `0/2001` is the other two.

### What the word at 8186 is, and what it is not

Printed, not attributed: 8186 is `ours 6 draws, theirs 54`, diverging at
index 4 where the original spends an unnamed `602129` and this crate
spends `Guy::set_anim < Animal::do_idle`; the sequence's own 8182 is
`Leader::make_stuff+0x63d`. **No dump on disk carries frame 8186's
positions** — run94's per-frame window is `[7754, 8045)` and run95's
`DUMP_ALL` is 7999–8003 — so the value diff beside this word is a capture
the successor books, not a number I can report. I checked the disk before
saying so, which is the rule that has paid three times today.

### Three lessons, and the first is the cheap one

- **Grep the dump before the decompile, not only before a capture.**
  `CLAUDE.md` says to grep the dump before booking a *reading*; this item
  says it one level earlier. `ever_seen` is a field the original prints on
  every building of every block, and the question "who lit those
  half-cells" was answerable by reading two bytes out of a file already on
  disk. The offset search was correct and would have got there; the dump
  got there first and for nothing.
- **The callers of a virtual are the callers of the slot.** A name grep
  finds four of ten here. The slot is in `vtables.txt`, one line, and
  `0x164))()` is the whole query.
- **An elimination that names a count should name where the count came
  from.** "`BUILDDATA` 56, identical at 7932 and 7999" was a count of
  `BEGIN` lines over a record nested four deep; the block carries **28**
  buildings. The conclusion it supported — no roster change — happened to
  be true, which is exactly why nobody checked it.
