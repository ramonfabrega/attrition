# Item 408 — the AI headline's own frame, widened at last (2026-09-19)

*Opus 5, on the AI lane. `docs/AI.md` §47.*

## The word did not move

**Great Lakes' word stands at 9510.** 172 blocks widened, and the rows that
*disagree* across them are 516 positions, 5,361 order entries, 3,216 city
fields, 257 queue fields, **one** unlinked unit, zero extra units and zero
building identities. Of all of that, exactly two keys first part inside the
window, and they are the same frame: `1/2007` and `1/2019` on 9382.

Nothing here moved a score, and nothing here was expected to. The item was
to widen the frame that scores and name its cause; the cause is a frame 128
earlier, and separating the two readings of it needs a capture this lane has
not taken yet.

## What the item was

Great Lakes' long capture stops at word frame **9510** of 24,000, and
**nothing had ever widened that frame**. Item 402 widened the `AMMO` record
and the word did not move, but run109 covers `[9420, 9480)` and 9510 is
outside it; run97's value window stops at 9349. So the frame that scores had
no compared record behind it at all — every widening on this map had run out
below it.

The brief named no mechanism, deliberately (`docs/DECISIONS.md` 42). This is
the report of what the data named.

## What the frame is

run100 covers `[9340, 10899]` of the same seeded game at run97's detail, so
the widening is `compare` — every unit's position and order stack, every
building's identity and queue slot, every field of every `CITY` — over its
blocks from the first to the word's own. 172 blocks.

**The word is the sixth scholar.** 9510's two extra draws are
`Guy::init_real+0x52` and `Guy::set_anim+0x97a < Unit::go_inside+0x280`, the
birth-and-seating pair `docs/CITIES.md` §6.5.2 gave the map's *first* scholar
on 8272. This one is `1/51`, `guy` 52, standing in block 9511 on University
`1/2019`. The word block says it and nothing else: of 172 blocks the only
object either side holds alone is that unit on that frame —
`unlinked [(1, 51)]`, `extra []`.

**The cause is 9382 and it is a purchase.** The only record that newly parts
anywhere inside the window is the production queue, on one frame, on two
buildings at once:

| block 9383 | ours | theirs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1/2007` (Village) `queued` | **2** | 0 |
| `1/2019` (University) `queued` | 0 | **1** |

The original queues one Scholar; this crate queues two Citizens, in the same
city. Its `job_counter` then runs +100 a frame from 100 to 12700 and a
clamped 12750, and block 9511 is `queued 0` with the unit standing.

Every other divergence in the window was already standing when it opened —
three positions, seventeen order stacks, twenty `CITY` fields, all first
parting on block 9340 — which is what makes 9382 *the* frame rather than one
of a hundred and seventy-two.

## A purchase draws nothing — the result worth carrying

This is the reusable half of the item, and it stands on its own rather than
as a detail of the scholar.

**9382 costs eight draws on both sides, entry for entry.** Two
`Leader::use_market+0x1ed`, two `Leader::make_stuff+0x221`, two `+0x63d`, the
farm animal, the farm clock. §41 and §42 say what the make-stuff sites count
— slots holding the head's type, and the slot loop's expiries. Neither counts
a purchase, and the queue_up path spends no draw of its own.

So two make lists **of the same shape** were walked to two different answers
and the stream said nothing. The word stayed blind for **128 frames**, and
what it finally noticed was a `Guy::init_real` in the animation layer — a
birth, four subsystems from the decision that caused it. Every frame between
agreed draw for draw.

Two things follow. A draw-stream word is a **lower bound** on when a decision
parted, not an estimate of it, and the distance scales with whatever was
bought. And **the queue record is the oracle for an AI purchase**, because it
is the only place a drawless decision marks the frame it was made on —
`BUILDQUEUE` is written from `BUILDS=1`, so this frame's evidence had been on
the disk since run100 was captured and went unread because nothing compared
it.

That is the case for widening the record rather than the frame, made on the
frame the headline itself stands on.

## What it cost, and what it did not buy

Four release runs of the long word test and two of the new one; no capture.
The item **did not move the word** — it names the frame that would. What is
on the disk cannot separate "the Citizen's value is wrong" from "the Citizen
should not be offered at all", because `LEADERS=1` prints `who`, `tribe`,
`score` and `leader_flags` and not the make list. A **`LEADERS=9` window over
`[9375, 9390]`** is the one thing on this disk that cannot answer it.

Two things to hand on:

- The successor is **9382 and those two queue rows**, not a mechanism.
- Our Scholar offer names **city 1** where the original's job runs in city
  2's University; **not measured**, may be the same defect or a second one.
  It is left there deliberately — the standing queue item on host choice is
  not closed by this and is not re-pointed by it.
