## What the disk could not answer, and the take

run1561a's 300 frames of the minted file's playback print no `CHECKSUM` record and no `ITEM` record (`end:MISC`
prints neither), and no `DUMP_ALL` playback was on disk. So run 1562 was taken: the minted file, a packet at
`RON_STATE_FRAME=91` and `91 @dumpall`, the click-free lane, 101.1 s launch to exit (`docs/RUNS.md` run1562).

The runner refused a `--cmd-file` beside `--recording` whole ("the recording owns the lobby"). An `@dumpall` line
issues nothing to the game, so the refusal now lets a command file of `@dumpall` lines alone through
(`refused_playback_cmd_file`, with its test, red with the refusal stubbed out). That is `d7bed58bc` and
`8f8085a81`.

**A playback prints the windowed record.** It prints two, 3201 under `BEGIN FRAME 91` and 3202 under
`BEGIN FRAME 92`. Each has the 67-line shape: `CHECKSUM n`, `FILE`, `LINE` and 64 labelled lines. The reader places
both at `Begin(n)`, since nothing else prints in the window. **The packet reproduces the first, 3201: 15 of 15.**
Run1100's packet reproduced 3502, which the reader places at `End(5408)`. In both runs the packet's record is the
window's first and the second is one tick later. The hook names where a record printed, not which half of the tick
it is. The test now takes "the first windowed record of frame n" (`docs/CHECKSUM.md` §2).
