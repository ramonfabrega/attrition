## What the frame said

The five extra candidates all fail `blocked_site` in the original, and all
in the mine's gather survey. `find_nearest` walks the range's
**solid cells** (`solid_mount_wx/wy`, from the listing's offsets) to each
cell's **centre**. This crate walked mountain **tiles**. Two cells is 1536,
past the reach of 1152. `docs/ECONOMY.md` had written "every tile of its
range", and the listing says otherwise.

## What would kill each reading, written before the fix

- **The region filter** in `find_nearest`: killed if the refused sites'
  `find_nearest` returns −1. It returned a range at all five. So the region
  test is not it.
- **The cell measure**: killed if the distance at a refused site were
  ≤ 1152. It was 1536 at all five, and 1152, 984 and 576 at three accepted
  sites.
- **The membership rule "centre tile is a mountain"**: killed if the
  packet's solid lists differ from it. **They do**: 32 extra cells, none
  missing. So the fix closes one of the five cells and not four.

## The fix, and what it moved

`Sim::nearest_mountain_cell` replaces `nearest_mountain_tile`. It measures
to cell centres, skips a cell in another region, and seeds the range from
the winning cell's centre tile.
