# Item 494 — the citizen was never idle-tasked at all

*2026-09-22, lane att-494, Opus 5. Great Lakes' long word **10294 →
10303**; blocks 10234, 10294 and 10295 — thirteen rows — to none.*

## What was booked, and what it turned out to be

489 left the word at 10294: one draw here against the original's two, the
extra `Guy::set_anim+0x97a < Unit::do_idle+0x7d`, and block 10295 holding
six rows, every one the human's citizen `0/5`. 489 named the frame and the
delta and **no mechanism**, and the brief said so twice.

That was the right call twice over. `Unit::do_idle` is eleven lines, this
crate already had all of them, and none of them is wrong. The brief's own
framing — "`idle` ours 0 theirs 1 is a state and `orders_x/y` 3,264 apart
is a destination; which is cause and which is consequence" — has a third
answer: **neither**. Both are consequences of a decision taken sixty
blocks earlier, and the thing that took it is one `switch`.

`Unit::think_peasant@005f5760:16` gates a human's job search on
`LeaderOptions +0x8`, `peasants_wait`. It is an **index into five waits**
— `1→7, 2→0xc, 3→0x11, 4→0x20, 5→0x3e`, `default→2` — and this crate used
the index as the wait. `LeaderOptions::init` writes 2, every capture on
disk prints `peasants_wait 2`, so the wait every human in every run has
had is **12** and this crate used **2**. The same digit in two roles.

**Only then the decompile**, and it was two functions and one `switch` —

**And then the dump a third time, for the option's value.** run100 does
not enable the leader-options category, but eleven other captures do, and
they all print `peasants_wait 2`. Index 2, wait 12. No capture was booked.

## The arm that was right and never reached

The four rows on 10294 are the prettiest part. `move_step@005faf30:329`
faces a unit along its finished order's own angle — `if (orderlist.length
== 1) set_angle(order->angle)` — and this crate has had that arm since
§4.5, correct, gate included. It could not fire, because the citizen
arrived holding **two** orders. So the same spurious `GATHERORDER` paid
twice: once for the turn this crate did not make, and once for the idle
frame it did not spend.

That is also why the value diff needed all three blocks. `orders_x/y` and
`dest_angle` follow the *leading transit move*, which is the flight on
both sides, so for sixty blocks the extra order is invisible to every
field but `orders.len`. A fix that merely delayed the departure would
leave 10234's pair standing; the pin names all three blocks for that
reason.

## What the guards gained

`run100_s_citizen_waits_twelve_idle_frames_for_the_same_job` compares
`idle`, `orders_x` and `orders_y` block for block over `[10291, 10450]` —
the whole wait, not its endpoint — so a wait of any other length or a
cadence of any other period parts inside it. Made to fail on purpose with
the wait back at 2: first parting 10295, `(10295, 0, 4056, 28776)`
against `(10295, 1, 792, 31800)`.
`stance::tests::peasants_wait_is_an_index_into_five_waits` asserts the
five arms, both out-of-range ends, and the line a reader gets wrong —
that the default *option* is arm 2 and not the default *arm*. Made to
fail by putting 2 back in the table.

`sim::Unit::idle_threshold` is gone. It was a per-unit copy of a
per-player option the original re-reads on every call, and a field that
can only ever hold one player's answer is a field that invites this
mistake back.
