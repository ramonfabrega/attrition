## The fix

`Sim::xport_alias` (`ai/ai_census.rs`) maps a region to where an unbounded `reg_xport_peasants[number]` lands. A
number under 64 stays in its own slot. A number in 64..127 goes to `reg_gatherers` in the region numbered
`number − 64`. A number in 128..191 goes to `reg_gather_slots`. Anything past that, or a number no region holds,
lands nowhere. `census_xport_bump` and `census_xport_get` go through it, and they serve the census rider arm and the
transport view's `bump_reg_xport` and `reg_xport`. `docs/AI.md` §58.1 states the missing bound.

**Test.** `row_105_s_rider_to_sea_region_65_lands_in_reg_gatherers_on_run1752_s_ranges` (`diff::held_out`) compares
every leaders range on 1975 and 1976, ours against theirs, byte for byte. It also names theirs' record 1
`reg_gatherers[1]`: 0 on 1975 and 1 on 1976. **Mutation**: `tools/mutate.py --edit crates/sim/src/ai/ai_census.rs
'64..=127 => (XportCell::Gatherers, n - 64),' → '64..=127 => return Some((XportCell::Xport, usize::from(r))),'` (the
alias taken out for the sea). It held: exit 101, and the one failed test was that one, at `leaders range 129 on
1976, record 1 who..ally_mask`.
