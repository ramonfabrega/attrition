## What was booked, and what it turned out to be

The booking had two halves. The first was the seven of M's 38 pieces that did not decode, resolved through the
original's loader. The second was M's 21 building types, resolved and decoded, with a ruling on what a decoded
building earns without a dumped frame. Neither half needed a new decoder. Both were resolution, and one was a
reading of §5.4 that the code had not followed.

- **The six gaia pieces were never decode failures.** `artdata::entry_pieces` gave no piece to a gaia entry. The
  loader, `unit_node_from_gpiece@00901c30`, builds `{GRAPH}-TYPE{(p − first_bird_piece) % 3}` for any piece past
  `first_bird_piece`, which `init_piece_ranges@008f70e0` writes as `0xea8d`. The sheep's 60063–60065 and the fish's
  60072–60074 are exactly that arithmetic. `artdata::bird_pieces` adds the gaia entries to `unit_art` only.
  `piece_lengths` and the other sim-fed tables keep `entry_pieces`, so no sim input moved.
- **38322 fails on a slot the run never plays.** `THECITIZEN-DEFAULT-AGE0-CREW3`'s `CHAR_IDLE2` cites `CEO Crew2
  Idle3`, which `anim_graphics.xml` points at `ceo_crew3_idle3.bha`, and the install does not ship that file.
  §5.4's condition 1 asks for "every `.BHa` its *captured* animation slots name". `piece_decoding` had asked for
  every slot. `PieceDecoding::decodes_on` now reads condition 1 on the slots the runner's scenes show each piece
  in. `PIECES_DECODING` stays the every-slot count over the install. The file is still named, as missing, in
  `the_pieces_decode`.
- **A building is a sprite with a depth model.** `build_node_from_gpiece@008fd550` names the `<BUILD>` from the
  piece: `GRAPH`, the style (`say_build_art_style_name@006f03f0`), `-AGE`, the raw age. `get_build_gpiece@0090bce0`
  adds `0x81·style + 0x489·age + type`. `init_build_data@008f2f20` reads `texture`, `zbuffer` (a `.bh3`) and
  `anim`. `init_build_events@008ea3c0` reads each `EXISTEVENT`'s ammo subject and `anim`. The dump prints the
  piece the original chose, at `WALLDATA`'s level. `BuildDump::gpiece` reads it, and the oracle codec and
  coverage pins went with it, as item 3965's `mylos` did. That makes the building resolution diff-backed instead
  of a model of the age and style walk.
- **Names compare without case.** `String::operator==@00a1f140` falls to `_wcsicmp`. Four addon subjects, such as
  `metro_6t_ADDON`, match their `<AMMO>` only that way.

## The rulings (for the pass)

Both are written into §5.4 in place.

1. **Condition 1 is read on the played slots**, as §5.4 writes it.
2. **A building type's condition 1** holds when every piece the dumps print for it names a `<BUILD>` whose
   `zbuffer` model, own `anim`, and addon models and `anim`s all decode. An entry naming no model does not meet it.
   **Its condition 2 is unmet, not vacuous.** The dumps print building positions on the logged frames, but the draw
   list has no building entry to put in register. So a decoded building earns no K. Nothing is stubbed true.
