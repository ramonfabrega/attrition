## The box, read

The box is `Console::on_left_up@007cde90`'s arm for a release that is no longer `clicking`. I read it and its
callees myself, off the decompile:

- **The units come first**, and buildings only when no unit was taken.
- **The slots** are walked from the console's own, and the first with a unit in the box owns the selection.
- **The objects** are walked in index order.
- **A unit is in the box** when one of its figures' bounding boxes has its centre, projected, within 30 px of the
  rectangle (`BoundingBox::around_bounding_volume@00544860`).
- **`SelectGroup::add@00716c10`** caps the selection at `0x80`. It inserts each unit before the first member whose
  `TypeIndex` is greater, so the list is sorted by type, and in index order within one.
- **The default profile sets pref `0x200000`**, and with it a plain box is filtered. A box of workers (and Merchants
  and fishermen) keeps only the workers. Any other box loses its civilians: a box of Citizens and a Scout takes the
  Scout alone.

`docs/CLIENT_INPUT.md` §7.1 has the rest.

## The move's fields, read and then checked against the recordings

Neither the item nor the scripts named this:

- **A right press sets the formation on the button's press.** `Console::down@007cd8f0` writes
  `drag_form = max(GroupData::get_form, 0)` and `drag_form_mod = get_form_mod_option`.
- **The ground arm hands them over.** `execute_at_cursor`'s ground arm passes them to `issue_move_to` as the
  command's `form` and `width`.
- **The minimap's right press differs.** It issues `MOVE_TO` with `-1, -1`.

So the scripts' `-1/-1` single move is the minimap's fields, not the main view's.

The recordings on disk say the same. The heavengames sample's `move_to`s with `orders` 1 and `set_angle` 0 carry
(0, 50) or (9, 50) at every group size, and (−1, −1) 114 times for one unit. Our own captures' 49 recordings carry
(0, 50) and (9, 50) only. Its multi-unit groups' lists are ascending 66 times and not 81 times, which fits a list
sorted by type first. This was a one-off probe test, reverted, not kept.
