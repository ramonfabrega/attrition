## What was read

- **The face**: `D3D11GLFont` (`sbl/d3dgl.pdb`, field list `0x75D0`) `mSGLTexFont` +144, an `SGLFont` (`0x52CC`)
  whose `m_fontFace` +44 is an `FT::FontFace` (`0x51D5`) whose `m_Face` +0 is an `FT_Face`. On run1559b's packet
  that face's `family_name` is `Georgia` and `style_name` `Bold`; the install ships `Data/Fonts/georgiab.ttf`
  (`name` table: `Georgia` / `Bold`; units per em 2048, `hhea` 1878 / −449; no `hdmx`).
- **The rasterising** (`llvm-objdump` on `d3dgl.dll`, names from `sbl/d3dgl.map`):
  `FT::FontFace::RenderGlyph@10009e40` calls `FT_Load_Glyph(face, index, 0)` and `FT_Render_Glyph(slot, 0)` and
  returns the slot's bitmap rows, width, pitch, `bitmap_left`, `bitmap_top` and `horiBearingY >> 6`;
  `FontRenderManager::render_pendings@1004ad60` (its one caller) copies the bitmap into the atlas at cell
  `(tex_x · 256, tex_y · 256)` and row `ascent − bitmap_top`, with no `bitmap_left`; the atlas is 256 × 256.
  `D3D11GLFont::add_character@10029140` lays the quad at `x + abs_a + dx − 0.5`, `y + dy − 0.5`, `pix_width` ×
  `pix_height` scaled by `mScale` (1.0) — the constants `0.5` and `256.0` read from the DLL's `.rdata`.
- **The atlas is not on the packet**: `D3D11GLTexture` is 28 bytes, a base class and nothing else.
- **The cells are**: `CharacterInfo` `pix_width` +19, `pix_height` +20, `abs_a` +21.
  `tools/render/plate_font_packet.py` now prints `code:advance:pix_width:pix_height:abs_a`; over the 144 packets
  (`~/ron-data/lab-experiments/2026-11-19-item-4426/plate-font.txt`, exit 0, empty stderr) every `pix_height` is 16
  and every `abs_a` 0. The font: `mMaxCharWidth` 19, `mFontHeight` 16, `mAscent` 13, `mDescent` −3, `mPointSize` 14.
