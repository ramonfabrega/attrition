## The reading (`Object::do_damage@0064a480`, lines 296–735 of the export)

Under `attacker != victim`, before `take_damage`:

- `local_14` = attacker type `is_siege` (type vslot `+0x10c`, PDB: `ObjectTypeData::is_siege`; `UnitTypeData::is_siege`
  = `unit_flags & 0x20000`) and target vslot `+0x1c` (a building).
- **Building branch** (active building, `BuildData +0x72` city ≥ 0): heavy (`local_34`) = `local_14`, or `damage +
  local_28 >= hits/10` when type vslot `+0x104` (PDB: `ObjectTypeData::is_defense` = `is_fort || is_tower`) or obj
  flag `0x20` (a city), else `hits/4`.
  - not capital: heavy → alarm (`local_30 = 1`) if `frame − c.attack_stamp > 300`; then `c.attack_stamp = c.raid_stamp =
    frame`; light → `c.raid_stamp = frame`.
  - capital: heavy → alarm if `frame − leader[victim].capital_stamp[att] (+0x134) > 300`, stamp then; **no city stamp**;
    light → raid.
  - leader `attack_stamp`/`raid_stamp`/`ally_stamp[att]` and the notices: not carried (only `LEADERS=9` prints them).
- **Worker arm** (unit branch, lines 400–424): a struck worker (`is_worker`, types 0x32–0x35) whose
  `UnitData::get_action@00608450` answers `get_gather_order` (order vslot `+0xe4`, PDB method record) with an active
  building of the victim's in a city → raid on that city (always light).
- `Armies::emergency(victim)` only when `local_30` and the take did **not** kill (a non-zero `take_damage` returns
  first) and the victim is a computer. This crate fired it on every cross-owner hit on a city building.
