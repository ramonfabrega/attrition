any capture. 105 packets are under `~/ron-golden`. Each packet's header frame was held against the recon's
`scan.tsv` and its run's logs (`pair4060.py`, scratch), and one pair exists: **run1100**. Its packet is at logger
frame 5408 (trace frame 5407), its `rontrace.cmd` holds `5408 @dumpall`, and its `gamelog.txt` (archived as

of frame 5408's block with no `BEGIN FULL DUMP` above it. The other is 3503, the first child of frame 5409's

instrument settled that (below). **Run 1562 was not taken.** The two questions it would also have settled stay
open in `docs/CHECKSUM.md` §10: does the minted file's playback print the windowed record, and at 67 lines?

## The order of work, by SHA

1. **`32dc17a0b`, the instrument.** `tools/recomp/checksum.py` enters `CheckSums::check_all@00936560` on the
   packet under unicorn. It answers the two sync-logger calls (`retn 0x10` and `retn 0x14`, read on the
   listing); each value-carrying call ends a line and carries the original's `accum`. The fold
   `CheckSum::walk_function@00936ff0` is hooked and left to run. **The first run reproduced 15 of 15 printed
   lines, and its own Adler over the stream parted on `groups`**: 2145712412 against the original's
   3519151140. `CheckSums::check_groups@00937530` folds eight words with zlib's `adler32@005089d0` from a
   stack copy, which never passes the fold. With both Adlers hooked, the stream sums to the original's value
   on every line. Red on purpose, the wrong frame:
   - Packet 5408 against record 3503 gives 9 of 15. Units, builds, guys, leaders, goods and world part.
   - run1420's packet (logger frame 4586) against 3502 gives 2 of 15.
2. **`a2bf26ff0`, the reader and the score.** `rondata::checksum` reads every `CHECKSUM` record whole (64
   labels in print order) and places it by its hook. It is line-based because 3502 has no block of its own.
   It is not one of `SCHEMA_SOURCES`. Its four tests were red under a stubbed reader, then green. They cover
   a synthetic log, a changed line, run1100's two records against `frame_seeds` and the quiet lines, and the
   instrument's values file. `floors::checksum_line` and `the_handoff_s_checksum_line_is_the_pinned_counter`
   are red on the queue's line alone. `release_gate.py`'s `COMMANDERS_LINES` names the test.
3. **`fcfccf250`, the ledger.** The owner of a range is the function of its call site, and its `this` is
   that of the innermost live walker frame. A frame counts as live while its entry slot holds its return
   address. The first cut judged by depth alone and gave `check_cities`' ranges to a dead
   `LeaderData::walk_data` frame. The leaves come from `types.txt`, with nested records and arrays
   expanded. Where the export's order records are incomplete (`AttackToOrder` has no named field), the PDB's
   own field lists (`llvm-pdbutil dump --types`) place `TargetOrder` and `MoveOrder` and the `UnitOrder`
   virtual base at +0x2c and +0x54. `World::walk_data@006b5cf0` takes its world from the global at
   0x00c06188, not `ecx` (listing).
4. **`1f5a4d1a5`, `docs/CHECKSUM.md`.** The checksum, the reader, the instrument, the ledger per line, the
   pointer/float/padding census, the score, and what is not established. Two misreadings caught against
   the stream before landing:
   - The world's oil arrays are empty. Section 3 prints 524289, the Adler-32 of two zero counts.
   - LeaderData's two uncovered runs are zero. An awk offset first read them as the leader's index.
