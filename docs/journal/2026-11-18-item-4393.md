## The probe

`RON_CARD_POST`, under `RON_CARD_PROBE`, adds five hooks to the card's six. A logged pass's `begin_drawing` arms them,
and the second `flip` after the arm disarms them. Each copies its textures back:
- `ID3D11Texture2D::GetDesc`;
- a staging twin (`CreateTexture2D`: usage STAGING, no binds, CPU read);
- `CopyResource`, then `Map` for READ;
- the rows written to `post-NN.raw` beside the exe.

The device context is `D3D11GLSys::mD3DDeviceContext` (+1148). The device is the context's `GetDevice`. The buffers
are `d3dgl.pdb`'s type records:

| hook (d3dgl) | read | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `D3D11ToneMapper::do_run` `0x10044a30` | `mHDRBuffer` (+3408) `mTexture` (+1208); `mScreen` (+2112) `mTexture` | the chain's input; the screen, cleared |
| `do_blur` `0x10044de0` | `mTonedRTV` (+184), by `GetResource` | the toned frame, before the bloom |
| `do_blend` `0x10045040` | `mPingRTV` (+168), `mPongRTV` (+176) | the bloom's blur |
| `D3D11AntiAlias::run` `0x100186d0` | `mScreen` `mTexture` | FXAA's input |
| `D3D11GLSys::flip` `0x10023290` | `mScreen` `mTexture` | the frame presented |

The order comes from the listing:
- `do_run`'s no-bloom branch copies the toned buffer into `mScreen.mTexture` (`CopyResource`, vtable +0xbc).
- `D3D11AntiAlias::run` copies the screen into `mTempTexture`, then draws FXAA into `mScreen`'s RTV (+0xcf4).
- So the screen at `flip` is the chain's output.

`flip`'s first instruction is `cmp dword [0x100fb36c], 0`, and its `je` follows. The stub's displaced copy runs after
the observer and before the jump back, so the flags reach the `je`.
