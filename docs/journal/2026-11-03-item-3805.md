## Stage 1 — step 3's tail, 7861..7870 (`6d3e6ac0c`)

`sim::terrain_groups` gains three ports, read from the decompile and the
listing by me:
- `nubify_forest@006a93b0`, public. It walks columns, then rows. A run of
  four cells with forest on the same side draws `r & 3`, then re-forests
  the side cell that many back. That cell is skipped if it is a start-city
  cell, ocean, or on a region edge (`World::is_edge_of_region`).
- `change_bases`: the `change_*_base` passes, then `fix_transitions` and
  its `nubify_transitions(3, 2, 1)`.
  - The base passes use `orthog_x/y[1..=4]` straight from the tables:
    N, E, S, W.
  - The row pass of `nubify_transitions` bounds its rows by `xs`.
- `TilesetGroups` gains `FOREST_BASE`, `MOUNTAIN_BASE`, `COAST_BASE` and
  their growth chances, read by `TileSet::init@0087b370` (−1 when absent).
  GS_Desert's values are 3/30, 2/30, 3/30.

The trace's draw counts at 2 players:

- 370 `change_forest_base`;

- 0 coast (no Great Sahara cell is COAST);

## Stage 2 — `Map::place_resources`, 7927 (`2eef4b914`, `e42b39efc`)

**The readings.** Three Opus 5.5 readers each wrote a specification. They
are kept outside the repo at `~/attrition-readers/3805/{A,B,C}.md`:
- A: the ladder, the pool, `scale_number` and `find_avail_regions`, with
  the Great Sahara rows resolved.
- B: `place_player_resource`, the object writes, and `Good::init`'s
  footprint.
- C: `place_region_resource`'s walk and tests.

Each reproduced its part of both traces' draw counts and interleaving.

What the readings settled:
- **MAPGEN §14's `FISH ` question.** The style's key keeps its trailing
  space, and `selectSingleNode` still finds SAHARA.xml's empty `<FISH/>`.
  The trace's 10 ladder rolls are 5 BONUSES and 5 default GOODIES; default
  FISH would add 2 rolls.
- **The divvy pool.** The pool's two lists are diff-backed, and their
  counts tell them apart:
  - early has 23 goods and late has 6 (Rubber, Sulphur, Aluminum, Coal,
    Uranium and Titanium; late is `PREQ0` Gunpowder or Industrial Age);
  - every repeat draw in both traces lands on an index already taken;
  - modulus 24 or 5 contradicts run1000;
  - `Random` takes `get_late`.
- **`player_keep_away` is never scaled**, so `16 SCALE` reads as 16.
- **The region walk is `place_all`'s 16-bit LFSR** (0x16800 before the
  shift).
- **Every Great Sahara region call saw exactly one available region**, so
  no `find_avail_regions` draw happens.

**The port.**
- **`sim::map_resources`** (new). It holds:
  - the ladder: roll and group carry across sections, and the selected
    flag resets per section;
  - the pool's rejection draws, with an automatic refresh;
  - `place_player_resource`: the ring start `r % (len+1)`, then a scan with
    no retry;
  - `place_region_resource`: the LFSR walk, the world/nonplayer retry
    bookkeeping, and the starved-player balance;
  - `create`: the footprint's 0x200 mask with `set_blocked_at` (moved out
    of the `place_all` pass into a free function in `terrain_groups`), then
    the cell's flag (1 for a good, 0x8000 for a goody box) and its `down`
    (−2 for a good, −3 for a goody box) and `down_who`.
- **`rondata::mapgen::resource_inputs`** (new) reads the sections (style
  first, an empty section still wins), resourcerules.xml's footprints and
  pool flags, and default.xml's `LAND_<c>` rare lists. Only `LAND_0` lists
  land goods.

**The one parting, and its fix.** The first run agreed on the seed and on
parts 2 and 6 at 7927, and parted on part 5 alone. The cells' `down_who`
was off: the good on cell (53, 2) had 10 at 2 players, exactly the 10 oil
cells there.

`World::set_oil_at@006b2a10` kills any live oil good on its cell, on both
arms (listing 0x6b2a10..0x6b2b05). The on arm then calls
`Objects::init_good(OIL)`, and init_good takes the first dead slot. So a
resource good's index counts `place_all`'s oil.

`MapGrid` gains `goods` (each slot's live `(type, x, y)`) and `init_good`,
and `place_all`'s `set_oil` now maintains it. The 7859 check still agrees.

The check is `great_sahara_s_resources_are_the_traced_stream_and_7927`.
- The whole segment from `place_all` to `compute_all_vals` agrees, on all
  280 traces.
- Seed and parts 2, 5 and 6 agree at 7877, 7880 and 7927, on 105 files
  each.
- 36 objects are placed at 2 players and 45 at 3.

Mutations:
- **Held**, five of them:
  - LFSR tap 0x16800 → 0x16900;
  - the pool accepting a taken index;
  - the ring start `% len`;
  - a start-distance tie going to the cell;
  - `Early` drawing from the late list.
- **Failed nothing**: goods never reusing a dead slot. On Great Sahara no
  oil good dies before a later good is made, so no slot is ever reused.
  Slot reuse stays a reading; park it with its branch.
