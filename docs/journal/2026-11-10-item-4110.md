## 1. The implied colour

**The owners' colours, through the type record.** Read off run1559b's packet

- `unit` is a `Color` at +0x4. Its union's struct is `red`, `green`, `blue`, `alpha` at +0 to +3 (`types.txt`).
- `unit` reads (255, 0, 0) for list 0 and (34, 82, 255) for list 1, alpha 255, flags 2. That is 4102's `TEAM_UNIT`.
- `unit_tint` (+0x4c) reads (127, 47, 47) for list 0 and (47, 47, 159) for list 1.

**The table.** Per (owner, piece) and per alpha band of 32, the test prints:

- the mean photograph `p̅` and mean texel `t̅`;
- the implied colour `(p − t·α)/(1 − α)`, its per-pixel mean and spread;
- that colour pooled by least squares, `Σ(1−α)(p − tα) / Σ(1−α)²` (`ls`);
- the same with the photograph divided by `k` (`ls/k`). `k = Σp·t / Σt·t` is taken on the same piece's alpha-255
  pixels, so it is measured, not fitted to these;
- the least-squares share `s` in `p = t·s + c·(1 − s)`, with and without `k`;
- each pixel's agreement under the texel, the blend, the owner's colour and `k` times the blend.

## 2. The implied share

**The share is no rule.**

- With `k` applied, `s/k` sits near α only on the dragoon 26352 (0.31 against 0.31 at α 64–95, 0.44 against 0.56).
- On the riflemen 60 it is over α: 0.48 against 0.29, and 0.74 against 0.56.

- On the medium tank 196 it is 0.83 to 0.94 in every band from 64 to 223: the texel nearly alone. There the implied
  colour is grey near the texel's own gain. Under α 32 it is olive, (130, 134, 66), over a white texel.

At one α the share differs by piece, so it is not a curve of α. And no state would draw one piece's mask and not
another's at the same draw, since the tank and the riflemen share owner 1's `tfactor`.

**The tank's variant, checked.** `mediumtank_desert.tga` holds the plain texture's masked texels byte for byte.

64–95 at (67, 67, 67). Only the opaque texels differ, (76, 73, 32) against (100, 84, 45). So no variant explains it.
The tank holds 1,713 masked texels of 65,536 (2.6 %), yet 1,035 of its 9,953 lit pixels here (10 %) take one.

state, and not a second colour.

- No candidate is fixed by the packet or the listing. The decompile of `GraphicPieces::draw_unit@008e7da0` fails
