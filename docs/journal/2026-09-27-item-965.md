## Step 1: the gate in the listing, end to end

959 had the two arms right and the gate's entry wrong. The block holding
the call, `00652df1..00652f41`, has exactly one way in: `jge 0x652ab5` at
`006529eb`, `damage >= share`. I checked every jump into the range. A hit
the caravan survives takes the fall-through to COMBAT §7.2 step 9 and
returns at `006526fa`. So **`new_danger` is called only on the killing
blow**, and the question "what does a hit pass" had been asked of hits
that could never reach it.

Between the death compare and the call there are seven gates:

- `is_unit` (`+0x18`)
- `is_captain` (`+0xe8`)
- `is_caravan` (`+0xd0`)
- the type's domain `+0x218 == 0`
- the tile owner `< 0`, or `!is_ally`, or: the dealer (`o >= 0`,
  `who >= 0`) answering `+0x1c`
- the unit's slot `+0x86 >= 0`
- `caravan_flags & 3 == 3`

The slots come from the type records, found by walking `rise.pdb`'s base
chain from `Unit` down to `SubObjectData`. `+0x1c` is `is_wallbuild`;
COMBAT had called it `is_build`, and the return values it gave are right.
`ebx` holds argument 8 from `00652d60` to the gate. There are no other
writes.

Every caller of `take_damage` dispatches through vslot `+0x16c`; there
are no direct calls. `Object::do_damage` pushes its own `this`'s `+0xa`
and `+0x9` as arguments 7 and 8 (`0064ba18`), with `edi` = `this` on
every path in. `Unit::fight` calls it on the attacker. `Ammo::do_damage`
calls it on `objects[ammo.who][ammo.o]`, the shooter. So an arrow names
its Tower, and the same `this` writes the target's
`damage_o`/`damage_who`. `Unit::suffer_attrition` and
`SpellType::cast_sabotage` pass −1, −1. `Wall::process` and sabotage
damage non-units. The decoy's overflow recursion names the captain.
