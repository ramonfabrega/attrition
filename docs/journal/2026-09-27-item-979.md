  82 draws against 121 (four `Guy::set_anim < Unit::do_idle` rolls read
  their clocks). The gate is now on the frame products only.

  variants are run1–5, run20 (`flags 1`) and the second pair.
- **Unit test**: `a_sibling_s_frame_words_are_borrowed_only_from_the_same_lobby`.
- **The mutation**, on the committed build (`b9a315a6`): `same_lobby` made
  `true`. It failed the unit test, run346's and run347's walks (the words
