## What was asked, and what it cost

One `LEADERS≥2` window anywhere inside (7616, 8174] on Great Lakes, to turn
item 385's bracket into a frame. It landed as run115 — `[7880, 8010)`, 130
blocks, 254 MB, eleven minutes launch to archive, all five checks green

## The grep before the capture, which nearly cancelled it and then justified it

Two readings had to be corrected before the stanza could be written, and
both came out of grepping rather than reading.

**`treaties` needs `LEADERS≥3`, not `≥2`.** `LeaderData::log_data@006e5110`
raises the detail to 3 at line 213, immediately before the eight-iteration
`diplos`/`treaties` loop. `docs/AI.md` §45 and `docs/VISION.md` §6.2 both
say `≥2`. It changes none of their conclusions — every one of the four
bracketing windows is `LEADERS=9`, and run19 is a `DUMP_ALL` — but it is
precisely the sentence a future session would use to decide whether a
capture is owed, and read as `≥2` it would have said a `LEADERS=2` window
was enough.

**run94 covers the gap and does not answer it.** run94's window is
`[7754, 8045)`, which contains 7944 outright, and a first grep for
`treaties[scan]` in it returns hits — so for about a minute this item
looked like it was already on disk. It is not: run94 is `LEADERS=1`, and of
its 1,172 `LEADERDATA` records exactly **32** carry a `treaties[scan]`
line, all 32 in the four **start-dump** records, whose `start:` detail is
run10's `LEADERS=9`. The 1,168 per-frame records carry none. The trap is
that the start dump is always at a detail the window is not, so
`head -c 20000000 | grep` on a capture answers a question about frame 0.

That second reading is also what made run94 the right overlap partner
rather than a reason to skip: same game, same detail but for the one raised
category, so `samegame.py --exclude LEADERDATA` compares **all 130 blocks**
instead of the usual six at each end. It came back 130 in common, 0
differing.

## What the window opened, which is not this item's

`0/wars`, `0/active_wars` and `0/active_wars_with` agree at nought for 121
blocks and part on **8001**: the original writes the **human** leader a war
census there — `wars 1`, `active_wars 1`, `active_wars_with 2` — beside a
single `production_step` tick that falls back to 0 on 8002. Fifty-six
blocks after first contact.

That is the first direct evidence on this disk that the original runs its
census for a human leader at all, and it is exactly what `docs/AI.md` §45
argued from the decompile: `plan_strategy@006b9620:1511,1557` gates on
`leader_flags & 2`, `i != who` and the met bit, and on nothing about
humans. §43's `human` skip is a stand-in that this record contradicts
directly rather than by inference. This crate leaves the human's census at
zero forever; the three fields are pinned in `PARTS_ON_RUN115` so a
successor has a standing row to move.

It is booked nowhere. It names no score yet, so by the working agreement it
parks rather than queues — but it is a takes-chain candidate: the human's
`active_wars` is an input to nothing the AI reads (the AI reads its *own*),
so what it would move is the human leader's own production, and whether
that is on any word's frame is unmeasured.
