## What the reading found

- **One grid, four levels.** `fill_colors_fog@008d1420` writes each fog cell a level: 3 `is_seen`, 2
  `was_really_seen` (`seen2`), 1 `was_seen` and not really seen, 0 never. The same pass writes the minimap's grey
  (255, `0x80`, `0x50`, 0). §3.32 called `0x80` "under a reveal" and `0x50` "once seen". Both are amended: `0x80` is
  the `seen2` bit, and `0x50` is `was_seen@006b53f0`'s region rule (an allied owner holding a city or fort in the
  cell's region, `reg_cities`/`reg_forts`).
- **The main view** is `TerrainOut::render_fow@00854630`, after the ground and before the objects, in HDR. Each of a
  terrain cell's 25 vertices takes `u = fog_tcoord_lookup[Σ]`. Σ is the touching fog cells' levels, scaled to 0
  to 12, and the table is 13 floats in `.rdata` at `0x00af2df4`. The pass samples the tileset's `fow_fade.tga`, a
  256 × 1 black ramp in alpha, under `fow_rs`. That state is `SRCALPHA`/`INVSRCALPHA` with stage 0
  `SELECTARG1(TEX)`, so the ground keeps `1 − α(u)`: 0, 0.3137, 0.7023 and 1 for levels 0 to 3.
- **The minimap** multiplies its grey per fog cell, filtered by point (`fog_render_state`: `ZERO`/`SRCCOLOR`).
