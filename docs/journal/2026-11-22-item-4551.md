## The reading

`Group::kill@00714110`, the decompile read whole:

- It shifts `list`, `angles` and the four offset arrays over the leaving index.
- Then `num -= 1`. On zero it runs `clear(-1)`; otherwise it writes `stamp = game->frame`.
- Then it runs the speed tail: `find_leader`, then `speed = new_speed = UnitData::speed(leader)`, or 0.
- It never calls `find_role`, so `+0x34` stands.

`docs/GROUPS.md` §3.4 already lists `find_role`'s two callers, `normalize` and `Groups::process`, and the kill is
neither of them.
