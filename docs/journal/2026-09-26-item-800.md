## The cause

`Group::sort@00708090` is `Form::categorize`'s first statement, and it
runs on the group being laid out. The siege arm's sub-group is on the
stack, so in the original the sort is a no-op on `[60]`. Ours sorted the
**seat's** list `[60, 69]`. `1/69` is the squad's tail without its
captain, so the sort re-seated the squad as `[60, 67, 68, 69]`, and the
changed list swapped the whole army in for the sub-group. GROUPS §26.6
had written this seam down and judged it harmless for a sub-group of
one. It was harmful whenever the army's list needed re-seating.

## The fix, and what it moved

`group_action_move_to` reads at entry whether its value is a group on
the stack that carries a seat, meaning its list is not the seat's. If
so, `Sim::stack_sort` sorts that list alone. A seated group still sorts
its seat (§23). `the_anchor_s_sub_group_sorts_its_own_list_and_walks_
alone` fails when the stack arm is switched off; I checked that, and the
restore came from git.

- **The value diff on 17403** (`run233_s_word_frame_is_widened_whole`):
  - `1/60` holds `ATTACK_TO` (2) to (38136, 42024) on both sides. Before

  - Army 1's list is **`[60, 69]` on both sides** on 17401..17405.

  - All 52 of `1/60`'s rows go, as do `1/15`'s two and the escort's

  - The floor goes **293/312/324/1,036 → 293/312/324/446**.
  - No figure's animation changes on one side only near either word.
