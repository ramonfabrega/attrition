Rules track, Opus 5, its own worktree. The brief: `docs/GOLDEN.md` and the
`.cmd` files, so that the chapters after the first are a queue rather than an

## Three findings, in the order they cost something

**The channel does issue an order, exactly once.** `docs/ORACLE.md`'s "The
channel's vocabulary" says "No console command issues an order at all", and
`docs/INPUT.md` §11 restates it. Grepping `run_cmd@007d6a70` for `_order`
returns three hits: two `Unit::clear_orders` inside `pack` and `deploy`, and
**`Unit::add_air_patrol_order@005e4350` inside case 82, `bird`** — "Drop a
Wild Bird at Mouse". It is the one door the channel has into the order
family, and `AirPatrolOrder` is one of the six order classes `docs/CENSUS.md`
shows with no citation at all. I checked the two nearby suspects rather than
assuming: `anim` reaches `Guy::set_anim@005da300` directly and `pack`
reaches `SpellType::cast_pack@00670be0`, which is a state poke. Neither is an
order. The correction belongs in `docs/ORACLE.md` and `docs/INPUT.md`, which
are not mine to edit this item; it is one line each and it is in §10 and §16
of the new document instead.

**Chapter one's squads are not where its own header said.** The file's
comment said "near player 0's capital". They are at tile (4,40), which the
tile arm of `parse_coord` reads as world cell (1,10) — unowned `BASELAND`,
region 1, thirty cells from Napata at cell (4,40). That is item 364's
tile-versus-world finding showing up one layer out: whoever wrote the line
wrote it on the world-arm reading, and five captures went past it because the
arena is clear ground either way. Corrected in the file, in a comment only —
the six staged lines the pinned test counts are untouched.

**The map is on disk and it is exact.** The whole of the coordinate work in
this document is a grep of run105's own `[Start Game]` `WORLD` block rather
than a guess: 3,600 cells, row-major with x fastest — which is not assumed,
because player 0's owned cells centre on (5.7, 39.3) and player 1's on
(53.4, 20.7), their two capitals to within a cell. Napata is cell (4,40),
London (55,21); the territories are 266 and 261 cells; and Great Lakes
really does have four lakes, sea regions 66, 67, 69 and 70, with 164 ocean
cells between them. Region 70 sits beside player 0 and its deepest water is
cells (15,45), (15,46), (16,46). That is what makes the naval chapter
writable at all, and it cost a `grep`, which is the rule working.

## What the design actually concluded, which is not what the brief assumed

The brief asks for "every order class". The chapters cannot deliver it and
the document says so in a table: the cheat channel stages *state*, and every
order in a golden capture is one the original's automatic play issued. Seven
of the eight chapters below chapter one add **no** order class the tree does
not already enter; chapter six adds one, through `bird`. Order coverage is a
second axis whose unit of work is a native issuer validated the way the lab
validated `issue_move_to` — and §13 is the list of which issuer each class
wants, which is the thing entry 41 §5 was waiting for. What the chapters buy
instead is the **unit lines** and the **ages**, which no issuer buys, and a
staged cast for the eventual issuer to order about.

The other structural call: **chapters are independent launches, not segments
of one game.** Entry 41 §1 says "one staged game … in chapters" and, in the
same sentence, that chapters are independent so rules items run in parallel
lanes. Those pull apart, and independence wins: if chapter four were frames
2700–3600 of one record, a parting in chapter two would block every chapter
after it. Each chapter re-uses the lobby — same map, same seed, same 34
option fields — which is what keeps the setup genuinely shared and the
sibling borrow legitimate.

## The guard, made to fail first

`every_chapter_stages_what_it_says_it_stages` reads every `.cmd` in
`tools/gamelog/golden/`, checks each line is on the right half of `run_cmd`'s
two disjoint switches, and asserts that the verbs the interpreter will *not*
act on are exactly what `CHAPTER_DEBT` pins — chapter one's bare `war` and
chapter six's `bird`, and nothing else. It failed twice on purpose before it
landed: `700 nuke` appended to chapter five gave
`left: ["nuke"] right: []`, and `700 !add trireme …` gave "`add trireme
who=0 60,180` is on the wrong half of run_cmd's two switches". Both restored.

The failure mode it exists for is the expensive one: a new chapter reaching
for a verb the harness silently drops, discovered by a capture instead of by
a test.
