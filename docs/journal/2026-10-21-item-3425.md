## The DLL

Both issuers are read off the listing (`llvm-objdump` at `9417a0` and `942c90`):

- **`issue_attack_ground(group, x, y, queue)`** has `sub esp, 0x10` and `ret 0x10`. It takes a group and builds a
  10-byte type-0x09 command.
- **`issue_come_out(unit)`** has `sub esp, 0xc` and `ret 4`. It reads the unit's `+9`, `+0xa` and `+0x30` and builds an
  11-byte type-0x31 command with **no group**. So `@comeout` loops its units the way `@unqueue` loops buildings.

These are verbs 27 and 28 in `issue_guard.h`, with their prologues. The tracer compiles clean under `-Wall` in the
plain and `RON_AUTOSTART` builds. Loop 3430's twin check (`INDEX.tsv`): each has a game twin that builds the same bytes
inline, `GroupOut::issue_attack_ground@0070ae60` and `UnitOut::issue_come_out@00607be0`. The DLL calls the manager
copies, which nothing in the image references. Both move to `ENTERED_BY_THE_DLL_ONLY`, and the install-backed test
checks that.
