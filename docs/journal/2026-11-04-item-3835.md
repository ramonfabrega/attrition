## Built (`sim::map_heights`)

Each one was read from the listing, end to end:
- **The shore blend**: `find_closest_coordinfo@0086a260` and `determine_land_height_color`'s arm (0x86c2ff–0x86c35a).
  - The search goes ring by ring round the vertex's cell (`radius[]` 1, 9, 25, 49) and stops two rings past the first
    that finds a `CoordInfo` 0x20 cell.
  - The answer is `sqrtf(best) · oo_WCoordSize`, or −1.0 when nothing is in reach.
  - The blend is `0.25·(d·h) + ((d·0.05·7.5·192 + 30)·(4 − d))·0.25`.
  - A blended vertex is listed for smoothing.
- **`smooth_tcoord@0086c3a0`**: a copy of the 4×4 vertices from (x−1, y−1), bounded by the tile grid. Then each vertex
  of the 2×2 whose height is above 0 takes the mean of the non-zero values in its 3×3. It runs twice when either
  coordinate is odd.
- **`build_coastal_data@0086c540`'s height half**, in four steps:
  - `mark_valid_coastal_influencers` keeps a 0x20 cell unless it sits inside a bend: two orthogonal 0x20 neighbours
    round a diagonal 0x4 one (0x869243–0x869299).
  - `mark_coast_vert_influence` lays `LineState` lines between influencers, at sub-tile offsets from
    `texture_fractal` (xs+1 by ys+1, smoothing 0, flags 2, `World.seed`). Two listing details:
    - both lines end at the offset of the last neighbour joined;
    - the second line reads its end and its count from the *current* cell, which a diagonal step moved (0x868e80
      reads `[ebx+2]` after `ebx` changed).
  - `adjust_aquatic_height` gives each entered cell's 5×5 vertices `min(d/768, 10000)²·(1/9)·(−273) + 30` from the
    nearest influence tile. The best index carries over from the previous vertex.
  - `relax_aquatic_verts` makes five in-place passes of `(below + above + left + right + self)·0.2`.
- `water_subtiles`: `_wcoord`'s height test, all four corners strictly below −30.0f. Only the test is built, not the
  writes.

All of it is in `sim::single`. No float: `beach_steepness` is a registered tweak defaulting to 1, and the only
transcendental is a correctly rounded sqrt.
