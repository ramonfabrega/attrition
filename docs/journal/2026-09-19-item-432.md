# Item 432 — the pair was one thing, and it is worth seventy-two (2026-09-19)

*Opus 5, on the AI lane. `docs/AI.md` §50.*

## The answer

**One defect, not two. Seventy-two frames, not eight.**

The AI headline's frame at Great Lakes 9382 had two causes booked against
it — a valuation and an expiry count, "a pair that has to fall together",
priced at eight frames by item 430's probe 4. The expiry was never a
cause. Its extra `Leader::make_stuff+0x63d` draw was **probe 3's own
artefact**, and with the original's two per-city Scholar values forced on
9380 and **no expiry cap at all**, frame 9382 agrees draw for draw and the
word moves **9510 → 9582**.

## What the item was briefed as, and what it was

Booked as *"9382, nine draws against eight, the extra at index 6"*, with
`Leader::make_stuff+0x63d` as the hypothesis and this caveat leading: *the
expiry count is a function of the make list, so the third call may follow
from the list's shape rather than the purchase.*

It did. The caveat was the answer, and the brief asked for that to be
found in the first hour by design. It was — the first two measurements
were both greps.

## The mechanism

`create_units` offers the Scholar **once per city**. This crate computes
**45,568 for both** — `want 20, val 42,000,000, divisor 25`, identical
terms for both cities. The original computes **4,891,136** for its first
city and **5,755,741** for its second, on the same frame.

`MakeList::make_me` ranks on `list[k].val <= val` and fills the category
slot on `list[cat].val < val`. Two different senses, and the difference is
invisible until two offers share a number. Then the later offer displaces
the earlier in the ranked list and does not in the category slot, and the
two copies of one type end up **naming different cities**. `make_stuff`
step 6's duplicate test is `t == list[k].t && city == list[k].city`, so it
does not match — and the loop buys the same Scholar a second time. One
extra `make_this`, one extra `expire` call, one extra `+0x63d`.

430's probe forced "the Scholar offer" and thereby forced **both** of
them to one value. It manufactured the tie it then measured.

## Why the eight frames were wrong

Not a small payoff honestly measured — the expiry cap was suppressing the
fix it was meant to complete. Capping the expiry at the original's two
stopped the second purchase, which is the right *count*, but it left the
category slot naming the wrong city, so everything past 9518 ran on a list
the original never had. Give the two offers their own values and the count
and the city come right together, and it buys nine times as much.

9518's scholar-birth divergence goes with it: probe 4's artefact through
and through. The parting behind the blockage is **9582, one
`Leader::use_market+0x1ed` short** — ours one where theirs is two, the
market's sell rotation, with 9581 and 9583 agreeing whole.

Seventy-two is still a probe number and is booked as one. It measures what
fixing the 9380 valuation is worth given everything else this crate does.
The difference from the eight is that the eight was compensating for an
artefact and the seventy-two adds nothing: the probe changes two values on
one frame, and that is all.

## The four offers, recovered rather than read

A `LEADERS=9` record prints slots and never offers, so what the original
actually offered on 9380 had never been recoverable. It is:

| offer | `t` | `val` | its `city` | `cat` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Merchant | 61 | 869,565 | 0 | 4 |
| Scholar | 52 | **4,891,136** | 0 | 4 |
| Citizen | 50 | 234,782 | 1 | 5 |
| Scholar | 52 | **5,755,741** | 1 | 4 |

Those four, replayed through `make_me` from the original's **block 9380**
list, land on its **block 9381** list slot for slot and field for field.
Both ends are the dump's; nothing is simulated. It also fixes the order —
swapped, the second `make_me` breaks out on `t == list[k].t` instead of
inserting and the record is not reproduced — and it says this crate
**offers no Merchant at all** on that frame, which is what has been
sitting in `MAKE[1..3]` as standing residue since run107.

The city-0 value is the sharp end. Block 9381's slot 2 stands at `t −1,
val 4,891,136, city 0, cat 4`; no expiry ran on 9380; and the only other
thing that clears a ranked slot's `t` is `make_me` clearing a same-type
entry as it inserts. So slot 2 held a `t52` for city 0, and its `val` is
that offer's — a number nobody could read off the record, standing in
plain sight behind a `−1`.

## Three method notes, each of which paid

**Check the predicate before you believe the hypothesis.** The booked
mechanism was `make_stuff`'s expiry. Both predicates it depends on —
`make_me`'s two comparison senses at `006c9be0` and step 6's `(t, city)`
test at `006c8af0:147–167` — turned out to be faithful, which is what
redirected the item from "our expiry is wrong" to "our *input* is
degenerate". Twenty minutes of decompile against a day of chasing a loop.

**The instrument was already in the tree.** I built a scratch test that
printed our draws beside the make list per frame, and `RON_DEBUG_SITES`
and `RON_DEBUG_LEADER` had been doing exactly that for weeks. Reverted it.
The one thing neither did was print **the original's** draws beside ours,
so the site window now does, in four lines: silent when the frame agrees,
the original's whole frame when it parts. That is the third time in two
days the disk answered before a run did, and the second time the answer
was a tool nobody had asked a question.

**A payoff probe must change only the offer under test, not the type.**
430 recorded the coarse version of this from its probe 1 — a probe wide
enough to touch a frame that already agrees reports that frame's breakage
as a finding. This is the same rule one level finer: **two offers that
share a computed value are two offers**, and a force keyed on the value
hits both. Probe 3's extra draw was that, and it was booked as a cause.

## What this cost, and what it opened

Two release runs of the long capture, one decompile read of two functions,
no capture, no screen, no reading of `create_units`. The successor is that
reading, and it is a different instrument now than when it was held: it
has **two numbers to hit on one frame** and a landed reconstruction to
check itself against, where before it had a single unexplained value and
the question "which of three terms differs".
