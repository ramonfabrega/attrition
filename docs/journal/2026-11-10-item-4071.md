## What was booked, and what the evidence says

The booking: the colour reader takes an olive tank on grass for ground. **Half right.** The colour reader keeps the
*union* of the ring's colours, and the ring is placed by our draw. Our tanks sit ~40 px above the photograph's
(4065c), so the photograph's 1/15 and 1/16 stand past the bottom of their windows. Their own olive crosses the
ring and becomes the ground's colour. On the photograph, 1/15's tank spans y 751–829 against a window ending at
794, with the ring at 794–800, and 1/16 spans 773–844 against 825. So the reader is blind through the place as
much as through the colour.

Two readers were tried. Each one's constants were written down before any count of it was read.

1. **A robust ring** (`render::silhouette::ROBUST`, `Reader::Robust`). The ground is each channel's median and
   median absolute deviation over the ring, taken on a 3×3 mean. A pixel is an object at 3σ (1.4826·MAD, floor 4
   levels) on any channel, kept when 5 of its 3×3 are candidates. It takes every ring pixel and reads no key buffer.
   I chose it because a median ignores a minority of tank pixels in the ring. **Refused**: it reads bare grass as
   objects, 323 of 12,000 and 113 of 16,000 pixels (each above the 40-pixel floor). It also loses 0/9 (colour
   0.571, robust 0.023), whose ring is the derrick's trees. It scores 9 of 23, which counts for nothing under the
   rule. It is kept as the record of the kill (`ROBUST_3294`, `ROBUST_BARE`), never as the instrument.
2. **A hand-traced mask** (`client::silhouette::TRACED`, `Reader::Mask`). The three tanks and four figures that
   colour passes are traced as polygons in the photograph's own pixels, on 4× crops of the photograph with a
   10-pixel grid. I never looked at our draw while tracing. A pixel is the original's when its centre lies inside a
   polygon (an integer crossing test). It reads neither our key buffer nor a colour. **It meets the rule.** It
   reads both bare windows empty, scores 0 of 25 at run1441's camera, and agrees with colour wherever colour
   passes. With the tanks read by it, **candidate C reads 7 of 23** (`RUN1559B_3294_MASKED`, 6 → 7).

Of the two ways parked 4065b names, I took the ground model first: if it had passed, it would have served any
frame. It failed, so I took the trace. A trace holds for this photograph alone (parked 4071a).
