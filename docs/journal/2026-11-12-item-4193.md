## 3. The rule for a figure tile under a shadow (unchanged from §3.7; restated before any count)

A photograph pixel is under a figure's shadow when three things hold:
- it lies in a projected decal of the frame: every tree `tree_list` stands, and every drawn guy whose piece names
  a `<SHADOW>`. The selection is laid with the guys' decals whether or not they are drawn;
- the decals' combined `1 − Π(1 − α)` there is at least `1 − 1/1.2` (level 0, nearest);
- `ground_why` calls it ground or window.

A tile is a *figure tile* when no tree decal falls on any of its selected pixels, and it scores on 40 or more such
pixels. The still's mean and the photograph's mean over those pixels are read by `LUMA_READER`. The row can fail
both ways:
- drawn as read it falls (28 → 5 of 37);
- its made-to-fail (mirrored) is pinned at 59 of 108.

The item's score is figure tiles over 28 of 37 with the guys' decals drawn, `_TRACED_STILL` holding 32.
