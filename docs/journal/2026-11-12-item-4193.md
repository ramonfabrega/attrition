## 1. What was read

- **The predicate** (`GuyOut::draw_shadows@005dc070`, listing `5dc380`–`5dc48c`), unchanged from the draft. A
  guy's decal is laid when four things hold:
  - its point, interpolated and moved by `shadow_offset`, projects inside NDC;
  - its piece's `get_gpiece_type` is 0, 9 or 10;
  - the stealth arm passes;
  - `UnitOut::draw_shadows@00614050` runs at `zoom_level > 2`.

  The chad is fresh, `transparent` 0xff, so the vertex is `BLACK` at 255, as a tree's is.
- **The angle** (`5dc41b`–`5dc43c`). It is `180.0` (`0xb696c0`) less `fast_angle_to_degrees(GuyData::
  get_render_angle@005de570)`, the render angle blended from `last_angle` by `graphic_percent`. It is unfolded,
  so it lies in −180 to 180.
- **The turn** (`DecalTransformMethod::execute@00923a70`, listing `923bc9`–`923cea`).
  - `cvttss2si` truncates the angle. A negative `a` folds to `359 − (−a mod 360)`, as `missile_lead`'s fold does.
  - The quaternion is `(0, 0, sin a/2, cos a/2)`: `fast_half_degree_to_cosine` and `_sine` at `a`, x and y
    `sin·0`, stored x, y, z, w (`Quat<float>`'s type record).
  - `(1, 0, 0)` and `(0, 1, 0)` go through `Point<float>::rotate@00420b10`. That function is ported as
    `sim::combat::quat_rotate` and diff-backed on run1471's missile lead. For this quaternion its rows give
    `x' = cos a·x + sin a·y`, `y' = cos a·y − sin a·x`: **a turn by −a**.
  - So `r1 = (cos a, −sin a)` and `r2 = (sin a, cos a)`. 4182 wrote `r1 = (cos a, sin a)` and `r2 = (−sin a,
    cos a)`. Every tree has `a` = 0, so no tree could show the difference.
  - The corners (`923cef`–`923e13`) and texcoords (`923e18`–`923e5a`) were re-read and are ours as written:
    `(u0, v0), (u1, v0), (u0, v1), (u1, v1)`.
- **Why the hand matters.** The biped, horse and tank textures are elongated along v: the biped about 10 × 22
  texels, alpha peak 57, RGB black. A mirrored turn lays the long axis across the body for every facing but 0
  and 180.
- **What the first session's next steps read, and what they kill.**
  - The state. The decals' map is `rmaps[0x12]` (`GraphicPieces::init_shadow_map@008f7580`), under
    `render_states[23]` (`+0x5c`; `Scene::init_render_states@008ba450`): colour `SELECTARG2`, alpha `MODULATE`,
    mag and min `MIP_LINEAR`, `MIP_POINT`, **`mipmap_bias` +1.0**, alpha Greater, src/inv-src alpha, depth
    off. The enumerators come from `rise.pdb`.
  - The texture. It loads through `DecalFlushMethod::load@00924220` → `TextureMethod::load@00916a60` →
    `TexturePacket::set_texture@00917bc0` → `Texture::init@005245f0` with `(PF_32, MEM_HARDWARE, 0, 1)`.
    `Texture::init` calls `set_mip_levels(1)` and then `ImageIO::load_tga@00512ac0`. That calls the texture's
    `init(w, h, fmt, mem, 0, 1, name)`, and d3dgl's `TextureCacheEntry::init` (DLL `0x100523d0`) stores the 1
    as `mip_levels`, clamped to at least 1. It writes it as `D3D11_TEXTURE2D_DESC.MipLevels` (`0x10052803`)
    for `CreateTexture2D` (device slot `+0x14`).
  - **So the decal textures have one level.** `generate_mips` (d3dgl `0x1003b2b0`, context slot `0xd8`,
    `GenerateMips`) has nothing to fill, and the bias clamps to level 0. Mipmapping is not the gap.
  - `ImageIO::tint@00511aa0` adds RGB, saturating, and never alpha. `BLACK` turns it off.
  - `GraphicPieces::flush_shadows@00906480`'s card call at `+0x30` takes the `<SHADOW>` element's name string,
    a marker, not state.
  - `GraphicPieces::scale_gpiece@008ea0c0` swaps in the culture's piece and nothing else.
  - The size's ×3 is confirmed (`init_unit_data`, 347 and 394).
- **The card trace** (run1566b, the pass after 3279). It records no vertex stream for draws 108–126: the decals'
  `Vert3Packet` does not pass `get_internal_stream`. The corners have no value diff there.

## 3. The rule (unchanged from §3.7; restated)

A photograph pixel is under a figure's shadow when three things hold:
- it lies in a projected decal of the frame;
- the decals' `1 − Π(1 − α)` is at least `1 − 1/1.2`, at level 0, nearest;
- `ground_why` calls it ground or window.

A figure tile is one where no tree decal falls on its selected pixels, scored on 40 or more such pixels and read by
`LUMA_READER`.
