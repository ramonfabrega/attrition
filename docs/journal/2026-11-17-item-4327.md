## What was built

- **The HDR land pass** (`Gpu::land_hdr_pass`). The frame so far is copied, then taken back through the curve onto an
  `Rgba16Float` target (`fs_untone`, the water's bisection). The run of faded land, floor and `Lit` patches that the
  world lays contiguously is drawn there by HDR variants of their shaders, which write the lit value before the curve.
  The target's own blend runs on those values, and `fs_retone` writes the curve back onto the frame's colour and leaves
  its alpha alone. Each layer is then `T(T⁻¹(D) · (1 − a) + L · a)`, the card's.
- **The coral on the sand's light** (`World::coral_lit`). §3.18 read 131 as the sand layer's own stream: the same
  positions, texcoords and colours. Ours drew it at a white vertex and the flat light. With the switch on, it takes
  the faded layer's `light` and the deep re-lay.
