# Item 4289: `leader_options` from the client's own prefs

*Opus 5.5, one session (lane-4289), no subagents. The layer now mints the frame-0 `leader_options` from a prefs
record of its own: `rondata::prefs::Prefs`, the fresh profile's, with no control. All 50 recordings' frame-0
packages are minted byte for byte from the two values they carry. `INPUT_KINDS` 25 → 26 of 26, `INPUT_WHOLE`
0 → 42 of 50, `INPUT_WHOLE_ORDERS` 363 · 1,076 · 990 → **413 · 1,076 · 940** of 2,429. 38 of the 42 carry no other
order. This branch does not touch `crates/sim`.*

## The first act: the prefix's fresh profile is the original's default

- `PlayerProfile::load@005cb430` calls `default_profile@005ceca0` when it finds no profile.
- `default_profile` clears `prefs` and calls `default_game@005d0220` (`& 0xff6efefd | 0x0d0e4045`; `peasants` 0,
  `peasants_wait` 2, `buildings` 0). Its own mouse lines, `default_video` and `default_audio` follow.
- The composed word is `0x1f2e5055` = 523,128,917.
- All three lanes' `PlayerProfile/Player.dat` hold `<PREFS value="523128917"/>`, the whole word, with
  `PEASANT_STANCE` 0, `PEASANTS_WAIT` 2 and `BUILDING_STANCE` 0. Those are the struct's `+0x38`, `+0x3c` and `+0x40`,
  which the loader reads in field order.

So the answer is **the original's default**, not just "the prefix's". No launch was owed, and run1629 is unused.

**Every byte of the payload is explained** (`docs/CLIENT_INPUT.md` §26.1's table): `issue_leader_option@00942080`
writes `0x49` and `who` = `console->who`. It copies the three counts and the whole `BitMask` by
`BitMask<8>::operator=@0042ef10` (`bits` 32, `size` 4, `flags`, `ptr[4]`). `synch_with_prefs@006f1b50` sets
`flags` and `ptr[0]`, and the reset's `memset` leaves `ptr[1..3]` at 0. No stop.

**One surprise, read off the disk.** The 2017 sample's order carries `who` 3, but its frame-0 package's `play` is 0.
Its human slot is `who` 3 on play 0, and every package of the file is play 0. My first draft took the package's
`play` for `who`, and the sample parted (49 of 50). The builder now takes the console's `who` as its argument, and
the test reads it off the header's active slot on the frame-0 package's play.
