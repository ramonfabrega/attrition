## Why 3 frames

The Galley's `unit_graphics.xml` entry cites `CHAR_DEFAULT` as
`file="Galley Default"`. `anim_graphics.xml` defines `name="Galley
default"`. `artdata::anim_files` was a case-sensitive `BTreeMap`, so the
lookup failed, the slot was left out, and a slot left out of a known piece
is `MISSING`'s three frames. The original resolves the name in
`GraphicPieces::decipher_animation@008face0`: a first-match walk of
`anim_names` compared with `_wcsicmp`.

A survey of the install: 46 of the 1,894 cited names resolve only
case-folded, including the Sloop's and Transport's defaults, Macemen,
longbow, balamob and bombvessel. The one duplicate name is byte-identical.
