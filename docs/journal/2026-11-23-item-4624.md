## What was built

- **The plane.** `Sim::observe_fog(p)` gives each fog cell the first level whose predicate holds, in
  `fill_colors_fog`'s order: `world_is_seen_fog` 3, `was_really_seen_fog` 2, `was_seen_fog` 1, else 0. The
  sim already ported all three. `Scene::fog` carries it, so `render::draw::Sight` now reads level 3 as
  visible.
- **The main view.** `minimap::fog::mesh` lays the terrain's own mesh again, its floats the ground's. Each
  corner's `u` comes from `corner_u`: the decompile's 4 × 4 lookup block (`fill_fog_texcoords@008b19f0`) as one
  cell ×4, two ×2 or four ×1, then `fog_tcoord_lookup`. The 13 singles are read off `.rdata` `0x00af2df4`.
  - `fs_fow` samples the tileset's `<FOGTEX>` and writes `1 − α` into a keep map, under the ground's depth. The
    ramp is linear, clamped, and its white texels all carry α 0, so it is a keep exactly.
  - The frame pass is split after its ground runs, mountains included. The keep is composed there through the
    tone map, `T(T⁻¹(g) · keep)`, with the shadows' shade pipeline.
- **The minimap.** `minimap::fog::layers` lays black quads, one per fog-cell diamond, at alpha `255 − grey`. Under
  the screen pass's `c·a + d·(1 − a)` that leaves `d · grey`. It is drawn after the borders and before the blips.
- **Off by default** (`World::fog` `None`). Every pool lane plays with the display reveal on, so every row before
  this one is drawn as it was. `client --fog` draws slot 0's fog.
