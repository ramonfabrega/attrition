## Step 3: run314, and why the chapter did not happen

The cheapest capture for the most: chapter twenty-three's own staged
`rontrace.cmd` (the flight line whole, the launch and the repeat toggle,
14 of the 77) as `cmd:` lines on the queue lane, `cover=1`, the golden
lobby (`cfg: -`, seed 12345, map 14). Two things in `longtrace.sh` shaped
the stanza before it ran: its `FFWD` line is written before the stanza's
lines, and the channel clamps a frame below its predecessor, so the
fast-forward went in as the chapter's own `37 !ffwd 3`; and the lane
never builds the DLL.

That second one I found only after **take one** died before frame 0 on
the bop page fault (`read access to 00004ECD`), 2,101 `HIT`s in. The
install's `rontrace.dll` was a 2026-09-23 build, older than the
chapter's verbs — it would not have staged the chapter had it lived. It
had run `cover=1` whole at run226 two days before, so the fault reads as
the intermittent one ORACLE documents rather than a regression; one take
is not enough to say more. `waitwin.sh` waits forever for a process that
is gone, so I stopped the runner by hand, ran `window.py restore`, kept
the trace and wine log, and rebuilt the DLL plain from the base's
`tracer.c`.

**Take two** ran whole in fourteen minutes. The cheat lines all ran; the
word is ch23's to 642; and then it parts, because **all six `@` lines were
refused with code 2: the issuer's prologue is not the shipped one.** The
coverage stubs are a five-byte `jmp` over every listed function's
prologue, planted at attach, and `issue_line` checks the issuer's first
eleven bytes against the shipped ones before calling it. The two
instruments cannot both be on. That is a second wall behind parked 697:
even a click-free lane that survives `cover=1` would refuse every issuer.

What run314 did enter that nothing else does: `Object::do_launch` (the
staged Airbase, 606) and `Leader::set_age` (the `library` cheat, 600). The
list is 227.

## Step 4: the pin

`rondata::blind` (new): `TRACES`, the twenty-one and run314;
`NEVER`, the 227 by address; and `the_blind_list_is_the_pinned_residue`,
which recomputes the list from `docs/`, the export's index and the traces
through `testenv::dump`, and names each function that moved in either
direction. Pinning the list rather than the count is what makes a failure
say which function and which document. **Made to fail three ways before it
landed**: with two hook addresses mistyped (it named `Random::get` as new
on the list), with `run29` dropped from `TRACES` (it named
`AmmoData::log_data`, which only run29 enters), and with run314 added
before the re-pin (it named `Object::do_launch` as off the list).

`census.py --never` prints the same list grouped by class and a `never`
count on the total line, and over the pinned traces it gives 227, the
guard's figure and the blind verb's over all eighty-nine plus run314.
