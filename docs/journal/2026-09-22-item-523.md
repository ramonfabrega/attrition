# 523: the captain the roll reads, and chapter two runs to its end

**Booked** as the rules headline: golden chapter two's word at 762. The
original spent one more `Unit::fight+0x9b0` than this crate, nothing
parted on 762 or 763, and no mechanism was named. The section is
`docs/COMBAT.md` §47.

**The word moved 762 → 900**, which is the end of run112's trace. No draw
parts anywhere in chapter two and no word parts. It is the first golden
chapter to run in lockstep end to end. Chapter one (626) is unchanged; the
long captures are in the gate's report below.

## The site first, as the brief said

`Unit::fight+0x9b0` is the return from `Random::get` at `0x5fde7b`. The
branch that reaches it is the captain arm: `mandatory`, `param_4`,
`recharging`, `is_captain()`, a group-order test, and "is the target a
unit". Written against the dump input by input (§47.1), all of them were
printed or trivially known, except that `is_captain()` is `o_up < 0` and
`o_up`, though printed on every unit block, **was compared nowhere**.

The dump said the rest in one look. `1/7` (the original's last hoplite)
had `o_up 6` until block 743 and `o_up -1` from 744: `1/6` had died and
`1/7` was its squad's captain. On 762 `1/7` retargets onto `0/10` and
spends the roll. A one-line probe at this crate's gate printed `1/7 cap6`.
It held the dead captain's index for nineteen frames.

## Three links, each off the frame the last one left

1. **The relink.** `Unit::close`'s listing (`0060f28e`–`0060f758`)
   unlinks the dying figure and re-appends it at its chain's tail, so a
   dying head promotes the figure below it. A dying tail changes nothing
   visible, which is why `0/10` kept `o_down 11` after `0/11` died. The
   decompile of this block has shorts printed as floats and its control
   flow is misleading, and the listing settled it in ten minutes.
   Implemented as `Sim::relink_squad` on both death paths, re-pointing
   both captain caches down the chain. The word went 762 → **816**.
2. **The shot's bearing.** At 816 the original's `1/7` dies and ours does
   not. The widening put the cause at 771: one shot from `0/9`, same
   frame, same `damage_o`, 85/16 against 58/16. That is flank 2 against 1.
   `Ammo::do_damage`'s decompile prints `find_angle(num_guys, index)`:
   the register pair dropped, and the two stack pushes behind it shown
   in its place. The listing loads `ex − sx`, `ey − sy`. This crate took
   the target's position at landing. The word did not move.
3. **The lead.** The shot also landed elsewhere, at (1772, 8386) against
   the dump's (1717, 8421). §9.1's lead was "the natural reading" of
   another dropped pair. The listing loads `T.angle` and
   `guys[0]->avg_speed`, and `1/7`'s figure averaged 9 while the unit
   walked 24. With both, the word went to **900**.

The brief's second instruction, to distrust the decompiler's argument
lists at this site, turned out to be the whole of links 2 and 3. Both were
fastcall pairs in the same family as 495's `find_angle(0, 0)`.
`tools/ghidra/README.md` now has the trap, naming all three functions
(`find_angle`, `sinx`, `cosx`) and the grep that finds every site.
