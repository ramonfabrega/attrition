pointer reaches `0xe789dc`. `leaders` is `0xe3a390` (`rise_z.map`: `?leaders@@3VLeaders@@A 00e3a390`), and `Leader`

`init_unit(objects, 9, …)`, slot 9 (`gaia::BIRD_OWNER`). Herd animals are slot 8 (`PLAYER_SLOTS`) and are walked:
theirs' stream on 129 ends on `AnimalData::walk_data` records.
