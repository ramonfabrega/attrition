## What the bar reads, settled before the first count

`docs/HUD.md` §4 asked which of the leader's fields the original's bar shows, and at what scale. The
spec said to read the consumer before believing the digits. Two functions answer it:

- `IFaceResources::draw_resource@00818840`, for good `g`, reads the encrypted block
  (`LeaderData::data_encrypted`, `+0x6eb8`; `LeaderDataEncrypt` by its type record):
  - the number at `+0x0 + 4g` (`bucket`, the stockpile), written as a plain integer;
  - the flash at `+0x4c + 4g` (`over_cap`);
  - the rate at `+0x94 + 4g` (`income`), written behind a `+` as `(v + 8)` divided by 16, rounding
    toward zero (the listing's `(x + (x >> 31 & 0xf)) >> 4`).
- `IFaceMainBase::draw_pop_cap@0080ff90` writes `LeaderData +0x940` over `+0x7e4`. By the type
  record those are `control` over `pop_cap`, **not the census's `pop`**, which the record also prints.

So the bar's six numbers are `bucket`. They are not `resources`, which `diff::leader` keys to the
ledger's assembled rate. The population the bar shows is `control`. §6 had listed the rates as not
established; they are now read.

## The commander's three checks

1. **Independence.** Our side is the bar's numbers as the client derives them:
   - The walk is `FreeRunner::from_lobby` on the harness lobby, run53's `GAMEINFO` (`script::LOBBY_DUMP`;
     the same `client::input::tests::lobby` 4197 walks), stepped from frame 0 to frame 11,472 with no
     orders. That is the one lobby.
   - On each compared frame: `sim::observe` → `Scene::leaders` → `hud::Bar::of` → `Bar::text` → parsed
     back by `printed_bar`.
   - Their side is `rondata::gamelog::Log::parse` of each capture's frames, read by `record_bar` in the
     test.

   The two sides share no helper. The nation's name on our side is the scene's copy of the tech tree's
   `<TRIBE name>`. On theirs it is the record's `tribe` index joined through `FreeRunner::nations()`, a
   list read from the same tables. That join is the one place both sides read one table, so a nation
   check backs the index and not the table's spelling.

   The captures are the nine Great Lakes leader captures `diff::leader`'s merchant test stands up from
   the same run53 start: run84, run91, run107, run115, run19, run111, run100, run117, run123. They span
   frames 6950 to 11471. The level-9 block prints on 1,305 of their frames, the same 1,305 make-list
   blocks the merchant test counts. That gives 2,610 records for slots 0 and 1.
2. **Coverage against §4.**
   - Population: the bar's population *is* `control`, the field the original's population box writes
     (`draw_pop_cap@0080ff90`, `+0x940`). It is compared as the first number of `POP n/cap`.
     The census's `pop` (1 at the start, against `control` 6) is another field, and the original's bar
     does not show it.
   - The clock: the record prints none. The printed frame is the walk's step count, aligned to the
     dump's `FRAME` header by construction, so counting it would be a comparison that cannot fail. The
     clock's time rule is not read. `IFaceMainBase::draw_game_timer@0080a3c0` is the victory
     countdowns' (`(frames + 14) / 15` seconds), and it misses both photographs (3:39 against 3:38,
     3:43 against 3:41). Parked.
3. **The shown rate.** It is the decompile's, not the record's. The record prints `income` raw, in
   sixteenths. `IFaceResources::draw_resource@00818840` reads it at `LeaderDataEncrypt +0x94 + 4g`
   (`income` by the type record) and writes `(v + 8) / 16` with a `+`. The test applies that rule to the
   record's `income`, and the bar applies it to the scene's.
