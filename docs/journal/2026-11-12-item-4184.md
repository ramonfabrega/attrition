## Commander's three conditions

1. **The page's side comes from the page's own events.** Theirs is built by
   `rondata::lobby::lobbies_on_disk`. It reads each file's raw lines up to the first frame and parses
   the dump's own text with `gamelog::Log`. Ours is built by `client::lobby::Page::key`: key presses
   (`Page::keys_for` each event `events_for` reads off the lobby), and each key makes at most one
   `Event` through `Choices::apply`. The test keeps only the events the page emitted, and asserts that
   replaying them alone gives the page's state. The page's block is written by `Choices::block`. The
   two compared:
   - the text, `Choices::lines` against `OnDisk::lines`, with no parser on either side;
   - the fields, `Lobby::partings`, which share only the dump's own parser.

   `Choices` is never filled from a parsed block.
2. **Red once**, each one line, reverted:
   - the page's nation mapping off by one (`s.tribe = (*tribe + 1) % 24`): **0 of 161**. The first to
     part is the harness lobby, `PLAYER 0 tribe` ours 5 theirs 4, then `PLAYER 1 tribe` 12/11 and its
     name.
   - the difficulty control dropped from `events_for`: **30 of 161** (the lobbies at `DIFFICULTY 0`).
     The first to part is run1000, `GAMEINFO DIFFICULTY` ours 0 theirs 3.
   - a typed seed 12346 builds another `Sim` (kept as a test).
3. **What the page cannot set** is `lobby::NOT_SET` and `docs/LOBBY.md` §6: the build string and
   number, `PLAYERS` (derived), `SCENARIO_TYPE`, `SCRIPT_TYPE`, `MODS`, the three checksum fields, a
   row's `flags`, `who`, `play`, `pauses`, `diff`, `pop_cap_frame` and `synced_*`, a random nation, and
   an open, closed or observing seat. **What the comparison does not read**: nothing inside
   `GAMEINFO`. On run53 the parser reads 77 fields of the block's 79 lines; the other two are the
   `BEGIN PLAYER` heads, and the raw-line comparison covers every line. The root `GAME INFO` block's own
   fields (`RUN COUNT`, `Player`, `WHO`) are outside `GAMEINFO` and not compared. The built start does
   not read them.
