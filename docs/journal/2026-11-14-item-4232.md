## 4. Drawn

**The floor.** It is one patch layer:
- its texture is the first layer times the clamped corner, out of 255 (`floor_product`);
- its grey is `floor_grey` of the screen's ramp, laid as an alpha-ramp texcoord and re-laid when the ramp moves.

`fs_floor` takes `min(2 · grey · texel, 1)` through `land_lit` at the flat light, depth written. Before, the stage
was baked at the brightest grey (153), the blue channel already clamped there, and a black layer over it took it
down in LDR.

**The water.** `Gpu::ground_passes` draws the base layer and the patches up to the water into the target, cleared.
It copies the target to a `lake` texture, then draws the water and the patches after it in a second pass that loads
the first. `fs_water` does three things:
- it lights the water's ramp colour: `5 · c^1.45 · (WATER_DIFFUSE · N·L / π + WATER_AMBIENT · SCENE_AMBIENT)`,
  item 4227's `lit` at (1, 1, 1.5) and 0.2;
- it takes each channel of the copy back through the curve by bisection, as `shade.wgsl` does;
- it blends at the lattice's alpha and tones the result.

With shadows on, the same passes draw into the shade pass's `under` target, up to the first mountain. The curve
leaves a byte about 0.045 HDR wide near the top (slope 22 bytes per unit at 4.5), so the copy's precision costs
little. A texel brighter than the white (4.9) clips. That is parked under §3.24's "not established".
