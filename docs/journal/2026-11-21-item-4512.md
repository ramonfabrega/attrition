## The first act: leaders on 3583

**What parted.** Range 191 is the `size` leaf of who=1's `oil_patches` (`SimpleArray<int>`). On 3582 both sides
hold twelve patches `[0, 12, 16, 9, 11, 7, 5, 13, 10, 3, 17, 4]`, `size` 20. On 3583 both drop to ten (patches 3
and 4 gone: arm 4.2 of `Leader::produce_building@006e1400` prunes a land patch whose cell the leader no longer

**The direction.** `SimpleArray<int>::remove@00462e70` and `remove_position@0046c340` only shorten the length;
nothing gives capacity back. This crate computed the size from the length each frame (`simple_array_size`), so an
array that lost elements reported the smaller block. The old comment said as much ("an array that has lost
elements may report more; no packet shows one"). `mil_trainers` has the same flaw waiting
(`Sim::mil_trainer_close`, `Wall::decrement_stats@00642da0`'s arm); `new_rares` loses no element.

**The fix.** `Leader::oil_patches_size` and `Leader::mil_trainers_size` keep the capacity as of the last removal
(`Leader::remove_oil_patch`, `Leader::remove_mil_trainer`, each taking `max(kept, step(len))` before it removes),
and the checksum reports `max(step(len), kept)`. Growth past the kept capacity still steps by ten from the length,
which is what a capacity that grows by ten from 20 gives.

**The value diff**: run1752, state 3583, leaders range 191, record 1, `oil_patches` `size`: ours 10, theirs 20.
With the fix ours is 20, and every leaders range agrees on 3582 and 3583. Leaders 5,056 → 5,235, whole; the row's
