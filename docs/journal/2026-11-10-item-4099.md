## 1. The reader, before its count

The rule went into RENDERER §5.3, "The colour reader", with `Hue` / `HUE` in `render::silhouette`. Both were
committed before any count was printed (`744093625`):

- **Which pixels.** For each object the traced reader scores, the pixels in its window that:
  - hold the object's own key (its own key only, not the scene's), and
  - the traced reader counts as the original's footprint (kind 2, its own trace; kind 4, off the ground).
  A pixel whose channel sum is under 60, on either side, is dark and left out.
- **What distance.** The Euclidean distance between the two sides' chromaticities, (r, g) / (r + g + b).
- **The light.** A white light's strength does not move chromaticity, so the photograph's shading is not scored
  as texture. Our side is the texel, unlit.
- **What passes.** A pixel agrees within 0.05. An object passes when it has 40 lit pixels and half of them agree.
- **The split.** Pixels that part are split by the texel's alpha: under 255 is the mask, by supposition.

The key buffer and the painter share one fragment walk, `render::silhouette::Projector::fragments`, so they cannot
disagree on a pixel. `rasterize_cut` was refactored onto it, and its tests and every pinned row are unchanged.

## 3. The draw

- **The world.** `World::objects` carries each triangle's texcoords (`Drawing::uvs`). `World::figure_texture`
  decodes the `<UNIT>`'s `texture=` once with its levels (`render::silhouette::Mips`, 2 × 2 box means).
  `World::figure_meshes` hands the GPU one `TexturedMesh` per textured figure and the rest as the tint soup.
  `World::figures` had no callers left and is gone.
- **The client's shader.** `client::gpu`'s `TEXTURED_SHADER` is the world pipeline's projection and depth, line
  for line, with a texcoord in place of a colour. It samples unlit: bilinear within a level, the level nearest,
  wrapped. `--tint` draws the tint as before.
- **The CPU painter.** `render::silhouette::paint` samples the same way, through the same fragments as the key
  buffer. The card picks the level per pixel and the CPU picks it per triangle; that is the one difference.
- **The still.** At frame 3279 the GPU draws the figures textured (by eye, crops in
  `~/ron-data/lab-experiments/2026-11-10-item-4099/`). A screenshot is not a gate.
- **The buildings are sprites already**, read by `paint_stamp` as they are blitted, and they read the same on both
  draws: 0/2001 2,628 of 4,618 agree (in), 0/2000 20,421 of 36,665 (in).
