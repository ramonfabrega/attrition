## Step 2: the listing

- **`Build::close@00628980`, `006292a1`–`00629335`**. The gate is
  `is_gather_type` (vtable `+0x90`) and `!is_flat` (`+0x94`), the pair
  `Build::init` uses. Under it, every tile of the list loses `0x1000`,
  and the region of the building's own cell takes `0x20`. Then, for
  every building, `mtn`/`cliff` go to −1 and the list's length to 0.
  One `return` in the whole function, at its end, so every close takes
  the tail.
- **`destroy_building`** calls vtable `+0x150`, which is `Build::close`.
- **`GameDaemon::process_all`** cycles the flag every frame: clear
  `0x10`, and `0x20` → `0x10`.
- **`Build::process`**, after `do_queue`, on an active (`flags & 4`,
  `Wall::activate`'s bit) gather, non-flat building:
  - `0x20` → `verify_gather_tiles` (no draw);
  - `0x10` → `find_gather_tiles` again, which draws when the list grows.
