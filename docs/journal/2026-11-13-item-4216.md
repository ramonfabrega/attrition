## Stage 1: the group unit

As the document says. What the building showed:

- **A group's memory is not a member's.** The first whole run stopped `game:run346` (28 tests) at 789 MiB
  against 768: the limit was 1.5 times a member's row of 2026-10-16. The 145 groups peak at a median 1.7
  times, and up to 3.7 times, their largest member alone. A group now reserves 1.5 times its own measured
  peak under a 32 GiB ceiling. **Seen red**: the old rule put back, `[(8192, 12288), (8192, 12288)] !=
  [(1280, 32768), (8192, 32768)]`. A test that printed `ok` in a stopped process stays red.
- **A cache one test fills and another reads loses the reader's rows.** `testenv::Shared` on map
  generation's five caches (**seen red**: with the credit removed the nested-cache test read `inner, outer,
  inner, outer, inner` for `inner, outer`), and one `Shared` cell a capture in `closing_quit`:
  `american_modern_pair_run1151c_s_word_frame_is_widened_whole` had lost `rontrace-run1151c.log` to another
  test's fill.
- **The groups come from a gate's fixture audit**, with `GAME_TRACES = 2`: without the bound one endpoint
  test joined 157 tests of six modules.
- **Made to fail** (`test_isolated_tests.Groups`, eleven tests on a fake binary through the real process
  path): with every row forced green, four turn red — the failing test, the group that dies, the group that
  drops a test, the broken verdict line.
- **One process for a game buys nothing by itself**: `game:run1149`'s 28 tests retire 1,217 G alone and
  1,208 G in one process. The gain of stage 1 is map generation, 1,574 CPU-seconds to 109.

## Where this departs from the pass's table, and why

The commander's word (15:45): the table is the design, not an order.

- **Stages 1 and 2 were designed together**: the windows are the bulk of a game's group.
- **The groups come from the audit, not from names.** No registry of tests exists.
- **"Ascending frame order" is not built**: libtest runs a filter's tests in name order.
- **The audit is held equal as a multiset, not as a set.**
- **The kept walk lands off.** Turning it on is its own landing, on the whole library under the verifier.
