## The fix, judged out of reach

This crate derives a unit's pool group from army membership
(`Sim::pool_group_of`). The original keeps a per-unit back-pointer
(`UnitData +0x80`) that `push_group`'s walks, `get_open_slot`'s eviction
and `kill` can clear while the unit stays in the army's list. On 11512
the two disagree for `1/64`, and the stance's reach says the original's
list and pointers disagree for `1/62` and `1/63` too. Reaching that means
modelling the pointer and reading the membership family: GROUPS §3.1,
§3.2 and §4.2, and ARMY §3.2's `add_unit` → `Unit::set_group`. No draw on
11512 names the call, so the cheapest next step is a capture, not a
reading.
