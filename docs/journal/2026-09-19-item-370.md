# Item 370 — the ladder's extras, typed: substituted, not overshot

*2026-09-19, Opus 5, worker on `worktree-att-365`, measured on the tree at
`5828028`. No screen, no dump, no capture — which is why this item fitted
between two capture items.*

362 moved rung C's `extra` floor 13 → 25 and rung B's 6 → 19, and named the
disjunction it could not settle: **all scholars and caravans** means the
batch fix overshoots somewhere the word is long past, which is benign;
**a mixed bag** means sibling divergence on borrowed captures 7,590 and
8,700 frames past the word, which is not.

It is neither, and the third answer is sharper than either.

## What they are

`score` already builds the simulation it compares and then drops it, so the
one line 362 named — type each entry of `EndpointResult::extra` from
`built.sim.units[u].ty` — was enough. `extra_types` is a new field on the
result, filled by the caller because the type table is the loader's.

`crates/sim/src/ai_units.rs` to its parent of `9321676`, walk, restore.

## Why "substituted" is the word

**The two rosters are not nested.** Only four object numbers — 1/10, 1/12,
1/25 and 1/29, two citizens and two scholars — carry the same type in both
trees. From 1/48 up, every shared object number is a *different unit*: 1/48
is a Cataphract before and a Citizen after, 1/54 a Bowman before and a
Cataphract after, and so on to 1/60, on top of which the post-fix tree has
twelve more ids the pre-fix tree never reaches.

That decides the disjunction, because the two branches make different
predictions about *shape* and not only about type:

- **An overshoot adds units of the batched types and leaves the rest
  alone.** The batch fix's scope is the scholar (`k`), the caravan (3/2/1)
  and the citizen (`min(deficit, room)`). Scholars are 2 before and 2 after.
  Caravans are 0 in both. Neither of the two types whose `num` is a free
  count moved at all.
- **The one in-scope type that does move is the citizen**, 2 → 8 — and its
  `num` is `min(deficit, room)`, a quantity bounded by the leader's own
  deficit and the city's room, so a larger batch of citizens is not evidence
  of a larger batch than the original's.
- **Ten of the twelve are military of types the pre-fix tree does not hold
  at all**, and three military types it does hold vanish outright. Nothing
  in 362's change touches military production directly; this is the
  downstream roster of a game whose economy diverged earlier.

So: **ordinary divergence past the word, with a citizen-batch component**,
and no evidence that `num` is too large. The independent check is the one
362 already had and did not connect to this question — `extra` is **0** on
both endpoint captures at 24,001, before and after, and that is precisely
the counter an overshoot would move on the scored games.

## The thing the count could not have told anyone

Rung C's `extra` sat at 13 through items 344, 346, 348, 354 and 358 while it
was 21, 25, 21, 16, 17, 13 — and nobody could have seen, from the number,
that the *roster* was churning underneath. The pre/post comparison here is
the first time a rung's extras have been named at all. Two changes follow,
and both are cheap:

- **`pinned`'s failure message prints the typed extras** rather than bare
  `(who, o)` pairs. The next worker whose landing moves a rung sees what the
  units are without re-deriving this item.
- **Both rungs are walked before either is asserted.** The ladder test used
  to panic on rung C and take rung B's measurement with it — which is how
  rung B's own move "surfaced on the second run", as its comment says. They
  are two different games; the second is not a consequence of the first.

## The invariant that fell out, and it is the durable half

Rung B and rung C are the **same simulation** — the same East Indies game,
the same borrowed setup, walked to 16,489 and 15,401. So an object number
that is `extra` on both rungs is one unit seen 1,088 frames apart and must
carry the same type. It does: eighteen shared ids, and the block 1/56–1/72
is type-identical on the two rungs, with rung B holding one more citizen at
1/73 for the extra thousand frames.

`the_east_indies_ladder_is_pinned` now asserts it, with a floor of fifteen
shared ids so it cannot pass vacuously. **This is the one thing about
`extra` that does not churn with every AI landing**: the counts move
whenever the AI changes, but the two rungs agreeing with each other is
invariant under that, and it fails exactly when the borrowed setups stop
being one simulation — which is the failure that once read run28 at 12
units of 71 and was two edits from the scoreboard.
`the_ladder_s_borrowed_setup_is_the_endpoint_s` checks the same thing at
6,000, under the word; nothing checked it past the word, where the counts
live. Made to fail first: renaming 1/60's type on one rung gives
`["1/60 C Yak vs B Hoplites"]`.

## What did not move

**No floor moved.** Rung C is 25 and rung B is 19, measured again on the
current tree, exactly as pinned; both rows' comments now say what the units
are. Nothing in `crates/sim` changed — the pre-fix tree was built by a
revert and restored byte for byte. Nothing was written to the queue or the
handoff.

one-line pointer to this finding; I did not add one, because the finding is
about the ladder counter rather than about the mechanic, and the commander
holds that document's lane.
