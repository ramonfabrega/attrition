# Item 4297: the attack-move, ctrl on a right press

*Opus 5.5, one session (lane-4297), no subagents. The census came first. The 54 orders are 27 packages, all in the
2017 sample. Each carries every field the issuers write: `ATTACK_TO`, no facing, −1/−1, `QUEUE_NEW`. Each head is
minted byte for byte on the minimap at its recorded point. So the item's stop was not reached. Then the build: ctrl
on a unit's right press mints the attack-move in the main view and on the minimap, and ctrl with alt mints `MOVE_TO`
with the formation in the main view. `INPUT_WHOLE_ORDERS` 927 · 1,330 · 172 → **927 · 1,384 · 118** of 2,429.
`INPUT_WHOLE` holds at 42 of 50. This branch does not touch `crates/sim`.*

## The first act: the census

- **The question**: the 54 orders §30.2 missed as "the attack-move", field by field against
  `Console::execute_at_cursor@007c6630`'s ctrl arm and the minimap's.
- **The answer** (`the_attack_moves_of_the_recordings_on_disk`, CLIENT_INPUT §35.2):
  - 27 packages of one issue and its `group`, all in `seifer008` (2017), play 0.
  - `(orders; set_angle, angle, form, width, queued, disembark)` = `(2; 0, 0, −1, −1, 2, 0)` on all 27. No other
    `orders` value is recorded.
  - Minted on the minimap under ctrl at the recorded point: 27 of 27, byte for byte.
  - Selections: 4 send the last one again (`num = 0`), 3 list one object, and the largest lists 37.
- **Which issuer is not on disk.** With `QUEUE_NEW` the main view, the minimap and the panel's `ATTACK_TO` button
  write the same bytes.

## The reading

- `execute_at_cursor` reads ctrl and shift, and alt only under `prefs & 0x400` clear. With a unit selection and
  ctrl (or alt without shift), on a press (`param_2` 0), it takes its first arm before any object:
  - ctrl without alt: `move_to(drag_x2, drag_y2, queue, 0, 0, ATTACK_TO, −1, −1, 0)`;
  - ctrl with alt, or a debug flag: `MOVE_TO` with `drag_form`/`drag_form_mod`.
- `WorldMap::on_right_up` with no plane: `ctrl_key()` gives `ATTACK_TO` in place of `MOVE_TO`, alt or not; alt only
  moves the queue.
- `UnitOut::make_options` adds `OPTION_ATTACK_TO` to the panel; its spot calls `execute_at_cursor(0, 1)`, which on
  the ground issues the same order (4297a).
