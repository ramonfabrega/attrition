## The mechanism

§3.16 already says that under the land is the clear colour, black, and that `base_terrain_rs` blends source alpha
over. The card's target is HDR, tone mapped afterwards (`ps_tonemap.hlsl`; item 4232 drew the water on the same
model). So a faded land pixel is `T(L · a)`. Ours tone-mapped in the shader and blended `T(L)` at `a` over the
clear, giving `T(L) · a`. The curve is concave from `T(0) = 0`, so ours read darker. `fs_faded` writes `T(L · a)`
opaque. It is exact over black. Our `CLEAR` (0.01, 0.01, 0.015) no longer shows under a faded tile, as the
original's black would not.
