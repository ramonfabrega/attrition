  values was the mechanism, through the blend's place, not through the colour's packing or the alpha.

  in exactly when `RenderState::enables` carries 0x20000: 55 with it, 366 without, none apart. `rise.pdb`'s
  `RenderEnables` names that bit `TL_FAKE_LIGHT`. The surf (draw 163) carries 0x20085, the smoke's own value.
