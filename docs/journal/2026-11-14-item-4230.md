**`TradeOrder`'s walk.** The type record gives `oxx`, `whose`, `started`, `loaded` (ints at `+0x14..+0x20`) and `uid2`
(`+0x24`, a ushort) above `TargetOrder`'s `ox`, `whom`, `uid`. The walker hands over the virtual base's flags byte,
then `+0x8..+0x12`, then `+0x14..+0x26`, which is the same shape as `GatherOrder`'s. `Unit::add_trade_order@005e4dc0`
writes the home city's building as `ox`/`whom` and its `+0x30` as `uid`. It writes its third and fourth arguments as
`oxx`/`whose` (−1, −1 from `think_caravan`) and `uid2` 0xffff, and zeroes `started` and `loaded`.
`do_trade@005ed270:276`–`:278` writes the far city's building, owner and uid. The crate's `TradeOrder` holds city
indices, so the emitter reads each city's `building`. **Built**: `checksum/units.rs`'s `Body::Trade` arm and
`TRADE_FIELDS`. rondata's site map names `00483283`, `00483291` and `0048329f`.

**The road stack's size.** `Caravan::Caravan@0073d1e0` zeroes the road stack (`+0x10` list, `+0x14` size, `+0x18`
length). It writes `movb $-1, 0x1c(%ecx)`, a byte, because `Stack<PathData>::increment` is a `char` (`init@0046d720`'s
`(char)param_2`, `push`'s `(int)this->increment`). `increase_size@0046e8c0` with a negative step adds `size`, or 1 from
0, so the road doubles. Its pushes are `find_road@00688a40:99`/`:104`, the two ends on a fresh search (skipped under

`0x4000` tiles. `build_road`'s push-back (`:151`) keeps fewer. `clear_road` moves only the length, and
`Caravans::init_caravan@0073e1f0` reuses an existing object (`Caravan::init` touches no stack), so the size survives
the slot. `do_trade:501`'s `operator=` grows the unit's path by the difference when the road's size is larger.
**Built**: `Caravan::road_size` with `road_grow` (`economy/caravan.rs`), kept through `init_caravan` and
`close_caravan`. `PathStack::assign` (`orders/order.rs`) is the `operator=`, and `trade_walk` uses it in place of `set`.
