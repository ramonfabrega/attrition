## Step 1: the callers, before any capture

923 booked row 4 as four lobby variants: a continents map, the large-city
start, a handicap, another player count. Each was its reading of what a
function's name suggested. The brief asked for the callers first, and they
changed the plan.

The export's callers were not enough, because five of the nine have none
in the decompile. So I scanned the executable itself: every `call`/`jmp
rel32` in `.text` whose target is one of the nine, and every four-byte
little-endian pointer to one anywhere in the file. That is what finds a
vtable slot, and it found `MapGrass::make_continents`'s. What it said:

- **Five have no reference at all**: `Setup::init_wild_life`,
  `Setup::build_leader`, `LeaderData::get_handicap_level`,
  `LeaderData::is_human` and `Tribe::init`. There is no call, no jump and
  no pointer to any of them. `is_human` is `leader_flags & 4` and
  `get_handicap_level` is `return handicap`, so both are inlined at every
  use and the out-of-line copies are orphans. The other three have no
  caller. All nine are in the coverage table (`rontrace.funcs`), so a run
  that entered one would have recorded it; none can.
- **`get_handicap` is multiplayer-only.** Four callers: `train_time`
  twice, `compute_reg_territory`, and `get_gather_handicap` for a human.
  Each is behind game semaphore bit 2. `is_tournament` requires that bit,
  and `solo_init` and `is_sandbox` require it clear, so it is the
  multiplayer flag. A solo handicap reaches nothing.
- **`MapGrass::make_continents` is the scenario editor's.** It sits in a
  vtable slot, and `Map::new_map` builds a `MapGrass` for style 23
  (`BLANK_MAP`). Of `new_map`'s three callers, only
  `ScenarioEditor::generate_map` passes 23; `Setup::build_game` passes the
  lobby's style, and the lobby's list stops at 22.
- **The two that remain**: `place_start_in_region` is called by British
  Isles (style 22, one start per player per island) and by East Meets
  West. `large_city_buildings` is `build_cities`' branch on its third
  argument being 3, which `build_empire` feeds from the game's
  `starting_town`; `rules.xml`'s `startingtowns` list makes 3 "Large
  Town".

So two of 923's four variants, the handicap and the player count, enter
nothing, and the other two fit in one lobby. Nothing on disk had entered
either reachable function: both were on `NEVER` over all ninety traces.

## Step 2: one launch needed a profile field

British Isles is not style 14, so `-config` has to go (ORACLE: `-config`
pins 14), and the lobby is then the profile's. `PlayerProfile::load` reads
the `<SOLO>`/`<MULTI>` blocks into `game_params` by `gameinfo_strings`,
which includes `STARTING_TOWN`. The queue had no key to write one.
Hand-editing the profile would also have left a Large Town in the profile
every East Indies capture reads, silently. So I added `tools/gamelog/profile.py
KEY=N`, a stanza key `profile:`, and a `PROFILE` hook in `longtrace.sh`
that copies `Player.dat` before the edit and back at the end.
