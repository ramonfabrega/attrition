## 2026-09-17 — item 308: `start_dist` is a 48-grid Manhattan on twenty-three captures (Opus 5)

**East Indies 8193 → 8193, Great Lakes 7930 → 7930.** The headline did not
move and this entry says so first, because that is the rule. Both numbers
are measured on this branch's tip **after its last `ccc update`**, which
took item 314's `dc82d67` — so Great Lakes' 7679 → **7930** on that tree is
314's gain and none of it is this item's.

The item was booked against a different score — the suspend's *assertable
reach* — and that is what it moved: from two windows' worth of evidence to
twenty-three captures' worth, on rows that were already on disk. No capture
was run and the screen was never touched; item 314 had it for the whole
session.

### What was on disk

`UnitData::start_dist` (`+0x130`) appears **1,184,141 times** across the
captures in `RON_GAMELOG_DIR`, and is non-zero in **16,617** of them, over
**23 captures and 51 units**. run16 alone holds 8,752 stamped rows. The
whole sweep is a `grep` and a streamed parse; nothing was captured for it.

### The three writers, not one

§18.2 said `astar_path`'s suspend block is the field's "only writer in the
whole executable". That is right about *values* and incomplete about the
field. There are three:

| site | writes |
|---|---|
| `PathFinder::astar_path@00683770:517` | the value — the suspend, and the only source of a non-zero |
| `UnitData::UnitData@00606670:74` | 0, at construction |
| `Unit::init@00612100:567` | 0, beside the five container pointers |

The third is not pedantry. It is what the corpus caught acting.

### A row that disagreed, and it was the proof

Keyed on the per-player `o`, the corpus holds exactly **one** non-zero → zero
transition in 1,184,141 rows: run16's `1/9` reads 768 at block 3016 and 0 at
block 3960. If "nothing clears it" were simply true, that row falsifies §18.2.
It does not, and the dump says why in one field:

```
 block 3016   who 1  o 9   uid 17   myhits 40   start_dist 768
 block 3960   who 1  o 9   uid 25   myhits 70   start_dist 0
```

Different `uid`, 944 blocks apart with the unit absent from the dump between:
`o` was handed back out and a new unit was born into the slot. That is
`Unit::init@00612100:567`, observed. Keyed on `uid` the corpus holds **zero**
clears — and `uid` is parsed now (`UnitDump::uid`) precisely because a claim
about a permanent stamp cannot be carried by a slot number.

### The invariant that dates the parse as correct

**Every one of the 16,617 non-zero values is a positive multiple of 48.**
144, 240, 720, 768 … 42,960 — 29 distinct values, from 3 cells to 895, and
not one off the grid. 48 is the world grid's own quantum, and
`astar_path@00683770:453` builds the value as `|dx| + |dy|` between two points
the 48-grid search has snapped to cell *centres*, so a whole number of cells
is what the arithmetic must produce. That is a structural claim about the
parse which no single row can make: a field read at the wrong offset or the
wrong width — item 269's third axis — would land off the grid almost surely,
and this one never does. 144, the row item 301 proved, is 3 × 48.

Beside it, two more, each asserted per capture: **every stamped unit-frame has
`collide_frame >= 0`** (the suspend is reached through
`resolve_unit_collision` step 6, and there is no counterexample in 16,617
rows), and **nothing clears the stamp inside a unit's life**.

### What is asserted is the change, not the value

The commander's steer, and it was right. A field nothing clears is checked on
the block it *changes*. Once run90's `1/7` reads 144 it reads 144 on all 89 of
its later blocks, so a value assertion there passes 89 times for the price of
one — run85's first teeth check fell into exactly that shape. So the pinned
table is **every transition in every capture**, `(block, who, o, from, to)`:
22 of them, 17 first stamps and 5 re-stamps. The two rows the earlier items
proved are now ordinary members of it — run90's `(7811, 1, 7, 0, 144)` and
run76's `(6861, 1, 28, 0, 7680)`. Thirteen captures have none to pin, because
a window that opens after its unit suspended carries the stamp already
standing; for those the row and unit counts are what shows the reader could
see anything at all.

**And the stamp is not a high-water mark.** Five of the 22 transitions are
non-zero → non-zero and they go in both directions — run50's `0/21` falls
3120 → 816. A later suspend overwrites it, which is what one unconditional
assignment says and what a "furthest search so far" reading would have got
wrong.

### The field was dumped and uncompared, and now is not

`UnitData::log_data` writes `start_dist` at **every** detail level — it is in
the smallest capture on disk — and nothing in this crate compared it.
`FrameResult::search_compared` / `search_diverged` now do, on every unit-frame
whose position the two sides agree on, in both directions from zero: a stamp
this crate does *not* have is the interesting half, because it says the
original gave up on a search where this crate did not.

That needed the stamp to move out of `path::Search` and onto `sim::Unit`. In
the original it lives on `UnitData` and outlives the containers handed over
beside it, so it still reads back long after `clear_partial_path` has freed
them; a copy that died with the stash could never match a dump taken after the
search ended. One field, one write at the suspend, zero at birth — the
original's own three sites.

### Two counts, and the smaller one is the honest one

A raw `grep` of the same 23 files finds **16,643** stamped rows and 53 units;
the frames carry **16,617** and 51. The 26-row difference is root-level
`FULL DUMP` blocks written *outside* any `FRAME` — the four islands window
captures each end with one — which no per-frame comparison ever visits. It is
worth naming rather than rounding away: a sweep that counts the file and a
diff that walks the frames are not measuring the same thing, and the number a
test can hold is the frames'.

### Two traps worth writing down

Both were mine, both cost a wrong table before they were caught:

- **The dump is CRLF.** A `$`-anchored pattern over `start_dist [0-9]+$`
  matches nothing, silently — an empty result that reads exactly like "the
  field is not there".
- **`xargs -P 8` into one stdout interleaves.** The first sweep reported 22
  values that were not multiples of 48. Every one was two awk processes'
  lines spliced mid-write, not a row of the game's. Per-file outputs, then
  concatenate.

### The value diff, and it agrees

Measured on the base's own tip `7cfd663` — which carries the suspend wired,
so §18.3's "not wired" is struck through and pointed at it:

| | unit-frames compared | disagreements |
|---|---|---|
| run76 (Great Lakes) | 8,032 | **0** |
| run90 (East Indies) | 2,860 | **0** |

10,892 comparisons on a field nothing compared this morning, and nothing
apart. This crate's `1/7` stamps 144 on block 7811 and holds it to 7899;
its `1/28` stamps 7680 on 6861 — the frame §18.5's `kill_current_path` fix

A day ago the column would have been all disagreement, because an unwired
suspend stamps nothing, so this is now a standing check that the wiring
stays wired.

### Two ledger pins moved, deliberately

`rondata::ledger`'s widening ledger moves both ways and the queue is owed
both numbers, since a worker does not write them itself:

- **uncompared 19 → 18.** `UnitData::start_dist` is compared now. It had
  been parsed since item 301 and read only off the original's own dump —
  exactly the shape that half of the ledger exists to catch.
- **single-capture 45 → 46.** `UnitDump::uid` arrives, named by the corpus
  sweep alone. It stays single-capture until a window test has a reason to
  link on it.

### Both new checks were made to fail before they were kept

The transition table pinned at `(7812, 1, 7, 0, 144)` instead of 7811 fails
on run90; the suspend writing 0 instead of the Manhattan fails both window
columns. And the count assertion failed for real on its first run, on
run27 — 6 rows where the sweep said 14 — which is what turned up the
out-of-frame `FULL DUMP` blocks.

### What this does not establish

The three invariants are properties of the original's dump. They say the
field is what §18 claims; they do not say this crate reproduces a row on
any capture the comparison has not run, and the two windows above are the
only ones it has. The other twenty-one carry stamped rows that no test yet
replays — reach for the taking, each on a capture already on disk.
