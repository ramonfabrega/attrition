## The packet: who calls what

`step4.py --entry 610bc0` (`Unit::process`) on run1296. Each unit's address comes from `unitaddr.py`.
- **`1/93`** (`15acc43c`) runs `do_attack_to` → `find_melee_target(−1)` → `find_nearby_target(0x1200)`, and then
  nothing. That is ours.
- **`1/94`** (`15acc5ac`) runs `do_job` → `do_attack` → `Unit::fight`. At `fight+0xa1f` (`0x5fdeea`, reached from
  the roll arm `0x5fdf50`, not from `poor_target`) it calls `find_new_target`. That runs `find_melee_target(−1)`
  and then `find_nearby_target(0x1200)`, which finds `0/11`.

    then `set_up_insert`.
  - The member retarget then adds the attack to:

    - `1/94`, at 0x11a6;
    - `1/95`, at 0x15f2.
  - Then `finish_insert` re-lays the move from the leader `1/93`'s own attack-to.
  - The emulation stops in `action_move_near` at `704a3e` on an unmapped read. The calls above came before it.

**The cause.** Ours' `find_new_target` kills the attack and calls `find_melee_target_headed`. Its search arm called
`nearby_add`, which only adds the target alone. The original's add (`find_nearby_target:545`–`606`) is one arm for
every caller: a `GROUP_ATTACK_TO` head, on an active member of a group still walking its attack-move, hands the
find to `Group::action_attack`. Ours had written that as a SEAM in `find_melee_target_headed`: "this crate carries
that arm for the attack-move's own look alone".
