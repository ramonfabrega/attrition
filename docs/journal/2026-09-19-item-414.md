# Item 414 — the capture, and the dichotomy it was built on (2026-09-19)

*Opus 5, on the AI lane. `docs/AI.md` §48, `docs/RUNS.md` run111.*

## The word did not move

**Great Lakes' word stands at 9510.** This item bought a cause, not a score:
run111 names the frame and the number that make 408's purchase go the wrong
way, and the fix is the successor. 16 blocks captured, 32 leader-blocks and
33,536 field-frames compared, three readings tested, two killed.

## What it kills

408 closed with a pair — "the Citizen's value is wrong" or "the Citizen
should not have been offered" — and a capture booked to separate them. Both
are dead, and **neither was the answer**:

- The Citizen is `val 234782`, `num 2`, same city, **on both sides** at the
  deciding block. Identical.
- The original offers it too. It simply does not buy it.

What stands is the third reading, which only existed because the disk was
grepped before the capture ran: **the Scholar's own re-offer is too small**.

And the prediction written into the stanza is dead too, which is the part
worth keeping. It said the original's Scholar would *stay* at 9,999,999
with `num 5` where this crate's collapsed. Both collapse, on the same block
— the original to **5,755,741** on two slots, this crate to **45,568** on
one. 5,755,741 clears the Citizen's 234,782; 45,568 does not.

So the frame was right and the thing named around it was wrong again. The
re-offer on sim-frame 9379 is the event; the number `create_units`' scholar
arm puts on it is the parting.

## The three things the record gave for free

- **The original names its own purchase.** On the block after `make_stuff`,
  one of its two Scholar offers reads 57,557 — 5,755,741/100, the
  bought-slot devaluation — and the other is untouched. No inference from
  this crate at all.
- **The muster is a second witness.** `num_queued` on that block is two
  Citizens here against one Scholar there: §47.2's two building queues,
  seen from the leader instead of the building.
- **The goods agree entering the frame and part through it.** `bucket` is
  `107 127 53 129 80 0` on both sides, and **both sell 100 timber** on
  9382 — the two `use_market` draws §47.3 found agreeing. The original
  spends 40 on the Scholar and keeps 82; this crate buys food for two
  Citizens and keeps 14. Twice now a Great Lakes purchase divergence has
  had agreeing goods and a parting list.

## What it cost, and the three near-misses

The capture: 41.5 MB, three and a half minutes, **all five checks passing
first attempt** through the driver — rngcmp 9,406 in common and 0
differing, the window whole at 16 blocks, 352 `MAKEOBJECT` exactly, 0
differing against run100 bar `LEADERDATA`.

Three things nearly went wrong and each is a rule paying out:

- **Grep the disk before booking a capture.** run107 was already a
  `LEADERS=9` window on this game, thirty blocks of it, and it is what
  produced the third reading. It did not remove the need for run111 — it
  changed what run111 was *for*, from "which of two" to "what does the
  re-offer say".
- **Grep the writers of a number you call a ceiling.** 9,999,999 is an
  **overflow**, not a maximum: `val < 0 → 9999999` at
  `create_units@006c40a0:1567`, catching a 32-bit `imul` that wrapped. §45
  had already established it and I was one step from building a reading on
  the wrong meaning.
- **Compare on identity, not on position.** The first draft of the test
  compared `MAKE[1].val` to `MAKE[1].val` — and this crate's slot 1 at the
  re-offer is a **Phalanx**. It read as a finding. Keying the comparison on
  the slot's *type* is what made it true.

## A thing found and deliberately not fixed

`GOODS` in `diff/leader.rs` names index 2 "metal", 3 "wealth", 4
"knowledge". `sim::economy::Resource` has Wealth at 2, Knowledge at 3,
Metal at 4 — and index 2 is what pays this Scholar's 40 wealth, twice over
(run100's queue slot is `good[0] 2, cost[0] 40`; the bucket moves 53 → 82
across the purchase). Every value compared is index against index, so **no
verdict on any capture is affected**; every *name* printed or pinned at 2,
3 or 4 is the wrong resource. Renaming churns three pinned constants across
three items, so it is recorded in §48.4 and left for whoever owns them.
