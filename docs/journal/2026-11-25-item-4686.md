## How it was found

1. **The bit.** Bytes `[15, 17]` against `[15, 1]` are `0x1000`. In this crate that bit is
   `CityAi::near_border`, read into the word by `Sim::city_flags_word`. The original writes it in two places.
   `compute_reg_territory@006b0bb0:615` writes it with `| 0x1000` and is its only `|` writer. `City::capture`
   copies the whole word.
2. **The city, named.** Ours' probe: city 12 does not exist on 3618. On 3619 it is who=1's, at cell (25, 37). Its
   `bordering` is 0 on both sides, since the cities range `city..ter` agrees. Ours' border pass starts on 3619 and
   reaches city 12's cells from 3626. It calls `mark_bordering` there with the winner `Ambiguous`, so it marks
   nothing.
3. **The winner is `-2` because the city is unassimilated.** `compute_reg_territory`'s city arm sets `local_14 =
   0xfffffffe` unless the city's race (`+0x5f`) is its owner. The mark needs `-1 < local_14`. So city 12 is not a
   founding: it is a **capture**. Its race is the loser's, and the pass cannot mark it again.
4. **The write.** `City::capture@00736c40:86`–`:88` sets the new record's `city_flags` to the old one's `& 0xf13f`.
   The mask keeps `0x1000`, `0x2000` and `0x8000` with the attack bits. Ours' `city_capture_record` carried only
   `no_heal`, `attacking` and `ever_attacked`. It held `0x1000` on the loser's `city_ai[old]`, where the taker's
   `city_ai[new]` never saw it. `0x2000` (`no_muster`) and `0x8000` (`was_founding_capital`, set otherwise only
   when the old city was the founding capital) were dropped too.
5. **Writers counted.** `0x1000` has one `|` writer in the export (`compute_reg_territory:615`, grep over
   `funcs/` for `0x1000;` with a short store). `Wall::activate`'s `| 0x1000` is a different struct's `+0x60`. The
   whole-word writers are `City::init` (`= 1`) and `City::capture` (`old & 0xf13f`). Ours' `init_city` resets
   `city_ai` to the default, matching `City::init`.
