## What the booking said, and what the original allows

The booking named three arms: Blow Tread on a full-health vehicle, a paid Restock Supplies interrupted, a Spy's
Bribe from beyond base range (adjudication 3, rows 46, 49, 58, 47).

- **Blow Tread (row 46) and Restock Supplies (rows 49, 58) are castable by no unit on shipped data.**
  `craftrules.xml` gives both `FROM none`, `FROM2 None`, `PREQ0 Disable`. `SpellTypeData::is_castable@00675bc0`
  refuses any caster that is not FROM/FROM2 (bar 0x29a and 0x28a). `Build::finished`'s spell arm needs
  `LeaderData::has_spell` → `type_avail`, and `PREQ0 Disable` refuses that; the crate already models it as
  `Preq::Disabled`. Every craft with a non-empty `COST2` (Restock, Research Project, Move Capital, Morph, Upgrade,
  the diplomacy crafts) is `FROM none`. So `unpay_cast_costs`' resource half moves nothing a unit's cast can reach.
  The `SEAM` at `orders.rs` is rewritten as that statement, citing GOLDEN §178. `cast_blow_tread` stays never-entered,
  and the blind list is unmoved.
- **`cast_bribe`'s own refusal refunds (row 49's second caller) are also unreachable from a unit's cast.**
  `Unit::do_cast@005ebfe0:422` asks `is_valid_target` on every frame of the hold, the land frame included. Bribe's arm
  there opens with the same `has_general(0, 0x165)` (a CEO's reach) and own-target refusals, so the cast dies through
  `kill_current_order`, whose mana refund the crate carries (run346). The arm is built anyway in `cast_on`, through a
  new `Sim::unpay_cast_mana` that the kill arm now shares. It is faithful and dead; no test holds it yet (Left to do).
  The CEO itself is `BASE_GOV_HEROTYPES`, so it carries the supply bit and Bribe never targets it.
