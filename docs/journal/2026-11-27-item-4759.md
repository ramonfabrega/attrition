## The arithmetic, by hand, before the sweep

The Biplane's node-0 row was `(−13,010, 101,270)`. At 82° (`whole_degrees`), `y = (right·sin − fwd·cos) / 1000` =
−26.98, which truncates to −26. Theirs is −27. At 81° and at 83°, `x` or `y` misses by more, so the degree is right
and the row is not. Item 1200's comment explains why: the row is "the centre of the region that reproduces every
round" of run437's 26 rounds. That is a region, not a value, and the 82° heading falls outside the part of it that
the centre represents.
