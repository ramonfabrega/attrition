## What was built

`minimap::blips::build` lays the five layers off `runner.sim()`, and `draw` paints them one pixel a square:
the atlas (`blipmap.tga`) sampled bilinearly, times the vertex colour, the pixel centres on the integers.
`overlays` draws them between the borders and the view's rectangle. The still passes the photograph's
selection, slot 0's capital, which is the white city.

Settled by the quad test, against 4596a's design:
- **`stat_mode` 1.** `render_blips` switches on the profile's minimap mode. Under 1, a unit needs a
  combat-role type (`+0x2c8 & 0x10000`, `Profile::combat_role`). A building needs `attack` non-zero or a
  line whose basic type is `MILITARY_TRAINER` (`8cd944`–`8cda6d`). **This settles 4593c**: player 0's nine
  units are eight type 50 and one type 75, none of them combat-role. Player 1's own fourteen are not drawn
  either.
- **The console sees everything** (`SEES_ALL`). The console's flags are 7, so `semaphore[0] & 0x42` must be
  set. Our fog has player 0 seeing 0 of 36 goods, and the packet draws all 36.
- **The chain walk** over `ObjectChain` `up`/`up_who`. Ours agrees with the dump's words for 0/0 to 0/9 and
  1/0 to 1/15 (0/10 apart).
- **The blink.** `UnitOrder::is_attack` is the order slot `+0x18`, named by the PDB (`vftable offset = 24`).
  Three attackers at player 0's capital have no blip, so `graphic_tick & 8` was set. The test pins
  `BLINK_RUN1564`, the still passes `true`, and the live client takes the frame's bit 3.

The packet's units sit on the world at `PHOTO_FRAME` + 1 (3280) to the 0.01. At 3294 they have moved on,
which is the same pairing every run1564 row already uses.
