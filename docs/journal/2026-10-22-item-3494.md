## The hook

Parked 3463a gave the predicate, and the reading held. `IFaceResources::on_spot_move@0081b4e0` writes
`mouseover->type |= 0x200` and `t = good`. `Scene::render@008b3270.c:161-162` then sets `type = 0` before
`Scene::describe_mouseover@008b47e0` (`:517`), and `MouseOver::describe@007d09b0` has no case for 0.

`tools/trace/hover.h` (new) holds the `hover=N` cfg key and the predicate `hover_due`: the key is on, the pin is
installed, and a click line has moved it. `tracer.c` adds a hook site at `describe_mouseover`'s prologue (`push esi;
mov esi,ecx; lea ecx,[esi+0x104]`, 9 bytes, RVA 0x4b47e0) as kind `K_HOVER`. Like `K_LOOP`, it is installed, and
excluded from the coverage stubs, only under the key. On entry it **re-sends the pinned `OnMouseMove`** through the
shipped handler slot (`g_msg_orig[0x14 / 4]`). It does not call `on_spot_move` with that function's last arguments,
for three reasons:
- The re-send takes the same path the `@cursor` line took: the window lookup and the spot test.
- No second hook is needed to capture arguments.
- It cannot spoil `describe`'s gate. `IFaceMainBase::on_mouse_move@00811210` stamps `last_move` only when the point
  changes, and `MouseOver::same_as_before@007d2810` holds for an unchanged good.

`I_HOVER` (30) records `type` before and after the re-send, once a frame. `click.h` sets `g_hover_moved` when a click
line runs. The runner gained `--hover` (`live_session.py` stages `hover=1`, and `unattended_capture.py` passes it and
records `hover` in the receipt's `staged`).

The cost: under the key, `describe_mouseover` has no coverage stub, so it records no `HIT` of its own. Its `I_HOVER`
records stand for it.
