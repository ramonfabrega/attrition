# Item 4069: the figures' facing — a sign flip, not an offset

Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5[1m]`, `--effort medium`), lane `lane-4069`, base `dc1e1fd3`. A `render` item
(`docs/RENDERER.md` §5.3, §3.6; parked 4067a, 4065c). I ran no capture and took no new packet. The branch does not
touch `crates/sim`; the client reads `sim::data::pivot` and changes nothing in it. So the gate is the scoped render

## What was booked, and what the evidence says

The booking: posed, candidate C reads 1 of 23 at the drawn facing and 5 of 23 with 90° added to every figure. That
came from a 15° sweep, which cannot tell a constant offset from a sign flip. **It is a sign flip.** The client drew
every figure as the original's map reflected in world y through its anchor. Drawn by the original's map, the posed
frame reads **6 of 23** (`RUN1559B_3294_POSED` 1 → 6, one above the booking). Posed is now the client's default.
The rest row moves **3 → 2** (`RUN1559B_3294`). Run1441's camera, scored posed, holds **0 of 25**, still the
made-to-fail. K is untouched at **59 of 59**: no draw-list entry moved, and the change is the mesh's model-to-world
map only.

## The chain, from the listing

- **Which angle.** In `GuyOut::draw@005dc490`, `edi` is `this` from `5dc4b0` to the end; nothing else writes it.
  `5dcac4`–`5dcb3b` load `[edi+0x18]` and `[edi+0x1c]`, `GuyData::angle` and `last_angle` (types.txt). The code
  blends `last_angle + (angle − last_angle)·graphic_percent` with the wrap, calls `fast_angle_to_degrees` at `5dcb40`,
  adds `0xb696c0` (180.0) at `5dcb45`, and stores into `GraphicChad::angle` (`+0x3c`, `-0xa4(%ebp)`). The client
  draws at a blend of 0, the frame's own `angle`.
- **No sign in the degrees.** `fast_angle_to_degrees@00a28f70` builds its table from `angle_to_degrees(i << 24)` and
  indexes it by the top byte (`sar 0x18`, `movzbl`). `angle_to_degrees@00a28e00` is a rounded `a·360/2³²`, which is
  monotone. It differs from a floor at full precision on 128 of 256 steps, by 1°.
- **The consumer.** `GraphicPieces::draw_unit@008e7da0` truncates `chad.angle` into `Transform<float>::rotate_z`
  (`8e8887`, and `8e8706` for a parent). It adds a per-slot offset (`0xc8c774`) only for a slot in `0..7`
  (`8e80dc`–`8e8119`).
- **The sense and the mirror.** These come from the original's own model-to-world map. `GraphicPieces::get_position
  @0090b750`'s port, `sim::data::pivot::release_offset_at`, is packet-backed at all 256 headings (items 2304, 3975)
  and gives `v = s·(px·c + py·s_, px·s_ − py·c, pz)` at `d = fast_angle_to_degrees(angle − 2³¹)`. That `d` is the
  draw's degree mod 360. In matrix form this is `R(d)·diag(1, −1)`: the chad's `sy = −1`
  (`GraphicChad::GraphicChad@008e22f0`, item 4065), then a turn with x towards y. The client's `placement` built
  `(px·c + py·s_, −px·s_ + py·c)`. That is the same map with world y negated: a reflection, which flips every
  heading's sense. The error is twice the heading's angle from the world x axis, so on the riflemen near −45° it
  read as a quarter turn.

The comparison can fail, and I watched it fail: the old map scores 1 posed on the same frame.
`client::world::tests::a_figure_is_turned_as_get_position_turns_a_model_point` holds `guy_placement` against
`release_offset_at` at every top-byte facing, to the truncated unit, and requires the unmirrored turn to miss. With
`guy_placement`'s mirror removed it went red at facing `0x0` (drawn y −192 against the port's 192), and green again
when I restored it.
